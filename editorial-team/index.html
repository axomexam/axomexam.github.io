    Editorial Team

    The Axomexam Editorial Team: who plans, writes, fact-checks and reviews the bilingual study material published on axomexam.in.

    axomexam.in is published by a small editorial team that works only on study material for this platform. The team is led by its founder and editor-in-chief, and every page is checked before it is published. This page lists the people responsible for the site and explains what each of them does.

    Editorial leadership

    Bapon Barmon

    Founder & Editor-in-Chief

    Graduate (History Honours)

    Sets the editorial direction, maps topics to the official syllabus, carries out the final review before publishing and is responsible for corrections. He is the accountable publisher of axomexam.in.

    Harsh Barman

    Team Manager

    M.A. in History

    Manages the editorial workflow, schedules reviews and coordinates the team. Reviews history, Assam GK and social-studies material before it is published.

    Abhik Roy

    Team Member

    MBA

    Supports content planning, publication operations and quality checks, and helps keep the platform free and accessible for candidates.

    Gautam Das

    Team Member

    B.Sc.

    Supports review and fact-checking of general science, mathematics and computer-awareness content.

    Who does what

    • Editorial planning and syllabus mapping. Bapon Barmon tracks official notifications and syllabi, decides which topics belong to which exam section, and retires material that is no longer part of the syllabus.
    • Workflow and subject review. Harsh Barman manages the review schedule and checks history, Assam GK and social-studies content.
    • Publication and quality checks. Abhik Roy supports planning, publication and quality checks across sections.
    • Science and mathematics review. Gautam Das supports fact-checking and review of science, mathematics and computer-awareness material.
    • Language review. The Assamese version of each page is checked for terminology, spelling and natural usage before publishing.

    How a page is reviewed

    1. The topic is matched to the official syllabus or notification.
    2. A draft is written in English and Assamese with answers and explanations.
    3. A second reviewer checks the facts and the working of every numerical or reasoning question.
    4. The Assamese text is reviewed for language quality.
    5. The page is published with a published date and a last-updated date, and is revisited when the exam pattern changes.

    The full process, including our policy on AI-assisted drafting, is described in the editorial policy.

    Standards every contributor follows

    • Write original explanations; do not copy text, questions or notes from commercial books or other websites.
    • Check facts against official or public sources and do not present an unverified claim as fact.
    • Show the working for numerical and reasoning questions, not only the answer.
    • Keep the English and Assamese versions equivalent in coverage and difficulty.
    • Correct reported errors promptly and record the change.
    • Keep advertising and editorial decisions separate.

    Independence and funding

    axomexam.in is independently run and free to use. It is funded by advertising and does not accept payment for including any exam, coaching institute, book or product in its study material. Team members do not publish sponsored material as editorial content.

    Contributions and corrections

    Teachers, subject experts and candidates who spot an error are welcome to write to axomexam@outlook.com. Please include the page address and the source for the correction so that it can be verified quickly. You can also use the contact page. Corrections are reviewed by Bapon Barmon and the editorial team before a page is changed.

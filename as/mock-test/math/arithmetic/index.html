    A sample of the arithmetic questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. What is the smallest prime number? সৰ্বসৰু মৌলিক সংখ্যাটো কি?
      (A) 1   (B) 2   (C) 0   (D) 3
      Answer: 2 Explanation: 2 is the smallest prime number (1 is not a prime number).
    • Q2. What is the HCF of 36 and 84? 36 আৰু 84 ৰ মহা সাধাৰণ গুণনীয়ক (ম.সা.গু.) কি?
      (A) 6   (B) 18   (C) 12   (D) 24
      Answer: 12 Explanation: 36 = 2² × 3² and 84 = 2² × 3 × 7, so HCF = 2² × 3 = 12.
    • Q3. What is the LCM of 12, 18 and 24? 12, 18 আৰু 24 ৰ লঘিষ্ঠ সাধাৰণ গুণিতক (ল.সা.গু.) কি?
      (A) 72   (B) 48   (C) 36   (D) 96
      Answer: 72 Explanation: 12 = 2² × 3, 18 = 2 × 3², 24 = 2³ × 3, so LCM = 2³ × 3² = 72.
    • Q4. 0.75 expressed as a fraction in lowest terms is: সৰলতম ৰূপত 0.75 ৰ ভগ্নাংশ কি?
      (A) 2/3   (B) 3/4   (C) 4/5   (D) 5/8
      Answer: 3/4 Explanation: 0.75 = 75/100 = 3/4 in lowest terms.

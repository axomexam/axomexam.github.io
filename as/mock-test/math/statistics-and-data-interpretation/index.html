    A sample of the statistics and data interpretation questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. The arithmetic mean of 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 is: 10, 20, 30, 40 আৰু 50 ৰ পাটিগাণিতিক গড় কি?
      (A) 25   (B) 35   (C) 30   (D) 40
      Answer: 30 Explanation: Arithmetic mean = sum of observations / number of observations = (10+20+30+40+50)/5 = 150/5 = 30.
    • Q2. The median of 4, 7, 9, 12 and 15 is: 4, 7, 9, 12 আৰু 15 ৰ মধ্যমা কি?
      (A) 7   (B) 12   (C) 9   (D) 15
      Answer: 9 Explanation: The data is already in ascending order and n = 5 (odd), so median = ((n+1)/2)th term = 3rd term = 9.
    • Q3. The mode of 5, 6, 6, 7, 8, 6 and 9 is: 5, 6, 6, 7, 8, 6 আৰু 9 ৰ প্ৰচলক কি?
      (A) 6   (B) 7   (C) 8   (D) 5
      Answer: 6 Explanation: Mode is the most frequently occurring value. Here 6 appears three times, more than any other value, so mode = 6.
    • Q4. The range of 15, 22, 8, 30 and 18 is: 15, 22, 8, 30 আৰু 18 ৰ পৰিসৰ কিমান?
      (A) 18   (B) 30   (C) 22   (D) 8
      Answer: 22 Explanation: Range = maximum - minimum = 30 - 8 = 22.

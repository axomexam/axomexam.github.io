    A sample of the economics questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. Since 2017, the Union Budget of India is presented on which date? ২০১৭ চনৰ পৰা ভাৰতৰ কেন্দ্ৰীয় বাজেট কোন তাৰিখে উপস্থাপন কৰা হয়?
      (A) 28th February ২৮ ফেব্ৰুৱাৰী   (B) 1st March ১ মাৰ্চ   (C) 1st February ১ ফেব্ৰুৱাৰী   (D) 31st January ৩১ জানুৱাৰী
      Answer: 1st February Explanation: Since 2017 the Union Budget of India is presented on 1 February, instead of the earlier practice of presentation on the last day of February.
    • Q2. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was established in which year? ভাৰতীয় ৰিজাৰ্ভ বেংক (RBI) কোন চনত প্ৰতিষ্ঠা হৈছিল?
      (A) 1947 ১৯৪৭   (B) 1949 ১৯৪৯   (C) 1935 ১৯৩৫   (D) 1950 ১৯৫০
      Answer: 1935 Explanation: The Reserve Bank of India was established on 1 April 1935 under the RBI Act, 1934.
    • Q3. Assam accounts for approximately what share of India's total tea production? ভাৰতৰ মুঠ চাহ উৎপাদনৰ প্ৰায় কিমান অংশ অসমত উৎপাদন হয়?
      (A) Less than 10% ১০% ৰ তলত   (B) About 10% প্ৰায় ১০%   (C) More than half (above 50%) আধাতকৈ বেছি (৫০% ৰ ওপৰত)   (D) About 25% প্ৰায় ২৫%
      Answer: More than half (above 50%) Explanation: Assam produces more than half (over 50%) of India's total tea, making it the largest tea-producing state.
    • Q4. The Tocklai Tea Research Institute, the world's oldest tea research station, is located in which district of Assam? বিশ্বৰ আটাইতকৈ পুৰণি চাহ গৱেষণা কেন্দ্ৰ টকলাই চাহ গৱেষণা প্ৰতিষ্ঠান অসমৰ কোন জিলাত অৱস্থিত?
      (A) Sivasagar শিৱসাগৰ   (B) Dibrugarh ডিব্ৰুগড়   (C) Jorhat যোৰহাট   (D) Tinsukia তিনিচুকীয়া
      Answer: Jorhat Explanation: The Tocklai Tea Research Institute, the world's oldest tea research station, is located at Jorhat in Assam.

    How to use the economics mock test

    • Attempt a set under the timer without pausing, to copy exam conditions.
    • Read the explanation for every question you got wrong immediately after the set.
    • Note the topic behind each mistake and revise that topic before the next set.
    • Re-attempt the same set after a week and compare your score.

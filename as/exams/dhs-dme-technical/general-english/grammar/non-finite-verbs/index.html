    Non-Finite Verbs: study notes

    Read this before you attempt the questions. It is written for the DHS & DME Technical Grade III paper.

    The three non-finite forms

    A finite verb changes with the subject and the tense: I go, she goes, they went. A non-finite verb does not change in that way, and it cannot be the only verb of a sentence. English has three non-finite forms: the infinitive, which is to plus the base verb, as in to write; the gerund, which is the ing form used as a noun, as in writing; and the participle, which is either the present participle writing or the past participle written. The DHS and DME Technical Grade III paper tests these forms through verb patterns, error spotting and sentence correction, not through definitions.

    The infinitive has several jobs. It can be the subject of a sentence, though this is formal: To err is human. It can be the object of a verb: She wants to sing. It can express purpose: He went to the market to buy rice. It can follow an adjective: I am glad to meet you. It can follow a noun: He has the ability to solve it. And it can be the complement of be: My aim is to serve. The bare infinitive, without to, follows modals, let, make, see, hear, feel and watch: She can swim; Let him go; I saw him leave.

    The gerund is the ing form doing the work of a noun. It can be the subject: Swimming is good exercise. It can be the object: She enjoys reading. It can follow a preposition: He is good at painting; She insisted on coming. It can follow certain verbs: avoid, enjoy, mind, finish, suggest, recommend, consider, admit, deny, postpone, practise, risk, keep and look forward to. After a preposition, the verb must be a gerund, never an infinitive: She is interested in learning, not interested in to learn.

    Some verbs take either form but with a change of meaning. Stop doing means to end an action; stop to do means to pause in order to do something else. Remember doing means to recall a past action; remember to do means not to forget a future duty. Forget doing means to lose the memory of a past action; forget to do means to fail to do a duty. Regret doing means to be sorry about the past; regret to do is used for announcing bad news. Try doing means to experiment; try to do means to make an effort. Go on doing means to continue; go on to do means to move to the next item. Mean doing means to involve as a result; mean to do means to intend.

    The present participle has two main uses. It forms the continuous tenses with be: She is writing. It also works as an adjective: a running tap, a crying child, an interesting book. The past participle forms the perfect tenses with have and the passive with be: She has written; The letter was written. It also works as an adjective: a broken window, a written test, a tired traveller. The difference between an interesting book and an interested reader is the difference between the present participle, which causes the feeling, and the past participle, which receives it.

    The dangling participle is a common error. A participle at the beginning of a sentence must describe the subject of the main clause. Walking down the road, I saw a snake is correct, because I was walking. Walking down the road, a snake was seen is wrong, because the snake was not walking. The same rule applies to the perfect participle: Having finished the work, she went home is correct, because she had finished; Having finished the work, the lights were switched off is wrong. The correction is either to name the doer in the main clause or to expand the participle into a full clause.

    The possessive comes before a gerund when the gerund is the object of a verb or a preposition and the doer is a pronoun or a name. We say I do not like his smoking, not I do not like him smoking, in careful writing. We say She objected to Rina's going, not to Rina going. In informal English the object form is common, but the examination usually rewards the possessive. The reason is that the gerund is a noun, and a noun is modified by a possessive, not by an object pronoun.

    The infinitive after certain adjectives and nouns is a fixed pattern. Adjectives such as glad, sorry, happy, sad, eager, ready, able, unable, easy, difficult, hard and anxious take the infinitive: I am ready to go; It is difficult to say. Nouns such as ability, attempt, desire, decision, effort, need, plan, promise, refusal, willingness and wish take the infinitive: a decision to leave; a promise to help. A wrong preposition or a gerund in place of the infinitive is a common distractor.

    The verb help takes either the full or the bare infinitive: She helped me to carry it, or She helped me carry it. The verbs make, let, see, hear, feel, watch and notice take the bare infinitive in the active voice, but the full infinitive in the passive: They made him work; He was made to work. This active-passive difference is asked directly, and the passive form with to is the one candidates forget.

    A useful way to revise the topic is to group the verbs by pattern. Group one takes only the gerund: enjoy, avoid, mind, finish, suggest, recommend, consider, admit, deny, postpone, practise, risk, keep, dislike, imagine. Group two takes only the infinitive: want, wish, hope, expect, decide, promise, refuse, agree, offer, plan, manage, fail, afford, learn. Group three takes either with a change of meaning: stop, remember, forget, regret, try, go on, mean. Learning the groups as lists is faster than learning them one by one, and the examination rarely goes outside these lists.

    Rules to remember

    • The infinitive is to plus the base verb; the gerund is the ing form used as a noun; the participle is the ing or past participle form used as an adjective or in a tense.
    • After a preposition, use the gerund, never the infinitive.
    • Modals, let, make, see, hear, feel and watch take the bare infinitive.
    • In the passive, make, see and hear take the full infinitive with to.
    • Stop, remember, forget, regret, try, go on and mean change meaning with the form that follows.
    • A participle at the start of a sentence must describe the subject of the main clause.
    • Use a possessive before a gerund in careful writing.

    Worked examples

    She is interested in ___ (learn) French. The preposition in requires a gerund, so the answer is learning. The infinitive to learn would be wrong after a preposition, even though the meaning is clear. This is the single most common non-finite error in the bank.

    I remember ___ (lock) the door before I left. The action of locking happened in the past, so the gerund locking is correct. If the sentence meant that the speaker must not forget a future duty, it would be I must remember to lock the door.

    They made him ___ (work) late. The verb make takes the bare infinitive in the active voice, so the answer is work, without to. In the passive, the sentence becomes He was made to work late, with to.

    ___ (walk) down the road, I saw a snake. The participle walking describes I, the subject of the main clause, so it is correct. If the main clause were a snake was seen, the sentence would be dangling and would need to be rewritten as While I was walking down the road, I saw a snake.

    Where marks disappear

    • Using an infinitive after a preposition.
    • Using to after make, let, see or hear in the active voice.
    • Omitting to after make, see or hear in the passive voice.
    • Confusing stop doing with stop to do.
    • Confusing remember doing with remember to do.
    • Leaving a participle dangling at the start of a sentence.
    • Using an object pronoun instead of a possessive before a gerund.
    Exam tip: When you see a blank after a preposition or after enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest or finish, write the ing form at once. When you see a modal, let, make, see or hear, write the base form without to. Those two reflexes cover most of the questions on this topic.

    অসমাপিকা ক্ৰিয়া: অধ্যয়ন টোকা

    প্ৰশ্নবোৰত হাত দিয়াৰ আগতে এইখিনি পঢ়ি লওক। তলৰ প্ৰতিটো কথা DHS আৰু DME কাৰিকৰী গ্ৰেড III পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে লিখা।

    তিনিটা অ-সীমিত ৰূপ

    সীমিত ক্ৰিয়া (finite verb) কৰ্তা আৰু কাল অনুসৰি সলনি হয়: I go, she goes, they went। অ-সীমিত ক্ৰিয়া (non-finite verb) সেইদৰে নসলনি, আৰু সেয়া বাক্যৰ একমাত্ৰ ক্ৰিয়া হ'ব নোৱাৰে। ইংৰাজীত তিনিটা অ-সীমিত ৰূপ আছে: infinitive, যি to ৰ লগত base verb, যেনে to write; gerund, যি ing ৰূপ বিশেষ্য হিচাপে ব্যৱহৃত, যেনে writing; আৰু participle, যি present participle writing বা past participle written। DHS আৰু DME কাৰিকৰী গ্ৰেড III পত্ৰত এই ৰূপবোৰ সংজ্ঞাৰে নহয়, verb pattern, ভুল নিৰ্ণয় আৰু বাক্য শুধৰণিৰে সোধা হয়।

    Infinitive ৰ কেইবাটাও কাম আছে। ই বাক্যৰ কৰ্তা হ'ব পাৰে, যদিও ই আনুষ্ঠানিক: To err is human। ই ক্ৰিয়াৰ object হ'ব পাৰে: She wants to sing। ই উদ্দেশ্য বুজাব পাৰে: He went to the market to buy rice। ই বিশেষণৰ পিছত আহিব পাৰে: I am glad to meet you। ই বিশেষ্যৰ পিছত আহিব পাৰে: He has the ability to solve it। আৰু ই be ৰ complement হ'ব পাৰে: My aim is to serve। to ৰ অবিহনে bare infinitive modal, let, make, see, hear, feel আৰু watch ৰ পিছত আহে: She can swim; Let him go; I saw him leave।

    Gerund হৈছে ing ৰূপ যি বিশেষ্যৰ কাম কৰে। ই কৰ্তা হ'ব পাৰে: Swimming is good exercise। ই object হ'ব পাৰে: She enjoys reading। ই পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত আহিব পাৰে: He is good at painting; She insisted on coming। ই কিছুমান ক্ৰিয়াৰ পিছত আহিব পাৰে: avoid, enjoy, mind, finish, suggest, recommend, consider, admit, deny, postpone, practise, risk, keep আৰু look forward to। পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত ক্ৰিয়া gerund হ'বই লাগে, infinitive কেতিয়াও নহয়: She is interested in learning, interested in to learn নহয়।

    কিছুমান ক্ৰিয়াই দুয়ো ৰূপ লয়, কিন্তু অৰ্থ সলনি হয়। Stop doing মানে কাম এটা বন্ধ কৰা; stop to do মানে আন কাম কৰিবলৈ বিৰতি লোৱা। Remember doing মানে অতীতৰ কাম মনত পেলোৱা; remember to do মানে ভৱিষ্যতৰ কৰ্তব্য নাপাহৰা। Forget doing মানে অতীতৰ স্মৃতি হেৰুওৱা; forget to do মানে কৰ্তব্য কৰিবলৈ বিফল হোৱা। Regret doing মানে অতীতৰ বাবে অনুশোচনা; regret to do বেয়া বাতৰি জনাবলৈ ব্যৱহাৰ হয়। Try doing মানে পৰীক্ষা কৰা; try to do মানে চেষ্টা কৰা। Go on doing মানে অব্যাহত ৰখা; go on to do মানে পৰৱৰ্তী বিন্দুলৈ যোৱা। Mean doing মানে ফলস্বৰূপে জড়িত হোৱা; mean to do মানে অভিপ্ৰায় কৰা।

    Present participle ৰ দুটা মূল ব্যৱহাৰ। ই be ৰ লগত continuous tense গঠন কৰে: She is writing। ই বিশেষণ হিচাপেও কাম কৰে: a running tap, a crying child, an interesting book। Past participle এ have ৰ লগত perfect tense আৰু be ৰ লগত passive গঠন কৰে: She has written; The letter was written। ই বিশেষণ হিচাপেও কাম কৰে: a broken window, a written test, a tired traveller। an interesting book আৰু an interested reader ৰ পাৰ্থক্য হৈছে present participle, যি অনুভৱ সৃষ্টি কৰে, আৰু past participle, যি অনুভৱ গ্ৰহণ কৰে, তাৰ পাৰ্থক্য।

    Dangling participle এটা সাধাৰণ ভুল। বাক্যৰ আৰম্ভণিত থকা participle এ মূল উপবাক্যৰ কৰ্তাক বৰ্ণনা কৰিব লাগে। Walking down the road, I saw a snake শুদ্ধ, কাৰণ মই খোজ কাঢ়ি আছিলোঁ। Walking down the road, a snake was seen ভুল, কাৰণ সাপে খোজ কঢ়া নাছিল। একেই নিয়ম perfect participle ৰ বাবেও: Having finished the work, she went home শুদ্ধ, কাৰণ তাই কাম শেষ কৰিছিল; Having finished the work, the lights were switched off ভুল। শুধৰণি হৈছে মূল উপবাক্যত কৰ্তা নাম দিয়া, বা participle ক সম্পূৰ্ণ উপবাক্যলৈ বঢ়াই দিয়া।

    Gerund ৰ আগত possessive আহে যেতিয়া gerund ক্ৰিয়া বা পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ object হয় আৰু কৰ্তা সৰ্বনাম বা নাম হয়। যতনশীল লিখাত আমি I do not like his smoking লিখোঁ, I do not like him smoking নহয়। আমি She objected to Rina's going লিখোঁ, to Rina going নহয়। অনানুষ্ঠানিক ইংৰাজীত object form সাধাৰণ, কিন্তু পৰীক্ষাই সাধাৰণতে possessive ক পুৰস্কৃত কৰে। কাৰণ gerund এটা বিশেষ্য, আৰু বিশেষ্যক possessive ৰে বৰ্ণনা কৰা হয়, object pronoun ৰে নহয়।

    কিছুমান বিশেষণ আৰু বিশেষ্যৰ পিছত infinitive এটা নিৰ্দিষ্ট আৰ্হি। Glad, sorry, happy, sad, eager, ready, able, unable, easy, difficult, hard, anxious যেনে বিশেষণে infinitive লয়: I am ready to go; It is difficult to say। Ability, attempt, desire, decision, effort, need, plan, promise, refusal, willingness, wish যেনে বিশেষ্যই infinitive লয়: a decision to leave; a promise to help। ভুল পূৰ্বসৰ্গ বা infinitive ৰ ঠাইত gerund এটা সাধাৰণ বিভ্ৰান্তিকৰ বিকল্প।

    Help ক্ৰিয়াই পূৰ্ণ বা bare infinitive দুয়োটা লয়: She helped me to carry it, বা She helped me carry it। Make, let, see, hear, feel, watch, notice এ active voice ত bare infinitive লয়, কিন্তু passive ত পূৰ্ণ infinitive লয়: They made him work; He was made to work। এই active-passive পাৰ্থক্য প্ৰত্যক্ষভাৱে সোধা হয়, আৰু passive ত to থকা ৰূপটোৱেই প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে পাহৰে।

    বিষয়টো পুনৰালোচনাৰ এটা সহজ উপায় হৈছে ক্ৰিয়াবোৰ আৰ্হি অনুসৰি গোট কৰা। প্ৰথম গোটে কেৱল gerund লয়: enjoy, avoid, mind, finish, suggest, recommend, consider, admit, deny, postpone, practise, risk, keep, dislike, imagine। দ্বিতীয় গোটে কেৱল infinitive লয়: want, wish, hope, expect, decide, promise, refuse, agree, offer, plan, manage, fail, afford, learn। তৃতীয় গোটে অৰ্থ সলনি হ'লে দুয়োটা লয়: stop, remember, forget, regret, try, go on, mean। গোটবোৰ তালিকা হিচাপে শিকা এটা এটাকৈ শিকাতকৈ দ্ৰুত, আৰু পৰীক্ষাই বিৰলভাৱে এই তালিকাৰ বাহিৰলৈ যায়।

    Perfect gerund আৰু passive gerund ৰ ৰূপও চিনি ৰাখিব লাগে। Having finished the work, she left ত having finished হৈছে perfect participle, যি কামটো আগতে সম্পূৰ্ণ হোৱা দেখুৱায়। I remember having met him ত having met হৈছে perfect gerund, যি আগৰ অভিজ্ঞতা মনত পেলোৱা বুজায়। Passive gerund being + past participle: She hates being praised। Passive infinitive to be + past participle: She wants to be praised। Perfect infinitive to have + past participle: He seems to have forgotten। এই ৰূপবোৰ প্ৰায়ে tense আৰু voice ৰ প্ৰশ্নৰ সৈতে মিলি আহে, আৰু ৰূপটো ভুল কৰিলে অৰ্থই সলনি হয়।

    মনত ৰখাৰ নিয়ম

    • Infinitive হৈছে to + base verb; gerund হৈছে বিশেষ্য হিচাপে ব্যৱহৃত ing ৰূপ; participle হৈছে tense বা বিশেষণত ব্যৱহৃত ing বা past participle ৰূপ।
    • পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত gerund, infinitive কেতিয়াও নহয়।
    • Modal, let, make, see, hear, feel, watch এ bare infinitive লয়।
    • Passive ত make, see, hear এ to ৰে পূৰ্ণ infinitive লয়।
    • Stop, remember, forget, regret, try, go on, mean ৰ অৰ্থ পৰৱৰ্তী ৰূপৰ ওপৰত নিৰ্ভৰ কৰে।
    • বাক্যৰ আৰম্ভণিত থকা participle এ মূল উপবাক্যৰ কৰ্তাক বৰ্ণনা কৰিব লাগে।
    • যতনশীল লিখাত gerund ৰ আগত possessive ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক।
    • Perfect gerund ৰ ৰূপ having + past participle আৰু passive gerund ৰ ৰূপ being + past participle, দুয়োটা চিনি ৰাখক।

    সমাধান কৰা উদাহৰণ

    She is interested in ___ (learn) French। পূৰ্বসৰ্গ in এ gerund বিচাৰে, গতিকে উত্তৰ learning। to learn ভুল, কাৰণ পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত infinitive নাহে। এইটোৱেই প্ৰশ্নগোটৰ আটাইতকৈ সাধাৰণ non-finite ভুল।

    I remember ___ (lock) the door before I left। তলা দিয়া কাম অতীতত হৈছিল, গতিকে gerund locking শুদ্ধ। যদি বাক্যই ভৱিষ্যতৰ কৰ্তব্য নাপাহৰা বুজালেহেঁতেন, তেন্তে I must remember to lock the door হ'লহেঁতেন।

    They made him ___ (work) late। Make এ active voice ত bare infinitive লয়, গতিকে উত্তৰ work, to ৰ অবিহনে। Passive ত বাক্যটো He was made to work late হয়, to ৰ সৈতে।

    ___ (walk) down the road, I saw a snake। Participle walking এ I, মূল উপবাক্যৰ কৰ্তাক বৰ্ণনা কৰে, গতিকে শুদ্ধ। যদি মূল উপবাক্য a snake was seen হ'লহেঁতেন, বাক্যটো dangling হ'লহেঁতেন আৰু While I was walking down the road, I saw a snake হিচাপে পুনৰ লিখিব লাগিলেহেঁতেন।

    য'ত নম্বৰ হেৰায়

    • পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত infinitive ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
    • Active voice ত make, let, see, hear ৰ পিছত to ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
    • Passive voice ত make, see, hear ৰ পিছত to বাদ দিয়া।
    • stop doing আৰু stop to do ৰ মাজত বিভ্ৰান্ত হোৱা।
    • remember doing আৰু remember to do ৰ মাজত বিভ্ৰান্ত হোৱা।
    • বাক্যৰ আৰম্ভণিত participle dangling ৰখা।
    • Gerund ৰ আগত object pronoun ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা, possessive ৰ সলনি।
    পৰীক্ষাৰ টিপ: পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত বা enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest, finish ৰ পিছত ৰিক্ত স্থান দেখিলে লগে লগে ing ৰূপ লিখক। Modal, let, make, see বা hear দেখিলে to ৰ অবিহনে base form লিখক। এই দুটা অভ্যাসেই এই বিষয়ৰ বেছিভাগ প্ৰশ্ন সামৰি লয়, আৰু প্ৰতিটো ভুলৰ পিছত কোনটো গোটৰ ক্ৰিয়া আছিল তাক লিখি ৰাখিলে পুনৰাবৃত্তি কমি যায়।

    (B) Imperative আদেশাত্মক
    (C) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    (D) Optative ইচ্ছাসূচক
    B) আদেশাত্মক
    Answer: B) Imperative
    'Shut the door' য়ে আদেশ দিয়ে, সেয়ে ই imperative।
    Explanation: 'Shut the door' gives a command, so it is imperative.
    তেওঁ আৰু তেওঁৰ বন্ধুসকলে আহি আছে।
    Q47. He as well as his friends ___ coming.
    (A) are
    (B) were
    (C) is
    (D) have
    Answer: C) is
    'as well as' ৰ সৈতে verb প্ৰথম subject 'he' ৰ লগত মিলে।
    Explanation: With 'as well as', the verb agrees with the first subject 'he'.
    তেওঁ বা মই যাব লাগিব।
    Q48. Either he or I ___ to go.
    (A) are
    (B) is
    (C) am
    (D) were
    Answer: C) am
    'either ... or' ৰ সৈতে verb ওচৰৰ subject 'I' ৰ লগত মিলে, সেয়ে 'am' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: With 'either ... or', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'I', so 'am' is used.
    'arrival' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ কি?
    Q49. The opposite of 'arrival' is:
    (A) coming
    (B) departure
    (C) reach
    (D) entry
    Answer: B) departure
    'departure' হৈছে 'arrival' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ।
    Explanation: 'Departure' is the opposite of 'arrival'.
    খালী ঠাই পূৰ কৰক: 'He is ___ M.A. in English.'
    Q50. Fill in the blank: 'He is ___ M.A. in English.'
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: B) an
    'M.A.' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'em' স্বৰ ধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'M.A.' begins with the vowel sound 'em', so 'an' is used.
    তাই ইংৰাজীত ___ এম.এ.।
    Q51. She is ___ M.A. in English.
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: B) an
    M আখৰৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'em', যি স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: The letter M is pronounced 'em', which begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    ___ হিমালয় পৃথিৱীৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ পৰ্বতমালা।
    Q52. ___ Himalayas are the highest mountain range in the world.
    (A) A
    (B) An
    (C) The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: C) The
    পৰ্বতমালাৰ নামৰ আগত 'The' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'The' is used before the names of mountain ranges.
    'mouse' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    Q53. The plural of 'mouse' is:
    (A) mouses
    (B) mice
    (C) mices
    (D) mouse

    'child' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?

    ভেড়াৰ দলৰ সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?

    (D) swarm

    দুজন ল'ৰাৰ ___ শাস্তি পাইছিল।
    Q56. ___ of the two boys was punished.
    (A) Both
    (B) All
    (C) Either
    (D) Neither
    Answer: D) Neither
    'Neither' মানে দুজনৰ এজনো নহয়; ই singular verb লয়; সেয়ে 'Neither of the two boys was punished' শুদ্ধ।
    Explanation: 'Neither' means not one of the two; it takes a singular verb, so 'Neither of the two boys was punished' is correct.
    কালি মই কিনা কিতাপখন আমোদজনক।

    (A) which
    (B) who
    (C) whom
    (D) whose
    Answer: A) which
    বস্তুৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত relative pronoun হৈছে 'which'।
    Explanation: 'Which' is the relative pronoun used for things.
    তাই ভুলটোৰ বাবে নিজকে দোষ দিলে।
    Q58. She blamed ___ for the mistake.
    (A) her
    (B) hers
    (C) herself
    (D) she
    Answer: C) herself
    কৰ্তা আৰু কৰ্ম একেই ব্যক্তি হ'লে reflexive pronoun 'herself' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Herself' is the reflexive pronoun used when the subject and object are the same person.
    'little' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী কি?

    ___ সূৰ্য পূব দিশে উদয় হয়।

    Answer: C) The

    Explanation: 'The' is used before unique objects like the sun.
    তেওঁ গণিতত পাৰদৰ্শী।
    Q62. He is good ___ mathematics.
    (A) in
    (B) at
    (C) on
    (D) with
    Answer: B) at

    Explanation: The correct preposition after 'good' meaning skilled is 'at'.
    তাই ২০১৫ চনৰ পৰা ইয়াত বাস কৰি আছে।

    (A) for
    (B) from
    (C) since
    (D) by
    Answer: C) since
    perfect tense ত সময়ৰ নিৰ্দিষ্ট বিন্দুৰ সৈতে 'since' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Since' is used with a point of time in the perfect tense.
    তেওঁ অসুস্থ আছিল, ___ তেওঁ শ্ৰেণীত উপস্থিত থাকিল।

    Explanation: 'But' shows contrast between being ill and attending the class.

    তেওঁ যোৱা বছৰ দিল্লীলৈ ___।

    (C) went
    (D) has gone
    Answer: C) went
    'last year' এ অতীত সময় বুজায়; সেয়ে simple past 'went' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Last year' refers to past time, so the simple past 'went' is used.
    তেওঁলোক ৰাতিপুৱাৰ পৰা ইয়াত ___।

    Explanation: 'They' is plural, so the present perfect form is 'have been'.

    Answer: C) will meet

    Explanation: 'Tomorrow' refers to future time, so 'will meet' is used.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: They built the bridge in 1990.
    Q69. Change into passive voice: They built the bridge in 1990.
    (A) The bridge was built in 1990.
    (B) The bridge is built in 1990.
    (C) The bridge has built in 1990.
    (D) The bridge built in 1990.
    Answer: A) The bridge was built in 1990.
    simple past ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য 'built' কৰ্মবাচ্যত 'was built' হয়।
    Explanation: The simple past active 'built' becomes 'was built' in the passive voice.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The letter was written by her.
    Q70. Change into active voice: The letter was written by her.
    (A) She wrote the letter.
    (B) She writes the letter.
    (C) She has wrote the letter.
    (D) The letter wrote her.
    Answer: A) She wrote the letter.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যত কৰ্তাই কাৰ্য কৰে: 'She wrote the letter.'
    Explanation: In the active voice the subject performs the action: 'She wrote the letter.'
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: He said, 'I am busy.'
    Q71. Change into indirect speech: He said, 'I am busy.'
    (A) He said that he is busy.
    (B) He said that he was busy.
    (C) He says that he was busy.
    (D) He said that I am busy.
    Answer: B) He said that he was busy.
    reporting verb অতীতকালত হ'লে present tense 'am' 'was' লৈ সলনি হয়।
    Explanation: When the reporting verb is in the past, the present tense 'am' changes to 'was'.
    তোমালোকে তোমালোকৰ পিতৃ-মাতৃক সন্মান কৰা ___।
    Q72. You ___ respect your parents.
    (A) should
    (B) may
    (C) can
    (D) would
    Answer: A) should
    'should' য়ে নৈতিক কৰ্তব্য বা উপদেশ বুজায়।
    Explanation: 'Should' expresses moral duty or advice.
    তাই আহি আছে, ___?
    Q73. She is coming, ___?
    (A) is she
    (B) isn't she
    (C) does she
    (D) doesn't she
    Answer: B) isn't she
    ধনাত্মক বাক্যৰ সৈতে ঋণাত্মক question tag লগে; auxiliary 'is', সেয়ে tag 'isn't she'।
    Explanation: A positive statement takes a negative question tag; the auxiliary is 'is', so the tag is 'isn't she'.
    যদি বৰষুণ দিয়ে, আমি খেলখন ___।

    Explanation: In a first conditional sentence, the main clause uses 'will' plus the base verb.
    সৰল বৰ্তমান কালৰ শুদ্ধ ঋণাত্মক বাক্যটো বাছক:

    Explanation: In the present simple with 'he', the negative is formed with 'doesn't' and the base verb.

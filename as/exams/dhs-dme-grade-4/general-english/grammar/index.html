    'Let us' ৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা বাক্যৰ question tag হৈছে 'shall we'।
    Explanation: A sentence beginning with 'Let us' takes the question tag 'shall we'.
    যদি তুমি বৰফ গৰম কৰা, ই ___।
    Q43. If you heat ice, it ___.
    (A) melt
    (B) melted
    (C) melts
    (D) will melting
    Answer: C) melts
    সাধাৰণ সত্য zero conditional ত present simple 'melts' ৰে প্ৰকাশ কৰা হয়।
    Explanation: A general truth is expressed in the zero conditional with the present simple 'melts'.
    'one of' ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    Q44. Choose the correct sentence using 'one of':
    (A) One of my friends is a doctor.
    (B) One of my friend are a doctor.
    (C) One of my friends are a doctor.
    (D) One of my friend is doctor.
    Answer: A) One of my friends is a doctor.
    বাক্যাংশটো 'one of my friends', আৰু 'one' য়ে singular verb 'is' লয়।
    Explanation: The phrase is 'one of my friends', and 'one' takes the singular verb 'is'.
    তাই ___ সৎ ছোৱালী।
    Q45. She is ___ honest girl.
    (A) a
    (B) an
    (C) the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: B) an
    'honest' ৰ 'h' নীৰৱ, সেয়ে ইয়াৰ উচ্চাৰণ স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Honest' begins with a vowel sound because the 'h' is silent, so 'an' is used.
    ___ মহাত্মা গান্ধী ১৮৬৯ চনত জন্মগ্ৰহণ কৰিছিল।
    Q46. ___ Mahatma Gandhi was born in 1869.
    (A) A
    (B) An
    (C) The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    D) প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: D) No article
    মহাত্মা গান্ধীৰ দৰে ব্যক্তিনামৰ আগত কোনো article ব্যৱহাৰ নহয়।
    Explanation: No article is used before a proper name like Mahatma Gandhi.
    'cactus' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    Q47. The plural of 'cactus' is:
    (A) cactuses
    (B) cactus
    (C) cactis
    (D) cacti
    Answer: D) cacti
    'cactus' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'cacti'।
    Explanation: The plural of 'cactus' is 'cacti'.
    'sheep' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    Q48. The plural of 'sheep' is:
    (A) sheep
    (B) sheeps
    (C) sheepes
    (D) sheeppen
    Answer: A) sheep
    'sheep' ৰ বহুবচনও 'sheep'।
    Explanation: The plural of 'sheep' is also 'sheep'.
    'know' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    Q49. The abstract noun of 'know' is:
    (A) knowing
    (B) knowledge
    (C) known
    (D) knower
    Answer: B) knowledge
    'know' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য হৈছে 'knowledge'।
    Explanation: The abstract noun of 'know' is 'knowledge'.
    তৰাৰ গোটৰ সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    Q50. A collective noun for a group of stars is:
    (A) galaxy
    (B) swarm
    (C) herd
    (D) constellation
    Answer: D) constellation
    তৰাৰ গোটৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত collective noun হৈছে 'constellation'।
    Explanation: 'Constellation' is the collective noun used for a group of stars.
    মই নিজেই কামটো কৰিলোঁ।
    Q51. I ___ did the work.
    (A) myself
    (B) me
    (C) mine
    (D) I
    Answer: A) myself
    ইয়াত 'I' ক গুৰুত্ব দিবলৈ emphatic pronoun হিচাপে 'myself' ব্যৱহাৰ হৈছে।
    Explanation: 'Myself' is used here as an emphatic pronoun to emphasise 'I'.
    আমি লগ পোৱা মহিলাগৰাকী মোৰ মাহী।
    Q52. The woman ___ we met is my aunt.
    (A) who
    (B) which
    (C) whose
    (D) whom
    Answer: D) whom
    'met' ক্ৰিয়াৰ object হিচাপে 'whom' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Whom' is used as the object of the verb 'met'.
    ল'ৰাবোৰৰ ভিতৰত ___ য়ে নিজৰ গৃহকৰ্ম কৰিছে।
    Q53. ___ of the boys has done his homework.
    (A) Each
    (B) All
    (C) Both
    (D) Many
    Answer: A) Each
    'Each' singular আৰু ই singular verb 'has' লয়; সেয়ে 'Each of the boys has done' শুদ্ধ।
    Explanation: 'Each' is singular and takes the singular verb 'has', so 'Each of the boys has done' is correct.
    এইখন ___ কিতাপ।
    Q54. This is ___ book.
    (A) him
    (B) his
    (C) he
    (D) himself
    Answer: B) his
    'his' হৈছে possessive adjective, যি 'book' noun ৰ আগত ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'His' is the possessive adjective used before the noun 'book'.
    'bad' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    Q55. The comparative degree of 'bad' is:
    (A) badder
    (B) worst
    (C) worse
    (D) more bad
    Answer: C) worse
    'bad' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'worse'।
    Explanation: The comparative degree of 'bad' is 'worse'.
    'far' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    Q56. The superlative degree of 'far' is:
    (A) farthest
    (B) farther
    (C) more far
    (D) far
    Answer: A) farthest
    'far' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'farthest'।
    Explanation: The superlative degree of 'far' is 'farthest'.
    তেওঁ সোমবাৰৰ পৰা অনুপস্থিত।
    Q57. He has been absent ___ Monday.
    (A) for
    (B) since
    (C) from
    (D) by
    Answer: B) since
    সোমবাৰৰ দৰে সময়ৰ নিৰ্দিষ্ট বিন্দুৰ সৈতে 'since' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Since' is used with a point of time such as Monday.
    তাই এজন চিকিৎসকৰ সৈতে বিবাহপাশত আবদ্ধ।
    Q58. She is married ___ a doctor.
    (A) with
    (B) for
    (C) at
    (D) to
    Answer: D) to
    'married' ৰ পিছত শুদ্ধ preposition হৈছে 'to'।
    Explanation: The correct preposition after 'married' is 'to'.
    ___ তেওঁ দুখীয়া, তেওঁ সৎ।
    Q59. ___ he is poor, he is honest.
    (A) Although
    (B) Because
    (C) So
    (D) Unless
    Answer: A) Although
    'although' য়ে দুখীয়া আৰু সৎ হোৱাৰ মাজৰ বিপৰীত ভাৱ প্ৰকাশ কৰে।
    Explanation: 'Although' introduces a contrast between being poor and being honest.
    গণিত মোৰ প্ৰিয় বিষয় ___।
    Q60. Mathematics ___ my favourite subject.
    (A) are
    (B) is
    (C) were
    (D) have
    Answer: B) is
    'mathematics' দেখাত plural যেন লাগিলেও ই singular subject; সেয়ে 'is' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Mathematics' is a singular subject despite its plural-looking form, so 'is' is used.
    মই অহাৰ আগতে তাই চিঠিখন ___।
    Q61. She ___ the letter before I arrived.
    (A) wrote
    (B) has written
    (C) had written
    (D) writes
    Answer: C) had written
    past perfect 'had written' য়ে আন এটা অতীত কাৰ্যৰ আগতে কাৰ্য সম্পূৰ্ণ হোৱা দেখুৱায়।
    Explanation: The past perfect 'had written' shows that the action was completed before another past action.
    মই ২০১০ চনৰ পৰা এই চহৰত ___।
    Q62. I ___ in this city since 2010.
    (A) live
    (B) lived
    (C) am living
    (D) have lived
    Answer: D) have lived
    অতীতৰ নিৰ্দিষ্ট সময়ৰ পৰা এতিয়ালৈকে চলি থকা অৱস্থাৰ বাবে present perfect 'have lived' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: The present perfect 'have lived' is used for a state continuing from a past point until now.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The teacher will explain the lesson.
    Q63. Change into passive voice: The teacher will explain the lesson.
    (A) The lesson will be explained by the teacher.
    (B) The lesson is explained by the teacher.
    (C) The lesson was explained by the teacher.
    (D) The lesson will explained by the teacher.
    Answer: A) The lesson will be explained by the teacher.
    ভৱিষ্যৎ কালৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'will be' আৰু past participle ৰে গঠিত হয়।
    Explanation: The future passive is formed with 'will be' plus the past participle.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The thief was caught by the police.
    Q64. Change into active voice: The thief was caught by the police.
    (A) The police catch the thief.
    (B) The police has caught the thief.
    (C) The police caught the thief.
    (D) The police were caught the thief.
    Answer: C) The police caught the thief.
    simple past ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'was caught' ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য হৈছে 'caught'।
    Explanation: The simple past passive 'was caught' corresponds to the active 'caught'.
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: He said, 'I have finished my work.'
    Q65. Change into indirect speech: He said, 'I have finished my work.'
    (A) He said that he has finished his work.
    (B) He says that he had finished his work.
    (C) He said that he finished his work.
    (D) He said that he had finished his work.
    Answer: D) He said that he had finished his work.
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত present perfect 'have finished' 'had finished' লৈ সলনি হয়।
    Explanation: The present perfect 'have finished' changes to the past perfect 'had finished' in indirect speech.
    তুমি ইয়াত ধূমপান কৰা ___ নহয়।
    Q66. You ___ not smoke here.
    (A) may
    (B) must
    (C) should
    (D) can
    Answer: B) must
    'must not' য়ে কঠোৰ নিষেধাজ্ঞা প্ৰকাশ কৰে।
    Explanation: 'Must not' expresses a strong prohibition.
    তেওঁ সাঁতোৰিব পাৰে, ___?
    Q67. He can swim, ___?
    (A) can he
    (B) can't he
    (C) could he
    (D) doesn't he
    Answer: B) can't he
    ধনাত্মক বাক্যৰ সৈতে ঋণাত্মক tag লগে; auxiliary 'can', সেয়ে tag 'can't he'।
    Explanation: A positive statement takes a negative tag; the auxiliary is 'can', so the tag is 'can't he'.
    যদি তুমি পৰিশ্ৰম নকৰা, তুমি ব্যৰ্থ ___।
    Q68. Unless you work hard, you ___ fail.
    (A) would
    (B) won't
    (C) did
    (D) will
    Answer: D) will
    'unless' মানে 'যদি নহয়'; first conditional ৰ main clause ত 'will' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Explanation: 'Unless' means 'if not', and the main clause of a first conditional uses 'will'.
    'senior' ৰ সৈতে শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    Q69. Choose the correct sentence with 'senior':
    (A) He is senior than I.
    (B) He is more senior than me.
    (C) He is senior to me.
    (D) He is senior from me.
    Answer: C) He is senior to me.
    'senior' ৰ পিছত 'to' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, 'than' বা 'from' নহয়।
    Explanation: 'Senior' is followed by 'to', not 'than' or 'from'.

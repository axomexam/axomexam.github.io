    Reasoning & Mental Ability for ADRE 3.0 Grade III

    Reasoning & Mental Ability is one subject in the ADRE 3.0 Grade III syllabus. On axomexam it holds 2,474 bilingual practice questions across 3 sub-categories, every question carrying four options, the correct answer and a worked explanation.

    • Q1. Assertion (A): Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level. Reason (R): Boiling point depends on atmospheric pressure. দাবী (A): Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level. কাৰণ (R): Boiling point depends on atmospheric pressure.
      (A) Both are true but R does not explain A দুয়োটা সঁচা কিন্তু R এ A ব্যাখ্যা নকৰে   (B) A is true but R is false A সঁচা কিন্তু R অসঁচা   (C) Both A and R are true and R explains A A আৰু R দুয়োটা সঁচা আৰু R এ A ব্যাখ্যা কৰে   (D) A is false but R is true A অসঁচা কিন্তু R সঁচা
      Answer: Both A and R are true and R explains A Explanation: The dependence of boiling point on pressure is exactly why water boils at 100 degrees at sea level.
    • Q2. Assertion (A): The sun rises in the east. Reason (R): The earth rotates from west to east. দাবী (A): The sun rises in the east. কাৰণ (R): The earth rotates from west to east.

    • Q3. Assertion (A): Iron rusts in moist air. Reason (R): Rusting is oxidation in the presence of moisture. দাবী (A): Iron rusts in moist air. কাৰণ (R): Rusting is oxidation in the presence of moisture.
      (A) A is false but R is true A অসঁচা কিন্তু R সঁচা   (B) A is true but R is false A সঁচা কিন্তু R অসঁচা   (C) Both are true but R does not explain A দুয়োটা সঁচা কিন্তু R এ A ব্যাখ্যা নকৰে   (D) Both A and R are true and R explains A A আৰু R দুয়োটা সঁচা আৰু R এ A ব্যাখ্যা কৰে

    • Q4. Assertion (A): Ice floats on water. Reason (R): Ice is less dense than water. দাবী (A): Ice floats on water. কাৰণ (R): Ice is less dense than water.

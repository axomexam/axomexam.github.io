    Political Science & Constitution: notes

    Poverty, Unemployment & Social Sector

    Poverty means a situation in which a person is unable to get the minimum necessities of life, such as food, clothing, shelter and education. In India, poverty is measured with the help of a poverty line, which is fixed in terms of money income or consumption expenditure. The poverty line is revised from time to time. The Tendulkar Committee (2009) defined the poverty line and estimated that about 21.9 percent of India's population was below the poverty line in 2011–12. The Rangarajan Committee (2014) gave a new method and a higher poverty line. The Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI), which was prepared with the help of NITI Aayog and the United Nations, is also used to measure poverty in India on the basis of health, education and standard of living.

    Historical Background: Constitutional Development (1773–1947)

    The Constitution of India is not a sudden creation. It grew out of nearly two centuries of British rule in which the Parliament of Britain, step by step, took over the government of India from the East India Company. Every stage of this long process is a favourite area for questions in every competitive exam.

    1. The Regulating Act of 1773 was the first step taken by the British Parliament to regulate the affairs of the East India Company. It designated the Governor of Bengal as the Governor-General of Bengal and created an Executive Council of four members to assist him. Warren Hastings became the first Governor-General of Bengal. The Act also established the Supreme Court at Calcutta in 1774 with a Chief Justice and three other judges. The Company's servants were forbidden from receiving presents and from private trade.

    1. ১৭৭৩ চনৰ ৰেগুলেটিং আইনটো আছিল ব্ৰিটিছ সংসদে ইষ্ট ইণ্ডিয়া কোম্পানীৰ কাৰ্য নিয়ন্ত্ৰণ কৰিবলৈ লোৱা প্ৰথম পদক্ষেপ। এই আইনে বংগৰ গৱৰ্ণৰক বংগৰ গৱৰ্ণৰ-জেনেৰেল বুলি ঘোষণা কৰে আৰু তেওঁক সহায় কৰিবলৈ চাৰিজনীয়া এটা কাৰ্যনিৰ্বাহক পৰিষদ গঠন কৰে। ৱাৰেন হেষ্টিংছ প্ৰথম গৱৰ্ণৰ-জেনেৰেল হয়। আইনখনত ১৭৭৪ চনত কলকাতাত উচ্চতম ন্যায়ালয় (Supreme Court) স্থাপন কৰা হয়, যাৰ মুৰব্বী আছিল এজন মুখ্য বিচাৰপতি আৰু আন তিনিজন বিচাৰক। কোম্পানীৰ কৰ্মচাৰীয়ে উপহাৰ লোৱা আৰু ব্যক্তিগত ব্যৱসায় চলোৱা নিষিদ্ধ কৰা হয়।

    Political Science & Constitution: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. When was the Indian Constitution adopted? ভাৰতীয় সংবিধান কেতিয়া গ্ৰহণ কৰা হৈছিল?
      (A) 26 November 1949 ২৬ নৱেম্বৰ ১৯৪৯   (B) 9 December 1946 ৯ ডিচেম্বৰ ১৯৪৬   (C) 26 January 1950 ২৬ জানুৱাৰী ১৯৫০   (D) 15 August 1947 ১৫ আগষ্ট ১৯৪৭
      Answer: 26 November 1949 Explanation: The Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on 26 November 1949.
    • Q2. When did the Indian Constitution come into force? ভাৰতীয় সংবিধান কেতিয়া বলৱৎ হৈছিল?
      (A) 2 October 1950 ২ অক্টোবৰ ১৯৫০   (B) 26 January 1950 ২৬ জানুৱাৰী ১৯৫০   (C) 26 November 1949 ২৬ নৱেম্বৰ ১৯৪৯   (D) 15 August 1947 ১৫ আগষ্ট ১৯৪৭
      Answer: 26 January 1950 Explanation: The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, celebrated as Republic Day.
    • Q3. Who was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constitution? ভাৰতীয় সংবিধানৰ খচৰা সমিতিৰ অধ্যক্ষ কোন আছিল?
      (A) Sardar Patel সৰ্দাৰ পেটেল   (B) Dr. Rajendra Prasad ড০ ৰাজেন্দ্ৰ প্ৰসাদ   (C) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar ড০ বি. আৰ. আম্বেদকাৰ   (D) Jawaharlal Nehru জৱাহৰলাল নেহৰু
      Answer: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.
    • Q4. Who was the President of the Constituent Assembly? সংবিধান সভাৰ সভাপতি কোন আছিল?
      (A) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan ড০ এছ. ৰাধাকৃষ্ণণ   (B) Jawaharlal Nehru জৱাহৰলাল নেহৰু   (C) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar ড০ বি. আৰ. আম্বেদকাৰ   (D) Dr. Rajendra Prasad ড০ ৰাজেন্দ্ৰ প্ৰসাদ
      Answer: Dr. Rajendra Prasad Explanation: Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the permanent President of the Constituent Assembly.

    Exam-wise mock tests for Assam recruitment exams

    A subject-wise mock test drills one topic. An exam-wise mock test does the opposite: it hands you a complete question paper — every subject of the exam mixed together, in the order the real paper uses, with a live timer running while you attempt it. That is the part most candidates never practise, and it is where the real paper surprises them.

    On this page Assam Police Sub-Inspector (SI), Assam Police, ADRE 3.0 Grade III, ADRE 3.0 Grade IV, DHS & DME Technical Grade III, DHS & DME Non-Technical Grade III, DHS & DME Grade IV each have their own separate mock tests. The papers are not shared between exams: an Assam Police set draws on the Assam Police syllabus, and an ADRE Grade III set draws on the Grade III syllabus, so you never practise a question that will not appear in your paper.

    অসম আৰক্ষী উপ-পৰিদৰ্শক (SI) · অসম আৰক্ষী · ADRE 3.0 গ্ৰেড III · ADRE 3.0 গ্ৰেড IV · DHS আৰু DME কাৰিকৰী গ্ৰেড III · DHS আৰু DME অসামৰিক গ্ৰেড III · DHS আৰু DME গ্ৰেড IV — প্ৰতিখন পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে সুকীয়া সম্পূৰ্ণ পত্ৰৰ মক টেষ্ট।

    What each full paper contains

    • অসম আৰক্ষী উপ-পৰিদৰ্শক (SI) — 10 full papers, 100 questions each. Sections: Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability, Aptitude & Basic Mathematics, General Knowledge, Science & Current Affairs, History, Geography & Culture of Assam.
    • অসম আৰক্ষী — 5 full papers, 100 questions each. Sections: Elementary Mathematics, General English, Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability, Assam's History, Geography & Culture, General Knowledge & Current Affairs.
    • ADRE 3.0 গ্ৰেড III — 10 full papers, 150 questions each. Sections: General Mathematics, History, General English, General Knowledge & Assam GK, Reasoning & Mental Ability, Social Studies.
    • ADRE 3.0 গ্ৰেড IV — 10 full papers, 135 questions each. Sections: General Mathematics, General English, General Knowledge & Assam GK, Reasoning & Mental Ability, Social Studies.
    • DHS & DME Technical Grade III — 5 full papers, 100 questions each. Sections: Technical / Science (Diploma & Degree Level), General Knowledge & Current Affairs, General English, Reasoning & Mental Ability.
    • DHS & DME Non-Technical Grade III — 5 full papers, 100 questions each. Sections: General Knowledge (Assam & India), Elementary Mathematics, Reasoning & Mental Ability, General English, Computer Knowledge.
    • DHS & DME Grade IV — 5 full papers, 50 questions each. Sections: General Knowledge, Basic Mathematics, Basic English.

    How a set is built

    Each set carries bilingual questions with four options, the correct answer and a short explanation in both Assamese and English. The questions are pulled from the same verified question bank that powers the subject-wise practice and the exam books, then assembled per the exam's own paper blueprint — every set follows the same subject-wise question distribution as the real paper.

    How to attempt an exam-wise mock test

    1. Open the exam that matches your recruitment.
    2. Pick a full paper set — start with Set 1 and move up.
    3. Choose your question language and start the timed test.
    4. Submit to get an instant score, then read the bilingual answer review for every question.

    সঘনাই সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    What is an exam-wise mock test?

    An exam-wise mock test is a single full-length paper that mixes every subject of one recruitment exam — for example Assam Police or ADRE Grade III — and runs under a live timer, so it works exactly like the real examination hall instead of a single-subject practice drill.

    How many questions are in each set?

    Each full paper set carries the same number of bilingual questions as the real exam paper — 100 for Assam Police Sub-Inspector (SI), 100 for Assam Police, 150 for ADRE 3.0 Grade III, 135 for ADRE 3.0 Grade IV, 100 for DHS & DME Technical Grade III, 100 for DHS & DME Non-Technical Grade III, 50 for DHS & DME Grade IV — with four options each, an answer key and a short explanation in both Assamese and English.

    Are the mock tests different for each exam?

    Yes. Each exam has its own separate sets built from its own syllabus and question bank — Assam Police Sub-Inspector (SI), Assam Police, ADRE 3.0 Grade III, ADRE 3.0 Grade IV, DHS & DME Technical Grade III, DHS & DME Non-Technical Grade III, DHS & DME Grade IV are kept apart.

    Are these mock tests free?

    Yes. Every exam-wise mock test on axomexam is 100% free and can be reattempted as many times as you like.

    Can I practise in Assamese?

    Yes. Every question, option and explanation is available in both Assamese and English, and you can choose the question language before the test begins.

    grammar vocab: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. Choose the passive form of: 'She writes a letter.' 'She writes a letter.' ৰ passive ৰূপ বাছনি কৰক।
      (A) A letter is written by her   (B) A letter was written by her   (C) A letter is being written by her   (D) A letter has been written by her
      Answer: Present simple active (write) becomes 'is/am/are + past participle' in the passive. Explanation: The correct passive form is “A letter is written by her”. Present simple active (write) becomes 'is/am/are + past participle' in the passive. The other options either keep the active voice or use the wrong tense.
    • Q2. Choose the passive form of: 'They are building a house.' 'They are building a house.' ৰ passive ৰূপ বাছনি কৰক।
      (A) A house is being built by them   (B) A house is built by them   (C) A house was built by them   (D) A house has been built by them
      Answer: Present continuous active becomes 'is/am/are + being + past participle'. Explanation: The correct passive form is “A house is being built by them”. Present continuous active becomes 'is/am/are + being + past participle'. The remaining options do not follow the correct passive structure.
    • Q3. Choose the passive form of: 'He wrote a book.' 'He wrote a book.' ৰ passive ৰূপ বাছনি কৰক।
      (A) A book was written by him   (B) A book is written by him   (C) A book has been written by him   (D) A book was being written by him
      Answer: Past simple active becomes 'was/were + past participle'. Explanation: The correct passive form is “A book was written by him”. Past simple active becomes 'was/were + past participle'. None of the other options is a correctly formed passive sentence.
    • Q4. Choose the passive form of: 'She was cooking food.' 'She was cooking food.' ৰ passive ৰূপ বাছনি কৰক।
      (A) Food was being cooked by her   (B) Food was cooked by her   (C) Food is being cooked by her   (D) Food has been cooked by her
      Answer: Past continuous active becomes 'was/were + being + past participle'. Explanation: The correct passive form is “Food was being cooked by her”. Past continuous active becomes 'was/were + being + past participle'. The other options either keep the active voice or use the wrong tense.
    • Q5. Choose the passive form of: 'They have finished the work.' 'They have finished the work.' ৰ passive ৰূপ বাছনি কৰক।
      (A) The work has been finished by them   (B) The work was finished by them   (C) The work is finished by them   (D) The work had been finished by them
      Answer: Present perfect active becomes 'has/have been + past participle'. Explanation: The correct passive form is “The work has been finished by them”. Present perfect active becomes 'has/have been + past participle'. The remaining options do not follow the correct passive structure.

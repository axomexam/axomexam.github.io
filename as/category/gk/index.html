    সাধাৰণ জ্ঞান — পৰিচয়

    General Knowledge carries a large weight in almost every Assam recruitment examination. It combines Assam-specific history, literature, geography, art and culture with Indian polity, history, geography and economics. Because the section is factual and predictable, consistent revision usually converts into a high, reliable score in ADRE, Assam Police and APSC papers.

    কেনেকৈ প্ৰস্তুতি ল'ব: সাধাৰণ জ্ঞান

    • প্ৰথমে বিষয়ৰ টোকা এবাৰ পঢ়ক, তাৰ পিছত সম্পৰ্কিত প্ৰশ্ন সমাধান কৰি নিজৰ স্মৃতি পৰীক্ষা কৰক।
    • যি তথ্যবোৰ বাৰে বাৰে পাহৰি যাই সেইবোৰ চিহ্নিত কৰি চুটি চুটি অধিবেশনত পুনৰালোচনা কৰক।
    • দুয়োটা ভাষাত অনুশীলন কৰক, যাতে একেখিনি তথ্য অসমীয়া আৰু ইংৰাজী দুয়োতে চিনি পায়।
    • শুদ্ধ উত্তৰ দিয়া প্ৰশ্নৰো ব্যাখ্যা পঢ়ক, যাতে মনত ৰখাৰ শক্তি বাঢ়ে।

    How to study General Knowledge

    • Pick the subject you are weakest in and start there.
    • Finish one topic, read every explanation, then move to the next.
    • Revise the topics you keep forgetting at the end of each week.
    • Take a timed mock test once a whole subject is complete.

    How many General Knowledge questions are on axomexam?

    There are 9,900 General Knowledge questions across 7 subjects, each with four options, a marked answer and an explanation.

    What subjects are covered under General Knowledge?

    The subjects are Assam Literature, Assam History, Indian History, Political Science, Geography, Economics, Art & Culture.

    Is General Knowledge useful for ADRE, Assam Police and APSC?

    Yes. General Knowledge follows the syllabus shared by these examinations, so one round of preparation serves several papers.

    Is the material free?

    Yes. Every question, note and mock test on axomexam.in is free, with no login.

    সঘনাই সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    What is the Ahom period of Assamese literature?

    The Ahom period covers the centuries of Ahom rule in Assam, roughly from 1228 to 1826 CE. It is best known for the Buranjis, the historical chronicles that made Assamese one of the earliest Indian languages with a prose tradition, and for the court and scientific literature produced under royal patronage.

    What are the Buranjis and why are they important?

    The Buranjis are the official historical chronicles of the Ahom kingdom, recording reigns, wars, diplomacy and important events in prose. They are the most important source of Assam's medieval history and among the earliest specimens of Assamese prose.

    Who maintained the Buranjis and in which language were they written?

    The Buranjis were maintained by court officials called Buranji-kakats, while traditional lore was also preserved by the Deodhai and Bailung priests. The earliest chronicles were written in the Ahom (Tai) language, and later ones were written in Assamese.

    What scientific works were produced in the Ahom period?

    The Ahom period produced the Hastividyarnava on the care and management of elephants, the Ghoranidan on horses, Ayurvedic and medical texts, and works on astrology and astronomy, most of them written or translated under royal patronage.

    This section covers two topics: Buranjis (Historical Chronicles), which deals with the chronicles, their authors and their language, and Court & Scientific Literature, which deals with the royal, technical and translated works of the period. Together they cover the most frequently asked questions on the period.

    Ahom Period: study notes

    Buranji Literature of the Ahom Period

    The word 'buranji' means a chronicle or a storehouse of knowledge. The Ahoms, who came to Assam from their original homeland in the Shan region of south-east Asia, brought with them the tradition of keeping written chronicles of important events. The Ahom court kept this tradition with great care, and the records it produced are called Buranjis. The word is believed to have come from the Tai-Ahom language.

    At first the Buranjis were written in the Ahom language by specially appointed officers of the court, but in course of time many of them came to be written in Assamese, and some in a mixed language. They were written on the bark of the agaru or sachi tree in long folded sheets. The Ahom kings kept Buranjis for almost every department of state — there were Buranjis of the royal family, of war, of revenue, of diplomatic relations and of daily court events.

    'বুৰঞ্জী' শব্দৰ অৰ্থ হৈছে ক্ৰনিকল বা জ্ঞানৰ ভঁৰাল। দক্ষিণ-পূব এছিয়াৰ শান অঞ্চলৰ নিজা আদি ভূমিৰ পৰা অসমলৈ অহা আহোমসকলে গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ ঘটনাৰ লিখিত ক্ৰনিকল ৰখাৰ পৰম্পৰা লৈ আহিছিল। আহোম ৰাজসভাত এই পৰম্পৰা অতি যত্নেৰে ৰক্ষা কৰা হৈছিল, আৰু যিবোৰ অভিলেখ সৃষ্টি হৈছিল সেইবোৰক বুৰঞ্জী বোলা হয়। শব্দটো তাই-আহোম ভাষাৰ পৰা অহা বুলি বিশ্বাস কৰা হয়।

    আৰম্ভণিত আহোম ভাষাত ৰাজসভাৰ বিশেষভাৱে নিযুক্ত বিষয়া সকলে বুৰঞ্জী লিখিছিল, কিন্তু সময়ৰ লগে লগে বহুতো অসমীয়াত আৰু কিছুমান মিশ্ৰ ভাষাত লিখা হৈছিল। এইবোৰ আগৰু বা শাচি গছৰ বাকলিত দীঘল ভাঁজ কৰা পাতত লিখা হৈছিল। আহোম ৰজাসকলে ৰাজ্যৰ প্ৰায় প্ৰতিটো বিভাগৰ বাবে বুৰঞ্জী ৰাখিছিল — ৰাজবংশৰ বুৰঞ্জী, যুদ্ধৰ বুৰঞ্জী, ৰাজহৰ বুৰঞ্জী, কূটনৈতিক সম্পৰ্কৰ বুৰঞ্জী আৰু দৈনিক ৰাজসভাৰ ঘটনাৰ বুৰঞ্জী আছিল।

    Foundation of the Ahom Kingdom: Sukapha & Early Expansion

    The Ahoms were a Tai (Shan) people who originally lived in the land of Mong Mao (in present-day Yunnan / eastern Myanmar). Their leader Sukapha left his homeland around 1215 AD, wandered for many years with his followers, crossed the Patkai mountain range and finally entered the Brahmaputra valley in 1228 AD. This is the most important date in Ahom history. Sukapha established his first capital at Charaideo (near present-day Sivasagar) and slowly brought the local tribes such as the Morans and Borahis under his control. He married into local families, adopted local customs, and so the small Tai band gradually grew into a powerful kingdom.

    Sukapha himself was known by the title Chao-Pha, meaning 'Lord of Heaven' or 'the great ruler'.

    আহোমসকল আছিল তাই (শান) জনজাতিৰ লোক, যিসকল মূলতঃ বৰ্তমানৰ য়ুন্নান/পূৱ ম্যানমাৰ অঞ্চলৰ মং মাও (মাও) দেশত বাস কৰিছিল। তেওঁলোকৰ নেতা চ্যুকাফাই প্ৰায় ১২১৫ খ্ৰীষ্টাব্দত নিজৰ জন্মভূমি এৰি অনুগামীসকলৰ লগত বহু বছৰ ঘূৰি ফুৰি পাটকাই পৰ্বত পাৰ হৈ শেষত ১২২৮ খ্ৰীষ্টাব্দত ব্ৰহ্মপুত্ৰ উপত্যকাত প্ৰৱেশ কৰিছিল। আহোম ইতিহাসৰ এইটোৱেই আটাইতকৈ গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ তাৰিখ। চ্যুকাফাই চৰাইদেউত (বৰ্তমান শিৱসাগৰৰ ওচৰত) নিজৰ প্ৰথম ৰাজধানী স্থাপন কৰি লাহে লাহে মৰাণ আৰু বৰাহীৰ দৰে স্থানীয় জনজাতিসকলক নিজৰ নিয়ন্ত্ৰণলৈ আনিছিল। তেওঁ স্থানীয় পৰিয়ালত বিবাহপাশত আবদ্ধ হৈ স্থানীয় ৰীতি-নীতি গ্ৰহণ কৰিছিল আৰু এইদৰে সৰু তাই দলটো ক্ৰমে এক শক্তিশালী ৰাজ্যলৈ পৰিণত হৈছিল।

    চ্যুকাফাক 'চাও-ফা' উপাধিৰে জনা গৈছিল, যাৰ অৰ্থ 'স্বৰ্গৰ প্ৰভু' বা 'মহান শাসক'।

    Ahom Period: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. What is a Buranji? বুৰঞ্জী কি?
      Answer: A Buranji is a historical chronicle of the Ahom period of Assam, recording events, reigns, and important matters in prose.
    • Q2. What is the origin of the word 'Buranji'? 'বুৰঞ্জী' শব্দৰ উৎপত্তি কি?
      Answer: The word 'Buranji' is derived from the Tai-Ahom word, believed to mean 'a storehouse of knowledge' or 'to teach without books'.
    • Q3. In which language were the earliest Buranjis written? আটাইতকৈ পুৰণি বুৰঞ্জীবোৰ কোন ভাষাত লিখা হৈছিল?
      Answer: The earliest Buranjis were written in the Ahom (Tai) language, and later ones were written in Assamese.
    • Q4. What is the historical importance of the Buranjis? বুৰঞ্জীৰ ঐতিহাসিক গুৰুত্ব কি?
      Answer: The Buranjis are the most important sources of Assam's history, preserving the political, social, and military records of the Ahom period.

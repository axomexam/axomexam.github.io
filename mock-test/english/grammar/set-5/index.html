    Grammar Mock Test — Set 5

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. No sooner had he reached home ___ it started raining.
    তেওঁ ঘৰ পোৱাৰ লগে লগে বৰষুণ আৰম্ভ হ'ল।
    (A) when when
    (B) then then
    (C) than than
    (D) that that
    Answer: than
    than
    Explanation: 'No sooner ... than' is the correct correlative pair.
    'no sooner ... than' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Q2. Scarcely had he gone out ___ a guest arrived.
    তেওঁ ওলাই যোৱাৰ লগে লগে এজন অতিথি আহিল।
    (A) than than
    (B) when when
    (C) then then
    (D) that that
    Answer: when
    when
    Explanation: 'Scarcely ... when' is the correct correlative pair.
    'scarcely ... when' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Q3. The correct question tag: 'He is a teacher, ___?'
    শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন টেগ: 'He is a teacher, ___?'
    (A) is he is he
    (B) isn't he isn't he
    (C) doesn't he doesn't he
    (D) wasn't he wasn't he
    Answer: isn't he
    isn't he
    Explanation: A positive statement takes a negative question tag, so 'isn't he?'.
    ধনাত্মক বাক্যৰ বাবে ঋণাত্মক question tag 'isn't he?' হয়।
    Q4. The correct question tag: 'Let's go, ___?'
    শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন টেগ: 'Let's go, ___?'
    (A) will we will we
    (B) don't we don't we
    (C) shall we shall we
    (D) won't we won't we
    Answer: shall we
    shall we
    Explanation: 'Let's' takes the question tag 'shall we?'.
    'Let's' ৰ question tag 'shall we?' হয়।
    Q5. The correct question tag: 'I am right, ___?'
    শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন টেগ: 'I am right, ___?'
    (A) am I am I
    (B) aren't I aren't I
    (C) isn't I isn't I
    (D) don't I don't I
    Answer: aren't I
    aren't I
    Explanation: 'I am' takes the question tag 'aren't I?'.
    'I am' ৰ question tag 'aren't I?' হয়।
    Q6. Change into passive: 'Open the door.'
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলাওক: 'Open the door.'
    (A) The door is opened. The door is opened.
    (B) Let the door be opened. Let the door be opened.
    (C) The door was opened. The door was opened.
    (D) The door has been opened. The door has been opened.
    Answer: Let the door be opened.
    Let the door be opened.
    Explanation: An imperative sentence becomes 'Let ... be + past participle' in the passive.
    imperative বাক্য passive ত 'Let ... be + past participle' লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q7. Change into passive: 'Who wrote this book?'
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলাওক: 'Who wrote this book?'
    (A) By whom was this book written? By whom was this book written?
    (B) Who was this book written? Who was this book written?
    (C) By who this book was written? By who this book was written?
    (D) This book wrote by whom? This book wrote by whom?
    Answer: By whom was this book written?
    By whom was this book written?
    Explanation: The passive of 'Who wrote this book?' is 'By whom was this book written?'.
    'Who wrote this book?' ৰ passive হৈছে 'By whom was this book written?'।
    Q8. He said to me, 'Please help me.' The indirect form is:
    তেওঁ মোক ক'লে, 'Please help me.' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) He told me please help him. He told me please help him.
    (B) He requested me to help him. He requested me to help him.
    (C) He said me to help him. He said me to help him.
    (D) He asked that I help him. He asked that I help him.
    Answer: He requested me to help him.
    He requested me to help him.
    Explanation: A polite request becomes 'requested ... to' in indirect speech.
    নম্ৰ অনুৰোধ indirect speech ত 'requested ... to' লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q9. Choose the synonym of 'begin':
    'begin' ৰ সমাৰ্থক শব্দ বাছক:

    commence

    Q10. Choose the antonym of 'generous':

    Q11. Choose the correctly spelt word:
    শুদ্ধ বানানৰ শব্দটো বাছক:

    receive

    Q12. Choose the correctly spelt word:

    occurrence

    Q13. 'A person who speaks many languages' is called a:
    বহু ভাষা কোৱা ব্যক্তিক কি বোলে?
    (A) Linguist Linguist
    (B) Polyglot Polyglot
    (C) Orator Orator
    (D) Interpreter Interpreter
    Answer: Polyglot
    Polyglot
    Explanation: A person who speaks many languages is called a polyglot.
    বহু ভাষা কোৱা ব্যক্তিক polyglot বোলা হয়।
    Q14. 'One who looks at the bright side of things' is an:
    যিজনে বিষয়ৰ উজ্জ্বল দিশ চাই তেওঁক কি বোলে?
    (A) Pessimist Pessimist
    (B) Optimist Optimist
    (C) Atheist Atheist
    (D) Theist Theist
    Answer: Optimist
    Optimist
    Explanation: One who looks at the bright side of things is an optimist.
    বস্তুৰ উজ্জ্বল দিশ চোৱা ব্যক্তিক optimist বোলা হয়।
    Q15. The idiom 'to hit the nail on the head' means:
    'to hit the nail on the head' ৰ অৰ্থ কি?
    (A) To hurt someone কাকোবা আঘাত কৰা
    (B) To do the right thing exactly ঠিক কথাটো কৰা
    (C) To work with a hammer ঘাণেৰে কাম কৰা
    (D) To make a mistake ভুল কৰা
    Answer: To do the right thing exactly
    ঠিক কথাটো কৰা
    Explanation: 'To hit the nail on the head' means to do the right thing exactly.
    'to hit the nail on the head' মানে একেবাৰে শুদ্ধ কাম কৰা।
    Q16. 'To bury the hatchet' means:
    'To bury the hatchet' ৰ অৰ্থ কি?
    (A) To hide a weapon অস্ত্ৰ লুকুৱা
    (B) To end a quarrel বিবাদ সমাপ্ত কৰা
    (C) To start a fight যুঁজ আৰম্ভ কৰা
    (D) To dig the earth মাটি খন্দা
    Answer: To end a quarrel
    বিবাদ সমাপ্ত কৰা
    Explanation: 'To bury the hatchet' means to end a quarrel.
    'to bury the hatchet' মানে কাজিয়া মিটমাট কৰা।
    Q17. Choose the correct sentence:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) He suggested me to go. He suggested me to go.
    (B) He suggested that I should go. He suggested that I should go.
    (C) He suggested me that I go. He suggested me that I go.
    (D) He suggested to me go. He suggested to me go.
    Answer: He suggested that I should go.
    He suggested that I should go.
    Explanation: 'Suggest' is followed by a 'that' clause, not 'me to'.
    'suggest' ৰ পিছত 'me to' নহয়, 'that' clause আহে।
    Q18. Choose the correct sentence:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) I prefer coffee than tea. I prefer coffee than tea.
    (B) I prefer coffee to tea. I prefer coffee to tea.
    (C) I prefer coffee from tea. I prefer coffee from tea.
    (D) I prefer coffee against tea. I prefer coffee against tea.
    Answer: I prefer coffee to tea.
    I prefer coffee to tea.
    Explanation: 'Prefer' is followed by 'to', so 'I prefer coffee to tea.' is correct.
    'prefer' ৰ পিছত 'to' আহে, সেয়ে 'I prefer coffee to tea.' শুদ্ধ।
    Q19. The feminine of 'duke' is:
    'duke' ৰ স্ত্ৰীলিংগ কি?
    (A) dukes dukes
    (B) duchess duchess
    (C) dukeess dukeess

    'phenomenon' ৰ বহুবচন কি?

    (B) phenomena phenomena
    (C) phenomenas phenomenas
    (D) phenomenae phenomenae

    phenomena
    Explanation: The plural of 'phenomenon' is 'phenomena'.

    Q21. The sentence 'Shut the door' is:
    'Shut the door' বাক্যটো কি প্ৰকাৰৰ?
    (A) Assertive নিৰ্দেশাত্মক
    (B) Imperative আদেশাত্মক
    (C) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    (D) Optative ইচ্ছাসূচক
    Answer: Imperative
    আদেশাত্মক
    Explanation: 'Shut the door' gives a command, so it is imperative.
    'Shut the door' য়ে আদেশ দিয়ে, সেয়ে ই imperative।
    Q22. He as well as his friends ___ coming.
    তেওঁ আৰু তেওঁৰ বন্ধুসকলে আহি আছে।
    (A) are are
    (B) were were
    (C) is is
    (D) have have
    Answer: is
    is
    Explanation: With 'as well as', the verb agrees with the first subject 'he'.
    'as well as' ৰ সৈতে verb প্ৰথম subject 'he' ৰ লগত মিলে।
    Q23. Either he or I ___ to go.
    তেওঁ বা মই যাব লাগিব।
    (A) are are
    (B) is is
    (C) am am
    (D) were were
    Answer: am
    am
    Explanation: With 'either ... or', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'I', so 'am' is used.
    'either ... or' ৰ সৈতে verb ওচৰৰ subject 'I' ৰ লগত মিলে, সেয়ে 'am' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q24. The opposite of 'arrival' is:
    'arrival' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ কি?
    (A) coming coming
    (B) departure departure
    (C) reach reach
    (D) entry entry
    Answer: departure
    departure
    Explanation: 'Departure' is the opposite of 'arrival'.
    'departure' হৈছে 'arrival' ৰ বিপৰীত শব্দ।
    Q25. Fill in the blank: 'He is ___ M.A. in English.'
    খালী ঠাই পূৰ কৰক: 'He is ___ M.A. in English.'
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: an
    an
    Explanation: 'M.A.' begins with the vowel sound 'em', so 'an' is used.
    'M.A.' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'em' স্বৰ ধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।

    Grammar Mock Test — Set 10

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. He is ___ European.
    তেওঁ ___ ইউৰোপীয়।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: a
    a
    Explanation: 'European' begins with the consonant sound 'yu', so 'a' is used.
    'European' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'yu' ব্যঞ্জন ধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'a' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. ___ Brahmaputra is a big river.
    ___ ব্ৰহ্মপুত্ৰ এখন ডাঙৰ নদী।
    (A) A A
    (B) An An
    (C) The The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: The
    The
    Explanation: 'The' is used before the names of rivers.
    নদীৰ নামৰ আগত 'The' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q3. The plural of 'phenomenon' is:
    'phenomenon' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) phenomenons phenomenons
    (B) phenomena phenomena
    (C) phenomenas phenomenas
    (D) phenomenon phenomenon
    Answer: phenomena
    phenomena
    Explanation: The plural of 'phenomenon' is 'phenomena'.
    'phenomenon' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'phenomena'।
    Q4. The plural of 'deer' is:
    'deer' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) deer deer
    (B) deers deers
    (C) deeres deeres
    (D) deeren deeren
    Answer: deer
    deer
    Explanation: The plural of 'deer' is also 'deer'.
    'deer' ৰ বহুবচনও 'deer'।
    Q5. The abstract noun of 'brave' is:
    'brave' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) bravely bravely
    (B) braveness braveness
    (C) bravery bravery
    (D) brave brave
    Answer: bravery
    bravery
    Explanation: The abstract noun of 'brave' is 'bravery'.
    'brave' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য হৈছে 'bravery'।
    Q6. A collective noun for a group of ships is:
    জাহাজৰ দলৰ সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) flock flock
    (B) herd herd
    (C) swarm swarm
    (D) fleet fleet
    Answer: fleet
    fleet
    Explanation: 'Fleet' is the collective noun used for a group of ships.
    জাহাজৰ গোটৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত collective noun হৈছে 'fleet'।
    Q7. ___ did you give the book?
    তুমি কিতাপখন ___ দিলা?
    (A) Who Who
    (B) Whom Whom
    (C) Whose Whose
    (D) Which Which
    Answer: Whom
    Whom
    Explanation: 'Whom' is used as the object of the verb 'give'.
    'give' ক্ৰিয়াৰ object হিচাপে 'whom' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q8. This is the house ___ roof is red.
    এইটো সেই ঘৰটো যাৰ চালি ৰঙা।
    (A) who who
    (B) whom whom
    (C) which which
    (D) whose whose
    Answer: whose
    whose
    Explanation: 'Whose' shows possession and refers to 'the house's roof'.
    'whose' য়ে মালিকানা দেখুৱায় আৰু 'the house's roof' লৈ ইংগিত কৰে।
    Q9. The children hurt ___ while playing.
    ল'ৰা-ছোৱালীবোৰ খেলি থাকোঁতে নিজকে আঘাত কৰিলে।
    (A) themselves themselves
    (B) theirselves theirselves
    (C) themself themself
    (D) their their
    Answer: themselves
    themselves
    Explanation: The correct reflexive pronoun for 'the children' is 'themselves'.
    'the children' ৰ বাবে শুদ্ধ reflexive pronoun হৈছে 'themselves'।
    Q10. She and ___ went to the market.
    তাই আৰু ___ বজাৰলৈ গ'ল।
    (A) I I
    (B) me me
    (C) mine mine
    (D) myself myself
    Answer: I
    I
    Explanation: The subject pronoun 'I' is used because it is part of the subject of the sentence.
    বাক্যৰ কৰ্তাৰ অংশ হোৱাৰ বাবে subject pronoun 'I' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q11. The comparative degree of 'many' is:
    'many' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) most most
    (B) much much
    (C) many many
    (D) more more
    Answer: more
    more
    Explanation: The comparative degree of 'many' is 'more'.
    'many' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'more'।
    Q12. The superlative degree of 'well' is:
    'well' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) wellest wellest
    (B) better better
    (C) most well most well
    (D) best best
    Answer: best
    best
    Explanation: The superlative degree of 'well' is 'best'.
    'well' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'best'।
    Q13. He is different ___ his brother.
    তেওঁ তেওঁৰ ভাইৰ পৰা পৃথক।
    (A) than than
    (B) to to
    (C) with with
    (D) from from
    Answer: from
    from
    Explanation: The correct preposition after 'different' is 'from'.
    'different' ৰ পিছত শুদ্ধ preposition হৈছে 'from'।
    Q14. The meeting was postponed ___ Monday.
    সভাখন সোমবাৰলৈ ___।
    (A) at at
    (B) in in
    (C) until until
    (D) on on
    Answer: until
    until
    Explanation: 'Postponed until Monday' means the meeting is delayed to that time.
    'postponed until Monday' মানে সভাখন সেই সময়লৈ পিছলৈ ঠেলি দিয়া হৈছে।
    Q15. He is neither tall ___ strong.
    তেওঁ ওখও নহয় ___ বলৱানও নহয়।
    (A) or or
    (B) and and
    (C) nor nor
    (D) but but
    Answer: nor
    nor
    Explanation: The correlative conjunction pair is 'neither...nor'.
    correlative conjunction ৰ যোৰ হৈছে 'neither...nor'।
    Q16. Either of the answers ___ correct.
    উত্তৰবোৰৰ এটা ___ শুদ্ধ।
    (A) are are
    (B) were were
    (C) have have
    (D) is is
    Answer: is
    is
    Explanation: 'Either' is singular, so it takes the singular verb 'is'.
    'either' singular; সেয়ে ই singular verb 'is' লয়।
    Q17. I ___ him yesterday.
    মই কালি তেওঁক ___।
    (A) meet meet
    (B) have met have met
    (C) met met
    (D) had met had met
    Answer: met
    met
    Explanation: 'Yesterday' refers to past time, so the simple past 'met' is used.
    'yesterday' এ অতীত সময় বুজায়; সেয়ে simple past 'met' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q18. He ___ TV when I called him.
    মই তেওঁক ফোন কৰোঁতে তেওঁ টি.ভি. ___।
    (A) watched watched
    (B) was watching was watching
    (C) watches watches
    (D) has watched has watched
    Answer: was watching
    was watching
    Explanation: The past continuous 'was watching' shows an action in progress when another past action happened.
    past continuous 'was watching' য়ে আন এটা অতীত কাৰ্য ঘটোঁতে চলি থকা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q19. Change into passive voice: People speak English all over the world.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: People speak English all over the world.
    (A) English is spoken all over the world. English is spoken all over the world.
    (B) English was spoken all over the world. English was spoken all over the world.
    (C) English speaks all over the world. English speaks all over the world.
    (D) English has spoken all over the world. English has spoken all over the world.
    Answer: English is spoken all over the world.
    English is spoken all over the world.
    Explanation: The present simple passive is formed with 'is/are' plus the past participle.
    present simple ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'is/are' আৰু past participle ৰে গঠিত হয়।
    Q20. Change into active voice: The report has been submitted by the committee.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The report has been submitted by the committee.
    (A) The committee submitted the report. The committee submitted the report.
    (B) The committee has submitted the report. The committee has submitted the report.
    (C) The committee had submitted the report. The committee had submitted the report.
    (D) The committee submits the report. The committee submits the report.
    Answer: The committee has submitted the report.
    The committee has submitted the report.
    Explanation: The present perfect passive 'has been submitted' corresponds to the active 'has submitted'.
    present perfect ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'has been submitted' ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য হৈছে 'has submitted'।
    Q21. Change into indirect speech: She said, 'Where are you going?'
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: She said, 'Where are you going?'
    (A) She asked where I was going. She asked where I was going.
    (B) She asked where was I going. She asked where was I going.
    (C) She said where I was going. She said where I was going.
    (D) She asked where I am going. She asked where I am going.
    Answer: She asked where I was going.
    She asked where I was going.
    Explanation: A wh-question in indirect speech uses statement word order: 'where I was going'.
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত wh-question এ statement ৰ word order লয়: 'where I was going'।
    Q22. ___ you please help me?
    আপুনি মোক সহায় কৰিব ___?
    (A) Should Should
    (B) Must Must
    (C) May May
    (D) Could Could
    Answer: Could
    Could
    Explanation: 'Could' is used to make a polite request.
    ভদ্ৰভাৱে অনুৰোধ কৰিবলৈ 'could' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q23. Everyone has arrived, ___?
    সকলোৱে আহি পাইছে, ___?
    (A) hasn't he hasn't he
    (B) haven't they haven't they
    (C) hasn't they hasn't they
    (D) have they have they
    Answer: haven't they
    haven't they
    Explanation: 'Everyone' is treated as plural in a question tag, so 'haven't they' is used.
    question tag ত 'everyone' ক plural হিচাপে ধৰা হয়; সেয়ে 'haven't they' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q24. If I were you, I ___ accept the offer.
    যদি মই তোমাৰ ঠাইত থাকিলোহেঁতেন, মই প্ৰস্তাৱটো ___ গ্ৰহণ কৰিলোহেঁতেন।
    (A) will will
    (B) would would
    (C) can can
    (D) shall shall
    Answer: would
    would
    Explanation: The second conditional uses 'would' plus the base verb in the main clause.
    second conditional ত main clause ত 'would' আৰু verb ৰ base form ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q25. Choose the correct negative sentence with 'doesn't':
    'doesn't' ৰ সৈতে শুদ্ধ ঋণাত্মক বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) She don't knows the answer. She don't knows the answer.
    (B) She doesn't knows the answer. She doesn't knows the answer.
    (C) She doesn't know the answer. She doesn't know the answer.
    (D) She not know the answer. She not know the answer.
    Answer: She doesn't know the answer.
    She doesn't know the answer.
    Explanation: After 'doesn't', the base form 'know' is used, not 'knows'.
    'doesn't' ৰ পিছত 'knows' নহয়, verb ৰ base form 'know' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।

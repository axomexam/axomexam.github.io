    Grammar Mock Test — Set 7

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. We saw ___ elephant in the zoo.
    আমি চিৰিয়াখানাত ___ হাতী দেখিলোঁ।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: an
    an
    Explanation: 'Elephant' begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    'elephant' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. He is ___ best student in the class.
    তেওঁ শ্ৰেণীৰ ___ শ্ৰেষ্ঠ ছাত্ৰ।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: the
    the
    Explanation: 'The' is used before a superlative degree.
    superlative degree ৰ আগত 'the' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q3. The plural of 'leaf' is:
    'leaf' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) leafs leafs
    (B) leaves leaves
    (C) leafes leafes
    (D) leaf leaf
    Answer: leaves
    leaves
    Explanation: The plural of 'leaf' is 'leaves'.
    'leaf' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'leaves'।
    Q4. The plural of 'crisis' is:
    'crisis' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) crisises crisises
    (B) crisis crisis
    (C) crises crises
    (D) crisi crisi
    Answer: crises
    crises
    Explanation: The plural of 'crisis' is 'crises'.
    'crisis' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'crises'।
    Q5. The abstract noun of 'able' is:
    'able' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) able able
    (B) ably ably
    (C) ability ability
    (D) ablement ablement
    Answer: ability
    ability
    Explanation: The abstract noun of 'able' is 'ability'.
    'able' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য হৈছে 'ability'।
    Q6. A collective noun for a group of lions is:
    সিংহৰ দলৰ সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) herd herd
    (B) pack pack
    (C) flock flock
    (D) pride pride
    Answer: pride
    pride
    Explanation: 'Pride' is the collective noun used for a group of lions.
    সিংহৰ গোটৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত collective noun হৈছে 'pride'।
    Q7. The man ___ helped me was very kind.
    যি মানুহজনে মোক সহায় কৰিছিল তেওঁ বৰ দয়ালু আছিল।
    (A) who who
    (B) whom whom
    (C) which which
    (D) whose whose
    Answer: who
    who
    Explanation: 'Who' is used as the subject relative pronoun for a person.
    ব্যক্তিৰ বাবে কৰ্তা হিচাপে relative pronoun 'who' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q8. The pen ___ I lost was new.
    মই হেৰুওৱা কলমটো নতুন আছিল।
    (A) who who
    (B) whose whose
    (C) which which
    (D) whom whom
    Answer: which
    which
    Explanation: 'Which' is used for things, so 'the pen which I lost' is correct.
    বস্তুৰ বাবে 'which' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়; সেয়ে 'the pen which I lost' শুদ্ধ।
    Q9. He made ___ a cup of tea.
    তেওঁ নিজৰ বাবে এক কাপ চাহ বনালে।
    (A) him him
    (B) his his
    (C) he he
    (D) himself himself
    Answer: himself
    himself
    Explanation: 'Himself' is the reflexive pronoun referring back to 'he'.
    'himself' reflexive pronoun য়ে 'he' লৈ উলটি সূচায়।
    Q10. This house is ___.
    এই ঘৰটো ___।
    (A) our our
    (B) us us
    (C) ours ours
    (D) we we
    Answer: ours
    ours
    Explanation: 'Ours' is the possessive pronoun that stands alone without a noun.
    'ours' হৈছে possessive pronoun, যি noun ৰ পিছত অকলে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q11. The comparative degree of 'far' (distance) is:
    'far' (দূৰত্ব) ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) farther farther
    (B) farthest farthest
    (C) more far more far
    (D) farer farer
    Answer: farther
    farther
    Explanation: For physical distance, the comparative degree of 'far' is 'farther'.
    শাৰীৰিক দূৰত্বৰ বাবে 'far' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী 'farther'।
    Q12. The superlative degree of 'many' is:
    'many' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) more more
    (B) many many
    (C) much much
    (D) most most
    Answer: most
    most
    Explanation: The superlative degree of 'many' is 'most'.
    'many' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'most'।
    Q13. She is afraid ___ dogs.
    তাই কুকুৰক ভয় পায়।
    (A) from from
    (B) of of
    (C) with with
    (D) at at
    Answer: of
    of
    Explanation: The correct preposition after 'afraid' is 'of'.
    'afraid' ৰ পিছত শুদ্ধ preposition হৈছে 'of'।
    Q14. The train arrived ___ time.
    ৰেলগাড়ীখন সময়মতে আহিল।
    (A) in in
    (B) at at
    (C) on on
    (D) by by
    Answer: on
    on
    Explanation: The fixed phrase is 'on time', meaning at the expected time.
    নিৰ্দিষ্ট বাক্যাংশ 'on time', যাৰ অৰ্থ হৈছে নিৰ্ধাৰিত সময়ত।
    Q15. Work hard ___ you will fail.
    পৰিশ্ৰম কৰা ___ তুমি ব্যৰ্থ হ'ব।
    (A) and and
    (B) but but
    (C) or or
    (D) so so
    Answer: or
    or
    Explanation: 'Or' joins two alternatives here: work hard, or else you will fail.
    ইয়াত 'or' য়ে দুটা বিকল্প সংযোগ কৰে: পৰিশ্ৰম কৰা, নহ'লে ব্যৰ্থ হ'ব।
    Q16. Neither Ram nor his friends ___ present.
    ৰাম বা তেওঁৰ বন্ধুবৰ্শে ___ উপস্থিত নাছিল।
    (A) was was
    (B) is is
    (C) were were
    (D) has has
    Answer: were
    were
    Explanation: With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'friends', so 'were' is used.
    'neither...nor' ৰ ক্ষেত্ৰত verb ই নিকটৱৰ্তী কৰ্তা 'friends' ৰ সৈতে মিলে; সেয়ে 'were' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q17. I ___ my homework before he came.
    তেওঁ অহাৰ আগতে মই মোৰ গৃহকৰ্ম ___।
    (A) had finished had finished
    (B) finished finished
    (C) have finished have finished
    (D) finish finish
    Answer: had finished
    had finished
    Explanation: The past perfect 'had finished' shows an action completed before another past action.
    past perfect 'had finished' য়ে আন এটা অতীত কাৰ্যৰ আগতে সম্পূৰ্ণ হোৱা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q18. She ___ in this school for ten years.
    তাই এই বিদ্যালয়ত দহ বছৰ ধৰি ___।
    (A) has taught has taught
    (B) has been teaching has been teaching
    (C) is teaching is teaching
    (D) taught taught
    Answer: has been teaching
    has been teaching
    Explanation: The present perfect continuous 'has been teaching' is used for an action continuing from the past until now.
    অতীতৰ পৰা এতিয়ালৈকে চলি থকা কাৰ্যৰ বাবে present perfect continuous 'has been teaching' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q19. Change into passive voice: Someone stole my pen.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: Someone stole my pen.
    (A) My pen was stolen. My pen was stolen.
    (B) My pen is stolen. My pen is stolen.
    (C) My pen has stolen. My pen has stolen.
    (D) My pen stole. My pen stole.
    Answer: My pen was stolen.
    My pen was stolen.
    Explanation: The simple past passive is formed with 'was/were' plus the past participle, so 'was stolen' is correct.
    simple past ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'was/were' আৰু past participle ৰে গঠিত; সেয়ে 'was stolen' শুদ্ধ।
    Q20. Change into active voice: The song is being sung by her.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The song is being sung by her.
    (A) She is singing the song. She is singing the song.
    (B) She sings the song. She sings the song.
    (C) She sang the song. She sang the song.
    (D) She has sung the song. She has sung the song.
    Answer: She is singing the song.
    She is singing the song.
    Explanation: The present continuous passive 'is being sung' corresponds to the active 'is singing'.
    present continuous ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'is being sung' ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য হৈছে 'is singing'।
    Q21. Change into indirect speech: She said, 'I will come tomorrow.'
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: She said, 'I will come tomorrow.'
    (A) She said that she will come tomorrow. She said that she will come tomorrow.
    (B) She said that she came tomorrow. She said that she came tomorrow.
    (C) She says that she would come tomorrow. She says that she would come tomorrow.
    (D) She said that she would come the next day. She said that she would come the next day.
    Answer: She said that she would come the next day.
    She said that she would come the next day.
    Explanation: In indirect speech, 'will' changes to 'would' and 'tomorrow' changes to 'the next day'.
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত 'will' 'would' লৈ আৰু 'tomorrow' 'the next day' লৈ সলনি হয়।
    Q22. ___ I open the window?
    মই খিৰিকীখন খোলোঁ ___?
    (A) May May
    (B) Should Should
    (C) Must Must
    (D) Need Need
    Answer: May
    May
    Explanation: 'May' is used to ask for permission politely.
    ভদ্ৰভাৱে অনুমতি বিচাৰিবলৈ 'may' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q23. They don't play cricket, ___?
    তেওঁলোকে ক্ৰিকেট নাখেলে, ___?
    (A) don't they don't they
    (B) do they do they
    (C) didn't they didn't they
    (D) are they are they
    Answer: do they
    do they
    Explanation: A negative statement takes a positive question tag, so 'do they' is correct.
    ঋণাত্মক বাক্যৰ সৈতে ধনাত্মক question tag লগে; সেয়ে 'do they' শুদ্ধ।
    Q24. If I had studied hard, I ___ passed.
    যদি মই কঠোৰ পৰিশ্ৰম কৰিলোহেঁতেন, মই ___ উত্তীৰ্ণ হ'লোহেঁতেন।
    (A) will have will have
    (B) would would
    (C) would have would have
    (D) had had
    Answer: would have
    would have
    Explanation: In the third conditional, the main clause uses 'would have' plus the past participle.
    third conditional ত main clause ত 'would have' আৰু past participle ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q25. Choose the correct comparative sentence:
    শুদ্ধ তুলনামূলক বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) She is taller than her sister. She is taller than her sister.
    (B) She is more tall than her sister. She is more tall than her sister.
    (C) She is tallest than her sister. She is tallest than her sister.
    (D) She is taller then her sister. She is taller then her sister.
    Answer: She is taller than her sister.
    She is taller than her sister.
    Explanation: The comparative form is 'taller', and comparison uses 'than', not 'then' or 'more tall'.
    তুলনামূলক ৰূপ 'taller' আৰু তুলনাত 'than' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, 'then' বা 'more tall' নহয়।

    Grammar Mock Test — Set 6

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. She is ___ M.A. in English.
    তাই ইংৰাজীত ___ এম.এ.।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: an
    an
    Explanation: The letter M is pronounced 'em', which begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    M আখৰৰ উচ্চাৰণ 'em', যি স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. ___ Himalayas are the highest mountain range in the world.
    ___ হিমালয় পৃথিৱীৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ পৰ্বতমালা।
    (A) A A
    (B) An An
    (C) The The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: The
    The
    Explanation: 'The' is used before the names of mountain ranges.
    পৰ্বতমালাৰ নামৰ আগত 'The' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q3. The plural of 'mouse' is:
    'mouse' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) mouses mouses
    (B) mice mice
    (C) mices mices
    (D) mouse mouse
    Answer: mice
    mice
    Explanation: The plural of 'mouse' is 'mice'.
    'mouse' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'mice'।
    Q4. The abstract noun of 'child' is:
    'child' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) children children
    (B) childish childish
    (C) childly childly
    (D) childhood childhood
    Answer: childhood
    childhood
    Explanation: The abstract noun of 'child' is 'childhood'.
    'child' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য হৈছে 'childhood'।
    Q5. A collective noun for a group of sheep is:
    ভেড়াৰ দলৰ সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) herd herd
    (B) flock flock
    (C) pack pack
    (D) swarm swarm
    Answer: flock
    flock
    Explanation: 'Flock' is the collective noun used for a group of sheep.
    ভেড়াৰ গোটৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত collective noun হৈছে 'flock'।
    Q6. ___ of the two boys was punished.
    দুজন ল'ৰাৰ ___ শাস্তি পাইছিল।
    (A) Both Both
    (B) All All
    (C) Either Either
    (D) Neither Neither
    Answer: Neither
    Neither
    Explanation: 'Neither' means not one of the two; it takes a singular verb, so 'Neither of the two boys was punished' is correct.
    'Neither' মানে দুজনৰ এজনো নহয়; ই singular verb লয়; সেয়ে 'Neither of the two boys was punished' শুদ্ধ।
    Q7. The book ___ I bought yesterday is interesting.
    কালি মই কিনা কিতাপখন আমোদজনক।
    (A) which which
    (B) who who
    (C) whom whom
    (D) whose whose
    Answer: which
    which
    Explanation: 'Which' is the relative pronoun used for things.
    বস্তুৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত relative pronoun হৈছে 'which'।
    Q8. She blamed ___ for the mistake.
    তাই ভুলটোৰ বাবে নিজকে দোষ দিলে।
    (A) her her
    (B) hers hers
    (C) herself herself
    (D) she she
    Answer: herself
    herself
    Explanation: 'Herself' is the reflexive pronoun used when the subject and object are the same person.
    কৰ্তা আৰু কৰ্ম একেই ব্যক্তি হ'লে reflexive pronoun 'herself' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q9. The comparative degree of 'little' is:
    'little' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) littler littler
    (B) lesser lesser
    (C) least least
    (D) less less
    Answer: less
    less
    Explanation: The comparative degree of 'little' is 'less'.
    'little' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'less'।
    Q10. The superlative degree of 'bad' is:
    'bad' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) badder badder
    (B) worse worse
    (C) worst worst
    (D) most bad most bad
    Answer: worst
    worst
    Explanation: The superlative degree of 'bad' is 'worst'.
    'bad' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'worst'।
    Q11. ___ Sun rises in the east.
    ___ সূৰ্য পূব দিশে উদয় হয়।
    (A) A A
    (B) An An
    (C) The The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: The
    The
    Explanation: 'The' is used before unique objects like the sun.
    সূৰ্যৰ দৰে একক বস্তুৰ আগত 'The' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q12. He is good ___ mathematics.
    তেওঁ গণিতত পাৰদৰ্শী।
    (A) in in
    (B) at at
    (C) on on
    (D) with with
    Answer: at
    at
    Explanation: The correct preposition after 'good' meaning skilled is 'at'.
    দক্ষতা বুজাবলৈ 'good' ৰ পিছত শুদ্ধ preposition হৈছে 'at'।
    Q13. She has been living here ___ 2015.
    তাই ২০১৫ চনৰ পৰা ইয়াত বাস কৰি আছে।
    (A) for for
    (B) from from
    (C) since since
    (D) by by
    Answer: since
    since
    Explanation: 'Since' is used with a point of time in the perfect tense.
    perfect tense ত সময়ৰ নিৰ্দিষ্ট বিন্দুৰ সৈতে 'since' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q14. He was ill, ___ he attended the class.
    তেওঁ অসুস্থ আছিল, ___ তেওঁ শ্ৰেণীত উপস্থিত থাকিল।
    (A) and and
    (B) or or
    (C) so so
    (D) but but
    Answer: but
    but
    Explanation: 'But' shows contrast between being ill and attending the class.
    অসুস্থ হোৱা আৰু শ্ৰেণীত উপস্থিত থকাৰ মাজৰ বিপৰীত ভাৱ দেখুৱাবলৈ 'but' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q15. Each of the students ___ a book.
    প্ৰতিজন ছাত্ৰৰ ওচৰত এখন কিতাপ ___।
    (A) have have
    (B) having having
    (C) are are
    (D) has has
    Answer: has
    has
    Explanation: 'Each' is singular, so it takes the singular verb 'has'.
    'Each' singular, সেয়ে ই singular verb 'has' লয়।
    Q16. He ___ to Delhi last year.
    তেওঁ যোৱা বছৰ দিল্লীলৈ ___।
    (A) go go
    (B) goes goes
    (C) went went
    (D) has gone has gone
    Answer: went
    went
    Explanation: 'Last year' refers to past time, so the simple past 'went' is used.
    'last year' এ অতীত সময় বুজায়; সেয়ে simple past 'went' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q17. They ___ here since morning.
    তেওঁলোক ৰাতিপুৱাৰ পৰা ইয়াত ___।
    (A) are are
    (B) has been has been
    (C) were were
    (D) have been have been
    Answer: have been
    have been
    Explanation: 'They' is plural, so the present perfect form is 'have been'.
    'They' plural, সেয়ে present perfect ৰূপ 'have been' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q18. I ___ him tomorrow.
    মই কাইলৈ তেওঁক ___।
    (A) meet meet
    (B) met met
    (C) will meet will meet
    (D) have met have met
    Answer: will meet
    will meet
    Explanation: 'Tomorrow' refers to future time, so 'will meet' is used.
    'tomorrow' এ ভৱিষ্যৎ সময় বুজায়; সেয়ে 'will meet' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q19. Change into passive voice: They built the bridge in 1990.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: They built the bridge in 1990.
    (A) The bridge was built in 1990. The bridge was built in 1990.
    (B) The bridge is built in 1990. The bridge is built in 1990.
    (C) The bridge has built in 1990. The bridge has built in 1990.
    (D) The bridge built in 1990. The bridge built in 1990.
    Answer: The bridge was built in 1990.
    The bridge was built in 1990.
    Explanation: The simple past active 'built' becomes 'was built' in the passive voice.
    simple past ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য 'built' কৰ্মবাচ্যত 'was built' হয়।
    Q20. Change into active voice: The letter was written by her.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The letter was written by her.
    (A) She wrote the letter. She wrote the letter.
    (B) She writes the letter. She writes the letter.
    (C) She has wrote the letter. She has wrote the letter.
    (D) The letter wrote her. The letter wrote her.
    Answer: She wrote the letter.
    She wrote the letter.
    Explanation: In the active voice the subject performs the action: 'She wrote the letter.'
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যত কৰ্তাই কাৰ্য কৰে: 'She wrote the letter.'
    Q21. Change into indirect speech: He said, 'I am busy.'
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: He said, 'I am busy.'
    (A) He said that he is busy. He said that he is busy.
    (B) He said that he was busy. He said that he was busy.
    (C) He says that he was busy. He says that he was busy.
    (D) He said that I am busy. He said that I am busy.
    Answer: He said that he was busy.
    He said that he was busy.
    Explanation: When the reporting verb is in the past, the present tense 'am' changes to 'was'.
    reporting verb অতীতকালত হ'লে present tense 'am' 'was' লৈ সলনি হয়।
    Q22. You ___ respect your parents.
    তোমালোকে তোমালোকৰ পিতৃ-মাতৃক সন্মান কৰা ___।
    (A) should should
    (B) may may
    (C) can can
    (D) would would
    Answer: should
    should
    Explanation: 'Should' expresses moral duty or advice.
    'should' য়ে নৈতিক কৰ্তব্য বা উপদেশ বুজায়।
    Q23. She is coming, ___?
    তাই আহি আছে, ___?
    (A) is she is she
    (B) isn't she isn't she
    (C) does she does she
    (D) doesn't she doesn't she
    Answer: isn't she
    isn't she
    Explanation: A positive statement takes a negative question tag; the auxiliary is 'is', so the tag is 'isn't she'.
    ধনাত্মক বাক্যৰ সৈতে ঋণাত্মক question tag লগে; auxiliary 'is', সেয়ে tag 'isn't she'।
    Q24. If it rains, we ___ the match.
    যদি বৰষুণ দিয়ে, আমি খেলখন ___।
    (A) cancel cancel
    (B) cancelled cancelled
    (C) will cancel will cancel
    (D) would cancel would cancel
    Answer: will cancel
    will cancel
    Explanation: In a first conditional sentence, the main clause uses 'will' plus the base verb.
    first conditional বাক্যত main clause ত 'will' আৰু verb ৰ base form ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q25. Choose the correct negative sentence in simple present:
    সৰল বৰ্তমান কালৰ শুদ্ধ ঋণাত্মক বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) He don't like tea. He don't like tea.
    (B) He not like tea. He not like tea.
    (C) He didn't likes tea. He didn't likes tea.
    (D) He doesn't like tea. He doesn't like tea.
    Answer: He doesn't like tea.
    He doesn't like tea.
    Explanation: In the present simple with 'he', the negative is formed with 'doesn't' and the base verb.
    'he' ৰ সৈতে present simple ৰ ঋণাত্মক ৰূপ 'doesn't' আৰু verb ৰ base form ৰে গঠন হয়।

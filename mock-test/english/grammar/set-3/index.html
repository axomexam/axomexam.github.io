    Grammar Mock Test — Set 3

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. He is fond ___ music.
    তেওঁ সংগীতৰ অনুৰাগী।
    (A) on on
    (B) of of
    (C) in in
    (D) at at
    Answer: of
    of
    Explanation: 'Fond' is followed by the preposition 'of'.
    'fond' ৰ পিছত preposition 'of' আহে।
    Q2. She is good ___ English.
    তাই ইংৰাজীত ভাল।
    (A) in in
    (B) on on
    (C) at at
    (D) with with
    Answer: at
    at
    Explanation: 'Good at' is the correct phrase to show skill.
    দক্ষতা দেখুৱাবলৈ শুদ্ধ phrase হৈছে 'good at'।
    Q3. The book is ___ the table.
    কিতাপখন মেজৰ ওপৰত আছে।
    (A) in in
    (B) at at
    (C) on on
    (D) into into
    Answer: on
    on
    Explanation: 'On' is used for something resting on a surface.
    কোনো বস্তু পৃষ্ঠৰ ওপৰত থকা বুজাবলৈ 'on' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q4. He died ___ cholera.
    তেওঁ কলেৰাত মৃত্যুবৰণ কৰিলে।
    (A) from from
    (B) of of
    (C) by by
    (D) with with
    Answer: of
    of
    Explanation: 'Die of' is used for a disease such as cholera.
    কলেৰাৰ দৰে ৰোগৰ ক্ষেত্ৰত 'die of' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q5. I prefer tea ___ coffee.
    মই কফিতকৈ চাহ পচন্দ কৰোঁ।
    (A) than than
    (B) from from
    (C) to to
    (D) over over
    Answer: to
    to
    Explanation: 'Prefer' is followed by 'to' when comparing two things.
    দুটা বস্তু তুলনা কৰোঁতে 'prefer' ৰ পিছত 'to' আহে।
    Q6. He congratulated me ___ my success.
    তেওঁ মোৰ সফলতাত অভিনন্দন জনাইছিল।
    (A) for for
    (B) on on
    (C) at at
    (D) to to
    Answer: on
    on
    Explanation: 'Congratulate ... on' is the correct preposition.
    'congratulate ... on' হৈছে শুদ্ধ preposition।
    Q7. She is afraid ___ dogs.
    তাই কুকুৰক ভয় কৰে।
    (A) from from
    (B) with with
    (C) of of
    (D) by by
    Answer: of
    of
    Explanation: 'Afraid of' is the correct prepositional phrase.
    'afraid of' হৈছে শুদ্ধ prepositional phrase।
    Q8. We have been living here ___ 2010.
    আমি 2010 চনৰ পৰা ইয়াত বাস কৰি আছোঁ।
    (A) for for
    (B) since since
    (C) from from
    (D) at at
    Answer: since
    since
    Explanation: 'Since' is used with a point of time in the present perfect continuous.
    present perfect continuous ত নিৰ্দিষ্ট সময়বিন্দুৰ সৈতে 'since' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q9. He has been ill ___ two weeks.
    তেওঁ দুসপ্তাহ ধৰি অসুস্থ।
    (A) since since
    (B) from from
    (C) for for
    (D) at at
    Answer: for
    for
    Explanation: 'For' is used with a period of time.
    সময়ছোৱা বুজাবলৈ 'for' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q10. Work hard ___ you should fail.
    কঠোৰ পৰিশ্ৰম কৰা, নহ'লে তুমি অনুত্তীৰ্ণ হ'বা।
    (A) lest lest
    (B) unless unless
    (C) until until
    (D) if if
    Answer: lest
    lest
    Explanation: 'Lest' is followed by 'should' and means 'for fear that'.
    'lest' ৰ পিছত 'should' আহে আৰু ই 'ভয়ত যাতে' বুজায়।
    Q11. He is poor ___ honest.
    তেওঁ দুখীয়া কিন্তু সৎ।
    (A) and and
    (B) or or
    (C) but but
    (D) so so
    Answer: but
    but
    Explanation: 'But' shows the contrast between poor and honest.
    'but' য়ে দৰিদ্ৰ আৰু সৎৰ মাজৰ বিপৰীততা দেখুৱায়।
    Q12. Wait here ___ I return.
    মই উভতি অহালৈকে ইয়াত অপেক্ষা কৰা।
    (A) unless unless
    (B) until until
    (C) if if
    (D) although although
    Answer: until
    until
    Explanation: 'Until' shows the time up to which an action continues.
    'until' য়ে কাৰ্য কেতিয়ালৈকে চলি থাকে দেখুৱায়।
    Q13. You will not pass ___ you work hard.
    কঠোৰ পৰিশ্ৰম নকৰিলে তুমি উত্তীৰ্ণ নহ'বা।
    (A) if if
    (B) until until
    (C) unless unless
    (D) because because
    Answer: unless
    unless
    Explanation: 'Unless' means 'if not'.
    'unless' মানে 'যদি নহয়'।
    Q14. He not only sings ___ dances.
    তেওঁ কেৱল গান নকৰে, নৃত্যও কৰে।
    (A) and and
    (B) but also but also
    (C) or or
    (D) nor nor
    Answer: but also
    but also
    Explanation: 'Not only ... but also' is the correct correlative pair.
    'not only ... but also' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Q15. Neither Ram ___ Shyam was present.
    ৰামও নাছিল, শ্যামও উপস্থিত নাছিল।
    (A) or or
    (B) and and
    (C) nor nor
    (D) but but
    Answer: nor
    nor
    Explanation: 'Neither ... nor' is the correct correlative pair.
    'neither ... nor' হৈছে শুদ্ধ যুগল।
    Q16. He is so tired ___ he cannot walk.
    তেওঁ ইমান ভাগৰুৱা যে খোজ কাঢ়িব নোৱাৰে।
    (A) so so
    (B) as as
    (C) that that
    (D) than than
    Answer: that
    that
    Explanation: 'So ... that' is the correct correlative pair.
    'so ... that' হৈছে শুদ্ধ correlative যুগল।
    Q17. Choose the correct sentence:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) Although he is rich, yet he is unhappy. Although he is rich, yet he is unhappy.
    (B) Although he is rich, but he is unhappy. Although he is rich, but he is unhappy.
    (C) Although he is rich, he is unhappy. Although he is rich, he is unhappy.
    (D) Although he is rich and he is unhappy. Although he is rich and he is unhappy.
    Answer: Although he is rich, he is unhappy.
    Although he is rich, he is unhappy.
    Explanation: 'Although' is not followed by 'yet' or 'but' in standard usage.
    মান্য ব্যৱহাৰত 'although' ৰ পিছত 'yet' বা 'but' নালাগে।
    Q18. The word 'and' is a:
    'and' শব্দটো কি?
    (A) Preposition অব্যয় (preposition)
    (B) Conjunction সংযোজক
    (C) Interjection বিস্ময়সূচক অব্যয়
    (D) Adverb ক্ৰিয়া বিশেষণ
    Answer: Conjunction
    সংযোজক
    Explanation: 'And' joins words or clauses, so it is a conjunction.
    'and' য়ে শব্দ বা উপবাক্য সংযোগ কৰে, সেয়ে ই conjunction।
    Q19. Alas! He is dead. The word 'Alas' is a/an:
    Alas! He is dead. 'Alas' শব্দটো কি?
    (A) Conjunction সংযোজক
    (B) Preposition অব্যয় (preposition)
    (C) Interjection বিস্ময়সূচক অব্যয়
    (D) Pronoun সৰ্বনাম
    Answer: Interjection
    বিস্ময়সূচক অব্যয়
    Explanation: 'Alas' expresses emotion, so it is an interjection.
    'Alas' য়ে ভাৱ প্ৰকাশ কৰে, সেয়ে ই interjection।
    Q20. He divided the money ___ the two brothers.
    তেওঁ দুজন ভাইৰ মাজত টকা ভাগ কৰিলে।
    (A) among among
    (B) between between
    (C) into into
    (D) with with
    Answer: between
    between
    Explanation: 'Between' is used for two persons.
    দুজন ব্যক্তিৰ বাবে 'between' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q21. The teacher distributed sweets ___ the students.
    শিক্ষকে ছাত্ৰসকলৰ মাজত মিঠাই বিতৰণ কৰিলে।
    (A) between between
    (B) among among
    (C) in in
    (D) into into
    Answer: among
    among
    Explanation: 'Among' is used for more than two persons.
    দুইতকৈ অধিক ব্যক্তিৰ বাবে 'among' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q22. He jumped ___ the river.
    তেওঁ নদীত জাপ মাৰিলে।
    (A) in in
    (B) on on
    (C) into into
    (D) at at
    Answer: into
    into
    Explanation: 'Jump into' shows movement from outside to inside.
    'jump into' য়ে বাহিৰৰ পৰা ভিতৰলৈ গতি দেখুৱায়।
    Q23. I agree ___ your proposal.
    মই আপোনাৰ প্ৰস্তাৱত সন্মত।
    (A) to to
    (B) with with
    (C) on on
    (D) for for
    Answer: to
    to
    Explanation: 'Agree to' is used with a proposal or plan.
    প্ৰস্তাৱ বা পৰিকল্পনাৰ সৈতে 'agree to' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q24. I agree ___ you on this point.
    এই বিষয়ত মই আপোনাৰ সৈতে সন্মত।
    (A) to to
    (B) with with
    (C) on on
    (D) at at
    Answer: with
    with
    Explanation: 'Agree with' is used with a person.
    ব্যক্তিৰ সৈতে 'agree with' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q25. The meeting will be held ___ Monday.
    সভাখন সোমবাৰে অনুষ্ঠিত হ'ব।
    (A) in in
    (B) at at
    (C) on on
    (D) by by
    Answer: on
    on
    Explanation: 'On' is used with days of the week.
    সপ্তাহৰ দিনবোৰৰ সৈতে 'on' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।

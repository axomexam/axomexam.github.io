    Grammar Mock Test — Set 8

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. He bought ___ umbrella.
    তেওঁ ___ ছাতি কিনিলে।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: an
    an
    Explanation: 'Umbrella' begins with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used.
    'umbrella' ৰ উচ্চাৰণ স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়; সেয়ে 'an' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. ___ Gita is a sacred book.
    ___ গীতা এখন পবিত্ৰ গ্ৰন্থ।
    (A) A A
    (B) An An
    (C) The The
    (D) No article প্ৰবন্ধ নাই
    Answer: The
    The
    Explanation: 'The' is used before the names of sacred books.
    পবিত্ৰ গ্ৰন্থৰ নামৰ আগত 'The' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q3. The plural of 'datum' is:
    'datum' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) datums datums
    (B) data data
    (C) datases datases
    (D) datum datum
    Answer: data
    data
    Explanation: The plural of 'datum' is 'data'.
    'datum' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'data'।
    Q4. The plural of 'tooth' is:
    'tooth' ৰ বহুবচন কি?
    (A) teeth teeth
    (B) tooths tooths
    (C) toothes toothes
    (D) tooth tooth
    Answer: teeth
    teeth
    Explanation: The plural of 'tooth' is 'teeth'.
    'tooth' ৰ বহুবচন হৈছে 'teeth'।
    Q5. The abstract noun of 'free' is:
    'free' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) freely freely
    (B) freeness freeness
    (C) freedom freedom
    (D) free free
    Answer: freedom
    freedom
    Explanation: The abstract noun of 'free' is 'freedom'.
    'free' ৰ ভাৱবাচক বিশেষ্য হৈছে 'freedom'।
    Q6. A collective noun for a group of bees is:
    মৌমাখিৰ দলৰ সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য কি?
    (A) flock flock
    (B) herd herd
    (C) swarm swarm
    (D) pack pack
    Answer: swarm
    swarm
    Explanation: 'Swarm' is the collective noun used for a group of bees.
    মৌমাখিৰ গোটৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত collective noun হৈছে 'swarm'।
    Q7. ___ book is this?
    এইখন কাৰ কিতাপ?
    (A) Who Who
    (B) Whose Whose
    (C) Whom Whom
    (D) Which Which
    Answer: Whose
    Whose
    Explanation: 'Whose' is used to ask about possession.
    মালিকানা সোধাৰ বাবে 'whose' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q8. He is the boy ___ father is a doctor.
    তেওঁ সেই ল'ৰাটো যাৰ পিতৃ এজন চিকিৎসক।
    (A) who who
    (B) whom whom
    (C) which which
    (D) whose whose
    Answer: whose
    whose
    Explanation: 'Whose' shows possession and is used here for 'the boy's father'.
    'whose' য়ে মালিকানা দেখুৱায়; ইয়াত 'the boy's father' বুজাবলৈ ব্যৱহাৰ হৈছে।
    Q9. They enjoyed ___ at the party.
    তেওঁলোকে মেলত নিজকে আমোদ দিলে।
    (A) themselves themselves
    (B) themself themself
    (C) theirselves theirselves
    (D) their their
    Answer: themselves
    themselves
    Explanation: The correct reflexive pronoun for 'they' is 'themselves'.
    'they' ৰ বাবে শুদ্ধ reflexive pronoun হৈছে 'themselves'।
    Q10. Between you and ___, this is wrong.
    তোমাৰ আৰু ___ মাজত, এইটো ভুল।
    (A) I I
    (B) me me
    (C) mine mine
    (D) myself myself
    Answer: me
    me
    Explanation: After the preposition 'between', the objective pronoun 'me' is used.
    preposition 'between' ৰ পিছত objective pronoun 'me' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q11. The comparative degree of 'good' is:
    'good' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) gooder gooder
    (B) more good more good
    (C) better better
    (D) best best
    Answer: better
    better
    Explanation: The comparative degree of 'good' is 'better'.
    'good' ৰ তুলনামূলক ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'better'।
    Q12. The superlative degree of 'little' is:
    'little' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী কি?
    (A) littlest littlest
    (B) less less
    (C) lesser lesser
    (D) least least
    Answer: least
    least
    Explanation: The superlative degree of 'little' is 'least'.
    'little' ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ ডিগ্ৰী হৈছে 'least'।
    Q13. He is angry ___ me.
    তেওঁ মোৰ ওপৰত খং কৰিছে।
    (A) on on
    (B) with with
    (C) at at
    (D) for for
    Answer: with
    with
    Explanation: The correct preposition after 'angry' with a person is 'with'.
    ব্যক্তিৰ সৈতে 'angry' ৰ পিছত শুদ্ধ preposition হৈছে 'with'।
    Q14. The cat jumped ___ the fence.
    মেকুৰীটো বেৰৰ ___ জাঁপ মাৰিলে।
    (A) on on
    (B) into into
    (C) over over
    (D) at at
    Answer: over
    over
    Explanation: 'Over' means across from one side to the other, which fits jumping across a fence.
    'over' মানে এফালৰ পৰা আনফাললৈ; বেৰৰ ওপৰেৰে জাঁপ মৰাৰ বাবে ই উপযুক্ত।
    Q15. She is rich ___ not happy.
    তাই ধনী ___ সুখী নহয়।
    (A) and and
    (B) but but
    (C) or or
    (D) so so
    Answer: but
    but
    Explanation: 'But' is used to join two contrasting ideas.
    দুটা বিপৰীত ভাৱ সংযোগ কৰিবলৈ 'but' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q16. The news ___ shocking.
    বাতৰিটো চমকপ্ৰদ ___।
    (A) are are
    (B) were were
    (C) is is
    (D) have have
    Answer: is
    is
    Explanation: 'News' is an uncountable noun and takes the singular verb 'is'.
    'news' uncountable noun; সেয়ে ই singular verb 'is' লয়।
    Q17. By next year, he ___ his degree.
    অহা বছৰৰ ভিতৰত তেওঁ তেওঁৰ ডিগ্ৰী ___।
    (A) will complete will complete
    (B) will have completed will have completed
    (C) has completed has completed
    (D) completed completed
    Answer: will have completed
    will have completed
    Explanation: The future perfect 'will have completed' shows an action finished before a future time.
    future perfect 'will have completed' য়ে ভৱিষ্যৎ সময়ৰ আগতে সম্পূৰ্ণ হোৱা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q18. Look! The baby ___.
    চোৱা! কেঁচুৱাটো ___।
    (A) cries cries
    (B) cried cried
    (C) is crying is crying
    (D) has cried has cried
    Answer: is crying
    is crying
    Explanation: 'Look!' signals an action happening now, so the present continuous 'is crying' is used.
    'Look!' য়ে এতিয়া ঘটি থকা কাৰ্য বুজায়; সেয়ে present continuous 'is crying' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q19. Change into passive voice: They are painting the wall.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: They are painting the wall.
    (A) The wall is painted. The wall is painted.
    (B) The wall is being painted. The wall is being painted.
    (C) The wall was being painted. The wall was being painted.
    (D) The wall has painted. The wall has painted.
    Answer: The wall is being painted.
    The wall is being painted.
    Explanation: The present continuous passive is formed with 'is/are being' plus the past participle.
    present continuous ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'is/are being' আৰু past participle ৰে গঠিত হয়।
    Q20. Change into active voice: The work has been done by him.
    কৰ্তৃবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The work has been done by him.
    (A) He has done the work. He has done the work.
    (B) He did the work. He did the work.
    (C) He does the work. He does the work.
    (D) He is doing the work. He is doing the work.
    Answer: He has done the work.
    He has done the work.
    Explanation: The present perfect passive 'has been done' corresponds to the active 'has done'.
    present perfect ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'has been done' ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য হৈছে 'has done'।
    Q21. Change into indirect speech: The teacher said to me, 'Open the door.'
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: The teacher said to me, 'Open the door.'
    (A) The teacher told me open the door. The teacher told me open the door.
    (B) The teacher said me to open the door. The teacher said me to open the door.
    (C) The teacher told me to open the door. The teacher told me to open the door.
    (D) The teacher asked me open the door. The teacher asked me open the door.
    Answer: The teacher told me to open the door.
    The teacher told me to open the door.
    Explanation: An imperative sentence is reported with 'told' plus 'to' and the base verb.
    আদেশবাচক বাক্যৰ পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত 'told' আৰু 'to' ৰ সৈতে verb ৰ base form ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q22. He ___ speak English fluently when he was young.
    তেওঁ ডেকা কালত ইংৰাজী অনৰ্গল ক'ব ___।
    (A) can can
    (B) could could
    (C) may may
    (D) must must
    Answer: could
    could
    Explanation: 'Could' is the past form of 'can' and shows past ability.
    'could' হৈছে 'can' ৰ অতীত ৰূপ আৰু ই অতীতৰ সামৰ্থ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q23. Let us go for a walk, ___?
    আহা, আমি খোজ কাঢ়িবলৈ যাওঁ, ___?
    (A) shall we shall we
    (B) will we will we
    (C) do we do we
    (D) don't we don't we
    Answer: shall we
    shall we
    Explanation: A sentence beginning with 'Let us' takes the question tag 'shall we'.
    'Let us' ৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা বাক্যৰ question tag হৈছে 'shall we'।
    Q24. If you heat ice, it ___.
    যদি তুমি বৰফ গৰম কৰা, ই ___।
    (A) melt melt
    (B) melted melted
    (C) melts melts
    (D) will melting will melting
    Answer: melts
    melts
    Explanation: A general truth is expressed in the zero conditional with the present simple 'melts'.
    সাধাৰণ সত্য zero conditional ত present simple 'melts' ৰে প্ৰকাশ কৰা হয়।
    Q25. Choose the correct sentence using 'one of':
    'one of' ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) One of my friends is a doctor. One of my friends is a doctor.
    (B) One of my friend are a doctor. One of my friend are a doctor.
    (C) One of my friends are a doctor. One of my friends are a doctor.
    (D) One of my friend is doctor. One of my friend is doctor.
    Answer: One of my friends is a doctor.
    One of my friends is a doctor.
    Explanation: The phrase is 'one of my friends', and 'one' takes the singular verb 'is'.
    বাক্যাংশটো 'one of my friends', আৰু 'one' য়ে singular verb 'is' লয়।

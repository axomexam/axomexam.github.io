    Grammar Mock Test — Set 2

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. She ___ to school every day.
    তাই প্ৰতিদিনে বিদ্যালয়লৈ যায়।
    (A) go go
    (B) goes goes
    (C) going going
    (D) gone gone
    Answer: goes
    goes
    Explanation: With a third-person singular subject in the simple present, 'goes' is used.
    simple present ত third-person singular subject ৰ সৈতে 'goes' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. They ___ playing football now.
    সিহঁতে এতিয়া ফুটবল খেলি আছে।
    (A) is is
    (B) was was
    (C) are are
    (D) were were
    Answer: are
    are
    Explanation: 'They' takes the plural verb 'are' in the present continuous.
    present continuous ত 'they' ৰ সৈতে plural verb 'are' লগে।
    Q3. He ___ a letter yesterday.
    তেওঁ কালি এখন চিঠি লিখিছিল।
    (A) write write
    (B) writes writes
    (C) written written
    (D) wrote wrote
    Answer: wrote
    wrote
    Explanation: 'Yesterday' shows past time, so 'wrote' is used.
    'yesterday' এ অতীত কাল বুজায়, সেয়ে 'wrote' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q4. I ___ this book for two hours.
    মই এই কিতাপখন দুঘণ্টা ধৰি পঢ়ি আছোঁ।
    (A) read read
    (B) have been reading have been reading
    (C) am read am read
    (D) was reading was reading
    Answer: have been reading
    have been reading
    Explanation: 'Have been reading' shows an action continuing for two hours.
    'have been reading' য়ে দুই ঘণ্টাৰ পৰা চলি থকা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q5. The past participle of 'go' is:
    'go' ৰ past participle কি?
    (A) went went
    (B) goed goed
    (C) gone gone
    (D) going going
    Answer: gone
    gone
    Explanation: The past participle of 'go' is 'gone'.
    'go' ৰ past participle হৈছে 'gone'।
    Q6. By next year, she ___ her degree.
    অহা বছৰৰ ভিতৰত তাই ডিগ্ৰী সম্পূৰ্ণ কৰিব।
    (A) will complete will complete
    (B) will have completed will have completed
    (C) completed completed
    (D) completes completes
    Answer: will have completed
    will have completed
    Explanation: 'Will have completed' is future perfect, used with 'by next year'.
    'by next year' ৰ সৈতে future perfect 'will have completed' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q7. If I ___ rich, I would help the poor.
    মই ধনী হ'লে দুখীয়াক সহায় কৰিলোঁহেঁতেন।
    (A) am am
    (B) was was
    (C) were were
    (D) will be will be
    Answer: were
    were
    Explanation: In a second conditional, 'were' is used with 'I'.
    দ্বিতীয় conditional ত 'I' ৰ সৈতে 'were' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q8. The sentence 'The letter was written by Ram' is in:
    'The letter was written by Ram' বাক্যটো কি voice ত আছে?
    (A) Active voice সক্ৰিয় বাচ্য
    (B) Passive voice কৰ্মবাচ্য
    (C) Direct speech প্ৰত্যক্ষ উক্তি
    (D) Interrogative প্ৰশ্নবোধক
    Answer: Passive voice
    কৰ্মবাচ্য
    Explanation: The subject receives the action, so the sentence is in the passive voice.
    কৰ্তাই কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণ কৰিছে, সেয়ে বাক্যটো passive voice ত আছে।
    Q9. Change into passive: 'She sings a song.'
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলাওক: 'She sings a song.'
    (A) A song is sung by her. A song is sung by her.
    (B) A song was sung by her. A song was sung by her.
    (C) A song is sang by her. A song is sang by her.
    (D) A song has sung by her. A song has sung by her.
    Answer: A song is sung by her.
    A song is sung by her.
    Explanation: The passive of 'She sings a song' is 'A song is sung by her.'
    'She sings a song' ৰ passive হৈছে 'A song is sung by her.'
    Q10. He said, 'I am busy.' The indirect form is:
    তেওঁ ক'লে, 'I am busy.' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) He said that he is busy. He said that he is busy.
    (B) He said that he was busy. He said that he was busy.
    (C) He said that I was busy. He said that I was busy.
    (D) He said that he has been busy. He said that he has been busy.
    Answer: He said that he was busy.
    He said that he was busy.
    Explanation: In indirect speech, 'am' changes to 'was' after a past reporting verb.
    indirect speech ত past reporting verb ৰ পিছত 'am' → 'was' হয়।
    Q11. She said to me, 'Are you coming?' The indirect form is:
    তাই মোক ক'লে, 'Are you coming?' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) She asked me if I was coming. She asked me if I was coming.
    (B) She told me that I was coming. She told me that I was coming.
    (C) She asked me that I am coming. She asked me that I am coming.
    (D) She said me if I am coming. She said me if I am coming.
    Answer: She asked me if I was coming.
    She asked me if I was coming.
    Explanation: A yes/no question becomes an 'if' clause in indirect speech.
    yes/no প্ৰশ্ন indirect speech ত 'if' clause লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q12. The teacher said, 'Do not make a noise.' The indirect form is:
    শিক্ষকে ক'লে, 'Do not make a noise.' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) The teacher said that do not make a noise. The teacher said that do not make a noise.
    (B) The teacher told not make a noise. The teacher told not make a noise.
    (C) The teacher forbade them to make a noise. The teacher forbade them to make a noise.
    (D) The teacher asked that they do not make a noise. The teacher asked that they do not make a noise.
    Answer: The teacher forbade them to make a noise.
    The teacher forbade them to make a noise.
    Explanation: A negative command becomes 'forbade ... to' in indirect speech.
    নিষেধাত্মক আদেশ indirect speech ত 'forbade ... to' লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q13. Choose the correct sentence:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) He has went to Guwahati. He has went to Guwahati.
    (B) He has gone to Guwahati. He has gone to Guwahati.
    (C) He have gone to Guwahati. He have gone to Guwahati.
    (D) He is gone to Guwahati yesterday. He is gone to Guwahati yesterday.
    Answer: He has gone to Guwahati.
    He has gone to Guwahati.
    Explanation: 'Has gone' is the correct present perfect form.
    'has gone' হৈছে শুদ্ধ present perfect ৰূপ।
    Q14. Neither he nor his friends ___ present.
    তেওঁও নাই, তেওঁৰ বন্ধুসকলেও উপস্থিত নাই।
    (A) is is
    (B) was was
    (C) are are
    (D) has has
    Answer: are
    are
    Explanation: With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'friends'.
    'neither...nor' ৰ সৈতে verb ওচৰৰ subject 'friends' ৰ লগত মিলে।
    Q15. The quality of the apples ___ good.
    আপেলবোৰৰ গুণ ভাল।
    (A) are are
    (B) were were
    (C) have have
    (D) is is
    Answer: is
    is
    Explanation: The subject is 'quality' (singular), so 'is' is used.
    subject হৈছে 'quality' (singular), সেয়ে 'is' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q16. Each of the boys ___ given a prize.
    প্ৰতিজন ল'ৰাক বঁটা দিয়া হৈছে।
    (A) were were
    (B) are are
    (C) was was
    (D) have have
    Answer: was
    was
    Explanation: 'Each' is singular, so it takes the singular verb 'was'.
    'each' singular, সেয়ে ই singular verb 'was' লয়।
    Q17. I look forward to ___ you.
    মই আপোনাক লগ পোৱাৰ বাবে অপেক্ষা কৰি আছোঁ।
    (A) meet meet
    (B) meeting meeting
    (C) met met
    (D) meets meets
    Answer: meeting
    meeting
    Explanation: The preposition 'to' is followed by a gerund 'meeting'.
    preposition 'to' ৰ পিছত gerund 'meeting' আহে।
    Q18. He is used to ___ early.
    তেওঁ সোনকালে উঠাৰ অভ্যাসী।
    (A) get up get up
    (B) got up got up
    (C) getting up getting up
    (D) gets up gets up
    Answer: getting up
    getting up
    Explanation: 'Used to' is followed by a gerund 'getting up'.
    'used to' ৰ পিছত gerund 'getting up' আহে।
    Q19. Would you mind ___ the window?
    খিনিখিনি জাননীখন খুলি দিবনে?
    (A) open open
    (B) to open to open
    (C) opened opened
    (D) opening opening
    Answer: opening
    opening
    Explanation: 'Would you mind' is followed by a gerund 'opening'.
    'would you mind' ৰ পিছত gerund 'opening' আহে।
    Q20. The infinitive in 'I want to go' is:
    'I want to go' ত অসমাপিকা ক্ৰিয়া কি?
    (A) want want
    (B) to go to go
    (C) I I
    (D) go go
    Answer: to go
    to go
    Explanation: 'To go' is the infinitive in the sentence.
    বাক্যটোত 'to go' হৈছে infinitive।
    Q21. The gerund in 'Swimming is a good exercise' is:
    'Swimming is a good exercise' ত gerund কি?
    (A) is is
    (B) good good
    (C) exercise exercise
    (D) Swimming Swimming
    Answer: Swimming
    Swimming
    Explanation: 'Swimming' acts as a noun here, so it is a gerund.
    'Swimming' ইয়াত noun হিচাপে কাম কৰে, সেয়ে ই gerund।
    Q22. He ___ finished his work before I arrived.
    মই অহাৰ আগতে তেওঁ কাম শেষ কৰিছিল।
    (A) has has
    (B) had had
    (C) have have
    (D) was was
    Answer: had
    had
    Explanation: 'Had finished' is past perfect, showing an action completed earlier.
    'had finished' past perfect, যি আগতে সম্পন্ন হোৱা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q23. It ___ raining since morning.
    ৰাতিপুৱাৰে পৰা বৰষুণ দি আছে।
    (A) is is
    (B) was was
    (C) has been has been
    (D) have been have been
    Answer: has been
    has been
    Explanation: 'Has been raining' is the present perfect continuous tense.
    'has been raining' হৈছে present perfect continuous tense।
    Q24. The sentence 'May you live long!' is:
    'May you live long!' বাক্যটো কি প্ৰকাৰৰ?
    (A) Assertive নিৰ্দেশাত্মক
    (B) Interrogative প্ৰশ্নবোধক
    (C) Optative ইচ্ছাসূচক
    (D) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    Answer: Optative
    ইচ্ছাসূচক
    Explanation: 'May you live long!' expresses a wish, so it is optative.
    'May you live long!' এ ইচ্ছা প্ৰকাশ কৰে, সেয়ে ই optative।
    Q25. What a beautiful flower! This sentence is:
    What a beautiful flower! এই বাক্যটো কি?
    (A) Assertive নিৰ্দেশাত্মক
    (B) Imperative আদেশাত্মক
    (C) Interrogative প্ৰশ্নবোধক
    (D) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    Answer: Exclamatory
    বিস্ময়সূচক
    Explanation: 'What a beautiful flower!' expresses strong feeling, so it is exclamatory.
    'What a beautiful flower!' য়ে তীব্ৰ অনুভূতি প্ৰকাশ কৰে, সেয়ে ই exclamatory।

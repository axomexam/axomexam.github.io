    Grammar Mock Test — Set 4

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. Find the error: 'He is senior than me.'
    ভুল উলিয়াওক: 'He is senior than me.'
    (A) He is He is
    (B) senior senior
    (C) than than
    (D) me me
    Answer: than
    than
    Explanation: 'Senior' is followed by 'to', not 'than'.
    'senior' ৰ পিছত 'than' নহয়, 'to' আহে।
    Q2. The correct form is:
    শুদ্ধ ৰূপ কি?
    (A) He is senior than me. He is senior than me.
    (B) He is senior to me. He is senior to me.
    (C) He is more senior than me. He is more senior than me.
    (D) He is senior from me. He is senior from me.
    Answer: He is senior to me.
    He is senior to me.
    Explanation: 'Senior to' is the correct form.
    'senior to' হৈছে শুদ্ধ ৰূপ।
    Q3. Choose the correct sentence:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) I am looking forward to meet you. I am looking forward to meet you.
    (B) I am looking forward to meeting you. I am looking forward to meeting you.
    (C) I am looking forward for meeting you. I am looking forward for meeting you.
    (D) I am looking forward meet you. I am looking forward meet you.
    Answer: I am looking forward to meeting you.
    I am looking forward to meeting you.
    Explanation: 'Looking forward to' is followed by a gerund.
    'looking forward to' ৰ পিছত gerund আহে।
    Q4. Find the error: 'One of my friend is a doctor.'
    ভুল উলিয়াওক: 'One of my friend is a doctor.'
    (A) One of One of
    (B) friend friend
    (C) is is
    (D) a doctor a doctor
    Answer: friend
    friend
    Explanation: 'One of' is followed by a plural noun, so 'friends' is correct.
    'one of' ৰ পিছত plural noun আহে, সেয়ে 'friends' শুদ্ধ।
    Q5. The correct sentence is:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো কি?
    (A) One of my friends is a doctor. One of my friends is a doctor.
    (B) One of my friend are a doctor. One of my friend are a doctor.
    (C) One of my friends are a doctor. One of my friends are a doctor.
    (D) One of my friend is a doctor. One of my friend is a doctor.
    Answer: One of my friends is a doctor.
    One of my friends is a doctor.
    Explanation: 'One of my friends is a doctor.' is the grammatically correct sentence.
    'One of my friends is a doctor.' হৈছে ব্যাকৰণগতভাৱে শুদ্ধ বাক্য।
    Q6. Choose the correct sentence:
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) The sceneries of Assam are beautiful. The sceneries of Assam are beautiful.
    (B) The scenery of Assam is beautiful. The scenery of Assam is beautiful.
    (C) The scenery of Assam are beautiful. The scenery of Assam are beautiful.
    (D) The sceneries of Assam is beautiful. The sceneries of Assam is beautiful.
    Answer: The scenery of Assam is beautiful.
    The scenery of Assam is beautiful.
    Explanation: 'Scenery' is an uncountable noun, so it has no plural form.
    'scenery' uncountable noun, সেয়ে ইয়াৰ বহুবচন নাই।
    Q7. 'A person who loves his country' is called a:

    Patriot

    (C) A false friend মিছা বন্ধু
    (D) A secret gift গোপন উপহাৰ

    প্ৰথমে বেয়া যেন লগা ভাল কথা
    Explanation: 'A blessing in disguise' means something good that seemed bad at first.
    'a blessing in disguise' মানে প্ৰথমে বেয়া যেন লাগি পিছত ভাল হোৱা বস্তু।
    Q13. 'To kick the bucket' means:

    (B) To start a journey যাত্ৰা আৰম্ভ কৰা
    (C) To die মৃত্যুবৰণ কৰা
    (D) To work hard কঠোৰ পৰিশ্ৰম কৰা

    Q24. Choose the correct sentence:

    (B) He is taller than his brother. He is taller than his brother.

    Explanation: 'Hardly had ... when' is the correct correlative pair.

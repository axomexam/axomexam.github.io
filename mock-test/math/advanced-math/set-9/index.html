    Advanced Math Mock Test — Set 9

    25 bilingual questions with answers and explanations. Take the timed test online or read the full set below.

    25 Questions with Answers

    Q1. The value of sin²θ + cos²θ is:
    sin²θ + cos²θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) 2 2
    (D) sin 2θ sin 2θ
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: By the fundamental identity, sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
    মৌলিক অভেদ অনুসৰি sin²θ + cos²θ = 1।
    Q2. The value of sin 30° is:
    sin 30° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1/2 1/2
    (B) √3/2 √3/2
    (C) 1/√2 1/√2
    (D) 1 1
    Answer: 1/2
    1/2
    Explanation: sin 30° = 1/2.
    sin 30° = 1/2।
    Q3. The value of cos 60° is:
    cos 60° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1/2 1/2
    (B) √3/2 √3/2
    (C) 1/√2 1/√2
    (D) 0 0
    Answer: 1/2
    1/2
    Explanation: cos 60° = 1/2.
    cos 60° = 1/2।
    Q4. The value of tan 45° is:
    tan 45° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) √3 √3
    (D) ∞ ∞
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: tan 45° = 1.
    tan 45° = 1।
    Q5. If sin θ = 3/5, then cos θ is:
    যদি sin θ = 3/5 হয়, তেন্তে cos θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 4/5 4/5
    (B) 3/5 3/5
    (C) 5/3 5/3
    (D) 5/4 5/4
    Answer: 4/5
    4/5
    Explanation: cos θ = √(1 − sin²θ) = √(1 − 9/25) = 4/5.
    cos θ = √(1 − sin²θ) = √(1 − 9/25) = 4/5।
    Q6. The value of sin 90° is:
    sin 90° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) 1/2 1/2
    (D) √3/2 √3/2
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: sin 90° = 1.
    sin 90° = 1।
    Q7. The value of sec²θ − tan²θ is:
    sec²θ − tan²θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) 2 2
    (D) −1 −1
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: By identity, sec²θ − tan²θ = 1.
    অভেদ অনুসৰি sec²θ − tan²θ = 1।
    Q8. The value of cos 0° is:
    cos 0° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) 1/2 1/2
    (D) −1 −1
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: cos 0° = 1.
    cos 0° = 1।
    Q9. If tan θ = 1, then θ is:
    যদি tan θ = 1 হয়, তেন্তে θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 45° 45°
    (B) 30° 30°
    (C) 60° 60°
    (D) 90° 90°
    Answer: 45°
    45°
    Explanation: tan 45° = 1, so θ = 45°.
    tan 45° = 1, গতিকে θ = 45°।
    Q10. The value of sin(90° − θ) is:
    sin(90° − θ) ৰ মান কি?
    (A) cos θ cos θ
    (B) sin θ sin θ
    (C) −cos θ −cos θ
    (D) tan θ tan θ
    Answer: cos θ
    cos θ
    Explanation: By complementary angle identity, sin(90° − θ) = cos θ.
    সম্পূৰক কোণৰ অভেদ অনুসৰি sin(90° − θ) = cos θ।
    Q11. The value of 2 sin 30° cos 30° is:
    2 sin 30° cos 30° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) √3/2 √3/2
    (B) 1/2 1/2
    (C) 1 1
    (D) √3 √3
    Answer: √3/2
    √3/2
    Explanation: 2 sin 30° cos 30° = sin 60° = √3/2.
    2 sin 30° cos 30° = sin 60° = √3/2।
    Q12. If sin θ = cos θ, then θ is:
    যদি sin θ = cos θ হয়, তেন্তে θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 45° 45°
    (B) 30° 30°
    (C) 60° 60°
    (D) 0° 0°
    Answer: 45°
    45°
    Explanation: sin θ = cos θ happens at θ = 45°.
    sin θ = cos θ ৰ মান θ = 45° ত হয়।
    Q13. The value of cosec²θ − cot²θ is:

    (D) −1 −1
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: By identity, cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1.
    অভেদ অনুসৰি cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1।
    Q14. The value of tan 60° is:

    Q16. If cos θ = 1/2, then θ is:
    যদি cos θ = 1/2 হয়, তেন্তে θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 60° 60°
    (B) 30° 30°
    (C) 45° 45°
    (D) 90° 90°
    Answer: 60°
    60°
    Explanation: cos 60° = 1/2, so θ = 60°.
    cos 60° = 1/2, গতিকে θ = 60°।
    Q17. The value of sin 45° is:
    sin 45° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1/√2 1/√2
    (B) 1/2 1/2
    (C) √3/2 √3/2
    (D) 1 1
    Answer: 1/√2
    1/√2
    Explanation: sin 45° = 1/√2.
    sin 45° = 1/√2।
    Q18. The value of 1 − cos²θ is:
    1 − cos²θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) sin²θ sin²θ
    (B) cos²θ cos²θ
    (C) tan²θ tan²θ
    (D) 1 1
    Answer: sin²θ
    sin²θ
    Explanation: 1 − cos²θ = sin²θ.
    1 − cos²θ = sin²θ।
    Q19. The maximum value of sin θ is:
    sin θ ৰ সৰ্বোচ্চ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) −1 −1
    (D) ∞ ∞
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: sin θ always lies between −1 and 1, so the maximum is 1.
    sin θ সদায় −1 আৰু 1 ৰ মাজত থাকে, গতিকে সৰ্বোচ্চ মান 1।
    Q20. The formula for sin 2θ is:
    sin 2θ ৰ সূত্ৰ কি?
    (A) 2 sin θ cos θ 2 sin θ cos θ
    (B) sin²θ − cos²θ sin²θ − cos²θ
    (C) sin θ + cos θ sin θ + cos θ
    (D) 2 sin θ 2 sin θ
    Answer: 2 sin θ cos θ
    2 sin θ cos θ
    Explanation: sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ.
    sin 2θ = 2 sin θ cos θ।
    Q21. The value of cos 90° is:
    cos 90° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 0 0

    (C) 1/2 1/2
    (D) −1 −1
    Answer: 0

    Q22. If θ = 30°, the value of sin 2θ is:
    যদি θ = 30° হয়, তেন্তে sin 2θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) √3/2 √3/2
    (B) 1/2 1/2
    (C) 1 1
    (D) 1/√2 1/√2
    Answer: √3/2
    √3/2
    Explanation: sin 2θ = sin 60° = √3/2.
    sin 2θ = sin 60° = √3/2।
    Q23. The value of sec 0° is:
    sec 0° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) 1 1
    (B) 0 0
    (C) ∞ ∞
    (D) −1 −1
    Answer: 1
    1
    Explanation: sec 0° = 1/cos 0° = 1.
    sec 0° = 1/cos 0° = 1।
    Q24. In a right triangle, tan θ equals:
    সমকোণী ত্ৰিভুজত tan θ ৰ মান কি?
    (A) Opposite / Adjacent সম্মুখ বাহু / সংলগ্ন বাহু
    (B) Adjacent / Hypotenuse সংলগ্ন বাহু / অতিভুজ
    (C) Opposite / Hypotenuse সম্মুখ বাহু / অতিভুজ
    (D) Hypotenuse / Opposite অতিভুজ / সম্মুখ বাহু
    Answer: Opposite / Adjacent
    সম্মুখ বাহু / সংলগ্ন বাহু
    Explanation: tan θ = opposite side / adjacent side.
    tan θ = সম্মুখ বাহু / সংলগ্ন বাহু।
    Q25. The value of sin 120° is:
    sin 120° ৰ মান কি?
    (A) √3/2 √3/2
    (B) 1/2 1/2
    (C) −√3/2 −√3/2
    (D) 1 1
    Answer: √3/2
    √3/2
    Explanation: sin 120° = sin 60° = √3/2.
    sin 120° = sin 60° = √3/2।

    A sample of the non verbal reasoning questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. The mirror image of the word LEFT (vertical mirror on the right) looks like which option? LEFT শব্দৰ দাপোন প্ৰতিবিম্ব (সোঁফালে উলম্ব দাপোন) কোনটো?
      (A) TFEL   (B) TFEJ   (C) LEFT   (D) FELT
      Answer: TFEL Explanation: In a vertical mirror the left-right order of the letters is reversed, so LEFT is read from the right as T-F-E-L, i.e. TFEL.
    • Q2. Which figure is odd: three triangles and one square? বেলেগ চিত্ৰ কোনটো: তিনিটা ত্ৰিভুজ আৰু এটা বৰ্গ?
      (A) Square বৰ্গ   (B) Equilateral triangle সমবাহু ত্ৰিভুজ   (C) Isosceles triangle সমদ্বিবাহু ত্ৰিভুজ   (D) Right triangle সমকোণী ত্ৰিভুজ
      Answer: Square Explanation: Three of the figures are triangles (equilateral, isosceles, right); the square is the only four-sided figure, so it is the odd one.
    • Q3. Circle : Sphere :: Square : ? বৃত্ত : গোলক :: বৰ্গ : ?
      (A) Cube ঘনক   (B) Rectangle আয়ত   (C) Triangle ত্ৰিভুজ   (D) Circle বৃত্ত
      Answer: Cube Explanation: A circle is the flat 2-D form of a sphere; similarly a square is the flat 2-D form of a cube.
    • Q4. A cube has how many faces? এটা ঘনকৰ কিমান পিঠি থাকে?
      (A) 6 ৬   (B) 8 ৮   (C) 12 ১২   (D) 4 ৪
      Answer: 6 Explanation: A cube has 6 faces: one top, one bottom and four side faces.

      Downloads - Free PDF Notes and Study Material

      The downloads section gives you printable PDF notes that you can keep on your phone or take printouts of for offline revision. Every file is prepared from the same bilingual question bank used on the website, so the facts you revise offline match exactly what you practise online. This page explains what is available, how to download it and how to use the notes so that they actually improve your score.

      What you can download

      The library contains subject-wise revision notes and quick-reference sheets covering General Knowledge, Assam History and Culture, Indian Polity, Geography, Economy and General Science, along with formula sheets for mathematics and reasoning. Because the material is designed for competitive exams, each note is short, factual and organised under headings so that it can be revised in a few minutes. Previous year paper sections are also available for exams such as SSC, Railway, Assam Police, the Guwahati High Court, DHS and DME.

      How to download a file

      Open the Downloads page, choose the subject or exam you need and tap the download link for that file. The PDF opens in your browser or saves to your device depending on your settings. No account, registration or payment is required, and there is no limit on the number of files you may download. If a download does not start, check your internet connection and try again, or open the link in a different browser. For any broken link, please use the contact page so that the file can be fixed quickly.

      How to use PDF notes effectively

      A PDF is only useful if you revise it actively. Do not read a note from top to bottom and move on. Instead, cover the answers and try to recall each fact before you read it, then mark the points you missed. Revise the marked points again the next day and once more at the end of the week. This spaced repetition is far more effective than reading the same note five times in one sitting. Keep a separate notebook for facts that you forget repeatedly and revise only that notebook before the exam.

      Printing tips

      The notes are formatted to print cleanly in black and white. If you are printing at a shop, select the "fit to page" option and use both sides of the paper to save cost. For diagrams and maps, colour printing helps, but the text is fully readable without it. If you prefer to study on a phone, save the file to your device so that it opens even without an internet connection.

      A simple revision plan

      • Monday to Wednesday: read one subject note and attempt the matching online question set.
      • Thursday: revise only the facts you marked as forgotten.
      • Friday: attempt a timed mock test on the same subject.
      • Weekend: revise two older notes to keep them fresh.

      Use the downloads together with the mock tests and previous year sections on axomexam. Offline notes build your knowledge, while online practice tells you whether that knowledge holds up under time pressure. Both are free, so use them together.

      Parts of Speech

      Parts of speech (noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection, articles/determiners) with types, examples and common errors asked in Assam competitive exams (APSC, Assam Police, DME, etc.).

      211 Questions with Answers

      Q1. Which of these is a proper noun?
      (A) Brahmaputra   (B) river   (C) village   (D) boy
      Answer: (A) Brahmaputra — Brahmaputra is the name of a particular river, so it is a proper noun.
      ব্ৰহ্মপুত্ৰ
      Q2. Which word is a common noun?
      (A) table   (B) India   (C) Ganga   (D) Monday
      Answer: (A) table — A common noun names any person, place or thing of the same class. 'Table' is a common noun.
      মেজ
      Q3. Which of these is a collective noun?
      (A) crowd   (B) city   (C) honesty   (D) water
      Answer: (A) crowd — A collective noun names a group. 'Crowd' means a group of people.
      ভিৰ
      Q4. Choose the correct collective noun: 'A ___ of cattle.'
      (A) herd   (B) flock   (C) swarm   (D) pride
      Answer: (A) herd — A group of cattle is called a herd.
      herd
      Q5. Which of these is an abstract noun?
      (A) kindness   (B) stone   (C) river   (D) box
      Answer: (A) kindness — An abstract noun names a quality or idea. 'Kindness' cannot be seen or touched.
      দয়া
      Q6. 'Wisdom' is what kind of noun?
      (A) Abstract noun   (B) Proper noun   (C) Material noun   (D) Collective noun
      Answer: (A) Abstract noun — Wisdom is an idea or quality, so it is an abstract noun.
      গুণবাচক বিশেষ্য
      Q7. Which of these is a material noun?
      (A) gold   (B) teacher   (C) anger   (D) army
      Answer: (A) gold — A material noun names a substance or material. 'Gold' is a material.
      সোণ
      Q8. Which of these is a countable noun?
      (A) book   (B) milk   (C) sugar   (D) water
      Answer: (A) book — 'Book' can be counted (one book, two books); the others are uncountable.
      কিতাপ
      Q9. Which of these is an uncountable noun?
      (A) advice   (B) chair   (C) pen   (D) apple
      Answer: (A) advice — 'Advice' is uncountable; we say 'some advice', not 'two advices'.
      পৰামৰ্শ
      Q10. Fill in the blank: 'The news ___ good.'
      (A) is   (B) are   (C) were   (D) have been
      Answer: (A) is — 'News' looks plural but is singular, so we use 'is'.
      is
      Q11. Choose the correct plural of 'child'.
      (A) children   (B) childs   (C) childes   (D) child
      Answer: (A) children — The plural of 'child' is 'children'.
      children
      Q12. Choose the correct plural of 'ox'.
      (A) oxen   (B) oxes   (C) ox   (D) oxis
      Answer: (A) oxen — The plural of 'ox' is 'oxen'.
      oxen
      Q13. Choose the correct plural of 'wife'.
      (A) wives   (B) wifes   (C) wife   (D) wive
      Answer: (A) wives — Words ending in 'f'/'fe' often change to 'ves': wife → wives.
      wives
      Q14. Choose the correct plural of 'tooth'.
      (A) teeth   (B) tooths   (C) tooth   (D) toothes
      Answer: (A) teeth — Irregular plural: tooth → teeth.
      teeth
      Q15. The plural of 'sheep' is ___
      (A) sheep   (B) sheeps   (C) sheepes   (D) shoop
      Answer: (A) sheep — 'Sheep' has the same form in singular and plural.
      sheep
      Q16. Fill in the blank: 'The scenery of Assam ___ beautiful.'
      (A) is   (B) are   (C) were   (D) have
      Answer: (A) is — 'Scenery' is uncountable and singular, so we use 'is'.
      is
      Q17. What is the feminine of 'actor'?
      (A) actress   (B) actoress   (C) actrix   (D) act
      Answer: (A) actress — The feminine of 'actor' is 'actress'.
      অভিনেত্ৰী
      Q18. What is the feminine of 'tiger'?
      (A) tigress   (B) tigeress   (C) tigris   (D) tigra
      Answer: (A) tigress — The feminine of 'tiger' is 'tigress'.
      tigress
      Q19. The masculine of 'peahen' is ___
      (A) peacock   (B) peacok   (C) hen   (D) cockerel
      Answer: (A) peacock — A male peafowl is a peacock; the female is a peahen.
      peacock
      Q20. Which noun is of common gender?
      (A) doctor   (B) king   (C) queen   (D) mother
      Answer: (A) doctor — 'Doctor' can refer to a male or a female, so it is common gender.
      ডাক্তৰ
      Q21. Which noun is of neuter gender?
      (A) table   (B) boy   (C) girl   (D) cow
      Answer: (A) table — Neuter gender is used for lifeless things. 'Table' is a lifeless object.
      মেজ
      Q22. What is the noun form of the verb 'speak'?
      (A) speech   (B) speaking   (C) spoken   (D) speaker
      Answer: (A) speech — The noun form of 'speak' is 'speech'. 'Speaker' is the person who speaks.
      বক্তৃতা
      Q23. Choose the correct collective noun: 'A ___ of ships.'
      (A) fleet   (B) pack   (C) herd   (D) bunch
      Answer: (A) fleet — A group of ships is called a fleet.
      fleet
      Q24. Fill in the blank: 'The police ___ caught the thief.'
      (A) have   (B) has   (C) is   (D) was
      Answer: (A) have — 'Police' is always used as a plural collective noun, so we use 'have'.
      have
      Q25. Which word is a personal pronoun in 'She is my friend.'?
      (A) She   (B) is   (C) my   (D) friend
      Answer: (A) She — 'She' stands for a person and is a personal pronoun.
      She

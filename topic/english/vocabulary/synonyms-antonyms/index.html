      Synonyms & Antonyms

      High-frequency synonyms and antonyms for Assam competitive exams.

      212 Questions with Answers

      Q1. Choose the antonym of 'Abandon'.
      (A) Forsake   (B) Retain   (C) Desert   (D) Quit
      Answer: (B) Retain — 'Retain' means to keep something; it is the opposite of 'abandon' (to give up).
      Retain
      Q2. Choose the synonym of 'Abhor'.
      (A) Detest   (B) Admire   (C) Cherish   (D) Love
      Answer: (A) Detest — 'Detest' means to hate someone or something very much.
      Detest
      Q3. Choose the antonym of 'Abolish'.
      (A) Annul   (B) Revoke   (C) Establish   (D) Cancel
      Answer: (C) Establish — 'Establish' means to set up permanently; it is the opposite of 'abolish' (to do away with).
      Establish
      Q4. Choose the antonym of 'Absurd'.
      (A) Ridiculous   (B) Sensible   (C) Silly   (D) Preposterous
      Answer: (B) Sensible — 'Sensible' means reasonable and practical; it is the opposite of 'absurd'.
      Sensible
      Q5. Choose the antonym of 'Accord'.
      (A) Discord   (B) Agreement   (C) Concord   (D) Harmony
      Answer: (A) Discord — 'Discord' means disagreement and conflict; it is the opposite of 'accord'.
      Discord
      Q6. Choose the synonym of 'Adhere'.
      (A) Detach   (B) Separate   (C) Cling   (D) Abandon
      Answer: (C) Cling — 'Cling' means to stick firmly to something, like 'adhere'.
      Cling
      Q7. Choose the antonym of 'Adversity'.
      (A) Misfortune   (B) Hardship   (C) Calamity   (D) Prosperity
      Answer: (D) Prosperity — 'Prosperity' means success and good fortune; it is the opposite of 'adversity'.
      Prosperity
      Q8. Choose the antonym of 'Agile'.
      (A) Clumsy   (B) Nimble   (C) Quick   (D) Supple
      Answer: (A) Clumsy — 'Clumsy' means awkward in movement; it is the opposite of 'agile'.
      Clumsy
      Q9. Choose the antonym of 'Alien'.
      (A) Foreign   (B) Strange   (C) Native   (D) Exotic
      Answer: (C) Native — 'Native' means belonging to one's own place; it is the opposite of 'alien'.
      Native
      Q10. Choose the synonym of 'Ambiguous'.
      (A) Clear   (B) Unclear   (C) Certain   (D) Obvious
      Answer: (B) Unclear — 'Unclear' means open to more than one interpretation, like 'ambiguous'.
      Unclear
      Q11. Choose the antonym of 'Ample'.
      (A) Insufficient   (B) Enough   (C) Abundant   (D) Sufficient
      Answer: (A) Insufficient — 'Insufficient' means not enough; it is the opposite of 'ample'.
      Insufficient
      Q12. Choose the antonym of 'Ancient'.
      (A) Old   (B) Archaic   (C) Antique   (D) Modern
      Answer: (D) Modern — 'Modern' means of the present time; it is the opposite of 'ancient'.
      Modern
      Q13. Choose the synonym of 'Apathy'.
      (A) Interest   (B) Concern   (C) Indifference   (D) Enthusiasm
      Answer: (C) Indifference — 'Indifference' means lack of interest, like 'apathy'.
      Indifference
      Q14. Choose the antonym of 'Apparent'.
      (A) Clear   (B) Obvious   (C) Hidden   (D) Evident
      Answer: (C) Hidden — 'Hidden' means not seen or not obvious; it is the opposite of 'apparent'.
      Hidden
      Q15. Choose the synonym of 'Arduous'.
      (A) Easy   (B) Simple   (C) Effortless   (D) Difficult
      Answer: (D) Difficult — 'Difficult' means needing much effort, like 'arduous'.
      Difficult
      Q16. Choose the antonym of 'Arid'.
      (A) Moist   (B) Dry   (C) Parched   (D) Waterless
      Answer: (A) Moist — 'Moist' means slightly wet; it is the opposite of 'arid' (very dry).
      Moist
      Q17. Choose the antonym of 'Artificial'.
      (A) Synthetic   (B) Natural   (C) Fake   (D) Man-made
      Answer: (B) Natural — 'Natural' means produced by nature; it is the opposite of 'artificial'.
      Natural
      Q18. Synonym of 'Audacious' is:
      (A) Bold   (B) Timid   (C) Cowardly   (D) Fearful
      Answer: (A) Bold — 'Bold' means brave and daring, like 'audacious'.
      Bold
      Q19. Choose the antonym of 'Authentic'.
      (A) Genuine   (B) Real   (C) Fake   (D) True
      Answer: (C) Fake — 'Fake' means not genuine; it is the opposite of 'authentic'.
      Fake
      Q20. Choose the synonym of 'Avarice'.
      (A) Generosity   (B) Greed   (C) Charity   (D) Liberality
      Answer: (B) Greed — 'Greed' means an intense desire for wealth, like 'avarice'.
      Greed
      Q21. Choose the antonym of 'Awkward'.
      (A) Graceful   (B) Clumsy   (C) Ungainly   (D) Gauche
      Answer: (A) Graceful — 'Graceful' means elegant and smooth; it is the opposite of 'awkward'.
      Graceful
      Q22. Choose the antonym of 'Barbarous'.
      (A) Cruel   (B) Savage   (C) Civilized   (D) Brutal
      Answer: (C) Civilized — 'Civilized' means polite and advanced; it is the opposite of 'barbarous'.
      Civilized
      Q23. Choose the antonym of 'Barren'.
      (A) Fertile   (B) Sterile   (C) Desert   (D) Unproductive
      Answer: (A) Fertile — 'Fertile' means able to produce crops; it is the opposite of 'barren'.
      Fertile
      Q24. Choose the synonym of 'Benevolent'.
      (A) Cruel   (B) Unkind   (C) Kind   (D) Malevolent
      Answer: (C) Kind — 'Kind' means gentle and helpful, like 'benevolent'.
      Kind
      Q25. Choose the antonym of 'Bewilder'.
      (A) Confuse   (B) Puzzle   (C) Clarify   (D) Perplex
      Answer: (C) Clarify — 'Clarify' means to make clear; it is the opposite of 'bewilder'.
      Clarify

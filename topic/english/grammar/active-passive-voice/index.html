      Active and Passive Voice: Types, Rules and Examples

      Voice is the form of a verb that shows whether the subject performs the action or receives it. When the subject does the action, the sentence is in the active voice, as in "Ram wrote the letter". When the subject receives the action, the sentence is in the passive voice, as in "The letter was written by Ram". The active voice is direct and strong; the passive voice is used when the doer is unknown, unimportant or better left unmentioned, and it is common in reports, notices and scientific writing.

      Active and passive voice is a favourite topic in APSC, ADRE, Assam Police, SSC and Railway examinations, and it is tested through direct voice change, error spotting and sentence improvement. It is closely linked to tense and to subject-verb agreement, because the passive is built from the correct form of "be" plus the past participle. This deep-study note gives the basic terms, the core rules, the kinds of questions, the step-by-step method, the common mistakes and a practical preparation plan.

      Basic Terms

      • Active voice: the subject performs the action, as in "She sings a song".
      • Passive voice: the subject receives the action, as in "A song is sung by her".
      • Agent: the doer of the action, usually placed after "by" in the passive.
      • Object: the receiver of the action, which becomes the subject in the passive.
      • Past participle: the third form of a verb, such as written, sung and taken, used in the passive.
      • Auxiliary "be": am, is, are, was, were, be, being and been, which carries the tense in the passive.
      • Dummy subject: "it" used in passives such as "It is said that ...".
      • Retained object: the object kept in a passive sentence that has two objects.
      • Impersonal passive: a passive with "it" and a clause, common with reporting verbs.
      • Voice change: the process of changing a sentence from active to passive or back.

      Core Concepts and Rules

      • Only transitive verbs, which take an object, can be made passive; intransitive verbs such as sleep, die and come have no passive form.
      • The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive, and the subject becomes the agent after "by".
      • The tense of the active verb is kept, but the form changes to the correct part of "be" plus the past participle.
      • Simple present becomes "am/is/are + past participle"; simple past becomes "was/were + past participle".
      • Present continuous becomes "am/is/are being + past participle"; past continuous becomes "was/were being + past participle".
      • Present perfect becomes "has/have been + past participle"; past perfect becomes "had been + past participle".
      • The future becomes "will be + past participle", and the future perfect becomes "will have been + past participle".
      • With modals, the passive is "modal + be + past participle", as in "The work can be done".
      • In the passive, the pronoun after "by" changes its form: I becomes me, we becomes us, he becomes him and so on.
      • When the active sentence has two objects, either object may become the subject, and the other is retained: "I gave him a book" becomes "He was given a book" or "A book was given to him".
      • Imperative sentences are made passive with "Let" or "You are requested to": "Open the door" becomes "Let the door be opened".
      • Questions are made passive by keeping the question form: "Did he write it?" becomes "Was it written by him?".
      • When the doer is unknown or unimportant, "by" is dropped: "My purse was stolen".

      Types of Active and Passive Voice Questions

      The first type gives a sentence in the active voice and asks for its passive form, or the reverse. The second type gives four options that differ only in the helping verb or the participle. The third type tests the passive of imperatives with "Let". The fourth type tests the passive of questions. The fifth type tests the passive of sentences with two objects, and the sixth type is error spotting where the wrong helping verb or the wrong participle has to be corrected.

      Steps to Solve a Voice-Change Question

      First, find the verb, the subject and the object, because the voice change moves them. Second, identify the tense of the active verb. Third, keep the same tense and build the passive with the right part of "be" plus the past participle. Fourth, make the old object the new subject and the old subject the agent after "by", changing the pronoun form. Fifth, adjust the verb to agree with the new subject. Finally, for imperatives add "Let", for questions keep the question form, and for an unknown doer drop "by", then read the sentence to check that it sounds natural.

      Examination Trends

      Voice change is a direct and predictable question. The examiner repeats the present continuous, present perfect and simple past passives, the passive of imperatives with "Let", and the passive of questions. Error spotting often hides a wrong participle or a missing "be". Sentences with two objects, such as "offer", "give", "teach" and "ask", appear because they have two correct passives. Sentences with modals and with "it is said that" are also tested, and a candidate who fixes the tense first usually avoids the common traps.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Do not make a passive from an intransitive verb; verbs such as sleep, die and arrive have no passive.
      • Do not drop the helping verb "be" before the past participle.
      • Do not use the past tense form instead of the past participle, as in "was wrote" instead of "was written".
      • Do not keep the subject pronoun unchanged after "by": use me, him, her, us and them.
      • Do not forget "Let" in the passive of an imperative sentence.
      • Do not let the passive verb disagree with the new subject.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is the difference between active and passive voice?

      In the active voice the subject performs the action, while in the passive voice the subject receives the action.

      How is the passive voice formed?

      The passive is formed with the correct part of the verb "be" plus the past participle of the main verb, keeping the same tense as the active sentence.

      Can intransitive verbs be made passive?

      No. Only transitive verbs, which take an object, can be made passive; intransitive verbs such as sleep and die have no passive form.

      How is the passive of an imperative sentence made?

      With "Let", as in "Open the door" becoming "Let the door be opened", or with "You are requested to".

      When is the passive voice used?

      It is used when the doer is unknown, unimportant or better left unmentioned, and it is common in reports, notices and scientific writing.

      প্ৰথম প্ৰকাৰত active voice ত এটা বাক্য দি তাৰ passive ৰূপ, বা উলটি বিচৰা হয়। দ্বিতীয় প্ৰকাৰত চাৰিটা বিকল্প দিয়া হয় যিবোৰ কেৱল auxiliary বা participle ত পৃথক। তৃতীয় প্ৰকাৰত "Let" ৰে imperative ৰ passive পৰীক্ষা কৰা হয়। চতুৰ্থ প্ৰকাৰত প্ৰশ্নবোধক বাক্যৰ passive পৰীক্ষা কৰা হয়। পঞ্চম প্ৰকাৰত দুটা object থকা বাক্যৰ passive পৰীক্ষা কৰা হয়, আৰু ষষ্ঠ প্ৰকাৰত ভুল auxiliary বা ভুল participle চিনি শুধৰাবলগীয়া error spotting দিয়া হয়।

      সমাধানৰ স্তৰ

      প্ৰথমে ক্ৰিয়া, কৰ্তা আৰু object বিচাৰক, কাৰণ voice change ে এইবোৰক স্থানান্তৰিত কৰে। দ্বিতীয়ে, active ক্ৰিয়াৰ tense চিনি লওক। তৃতীয়ে, একে tense ৰাখি "be" ৰ শুদ্ধ অংশ আৰু past participle ৰে passive গঠন কৰক। চতুৰ্থে, পুৰণি object-ক নতুন কৰ্তা আৰু পুৰণি কৰ্তা-ক "by" ৰ পিছত agent কৰক, pronoun ৰ ৰূপ সলাই। পঞ্চমে, ক্ৰিয়া নতুন কৰ্তাৰ লগত মিলাওক। শেষত, imperative হ'লে "Let" যোগ কৰক, প্ৰশ্ন হ'লে প্ৰশ্নৰ ৰূপ ৰাখক, কৰ্তা অজ্ঞাত হ'লে "by" এৰক, আৰু বাক্য পঢ়ি স্বাভাৱিক যেন লাগে নিশ্চিত হওক।

      পৰীক্ষাৰ প্ৰৱণতা

      Voice change এটা পোনপটীয়া আৰু অনুমানযোগ্য প্ৰশ্ন। পৰীক্ষকে present continuous, present perfect আৰু simple past ৰ passive, "Let" ৰে imperative ৰ passive, আৰু প্ৰশ্নৰ passive বাৰে বাৰে পুনৰায়। Error spotting-ত প্ৰায়ে ভুল participle বা অনুপস্থিত "be" লুকুৱাই ৰখা হয়। "offer", "give", "teach", "ask" আদি দুটা object থকা বাক্য আহে কাৰণ ইহঁতৰ দুটা শুদ্ধ passive থাকে। Modal আৰু "it is said that" থকা বাক্যও পৰীক্ষা কৰা হয়, আৰু যিজনে প্ৰথমে tense ঠিক কৰে তেওঁ সাধাৰণতে সাধাৰণ ফান্দবোৰ এৰাই যায়।

      সাধাৰণ ভুল

      • Intransitive verb ৰ পৰা passive নবনাব; sleep, die, arrive আদিৰ passive নাই।
      • Past participle ৰ আগত সহায়ক "be" এৰি নিদিব।
      • Past participle ৰ সলনি past tense ৰ ৰূপ নিদিব, যেনে "was written" ৰ ঠাইত "was wrote"।
      • "by" ৰ পিছত কৰ্তাৰ pronoun অপৰিৱৰ্তিত নিদিব: me, him, her, us, them ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক।
      • Imperative ৰ passive ত "Let" পাহৰি নিদিব।
      • Passive ক্ৰিয়া নতুন কৰ্তাৰ লগত অমিল হ'ব নিদিব।

      প্ৰস্তুতি কেনেকৈ কৰিব

      আৰম্ভণিতে এটা ক্ৰিয়াৰ বাৰটা tense ত active আৰু passive ৰূপ লিখক, কাৰণ দুয়ো ৰূপ কাষে কাষে দেখিলে ধৰণটো মনত দৃঢ় হয়। তাৰ পিছত বেলেগ বেলেগ tense ৰ দহটা active বাক্য passive লৈ সলনি কৰি প্ৰতিবাৰ auxiliary আৰু participle পৰীক্ষা কৰক। ইয়াৰ পিছত imperative আৰু প্ৰশ্ন অভ্যাস কৰক, কাৰণ প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে এই দুটা ৰূপতেই আটাইতকৈ বেছি ভুল কৰে।

      এটা উপযোগী অভ্যাস হ'ল - এটা বাতৰি পঢ়ি passive বাক্যবোৰ বিচাৰি প্ৰতিবাৰ সোধক যে লেখকে কিয় passive বাছনি কৰিছে আৰু কৰ্তাজন জনা নে নাই। ই দেখুৱায় যে passive এটা নিয়ম নহয় বৰং এটা বাছনি, আৰু ৰূপটোক অৰ্থপূৰ্ণ কৰে। পাহৰি যোৱা participle-ৰ এটা ব্যক্তিগত তালিকা ৰাখক, যেনে written, sung, taken, broken, chosen, আৰু প্ৰতিটো পৰীক্ষাৰ আগে পুনৰাবৃত্তি কৰক। ইংৰাজী আৰু অসমীয়াত দৈনিক বিশটা প্ৰশ্ন দুসপ্তাহ অভ্যাস কৰিলে voice change দ্ৰুত আৰু সঠিক হয়, লগতে tense আৰু subject-verb agreement-ও সবল হয়।

      দ্ৰুত পুনৰালোচনাৰ টিপছ

      • কেৱল transitive verb ৰ passive ৰূপ থাকে।
      • Passive হৈছে "be" ৰ শুদ্ধ অংশ আৰু past participle।
      • Voice সলনি কৰোঁতে একে tense ৰাখক।
      • "by" ৰ পিছত pronoun: me, him, her, us, them।
      • Imperative েলে "Let", আৰু প্ৰশ্নে প্ৰশ্নৰ ৰূপ ৰাখে।
      • কৰ্তা অজ্ঞাত বা কম গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ হ'লে "by" এৰি দিয়ক।

      শেষ পুনৰালোচনা

      শেষ পুনৰালোচনাত বেলেগ বেলেগ tense ৰ দহটা active বাক্য লৈ প্ৰতিটো passive লৈ সলনি কৰক, তাৰ পিছত পাঁচটা উলটি active লৈ কৰক। যিবোৰ participle ৰূপ আৰু সহায়ক ক্ৰিয়া পাহৰে সেইবোৰ টুকি লৈ কেৱল সেইবোৰ অভ্যাস কৰক। পৰীক্ষকে tense আৰু অৰ্থ অটুট ৰাখি কৰ্তাক স্থানান্তৰ কৰিব পাৰে নে নাই পৰীক্ষা কৰে, আৰু voice ৰ নিত্য অভ্যাসে এই বিষয় দ্ৰুত, সুৰক্ষিত আৰু গোটেই ইংৰাজী পত্ৰত উপযোগী কৰি তোলে।

      সঘনাই সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

      Active আৰু passive voice ৰ পাৰ্থক্য কি?

      Active voice ত কৰ্তাই কামটো কৰে, আনহাতে passive voice ত কৰ্তাই কামটো গ্ৰহণ কৰে।

      Passive voice কেনেকৈ গঠন কৰা হয়?

      "be" ক্ৰিয়াৰ শুদ্ধ ৰূপ আৰু মূল ক্ৰিয়াৰ past participle ৰে passive গঠন কৰা হয়, active বাক্যৰ একে tense ৰাখি।

      Intransitive verb ৰ passive কৰিব পাৰিনে?

      নোৱাৰি। কেৱল object থকা transitive verb ৰ passive কৰিব পাৰি; sleep, die আদি intransitive verb ৰ passive নাই।

      Imperative বাক্যৰ passive কেনেকৈ কৰা হয়?

      "Let" ৰে, যেনে "Open the door" - "Let the door be opened", বা "You are requested to" ৰে।

      Passive voice কেতিয়া ব্যৱহাৰ হয়?

      কৰ্তা অজ্ঞাত, কম গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ, বা নকৈ এৰি দিয়াটো ভাল হ'লে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, আৰু বাতৰি, জাননী আৰু বৈজ্ঞানিক লিখনিত সঘন।

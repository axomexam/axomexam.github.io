      Tenses

      English tenses - present, past and future forms with time markers and common errors asked in Assam competitive exams.

      25 Questions with Answers

      Q1. Fill in the blank: 'She ___ to school every day.'
      (A) goes   (B) go   (C) went   (D) has gone
      Answer: (A) goes — 'Every day' shows a habit, so we use the simple present. With 'she' we add 's/es'.
      goes
      Q2. Fill in the blank: 'The sun ___ in the east.'
      (A) rises   (B) rise   (C) rose   (D) has risen
      Answer: (A) rises — A universal truth is always in the simple present tense.
      rises
      Q3. Fill in the blank: 'Look! It ___ (rain) now.'
      (A) is raining   (B) rains   (C) rained   (D) has rained
      Answer: (A) is raining — An action happening now uses the present continuous (is + ing).
      is raining
      Q4. Fill in the blank: 'She ___ (cook) dinner at present.'
      (A) is cooking   (B) cooks   (C) cooked   (D) has cooked
      Answer: (A) is cooking — 'At present' indicates an action going on now, so present continuous.
      is cooking
      Q5. Fill in the blank: 'I ___ (finish) my homework just now.'
      (A) have finished   (B) finish   (C) finished   (D) was finishing
      Answer: (A) have finished — 'Just' suggests a recently completed action, so we use the present perfect.
      have finished
      Q6. Fill in the blank: 'He ___ (live) here since 2005.'
      (A) has lived   (B) lives   (C) is living   (D) lived
      Answer: (A) has lived — 'Since + a point of time' with an action still continuing takes the present perfect.
      has lived
      Q7. Fill in the blank: 'They ___ (play) cricket for two hours.'
      (A) have been playing   (B) are playing   (C) played   (D) play
      Answer: (A) have been playing — 'For + a period' with an action started in the past and still going on takes the present perfect continuous.
      have been playing
      Q8. Fill in the blank: 'I ___ (visit) my uncle last week.'
      (A) visited   (B) have visited   (C) visit   (D) was visiting
      Answer: (A) visited — 'Last week' is a finished past time, so we use the simple past.
      visited
      Q9. Fill in the blank: 'The baby ___ (cry) when I came in.'
      (A) was crying   (B) cried   (C) cries   (D) has cried
      Answer: (A) was crying — An action going on when another past action happened uses the past continuous.
      was crying
      Q10. Fill in the blank: 'The train ___ (leave) before we reached the station.'
      (A) had left   (B) left   (C) leaves   (D) was leaving
      Answer: (A) had left — An action completed before another past action takes the past perfect.
      had left
      Q11. Fill in the blank: 'He ___ (work) for three hours before the boss came.'
      (A) had been working   (B) has been working   (C) was working   (D) worked
      Answer: (A) had been working — An action continuing up to a past point uses the past perfect continuous.
      had been working
      Q12. Fill in the blank: 'I ___ (call) you tomorrow.'
      (A) will call   (B) call   (C) called   (D) have called
      Answer: (A) will call — 'Tomorrow' indicates a future action, so we use 'will + verb'.
      will call
      Q13. Fill in the blank: 'She ___ (finish) the work by evening.'
      (A) will have finished   (B) will finish   (C) finishes   (D) has finished
      Answer: (A) will have finished — 'By + a future time' showing completion takes the future perfect.
      will have finished
      Q14. Fill in the blank: 'This time next week I ___ (travel) to Delhi.'
      (A) will be travelling   (B) will travel   (C) am travelling   (D) have travelled
      Answer: (A) will be travelling — An action in progress at a certain future time uses the future continuous.
      will be travelling
      Q15. 'Since morning' is a marker mainly used with which tense?
      (A) Present perfect continuous   (B) Simple past   (C) Past perfect   (D) Future simple
      Answer: (A) Present perfect continuous — 'Since + a starting point' is typical of the present perfect / present perfect continuous tense.
      Present perfect continuous
      Q16. 'Yesterday' is a marker mainly used with which tense?
      (A) Simple past   (B) Simple present   (C) Future perfect   (D) Present continuous
      Answer: (A) Simple past — 'Yesterday' points to a finished past time, so the simple past is used.
      Simple past
      Q17. Fill in the blank: 'I met him two days ___.'
      (A) ago   (B) before   (C) since   (D) for
      Answer: (A) ago — 'Ago' is used with a past time and the simple past tense.
      ago
      Q18. Fill in the blank: 'She has been working here ___ 2019.'
      (A) since   (B) for   (C) from   (D) ago
      Answer: (A) since — A point of time (2019) takes 'since' in the present perfect.
      since
      Q19. Which verb does not take the continuous form?
      (A) know   (B) run   (C) sing   (D) dance
      Answer: (A) know — 'Know' is a stative verb (state, not action), so we say 'I know', not 'I am knowing'.
      know
      Q20. Fill in the blank: 'He ___ (know) the answer to this question.'
      (A) knows   (B) is knowing   (C) has been knowing   (D) knew
      Answer: (A) knows — Stative verbs like 'know' are not used in the continuous form.
      knows
      Q21. 'Every day' is a marker mainly used with which tense?
      (A) Simple present   (B) Past perfect   (C) Future continuous   (D) Present perfect continuous
      Answer: (A) Simple present — 'Every day' shows a routine, so the simple present tense is used.
      Simple present
      Q22. Fill in the blank: 'The earth ___ round the sun.'
      (A) moves   (B) moved   (C) is moving   (D) has moved
      Answer: (A) moves — Scientific truths are expressed in the simple present tense.
      moves
      Q23. Fill in the blank: 'I ___ (see) him yesterday.'
      (A) saw   (B) seen   (C) have seen   (D) see
      Answer: (A) saw — 'Yesterday' takes the simple past. The past of 'see' is 'saw'.
      saw
      Q24. Fill in the blank: 'She ___ (not, go) to Guwahati last month.'
      (A) did not go   (B) does not go   (C) has not gone   (D) was not going
      Answer: (A) did not go — The negative of the simple past is made with 'did not + base verb'.
      did not go
      Q25. Fill in the blank: '___ you ___ (eat) your breakfast yet?'
      (A) Have ... eaten   (B) Did ... ate   (C) Do ... ate   (D) Are ... eating
      Answer: (A) Have ... eaten — 'Yet' is used with the present perfect: 'Have + past participle'.
      Have ... eaten

      Sample questions with answers

      A quick look at the kind of questions you will practise here:

      1. Fill in the blank: 'I ___ (write) this letter since morning.'
        Answer: 'Since morning' with an action still going on takes the present perfect continuous.
      2. Fill in the blank: 'It ___ (not, rain) now.'
        Answer: The negative of the present continuous is 'is/am/are + not + ing'.
      3. Fill in the blank: 'How long ___ you ___ (learn) English?'
        Answer: 'How long' asks about duration up to now, so the present perfect continuous is used.
      4. Fill in the blank: 'My brother ___ (play) cricket every evening.'
        Answer: A routine with a third-person subject takes 'plays' (adds s/es).
      5. Fill in the blank: 'She ___ (wash) her clothes at present.'
        Answer: 'At present' shows an action happening now, so present continuous.
      6. Fill in the blank: 'The train ___ at 6 pm every day.'
        Answer: Fixed timetables and schedules are expressed in the simple present.

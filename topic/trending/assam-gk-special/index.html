      Assam GK Special - The Complete Revision Guide

      Assam general knowledge is the highest-scoring section in almost every state recruitment examination. Whether you are preparing for ADRE, APSC, Assam Police, or the Assam-specific portions of SSC and Railway tests, a strong hold on the history, geography, culture and polity of the state can lift your overall score significantly. This special revision guide brings the most repeated themes together in one place.

      Assam history in brief

      Ancient Assam was known as Kamarupa under the Varman and Pala dynasties, and the early rulers encouraged both Hinduism and Buddhism. The medieval period is dominated by the Ahom kingdom, founded by Sukapha in the thirteenth century, which ruled for nearly six hundred years. The Vaishnavite movement led by Srimanta Sankardeva and Madhavdeva reshaped the society, language and culture of the region and produced the Namghar and Satra institutions. The Battle of Saraighat in 1671, in which Lachit Borphukan defeated the Mughal forces, is the most celebrated military episode in Assamese history. In the modern period Assam was an important centre of the freedom struggle and of the Indian National Congress.

      Geography you must know

      The Brahmaputra is the lifeline of the state, entering India in Arunachal Pradesh and flowing through the Assam valley before turning south into Bangladesh, where it is called the Jamuna. The Barak river drains the southern part of the state. Majuli, in the Brahmaputra, is one of the largest river islands in the world and a centre of Vaishnavite culture. Assam is known for its tea gardens, oil fields at Digboi and Naharkatia, and one-horned rhinoceros population in Kaziranga National Park, which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Manas National Park is famous for the royal Bengal tiger and is also a UNESCO site, while Pobitora has one of the highest rhinoceros densities in the world.

      Culture and festivals

      Bihu is the principal festival and comes in three forms: Bohag Bihu in spring marking the Assamese new year, Kati Bihu in autumn, and Magh Bihu in winter. Other important celebrations include Ambubachi Mela at Kamakhya temple, the Raas Mahotsav, and Ali-Ai-Ligang of the Mishing community. The state's classical dance form is Sattriya, recognised as one of the eight classical dances of India. Assamese literature begins with the Charyapada and reaches its height in the writings of Sankardeva, and modern Assamese literature has won national recognition, including the Jnanpith Award.

      Polity and economy

      Assam is governed by a legislative assembly and sends representatives to both houses of Parliament. The Sixth Schedule of the Constitution provides for autonomous district councils in the hill areas of the state, a provision that is frequently asked in polity papers. The state's economy rests on tea, oil and natural gas, agriculture, tourism and, increasingly, services. Guwahati is the largest city and the gateway to the entire north-eastern region.

      How to prepare Assam GK

      • Learn a timeline of Assam history and place each dynasty and event on it.
      • Make a map and mark the rivers, national parks, oil fields and major towns.
      • Read about each festival with its community and season.
      • Revise the Sixth Schedule and the state's administrative structure.

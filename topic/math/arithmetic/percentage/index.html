      300 তকৈ 40% কম সংখ্যা নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      Answer: Step 1: 40% of 300 = (40/100) × 300 = 120,Step 2: Number = 300 − 120 = 180,Answer: (b) 180

      Q35. In an election, a candidate got 55% of the total votes and won by 200 votes. Find the total number of votes.

      Answer: Concept: Winning margin = 55% − 45% = 10%.,Step 1: 10% of total = 200,Step 2: Total = 200 × (100/10),Step 3: = 2000,Answer: (c) 2000
      ধাৰণা: জয়ৰ ব্যৱধান = 55% − 45% = 10%।,পদক্ষেপ ১: মুঠৰ 10% = 200,পদক্ষেপ ২: মুঠ = 200 × (100/10),পদক্ষেপ ৩: = 2000,উত্তৰ: (গ) 2000
      Q36. A shopkeeper marks an item at Rs 250 and gives a discount of 20%. Find the selling price.

      Q37. What percent of 5 kg is 750 g?

      Q38. The value of a machine depreciates by 10% per year. If its present value is Rs 50000, find its value after one year.

      পদক্ষেপ ১: হ্ৰাস = 50000 ৰ 10% = 5000,পদক্ষেপ ২: নতুন মূল্য = 50000 − 5000 = 45000,উত্তৰ: (খ) 45000 টকা
      Q39. If A's salary is 25% less than B's, then B's salary is what percent more than A's?
      A ৰ দৰমহা B তকৈ 25% কম হ'লে, B ৰ দৰমহা A তকৈ কিমান শতাংশ বেছি?
      Answer: Let B = 100, then A = 75.,Step 1: Difference = 100 − 75 = 25,Step 2: Percentage more than A = (25/75) × 100,Step 3: = 33 1/3%,Answer: (c) 33 1/3%
      B = 100 ধৰো, তেন্তে A = 75।,পদক্ষেপ ১: পাৰ্থক্য = 100 − 75 = 25,পদক্ষেপ ২: A তকৈ বেছি শতাংশ = (25/75) × 100,পদক্ষেপ ৩: = 33 1/3%,উত্তৰ: (গ) 33 1/3%
      Q40. Find 2/5 as a percentage.

      Q41. The price of rice increases by 20%. By what percent should consumption be reduced to keep expenditure the same?
      চাউলৰ দাম 20% বৃদ্ধি পায়। খৰচ একে ৰাখিবলৈ ব্যৱহাৰ কিমান শতাংশ হ্ৰাস কৰিব লাগিব?
      Answer: Formula: Reduction% = (R/(100+R)) × 100.,Step 1: R = 20,Step 2: Reduction% = (20/120) × 100,Step 3: = 16 2/3%,Answer: (a) 16 2/3%
      সূত্ৰ: হ্ৰাস% = (R/(100+R)) × 100।,পদক্ষেপ ১: R = 20,পদক্ষেপ ২: হ্ৰাস% = (20/120) × 100,পদক্ষেপ ৩: = 16 2/3%,উত্তৰ: (ক) 16 2/3%

      Q43. A student scored 75% marks. If he scored 450 marks, find the total marks.

      Q44. The ratio of boys to girls in a school is 3:2. If 60% of the students are boys, what is the total number of students if there are 720 boys?

      Q45. What is the percentage change when a number increases from 50 to 75?

      Q46. If 40% of a number is 80 more than 25% of the same number, find the number.

      Q48. The value of an article increases by 10% every year. If its present value is Rs 4000, find its value after 2 years.
      এটা সামগ্ৰীৰ মূল্য বছৰি 10% বৃদ্ধি পায়। বৰ্তমান মূল্য 4000 টকা হ'লে 2 বছৰৰ পিছত মূল্য নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      Answer: Formula: Final = P(1 + r/100)².,Step 1: Final = 4000 × (110/100)²,Step 2: = 4000 × 1.1 × 1.1,Step 3: = 4000 × 1.21 = 4840,Answer: (d) Rs 4840
      সূত্ৰ: চূড়ান্ত = P(1 + r/100)²।,পদক্ষেপ ১: চূড়ান্ত = 4000 × (110/100)²,পদক্ষেপ ২: = 4000 × 1.1 × 1.1,পদক্ষেপ ৩: = 4000 × 1.21 = 4840,উত্তৰ: (ঘ) 4840 টকা
      Q49. A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3:1. What percent is water?

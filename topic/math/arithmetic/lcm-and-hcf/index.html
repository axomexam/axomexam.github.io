      Q49. Four numbers are 12, 18, 24 and 36. Find their LCM.

      Q50. Find the greatest number that will divide 43, 91 and 183 so as to leave the same remainder in each case.
      43, 91 আৰু 183 ক ভাগ কৰিলে প্ৰতিটোতে একে ভাগশেষ ৰাখিব পৰা বৃহত্তম সংখ্যা নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      Answer: Method: Find HCF of the differences.,Step 1: 91 − 43 = 48; 183 − 91 = 92; 183 − 43 = 140,Step 2: HCF of 48, 92, 140: 48 = 2⁴×3; 92 = 2²×23; 140 = 2²×5×7,Step 3: Common = 2² = 4,Answer: (b) 4

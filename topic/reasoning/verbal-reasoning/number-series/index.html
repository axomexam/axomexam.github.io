      Number Series

      Number series practice — find the next or missing number in a sequence.

      3 Questions with Answers

      Q1. Find the next number in the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
      শৃংখলাটোৰ পৰৱৰ্তী সংখ্যা উলিওৱা: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
      Answer: 42. (Differences increase by 2 each time: +4, +6, +8, +10, +12)
      42। (পাৰ্থক্য প্ৰতিবাৰ 2 বাঢ়ে: +4, +6, +8, +10, +12)
      Q2. Find the missing number: 3, 9, 27, 81, ?
      অনুপস্থিত সংখ্যা উলিওৱা: 3, 9, 27, 81, ?
      Answer: 243. (Each number is multiplied by 3)
      243। (প্ৰতিটো সংখ্যা 3 ৰে পূৰণ হয়)
      Q3. Find the next number: 5, 11, 23, 47, ?
      পৰৱৰ্তী সংখ্যা উলিওৱা: 5, 11, 23, 47, ?
      Answer: 95. (Each term is doubled plus 1: 5×2+1=11, 11×2+1=23, 23×2+1=47, 47×2+1=95)
      95। (প্ৰতিটো পদ দ্বিগুণ কৰি 1 যোগ হয়: 5×2+1=11, 11×2+1=23, 23×2+1=47, 47×2+1=95)

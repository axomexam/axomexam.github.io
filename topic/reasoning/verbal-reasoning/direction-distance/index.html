      Direction & Distance Test (Assam Competitive Exams)

      Solve direction-facing puzzles, walking-path problems, and shadow-direction questions using compass logic and Pythagoras distance.

      21 Questions with Answers

      Q1. A man walks 5 km towards North, then turns right and walks 3 km. In which direction is he from his starting point?
      (A) North-East   (B) North-West   (C) South-East   (D) South-West
      Answer: A
      Q2. Rohan walks 10 m North, turns left and walks 5 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
      (A) 5 m East   (B) 5 m West   (C) 10 m East   (D) 10 m West
      Answer: B
      Q3. A person facing North turns 90° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
      (A) North   (B) East   (C) West   (D) South
      Answer: B
      Q4. A person facing South turns 45° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
      (A) South-East   (B) North-East   (C) South-West   (D) North-West
      Answer: A
      Q5. A person walks 4 km East, then 3 km North. What is the straight-line distance from the starting point?
      (A) 7 km   (B) 1 km   (C) 5 km   (D) 12 km
      Answer: C
      Q6. If A is to the North of B and C is to the East of B, in which direction is A from C?
      (A) North-East   (B) North-West   (C) South-East   (D) South-West
      Answer: B
      Q7. The sun rises in the East. In the morning, a man's shadow falls in which direction?
      (A) East   (B) West   (C) North   (D) South
      Answer: B
      Q8. A man walks 30 m North, 20 m East, then 30 m South. How far is he from the start?
      (A) 30 m West   (B) 20 m East   (C) 30 m East   (D) 20 m West
      Answer: B
      Q9. A person facing North turns right, then right again, then right again. Which direction is he facing now?
      (A) East   (B) South   (C) West   (D) North
      Answer: C
      Q10. A is 5 km East of B. C is 5 km North of A. In which direction is C from B?
      (A) North-East   (B) North-West   (C) South-East   (D) South-West
      Answer: A
      Q11. A man walks 10 km South, turns left and walks 10 km, then turns left again and walks 10 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
      (A) 10 km East   (B) 10 km West   (C) 20 km East   (D) 10 km South
      Answer: A
      Q12. Two persons A and B start from the same point. A walks 10 m North and B walks 10 m South. What is the distance between them?
      (A) 10 m   (B) 15 m   (C) 20 m   (D) 5 m
      Answer: C
      Q13. A car travels 20 km North, 10 km West, then 20 km South. Where is it relative to the starting point?
      (A) 10 km East   (B) 10 km West   (C) 20 km West   (D) At the start point
      Answer: B
      Q14. In the evening, a man's shadow falls to his left. In which direction is he facing?
      (A) North   (B) South   (C) East   (D) West
      Answer: B
      Q15. A man walks 5 km North, turns right and walks 5 km, then turns right again and walks 5 km. How far is he from the start?
      (A) 5 km East   (B) 5 km West   (C) 10 km East   (D) 10 km North
      Answer: A
      Q16. A person facing West turns 135° anticlockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
      (A) North-East   (B) North-West   (C) South-West   (D) South-East
      Answer: A
      Q17. In a map, North is shown at the top. Which direction is to the left of a person facing North?
      (A) East   (B) West   (C) South   (D) North-East
      Answer: B
      Q18. A boy walks 3 km East, then 4 km North. What is the shortest distance back to the start?
      (A) 7 km   (B) 5 km   (C) 6 km   (D) 4 km
      Answer: B
      Q19. If a person is facing North and turns to his left three times, which direction will he finally face?
      (A) North   (B) South   (C) East   (D) West
      Answer: B
      Q20. A, B, C and D stand in a square. B is to the East of A. C is to the South of B. D is to the West of C. In which direction is D from A?
      (A) South   (B) East   (C) North   (D) West
      Answer: A
      Q21. X walks 8 m North, turns right and walks 6 m. What is the shortest distance from the start?
      (A) 14 m   (B) 10 m   (C) 12 m   (D) 2 m
      Answer: B

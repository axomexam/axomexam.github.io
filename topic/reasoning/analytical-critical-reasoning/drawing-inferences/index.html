      Drawing Inferences (Assam Competitive Exams)

      A short passage or set of statements is given. Choose the inference that can most safely/logically be drawn from it.

      50 Questions with Answers

      Q1. The literacy rate of Assam has risen from 63.31 percent in 2001 to 72.19 percent in 2011, but the female literacy rate still lags behind the male literacy rate. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above passage?
      (A) Male literacy in Assam must have decreased between 2001 and 2011.   (B) The number of literate people in Assam was higher in 2011 than in 2001.   (C) Illiteracy in Assam was completely eliminated by 2011.   (D) Female literacy is higher than male literacy in all districts of Assam.
      Answer: B
      Q2. Every year during the monsoon, several districts of Assam experience floods, and large areas of standing crops are submerged. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above statement?
      (A) Flooding is the only natural calamity that affects Assam.   (B) No crops ever survive the monsoon in Assam.   (C) Monsoon floods can cause damage to crops in Assam.   (D) The Assam government has ended all floods permanently.
      Answer: C
      Q3. A large number of young people from rural Assam migrate to cities such as Guwahati and Delhi every year in search of employment. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above passage?
      (A) Rural Assam offers no employment to any of its residents.   (B) Young people leave rural Assam mainly because they cannot find enough jobs there.   (C) Cities are always a better place to live than villages.   (D) Migration from rural Assam has completely stopped in recent years.
      Answer: B
      Q4. The Superintendent of Police said that most road accidents in the district occur at night because of speeding and poor lighting. Which of the following is implied by the above statement?
      (A) All road accidents in the district occur only at night.   (B) No accidents ever occur during the daytime.   (C) Reducing speed and improving lighting could help lower night-time accidents.   (D) Speeding is never a cause of accidents during the day.
      Answer: C
      Q5. A study found that villages that have anganwadi centres with regular health check-up camps report fewer cases of child malnutrition. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above passage?
      (A) Regular health check-ups may be associated with better child nutrition.   (B) Health check-up camps alone guarantee that no child will ever be malnourished.   (C) Child malnutrition exists only in villages without anganwadi centres.   (D) Anganwadi centres have nothing to do with child health.
      Answer: A
      Q6. Tea garden workers in Assam often suffer from seasonal income gaps, because they are paid only for the days they work and work is not available throughout the year. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above statement?
      (A) Tea garden workers in Assam are always paid more than they deserve.   (B) All tea garden workers in Assam earn the same income throughout the year.   (C) Tea gardens in Assam have stopped employing local workers.   (D) The income of tea garden workers can vary depending on the availability of work.
      Answer: D
      Q7. In a panchayat meeting, the members decided to spend the annual development fund on repairing the village road instead of building a new community hall. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above information?
      (A) The panchayat considered repairing the road a more pressing need than the community hall.   (B) The panchayat has permanently given up the idea of a community hall.   (C) The village road is in perfect condition already.   (D) The community hall was more important to the villagers than the road.
      Answer: A
      Q8. Many shopkeepers in Guwahati now display QR codes at their counters, and customers increasingly pay by scanning them instead of using cash. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above passage?
      (A) Cash payment has been completely banned in Guwahati.   (B) Digital payment methods are gaining acceptance among Guwahati shopkeepers and customers.   (C) No customer in Guwahati ever uses cash anymore.   (D) All digital payments in Guwahati fail to work.
      Answer: B
      Q9. The principal issued a notice stating that from the next month, all students must wear the school uniform on every working day without exception. Which of the following is implied by the above statement?
      (A) Uniforms will no longer be required after next month.   (B) The school will remain closed from next month.   (C) Some students had been attending school without the proper uniform.   (D) Teachers are exempted from wearing any formal dress.
      Answer: C
      Q10. Farmers in the district have been advised to sow paddy only after the monsoon has properly set in, because early sowing has often led to crop failure. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above statement?
      (A) Paddy never fails if it is sown early.   (B) Farmers in the district grow only paddy and nothing else.   (C) The timing of sowing can affect the success of the paddy crop.   (D) Monsoon has stopped arriving in the district completely.
      Answer: C
      Q11. The weather report says that heavy rain is expected in Upper Assam for the next three days and that several rivers are already flowing above the danger level. Which of the following is the most likely conclusion?
      (A) The rain is expected only in Lower Assam.   (B) All rivers of Assam have crossed the danger level.   (C) No river in Assam has ever crossed the danger level before.   (D) There is a risk of flooding in parts of Upper Assam.
      Answer: D
      Q12. Female enrolment in higher education in Assam has risen steadily over the last decade, though the rise has been slower in rural colleges than in urban ones. Which of the following can be most safely inferred from the above statement?
      (A) Female enrolment has risen in every single college of Assam without exception.   (B) Rural colleges in Assam enrol more women than men.   (C) Female enrolment has been falling in urban colleges of Assam.   (D) More women in Assam are pursuing higher education than a decade ago.
      Answer: D
      Q13. The health department reported that most cases of dengue in the town are found in areas where stagnant water collects after rain. Which of the following cannot be logically inferred from the above statement?
      (A) Dengue cases in the town are not spread evenly across all areas.   (B) Stagnant water may be contributing to the spread of dengue.   (C) Dengue is spread only through contaminated drinking water.   (D) Removing stagnant water might reduce dengue cases.
      Answer: C
      Q14. In a class test, out of fifty students, forty passed in Mathematics and forty-five passed in English, while three failed in both subjects. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above information?
      (A) The number of students who passed both subjects was greater than the number who failed both.   (B) Every student who failed in Mathematics also failed in English.   (C) No student passed in more than one subject.   (D) English was easier than Mathematics for every student in the class.
      Answer: A
      Q15. A village that received a new all-weather road five years ago now has three new schools, and several families have started small businesses. Which of the following can be most safely inferred from the above passage?
      (A) The village has seen some development since the road was built.   (B) The new road was the only cause of every change in the village.   (C) Every village with a good road has three schools.   (D) No other village in the district has a new road.
      Answer: A
      Q16. The Chief Minister announced that every unemployed graduate of the state will be given a monthly allowance of five thousand rupees for one year. Which of the following is implied by the above announcement?
      (A) The state currently has no unemployed graduates.   (B) The allowance will continue for every graduate for life.   (C) Only graduates working in government jobs will get the allowance.   (D) The allowance is meant to be temporary and limited to one year.
      Answer: D
      Q17. The Assam government has announced that all new private vehicles sold in the state must be fitted with a tracking device from the next financial year. Which of the following can be most logically inferred from the above announcement?
      (A) Tracking devices are being banned from all vehicles in Assam.   (B) The tracking device rule will apply to vehicles sold after the new rule comes into force.   (C) The rule will apply only to old vehicles already on the road.   (D) No new vehicles will be sold in Assam from the next financial year.
      Answer: B
      Q18. Although Guwahati has many hospitals, people from smaller towns of Assam still travel long distances to reach it for serious treatment. Which of the following can be most reasonably inferred from the above statement?
      (A) People from smaller towns regard Guwahati as an important centre for serious medical treatment.   (B) Smaller towns of Assam have no roads to Guwahati.   (C) All treatment in Guwahati is completely free of cost.   (D) No one from Guwahati ever falls seriously ill.
      Answer: A
      Q19. The Panchayat has ordered that no one in the village may burn paddy straw in the open fields during the winter months, as this causes heavy smoke and breathing problems. Which of the following can be most safely inferred from the above order?
      (A) Paddy is not grown in this village at all.   (B) Burning paddy straw was considered harmful to people's health in the village.   (C) Burning straw in winter causes no smoke whatsoever.   (D) The panchayat has banned all agriculture in the village.
      Answer: B
      Q20. A recent report notes that internet use in the tea garden areas of Assam has grown rapidly, yet most families there still depend on television for their daily news. Which of the following is the most reasonable conclusion from the above passage?
      (A) Television use in these areas has fallen to zero.   (B) People in these areas have stopped using the internet completely.   (C) No family in these areas owns a mobile phone.   (D) Internet and television are being used side by side by many families in these areas.
      Answer: D
      Q21. The number of seats in the state's police recruitment examination has been increased this year because many posts have remained vacant for a long time. Which of the following can be most logically inferred from the above statement?
      (A) The number of vacant posts in the police force has come down to zero.   (B) No candidate applied for the police examination last year.   (C) The police force of the state now has more personnel than it needs.   (D) The police force in the state has been facing a shortage of personnel.
      Answer: D
      Q22. Villagers living close to the river were asked to move their cattle to higher ground, as the river has been rising steadily for two days. Which of the following is the most likely conclusion from the above information?
      (A) The river has never risen before in this region.   (B) The rising river was seen as a possible threat to the cattle.   (C) Villagers were asked to throw their cattle into the river.   (D) The river is flowing far below its normal level.
      Answer: B
      Q23. A study of two districts in Assam found that the district with better rural road connectivity also showed higher school attendance among children. Which of the following can be most safely inferred from the study?
      (A) Good roads are the only reason any child attends school.   (B) Children attend school only when they can travel by bus.   (C) The district with better roads had lower school attendance.   (D) Better road connectivity is associated with higher school attendance in the districts studied.
      Answer: D
      Q24. The number of tourists visiting Kaziranga National Park has increased every year for the past five years, and new lodges are being built near the park. Which of the following is the most likely conclusion from the above passage?
      (A) The demand for tourist accommodation near the park appears to be growing.   (B) Tourism in the area is declining sharply.   (C) No one visits Kaziranga National Park anymore.   (D) All the old lodges near the park have been shut down.
      Answer: A
      Q25. In a certain district, the block development officer observed that villages with active women's self-help groups recover faster from flood damage than those without them. Which of the following can be most safely inferred from this observation?
      (A) Women's self-help groups are the only thing that helps villages after a flood.   (B) Villages with self-help groups never face floods at all.   (C) Self-help groups cause flood damage to spread faster.   (D) Active women's self-help groups are associated with quicker flood recovery in the villages observed.
      Answer: D

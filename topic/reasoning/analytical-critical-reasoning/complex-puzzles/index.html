      Complex Puzzles Test (Assam Competitive Exams)

      Solve floor, box and scheduling puzzles using positional, relational and calendar clues.

      43 Questions with Answers

      Q1. Six persons A, B, C, D, E, F live on six different floors of a building (ground = 1st). A lives on the top floor. F lives two floors below A. Who lives on the floor immediately above D if D lives on the 2nd floor?
      (A) A   (B) B   (C) C   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: D
      Q2. Five people P, Q, R, S, T live on five floors of a building. P lives on the top floor. Q lives immediately below P. R lives immediately above S. If S lives on the 2nd floor from bottom, who lives on the bottom floor?
      (A) T   (B) R   (C) S   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: A
      Q3. Seven persons live in a seven-storey building (ground to 6th floor). Three persons live above C. How many persons live below C?
      (A) 3   (B) 4   (C) 2   (D) 5
      Answer: A
      Q4. In a five-floor building, A lives on the 3rd floor. B lives two floors above A. C lives immediately below B. Which floor does C live on?
      (A) 5th   (B) 4th   (C) 2nd   (D) 6th
      Answer: B
      Q5. Six friends live on six floors. Two persons live between A and B. A lives on the ground floor. B lives above A. How many floors are above B?
      (A) 3   (B) 4   (C) 2   (D) 1
      Answer: C
      Q6. Seven persons live on seven floors of a building. P lives on the 4th floor from the bottom. Q lives immediately above P. How many persons live below Q?
      (A) 4   (B) 3   (C) 5   (D) 2
      Answer: A
      Q7. Four people A, B, C, D live on four floors of a building. A lives on the top floor. C lives below B. D lives above C but below B. Who lives on the ground floor?
      (A) B   (B) C   (C) D   (D) A
      Answer: B
      Q8. Eight people live on eight floors. X lives on the 7th floor. Y lives three floors below X. Z lives immediately above Y. Which floor does Z live on?
      (A) 4th   (B) 5th   (C) 3rd   (D) 6th
      Answer: B
      Q9. Six persons live on six floors. M lives on the 2nd floor. N lives three floors above M. O lives immediately below N. P lives immediately above M. Who lives on the top (6th) floor?
      (A) N   (B) O   (C) P   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: D
      Q10. Five people live on five floors of a building. A lives above B but below C. D lives above C. E lives below B. Who lives on the top floor?
      (A) A   (B) B   (C) C   (D) D
      Answer: D
      Q11. A six-storey building has one person per floor. K lives on an odd-numbered floor above the 2nd floor. L lives immediately below K. If K does not live on the 5th floor, which floor does K live on?
      (A) 3rd   (B) 1st   (C) 5th   (D) 4th
      Answer: A
      Q12. Seven persons A to G live on seven floors. B lives immediately below A. C lives immediately above A. If A lives on the 4th floor, how many persons live above C?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 4   (D) 1
      Answer: A
      Q13. Six boxes A, B, C, D, E, F are stacked one above another. Box A is at the bottom. Box C is immediately above A. Box E is immediately below A. Which box is exactly in the middle of the stack of six?
      (A) B and C   (B) A and B   (C) D and E   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: A
      Q14. Five boxes P, Q, R, S, T are stacked. P is at the top. Q is immediately below P. R is immediately above S. T is at the bottom. Which box is immediately above T?
      (A) S   (B) R   (C) Q   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: D
      Q15. Seven boxes are stacked. Box X is the 3rd from the top. Box Y is immediately below X. How many boxes are above Y?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 4   (D) 1
      Answer: B
      Q16. Five boxes are stacked one above another. Box A is two places above box B. Box C is immediately below box B. If box A is 2nd from the top, what is the position of box C from the bottom?
      (A) 2nd   (B) 3rd   (C) 4th   (D) 1st
      Answer: A
      Q17. Eight boxes are stacked. Box P is at the top. Box Q is at the bottom. Box R is exactly in the middle. How many boxes are between R and Q?
      (A) 3   (B) 4   (C) 2   (D) 5
      Answer: A
      Q18. Six boxes M, N, O, P, Q, R are stacked. N is immediately above P. R is immediately below P. Which two boxes are adjacent to each other if the stack from top is M, N, P, R, O, Q?
      (A) M and O   (B) O and Q   (C) N and P   (D) M and N
      Answer: B
      Q19. Five boxes A, B, C, D, E are stacked. Two boxes are between A and B. A is at the top. How many boxes are above B?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 1   (D) 4
      Answer: B
      Q20. Seven boxes are stacked. Box G is 4th from the bottom. Box H is immediately above G. How many boxes are above H?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 4   (D) 1
      Answer: A
      Q21. Six boxes are stacked. Box X is at the bottom. Box Z is two places above box X. Box Y is immediately above box Z. Which box is 3rd from the top?
      (A) Y   (B) Z   (C) X   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: A
      Q22. Four boxes P, Q, R, S are stacked. Q is above P. R is above Q. S is at the bottom. Which box is at the top?
      (A) P   (B) Q   (C) R   (D) S
      Answer: C
      Q23. Six lectures A, B, C, D, E, F are scheduled on six days from Monday to Saturday, one per day. A is on Monday. B is two days after A. Which day is lecture B on?
      (A) Wednesday   (B) Thursday   (C) Tuesday   (D) Friday
      Answer: A
      Q24. Seven employees work on seven days from Monday to Sunday. P works on Monday. Q works on the 4th day after P. R works immediately after Q. Which day does R work?
      (A) Saturday   (B) Friday   (C) Thursday   (D) Sunday
      Answer: A
      Q25. Five subjects are taught from Monday to Friday. Maths is on Monday. Science is two days after Maths. English is immediately after Science. Which day is English taught?
      (A) Wednesday   (B) Thursday   (C) Friday   (D) Tuesday
      Answer: C

      Seating Arrangement Test (Assam Competitive Exams)

      Solve linear, circular and square seating puzzles using position, direction and neighbour clues.

      49 Questions with Answers

      Q1. Five friends A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing North. B sits to the immediate right of A. C sits to the immediate left of A. Who sits in the middle if there are exactly three people to the left of C?
      (A) A   (B) B   (C) C   (D) D
      Answer: B
      Q2. Six persons P, Q, R, S, T, U sit in a row facing North. P is at the extreme left end. Q sits second to the right of P. Who sits exactly in the middle of the row of six?
      (A) R and S   (B) S and T   (C) Q and R   (D) T and U
      Answer: B
      Q3. Seven people A, B, C, D, E, F, G sit in a row facing South. D sits in the middle. B sits third to the left of D. Which of the following is the correct position of B?
      (A) Immediate left of D   (B) Extreme left end   (C) Immediate right of D   (D) Third from the right end
      Answer: B
      Q4. In a row of five people facing North, Ravi is at the extreme left. Sita is second to the right of Ravi. Gita sits exactly in the middle. Who sits between Sita and the person at the extreme right?
      (A) Ravi   (B) Sita   (C) Gita   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: C
      Q5. Eight friends A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H sit in a row facing North. A sits at one of the extreme ends. F sits third to the right of A. How many persons sit between F and the other extreme end?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 4   (D) 5
      Answer: C
      Q6. Five people sit in a row. Two people sit between Amit and Bhaskar. Bhaskar sits at the extreme right end. How many people sit to the left of Amit?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 1   (D) 4
      Answer: A
      Q7. In a row of 7 persons facing North, P is third from the left end and Q is third from the right end. How many persons sit between P and Q?
      (A) 1   (B) 2   (C) 3   (D) 0
      Answer: A
      Q8. Four people A, B, C, D sit in a row facing North. A sits to the immediate right of B. C sits at the extreme left end. D sits second from the right end. Who sits to the immediate right of A?
      (A) B   (B) C   (C) D   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: C
      Q9. Six people X, Y, Z, P, Q, R sit in a row facing South. Y sits to the immediate left of X. Z sits to the immediate right of X. Who sits in the middle of the row?
      (A) X and P   (B) Z and Q   (C) Y and X   (D) R and P
      Answer: A
      Q10. In a row of 6 persons, the person at the extreme left is A. C is second to the right of A. E is exactly between C and the person at the extreme right. What is the position of E from the right end?
      (A) 2nd   (B) 3rd   (C) 4th   (D) 5th
      Answer: A
      Q11. Five girls sit in a row. Rekha sits in the middle. Meena sits to the immediate left of Rekha. Sita sits at the extreme left end. Who sits between Meena and Sita?
      (A) Rekha   (B) Meena   (C) No one   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: C
      Q12. Seven students A, B, C, D, E, F, G sit in a row facing North. A and B sit at the extreme ends. C sits exactly in the middle. How many persons sit between C and B if B is at the right end?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 4   (D) 1
      Answer: B
      Q13. In a row of 9 persons, a man is 4th from the left. What is his position from the right end?
      (A) 5th   (B) 4th   (C) 6th   (D) 3rd
      Answer: C
      Q14. Five persons A, B, C, D, E sit in a line facing South. B sits to the immediate right of A (from A's perspective). C sits at the extreme right end. If the order from left to right is D, A, E, C then where does B sit?
      (A) Immediate left of C   (B) Between A and E   (C) Immediate right of D   (D) Extreme left end
      Answer: C
      Q15. In a row of 10 people, Mohan is 7th from the left. How many people sit to his right?
      (A) 3   (B) 2   (C) 4   (D) 5
      Answer: A
      Q16. Eight boys sit in a row. Ramesh is 3rd from the left. Suresh is 2nd to the right of Ramesh. What is Suresh's position from the right end of the row?
      (A) 4th   (B) 5th   (C) 6th   (D) 3rd
      Answer: A
      Q17. Four friends A, B, C, D sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite B. C sits to the immediate right of A. Who sits to the immediate left of B?
      (A) C   (B) D   (C) A   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: D
      Q18. Six people P, Q, R, S, T, U sit around a circular table facing the centre. P sits opposite R. S sits to the immediate right of P. Which of the following pairs sit opposite each other?
      (A) Q and T   (B) S and U   (C) Q and U   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: D
      Q19. Five people sit around a circular table facing the centre. Each person faces the centre. Ram is second to the left of Shyam. If Shyam faces North, who faces the centre directly between them on the arc?
      (A) The person opposite Ram   (B) There is no one between them   (C) Shyam's immediate right   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: B
      Q20. Eight friends sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits third to the right of B. How many people sit between A and B when counted clockwise?
      (A) 2   (B) 3   (C) 4   (D) 5
      Answer: A
      Q21. In a circular arrangement of 6 people facing the centre, X sits opposite Y. Z sits to the immediate left of X. W sits to the immediate right of Y. Who sits opposite Z?
      (A) X   (B) W   (C) Y   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: B
      Q22. Four couples sit around a circular table facing the centre, with husbands and wives sitting opposite each other. If Mr. A's wife sits opposite him, who sits to the immediate right of Mr. A?
      (A) Mr. A's wife   (B) Another man   (C) Another woman   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: C
      Q23. Five friends sit around a circular table facing the centre. Anil sits to the immediate right of Bimal. Chitra sits to the immediate left of Bimal. Dinesh sits opposite Anil. Who sits opposite Bimal?
      (A) Chitra   (B) Dinesh   (C) Ekta   (D) Cannot be determined
      Answer: C
      Q24. Six people sit around a circular table facing the centre. Two people sit between A and B (clockwise). Two people sit between B and A (anticlockwise) on the other side. How many people are there in total around the table?
      (A) 4   (B) 6   (C) 8   (D) 5
      Answer: B

      Q1. Statement: Should the sale of tobacco products near schools and colleges be banned? Arguments: I. Yes, because it will reduce the chances of school students taking up smoking. II. No, because the small shopkeepers near the schools will lose their livelihood.

      Answer: A
      Q2. Statement: Should the use of mobile phones while driving a vehicle be made a punishable offence? Arguments: I. Yes, because talking on the phone while driving is one of the major causes of road accidents. II. No, because drivers need to inform their family members when they are delayed on the way.
      (A) Only argument I is strong   (B) Only argument II is strong   (C) Both arguments are strong   (D) Neither argument is strong
      Answer: A

      (A) Only argument I is strong   (B) Only argument II is strong   (C) Both arguments are strong   (D) Neither argument is strong
      Answer: A

      Answer: A

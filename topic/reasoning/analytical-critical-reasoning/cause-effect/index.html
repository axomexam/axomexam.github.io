      Cause & Effect (Assam Competitive Exams)

      Two statements I and II are given. Determine whether one is the cause of the other, or both are independent causes, or both are effects of a common cause.

      50 Questions with Answers

      Q1. I. Assam received exceptionally heavy rainfall during this year's monsoon. II. The Brahmaputra and several of its tributaries crossed the danger level in many districts. Which of the following is true?
      (A) I is the cause and II is its effect   (B) II is the cause and I is its effect   (C) Both I and II are independent causes   (D) Both I and II are effects of a common cause   (E) Both I and II are effects of independent causes
      Answer: A

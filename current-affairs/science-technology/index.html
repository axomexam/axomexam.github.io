      Science & Technology

      Space missions, ISRO and DRDO achievements, new technologies, inventions, defence technology and scientific research updates.

      Science and Technology Current Affairs Questions

      200 questions with answers and explanations in this section. Last updated 2026-10-01 by the axomexam team.

      Science and technology is a favourite of question setters because it links current events with the static science syllabus. ISRO launches, DRDO missile tests, new satellites, vaccine and health developments, and advances in artificial intelligence and computing are all fair game.

      Axomexam groups the year's science news by theme so that you can revise mission names, launch vehicles, test sites and the purpose of each development. Where a development connects to a class six to ten concept, the explanation notes the link, which helps in the general science paper as well.

      The science news that is asked most

      • ISRO missions — Chandrayaan, Aditya, Gaganyaan, Mangalyaan and satellite launches with their launch vehicles.
      • DRDO and defence technology — missile tests, radar systems and indigenous platforms.
      • Space and astronomy — telescopes, exoplanets, black holes and international space missions.
      • Health and medicine — vaccines, disease outbreaks and new treatments.
      • Information technology — artificial intelligence, semiconductors, quantum computing and cybersecurity.
      • Energy and environment technology — solar, green hydrogen and battery innovation.

      How to link current science to the static syllabus

      Most science current affairs are an application of a basic concept. A question on a satellite orbit connects to gravitation, one on a vaccine connects to immunity, and one on a new battery connects to chemistry. When you revise a piece of science news, ask which school-level chapter it belongs to. This short step turns one current affairs fact into revision for a whole topic in the general science paper.

      Revision strategy for science and technology

      • For every mission, note the agency, the launch vehicle, the purpose and the launch site.
      • Keep the full forms of the abbreviations, because examiners often ask what an acronym stands for.
      • Separate Indian developments from international ones to avoid confusion.
      • Link each development to its underlying science concept while reading the explanation.
      • Revise the list before the general science revision so the overlap is fresh.
      • Attempt the set regularly, as new missions are added through the year.

      Sample Science & Technology questions with answers

      These are a few of the questions from this section. Every question carries the correct answer and a short explanation, and the full list is read online in English or Assamese.

      1. What has the historic site where Chandrayaan-3's lander 'Vikram' touched the lunar south pole been named?
        Answer: Shiv Shakti Point
        On 23 August 2023, India became the first country in the world to land a spacecraft in the lunar south pole region.
        উত্তৰ: শিৱ শক্তি পইণ্ট (Shiv Shakti Point)
      2. What day did the Government of India officially declare 23 August as?
        Answer: National Space Day
        In memory of Chandrayaan-3's successful landing, this day is observed every year across the country to celebrate achievements in space science.
        উত্তৰ: ৰাষ্ট্ৰীয় মহাকাশ দিৱস (National Space Day)
      3. What name did the government give to the impact point of Chandrayaan-2?
        Answer: Tiranga Point
        The site of the crash landing on the lunar surface in 2019 was given this name as a symbol of effort and determination.
        উত্তৰ: তিৰংগা পইণ্ট (Tiranga Point)
      4. What was the name of Chandrayaan-3's rover and what elements' presence did it prove on the lunar surface?
        Answer: Pragyan Rover
        Through Laser-Induced Breakdown Spectroscopy (LIBS), Pragyan confirmed the presence of sulphur, aluminium, calcium and iron.
        উত্তৰ: প্ৰজ্ঞান (Pragyan Rover)
      5. What is the mission in which India plans to bring back samples of soil and rocks from the Moon to Earth?
        Answer: Chandrayaan-4 Mission (Lunar Sample Return Mission)
        In this mission approved by the Cabinet, two separate modules will dock in space and bring the samples to Earth.
        উত্তৰ: চন্দ্ৰযান-৪ অভিযান (Chandrayaan-4 / Lunar Sample Return Mission)
      6. What is India's first solar observation mission sent by ISRO to study the Sun's atmosphere and corona?
        Answer: Aditya-L1
        This satellite was successfully placed in a halo orbit at Lagrange Point 1 (L1), 1.5 million km from Earth.
        উত্তৰ: আদিত্য-এল১ (Aditya-L1)
      7. What is the name of the primary payload on Aditya-L1?
        Answer: VELC (Visible Emission Line Coronagraph)
        Built by the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, this instrument observes the heat of the Sun's corona and coronal mass ejections.
        উত্তৰ: ভেল্ক (VELC - Visible Emission Line Coronagraph)
      8. What is the satellite launched by ISRO to study the polarization of X-ray radiation coming from black holes and neutron stars?
        Answer: XPoSat (X-ray Polarimeter Satellite)
        After NASA's IXPE, India is the second country in the world to place an X-ray polarimeter observatory in space.
        উত্তৰ: এক্সপ'ছেট (XPoSat - X-ray Polarimeter Satellite)
      9. What are the two main scientific payloads on the XPoSat satellite?
        Answer: POLIX and XSPECT
        The Raman Research Institute and U R Rao Satellite Centre have together prepared these two sensitive instruments.
        উত্তৰ: পলিক্স (POLIX) আৰু এক্সপেক্ট (XSPECT)
      10. What is the dual-frequency radar satellite built under the joint collaboration of India and the American space agency NASA?
        Answer: NISAR (NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar)
        Using L-band and S-band radar, it will scan the Earth's ecosystems, ice sheets and earthquake-induced land displacement every 12 days.
        উত্তৰ: নিছাৰ (NISAR - NASA-ISRO Synthetic Aperture Radar)
      11. What are the names of the four astronauts selected for India's first human spaceflight mission 'Gaganyaan'?
        Answer: Prashant Balakrishnan Nair, Angad Pratap, Ajit Krishnan and Shubhanshu Shukla
        All four are Wing Commanders and Group Captains of the Indian Air Force and were given astronaut wings by the Prime Minister.
        উত্তৰ: প্ৰশান্ত বালাকৃষ্ণন নায়াৰ, অংগদ প্ৰতাপ, অজিত কৃষ্ণন আৰু শুভাংশু শুক্লা
      12. What is the name of the half-humanoid female robot to be sent experimentally to space before the Gaganyaan mission?
        Answer: Vyommitra
        This humanoid robot will monitor the pressure on the human body and cabin environment in space.
        উত্তৰ: ব্যোমমিত্ৰ (Vyommitra)

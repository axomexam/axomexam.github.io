      Summits & Conferences

      Major bilateral and multilateral summits — G20, BRICS, SAARC, ASEAN, UN conferences and their host cities and outcomes.

      Summits and Conferences Current Affairs Questions

      200 questions with answers and explanations in this section. Last updated 2026-10-01 by the axomexam team.

      Summits and conferences is a well-defined section built around a simple set of facts: who hosted, which countries took part, what was agreed, and where the next summit will be held. The questions are direct and the list of major summits is short, so this is an efficient topic to prepare.

      Axomexam records each summit with its host country or city, the participating group and the main outcome, and links it to the international affairs section so that the same event is not revised from two different places.

      The summits that are asked most

      • G20 and the G7 summits, with their host countries and themes.
      • BRICS, SCO and the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation meetings.
      • SAARC, BIMSTEC and ASEAN summits relevant to India and the North-East.
      • United Nations General Assembly sessions and climate conferences.
      • Bilateral summits between India and major powers.
      • Special summits such as the Voice of the Global South and the East Asia Summit.

      Host, theme and outcome are the three keys

      Almost every summit question can be answered from three facts. The host is asked as country or city, the theme or agenda is asked for the current year, and the outcome is asked as an agreement, a declaration or a joint statement. If you note these three for every major summit, the section is fully covered. When India hosts a summit, the host city and the Sherpa or chairperson are also worth noting.

      Preparation tips for summits

      • Make a table of summit name, host, year and main outcome.
      • Learn the member countries of the major groupings at a glance.
      • Note the theme of the current year for the largest summits.
      • For bilateral summits, record the two countries and the agreements signed.
      • Link each summit to the international affairs section so revision overlaps.
      • Attempt the set after major summit months, as the facts change through the year.

      Sample Summits & Conferences questions with answers

      These are a few of the questions from this section. Every question carries the correct answer and a short explanation, and the full list is read online in English or Assamese.

      1. In which country and city was the 19th G20 Summit held in November 2024?
        Answer: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
        The summit was held under the chairmanship of Brazilian President Lula da Silva with the theme 'Building a Just World and a Sustainable Planet'.
        উত্তৰ: ৰিঅ' ডি জেনেৰিঅ', ব্ৰাজিল (Rio de Janeiro, Brazil)
      2. What was the major global initiative launched under Brazil's presidency at the Rio de Janeiro G20 Summit?
        Answer: Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty
        Governments, international organisations and charitable bodies were brought together to eradicate hunger and poverty worldwide.
        উত্তৰ: ক্ষুধা আৰু দাৰিদ্ৰ্যৰ বিৰুদ্ধে গোলকীয় মিত্ৰজোঁট (Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty)
      3. Which African country has been decided to host the 20th G20 Summit in 2025?
        Answer: South Africa
        This will be the first historic G20 Summit to be held in the African continent.
        উত্তৰ: দক্ষিণ আফ্ৰিকা (South Africa)
      4. Where was the 16th BRICS Summit held in October 2024?
        Answer: Kazan, Russia
        The summit was held under the chairmanship of Russian President Vladimir Putin with the theme 'Strengthening Multilateralism for Just Global Development and Security'.
        উত্তৰ: কাজান, ৰাছিয়া (Kazan, Russia)
      5. Which important diplomatic declaration was adopted at the Kazan BRICS Summit?
        Answer: Kazan Declaration
        Member countries laid emphasis on trade in local currencies, reform of international financial institutions and free trade.
        উত্তৰ: কাজান ঘোষণাপত্ৰ (Kazan Declaration)
      6. Which five countries officially joined BRICS as full members from 1 January 2024?
        Answer: Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates (UAE)
        As a result, BRICS membership increased from five to ten countries (BRICS Plus).
        উত্তৰ: ইজিপ্ত, ইথিঅ'পিয়া, ইৰাণ, ছৌদি আৰব আৰু সংযুক্ত আৰব আমিৰাত (UAE)
      7. In which country was the 50th G7 Summit held in June 2024?
        Answer: Borgo Egnazia, Apulia, Italy
        At the meeting held under Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's chairmanship, Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi participated as a special invited guest.
        উত্তৰ: বৰ্গ' এগনাজিয়া, আপুলিয়া, ইটালী (Borgo Egnazia, Apulia, Italy)
      8. Whom did world leaders specially invite to discuss Artificial Intelligence (AI) at the Apulia G7 Summit?
        Answer: Pope Francis
        For the first time in history, a Pope attended a G7 meeting and spoke on the ethical and humane use of AI technology.
        উত্তৰ: পোপ ফ্ৰান্সিছ (Pope Francis)
      9. Which country will host the 51st G7 Summit in 2025?
        Answer: Kananaskis, Alberta, Canada
        Canada will assume the G7 presidency for 2025.
        উত্তৰ: কানানাস্কিছ, আলবাৰ্টা, কানাডা (Kananaskis, Canada)
      10. Which regional organisation representing 55 countries gained full membership at the New Delhi G20 Summit?
        Answer: African Union (AU)
        On India's proposal, the African Union was made a permanent member of the G20 on par with the European Union.
        উত্তৰ: আফ্ৰিকান ইউনিয়ন (African Union - AU)
      11. Where was the UN Climate Change Conference COP29 held in November 2024?
        Answer: Baku, Azerbaijan
        Held in Baku on the shore of the Caspian Sea, this conference is mainly known as the 'Finance COP'.
        উত্তৰ: বাকু, আজাৰবাইজান (Baku, Azerbaijan)
      12. What was the main focus of discussion at the COP29 Summit?
        Answer: New Collective Quantified Goal (NCQG) on climate finance
        Developing countries demanded new financial assistance of 1 trillion dollars in place of the old 100 billion dollar annual target for climate mitigation.
        উত্তৰ: নতুন সমষ্টিগত পৰিমাণগত লক্ষ্য (NCQG - New Collective Quantified Goal on Climate Finance)

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Are summits asked in Assam exams?

      Yes. A few questions on major summits, their hosts and outcomes appear in most papers, and the topic is easy because the list is limited.

      Which summits are most important?

      G20, BRICS, SCO, SAARC, BIMSTEC, ASEAN and the United Nations General Assembly are the most frequently asked.

      Do I need to know all member countries?

      It helps to know the members of the main groupings, as questions sometimes ask which country is not a member.

      Is the theme of the summit asked?

      For major summits such as the G20, yes. The theme of the current year is often asked directly.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about practising on axomexam

    Is axomexam free to use?

    Yes. axomexam is completely free. All mock tests, previous year solved papers and PDF notes are available without registration or payment.

    Which exams does axomexam cover?

    axomexam covers Assam state exams such as ADRE (Assam Direct Recruitment Examination), APSC, Assam Police / SLPRB, DHS and DME/DTE, as well as central exams including SSC (CGL, CHSL, GD) and Railway (RRB NTPC, Group D).

    Are the questions available in Assamese as well as English?

    Yes. Every question, answer and explanation on axomexam is bilingual, so you can practise in both Assamese and English.

    Does axomexam provide timed mock tests?

    Yes. axomexam mock tests run on a live timer and follow the real exam pattern, with instant results and answer explanations.

    Can I download PDF notes and previous year papers?

    Yes. PDF study notes, e-books and previous year solved papers can be downloaded for offline practice.

    Do I need an account to practise on axomexam?

    No. You can start practising mock tests and questions immediately without creating an account.

    What is the syllabus for the Assam Police written exam?

    The Assam Police (SLPRB) written paper generally covers Elementary Mathematics, General English, Logical Reasoning and Mental Ability, Assam's History, Geography and Culture, and General Knowledge and Current Affairs. The syllabus can change with each notification, so check the official advertisement. axomexam has a free Assam Police syllabus page and bilingual practice sets for every section.

    What is the exam pattern of ADRE 3.0 Grade IV?

    The ADRE 3.0 Grade IV examination is an objective (OMR-based) multiple-choice paper held bilingually in Assamese and English. It usually has sections on General Mathematics, General English, General Knowledge and Assam GK, Reasoning and Mental Ability, and Social Studies, followed by a document verification stage. See the ADRE syllabus page for the complete pattern.

    What is the eligibility for ADRE and Assam Police exams?

    Eligibility such as the age limit, minimum educational qualification and age relaxation for reserved categories is fixed by SLRC Assam for ADRE and by SLPRB for Assam Police in each official notification. Always confirm the exact criteria from the latest official advertisement before applying.

    What is the exam pattern of the APSC CCE?

    The APSC Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) is held in three stages: a Preliminary examination with objective papers, a Main written (descriptive) examination, and a Personality Test (interview). The exact papers and syllabus are set by the Assam Public Service Commission in its notification. axomexam's General Studies, reasoning, mathematics and English question banks help you build the foundation for both the Prelims and Mains.

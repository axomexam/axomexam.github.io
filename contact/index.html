      Contact Us

      Report a wrong answer, request a topic, contribute questions, or ask for help with your exam preparation.

      We read every message we receive and use the feedback to improve the question banks, notes and mock tests on axomexam.in. Whether you want to report a wrong answer, suggest a new topic, contribute questions or ask about study material, this page explains the best way to reach us.

      Email Support

      The fastest way to contact us is by email at axomexam@outlook.com. Please write from a valid email address so that we can reply. We usually respond within two to three working days.

      Send us a message

      This opens your email app with the details filled in. If it does not open, simply email us directly at axomexam@outlook.com.

      What to Include in Your Message

      • For a content correction: the exact page link, the question number, what you believe is wrong, and the correct answer with a short reason or source.
      • For a broken link or download problem: the page where the link appears and the name of the file or paper.
      • For a new topic request: the exam name, subject and the specific topic you would like us to add.
      • For contributing questions: the subject, topic, questions with four options, the correct option and a brief explanation.

      Response Time

      We aim to reply to every genuine message within two to three working days. Messages are handled in the order they arrive, and content corrections that include a verifiable source are usually processed first.

      Report Copyright Content

      If you believe any material on axomexam.in infringes your copyright, email us with the page link and proof of ownership. We will review the request and remove the material if required. We respect intellectual property and act on valid takedown requests promptly.

      Common Questions

      Is there a phone number or live chat? No. To keep the platform free and focused on content, support is handled by email only.

      Can I request a study plan? Yes. Tell us the exam you are preparing for and the date of the exam, and we will point you to the most relevant sections and a suggested order of study.

      Do you share or sell my details? Never. Your email is used only to reply to your query, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

      Can I contribute questions to the site? Yes. Send them with four options and a short explanation, and our team will review them before publishing.

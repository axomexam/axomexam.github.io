      Union and State Governance

      This section covers the machinery of government at the centre and in the states, including the executive, the legislature and the judiciary. It is a high-yield area because questions on constitutional offices and their powers are asked in almost every paper.

      Topics covered

      • The President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers.
      • The Governor, Chief Minister and the State Council of Ministers.
      • Parliament — the Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha and the law-making process.
      • State Legislature and the relationship between the Union and the states.
      • The Supreme Court and the High Courts, and the idea of judicial review.

      How to prepare

      Group the material office by office. For each office, learn how the person is appointed, the qualifications, the term, and the main powers and removal process. Compare the President with the Governor and the Prime Minister with the Chief Minister, because comparison-based questions are common. Revise the articles associated with each office along with the functions.

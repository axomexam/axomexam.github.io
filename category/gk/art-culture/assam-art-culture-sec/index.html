    Assam Art and Culture: study notes

    Tribal Arts and Culture of Assam

    Assam is home to many tribal communities, each with its own language, dress, festivals and arts. They form a very rich and living part of the state's culture.

    The Bodo community is one of the largest. Their language belongs to the Tibeto-Burman family. The Bodos celebrate the spring festival Bwisagu (Baishagu), perform the famous Bagurumba (butterfly) dance, and worship Bathou, whose symbol is the siju (Euphorbia) plant. The colourful dokhna is the dress of Bodo women. Their festivals and dances are full of colour and rhythm.

    The Misings live mainly on the northern bank of the Brahmaputra. Their sowing festival is Ali-ai-ligang, and their most important dance is the Gumrag. The Karbis live in the hills and plains of central Assam; their festivals include Rongker and Chomangkan, and their dance and music are performed at every festival.

    Art, Architecture & Culture of India

    Indian culture is one of the oldest and richest in the world. For competitive exams, questions are regularly asked on temple architecture, schools of painting, classical dances and music. Remember the important points below.

    Architecture: Indian architecture is broadly divided into three periods. (1) Ancient or temple architecture — Hindu temples follow three styles. The Nagara style of northern India is marked by the curvilinear (beehive-shaped) shikhara; famous examples are the Sun temple of Konark, the Lingaraja temple of Bhubaneswar and the Kandariya Mahadeva temple of Khajuraho. The Dravida style of the south has a pyramidal vimana and a large gateway called the gopuram; examples are the Brihadeshwara temple of Thanjavur, the temples of Kanchipuram and the Meenakshi temple of Madurai. The Vesara style is a mixed style of the Deccan, seen in the Chalukya temples of Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal.

    ভাৰতীয় সংস্কৃতি বিশ্বৰ আটাইতকৈ পুৰণি আৰু সমৃদ্ধ সংস্কৃতিৰ ভিতৰত অন্যতম। প্ৰতিযোগিতামূলক পৰীক্ষাসমূহত মন্দিৰ স্থাপত্য, চিত্ৰকলা শৈলী, শাস্ত্ৰীয় নৃত্য আৰু সংগীতৰ ওপৰত নিয়মিতভাৱে প্ৰশ্ন আহে। তলৰ গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ কথাবোৰ মনত ৰাখিব।

    স্থাপত্য: ভাৰতীয় স্থাপত্যক বহলভাৱে তিনিটা যুগত ভাগ কৰা হয়। (১) প্ৰাচীন বা মন্দিৰ স্থাপত্য — হিন্দু মন্দিৰ তিনিটা শৈলীৰ। উত্তৰ ভাৰতৰ নগৰা শৈলী বক্ৰাকৃতি (মৌ-চাকৰ দৰে) শিখৰৰ দ্বাৰা চিনাক্ত; বিখ্যাত উদাহৰণ হৈছে ক'ণাৰ্কৰ সূৰ্য মন্দিৰ, ভুৱনেশ্বৰৰ লিংগৰাজ মন্দিৰ আৰু খাজুৰাহোৰ কন্দৰিয়া মহাদেৱ মন্দিৰ। দক্ষিণৰ দ্ৰাবিড় শৈলীত পিৰামিড আকৃতিৰ বিমান আৰু গোপুৰম নামৰ ডাঙৰ দুৱাৰ থাকে; উদাহৰণ হৈছে থাঞ্জাভুৰৰ বৃহদীশ্বৰ মন্দিৰ, কাঞ্চীপুৰমৰ মন্দিৰ আৰু মাদুৰাইৰ মীনাক্ষী মন্দিৰ। ভেসৰা শৈলী দাক্ষিণাত্যৰ মিশ্ৰ শৈলী, যি বাদামী, আইহোল আৰু পট্টদকলৰ চালুক্য মন্দিৰত দেখা যায়। গুহা স্থাপত্য অজন্তা আৰু ইলোৰাৰ চৈত্য গৃহ আৰু বিহাৰত সৰ্বোচ্চ শিখৰত উপনীত হৈছিল; ইলোৰাৰ কৈলাস মন্দিৰ (ৰাষ্ট্ৰকূট ৰজা প্ৰথম কৃষ্ণই নিৰ্মাণ কৰা) বিশ্বৰ আটাইতকৈ ডাঙৰ শিলা-কটা মন্দিৰ।

    Assam Art and Culture: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. Which is the famous Ahom palace located at Garhgaon? গড়গাঁওত অৱস্থিত বিখ্যাত আহোম ৰাজপ্ৰাসাদ কোনটো?
      Answer: The Kareng Ghar (Gargaon Palace) is the famous Ahom palace at Garhgaon. Explanation: Kareng Ghar, also called the Gargaon Palace, is the famous Ahom palace at Garhgaon.
    • Q2. Who built the Kareng Ghar at Gargaon? গড়গাঁৱৰ কাৰেঙ ঘৰ কোনে নিৰ্মাণ কৰিছিল?
      Answer: The Kareng Ghar was originally built by Suklenmung (Garh Gayan) in 1540 and rebuilt by later kings. Explanation: The Kareng Ghar at Gargaon was originally built by Suklenmung (Garh Gayan) in 1540 and was later rebuilt by subsequent Ahom kings.
    • Q3. Which Ahom king shifted the capital from Gargaon to Rangpur? গড়গাঁওৰ পৰা ৰংপুৰলৈ ৰাজধানী স্থানান্তৰ কৰা আহোম ৰজা কোন?
      Answer: Rudra Singha shifted the capital from Gargaon to Rangpur in the early 18th century. Explanation: Swargadeo Rudra Singha shifted the Ahom capital from Gargaon to Rangpur in the early 18th century.
    • Q4. Which is the famous Ahom palace at Rangpur? ৰংপুৰত অৱস্থিত বিখ্যাত আহোম ৰাজপ্ৰাসাদ কোনটো?
      Answer: The Talatal Ghar at Rangpur is the famous Ahom palace built by Swargadeo Rudra Singha. Explanation: The Talatal Ghar, built by Swargadeo Rudra Singha, is the famous Ahom palace located at Rangpur.

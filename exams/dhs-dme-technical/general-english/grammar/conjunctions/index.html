    Conjunctions: study notes

    Read this before you attempt the questions. It is written for the DHS & DME Technical Grade III paper.

    What a conjunction does

    A conjunction is a word that joins two words, two phrases or two clauses. It is the hinge of the sentence: without it the two parts either stand apart or collide. In the DHS and DME Technical Grade III paper the conjunction is not asked as a definition but as a choice between four linkers, and the choice depends on the relation between the two ideas. The candidate has to decide whether the second idea adds, contrasts, gives a reason, gives a result, states a condition or states a concession, and then pick the linker that carries that relation.

    There are three families. Coordinating conjunctions join two parts of equal grammatical rank, and the common ones are remembered by the group for, and, nor, but, or, yet and so. Subordinating conjunctions join a dependent clause to a main clause, and they carry the meaning of time, cause, condition, concession, purpose or result. Correlative conjunctions work in pairs, such as either ... or, neither ... nor, both ... and, not only ... but also, and no sooner ... than. A fourth group, the conjunctive adverbs such as however, therefore, moreover and nevertheless, looks like a conjunction but behaves like an adverb and needs a semicolon or a full stop before it.

    The coordinating conjunctions are the easiest to master because there are only seven. And adds; but contrasts; or offers an alternative; nor adds a second negative; for gives a reason in a formal style; yet contrasts with a sense of surprise; so gives a result. A useful memory aid is the word FANBOYS, formed from the first letters of for, and, nor, but, or, yet and so. Each of these can join two words, two phrases or two independent clauses, and when it joins two independent clauses a comma usually comes before it.

    The subordinating conjunctions are larger in number and are grouped by meaning. Time: when, while, before, after, until, till, since, as soon as, whenever. Cause: because, since, as, now that. Condition: if, unless, provided that, in case, on condition that. Concession: although, though, even though, whereas, while. Purpose: so that, in order that, lest. Result: so ... that, such ... that. Comparison: than, as ... as. A candidate who can name the group of a linker can reject the options that belong to another group without reading the whole sentence.

    Correlative conjunctions must be balanced. Either goes with or, neither with nor, both with and, not only with but also, and no sooner with than. The parts joined must be parallel: either the book or the pen, not either the book or pen. When not only comes at the beginning of a sentence for emphasis, the subject and auxiliary are inverted: Not only did he sing, but he also danced. When neither ... nor joins two subjects, the verb agrees with the nearer subject: Neither the teacher nor the students were present.

    Two fixed patterns cause most of the errors. No sooner is followed by than, and the clause after no sooner takes the past perfect with inversion: No sooner had he arrived than it began to rain. Hardly and scarcely are followed by when: Hardly had he arrived when it began to rain. The word than never follows hardly or scarcely, and when never follows no sooner. These three patterns are asked so often that they are worth writing on a card.

    A second fixed pattern is the pair though ... yet and although ... yet. In careful English we do not use though with but: Although he was tired, but he continued is wrong. The correct forms are Although he was tired, he continued, or He was tired, but he continued. The same applies to because and so: Because it rained, so the match was cancelled is wrong; use either Because it rained, the match was cancelled, or It rained, so the match was cancelled.

    Lest is a formal conjunction that already carries a negative sense, so it is followed by should and never by not: Work hard lest you should fail. The phrase for fear that works in the same way. Unless already means if not, so unless you do not go is a double negative; the correct form is unless you go. Since can mean because or from the time that, and the context decides which; a sentence such as Since he left, we have heard nothing uses the time sense, while Since he is ill, he will not come uses the cause sense.

    Conjunctive adverbs are often mistaken for conjunctions. However, therefore, moreover, nevertheless, consequently and otherwise join two independent clauses, but they do not join them grammatically; they need a semicolon or a full stop before them, and a comma after them. He was tired; however, he continued is correct. He was tired, however he continued is wrong because a comma alone cannot join two independent clauses. This is a favourite of the punctuation-and-correction question.

    The conjunction must also match the logic of the sentence. A cause needs because or since, not but. A contrast needs but, yet, although or whereas, not so. A result needs so or therefore, not because. A condition needs if or unless, not although. In the examination the four options often belong to four different groups, and the logic of the two clauses alone is enough to reject three of them. Reading the relation first, and the linker second, is the fastest safe method.

    Rules to remember

    • Coordinating conjunctions join equals: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so.
    • Subordinating conjunctions join a dependent clause to a main clause and carry time, cause, condition, concession, purpose or result.
    • Correlative pairs must be balanced: either ... or, neither ... nor, both ... and, not only ... but also, no sooner ... than.
    • No sooner takes than; hardly and scarcely take when.
    • Do not use though with but, or because with so.
    • Lest takes should, not not.
    • Conjunctive adverbs need a semicolon or full stop before them.
    • Match the linker to the relation between the clauses.

    Worked examples

    He was ill, ___ he attended the class. The relation is contrast, so the linker is but or yet. Because would give a reason, so would give a result, and and would add, none of which fits the surprise of attending while ill.

    ___ he had finished his work, he went home. The relation is time, and the action in the first clause is complete before the second, so after is correct. Before would reverse the order, while would overlap the two actions, and unless would make the first clause a condition.

    No sooner ___ the bell rang than the students left. The correct form is had the bell rung, because no sooner takes the past perfect with inversion. The word than is already in the sentence and must not be replaced by when. The full sentence is No sooner had the bell rung than the students left.

    She is not only a good singer ___ a good dancer. The pair is not only ... but also, so the blank takes but also. The word and alone would break the pair, and or would change the meaning. The two qualities are both true, so the additive pair is required.

    Where marks disappear

    • Using though with but, or because with so.
    • Writing no sooner ... when, or hardly ... than.
    • Breaking a correlative pair, as in not only ... and.
    • Using lest ... not.
    • Using unless with another negative.
    • Joining two independent clauses with only a comma and however.
    • Choosing a linker from the wrong meaning group.
    Exam tip: Before you look at the options, name the relation between the two clauses in one word: addition, contrast, reason, result, condition, concession, time or purpose. Then choose the linker from that group. The relation is in the sentence; the linker is only its label.

    সমপদীয় সংযোগ সাতটাই, গতিকে আয়ত্ত কৰা সহজ। And যোগ কৰে; but বিৰোধ কৰে; or বিকল্প দিয়ে; nor দ্বিতীয় ঋণাত্মক যোগ কৰে; for আনুষ্ঠানিক ৰীতিত কাৰণ দিয়ে; yet আচৰিত ভাৱে বিৰোধ কৰে; so ফল দিয়ে। FANBOYS শব্দটো মনত ৰখাৰ সহায়, যি for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so ৰ প্ৰথম আখৰেৰে গঠিত। ইহঁতৰ প্ৰতিটোৱে দুটা শব্দ, দুটা বাক্যাংশ বা দুটা স্বাধীন উপবাক্য জোড়ে, আৰু দুটা স্বাধীন উপবাক্য জোড়িলে সাধাৰণতে তাৰ আগত কমা আহে।

    Lest এটা আনুষ্ঠানিক সংযোগ যিয়ে ইতিমধ্যে ঋণাত্মক ভাৱ বহন কৰে, গতিকে তাৰ পিছত should আহে, not কেতিয়াও নাহে: Work hard lest you should fail। for fear that বাক্যাংশও একেই ধৰণে চলে। Unless ৰ অৰ্থ ইতিমধ্যে if not, গতিকে unless you do not go দ্বৈত ঋণাত্মক; শুদ্ধ ৰূপ unless you go। Since ৰ অৰ্থ because বা from the time that হ'ব পাৰে, আৰু প্ৰসংগে নিৰ্ণয় কৰে; Since he left, we have heard nothing ত সময়ৰ অৰ্থ, আনহাতে Since he is ill, he will not come ত কাৰণৰ অৰ্থ।

    Conjunctive adverb ক প্ৰায়ে সংযোগ বুলি ভুল কৰা হয়। However, therefore, moreover, nevertheless, consequently, otherwise এ দুটা স্বাধীন উপবাক্য জোড়ে, কিন্তু ব্যাকৰণিকভাৱে জোড়া নিদিয়ে; তাৰ আগত semicolon বা পূৰ্ণবিৰাম লাগে, আৰু পিছত কমা লাগে। He was tired; however, he continued শুদ্ধ। He was tired, however he continued ভুল, কাৰণ কেৱল কমাৰে দুটা স্বাধীন উপবাক্য জোড়া নিদিয়ে। এইটো যতি-চিহ্ন আৰু শুধৰণিৰ প্ৰশ্নৰ প্ৰিয়।

    সংযোগে বাক্যৰ যুক্তিৰ সৈতেও মিলিব লাগে। কাৰণৰ বাবে because বা since, but নহয়। বিৰোধৰ বাবে but, yet, although বা whereas, so নহয়। ফলৰ বাবে so বা therefore, because নহয়। চৰ্তৰ বাবে if বা unless, although নহয়। পৰীক্ষাত চাৰিটা বিকল্প প্ৰায়ে চাৰিটা বেলেগ গোটৰ হয়, আৰু দুয়ো উপবাক্যৰ যুক্তিয়েই তিনিটা নাকচ কৰিবলৈ যথেষ্ট। প্ৰথমে সম্পৰ্ক আৰু দ্বিতীয়তে linker পঢ়াই আটাইতকৈ দ্ৰুত নিৰাপদ পদ্ধতি।

    একে শব্দই সংযোগ আৰু পূৰ্বসৰ্গ দুয়োটা হ'ব পাৰে, আৰু পৰীক্ষাই এই পাৰ্থক্যটো সোধে। Before, after, since, till, until আৰু as ৰ পিছত উপবাক্য থাকিলে সেয়া সংযোগ, কিন্তু বিশেষ্য বা বাক্যাংশ থাকিলে পূৰ্বসৰ্গ: He left before the bell rang (সংযোগ), He left before the bell (পূৰ্বসৰ্গ)। But সাধাৰণতে সংযোগ, কিন্তু no one but him ত সেয়া পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ দৰে কাম কৰে। গতিকে শব্দটো নহয়, তাৰ পিছত কি আহিছে সেয়াই ভূমিকা নিৰ্ণয় কৰে।

    তুলনাৰ যোৰতো সাৱধানতা লাগে। সমতাৰ বাবে as ... as, অসমতাৰ বাবে not so ... as: She is as tall as her sister, She is not so tall as her sister। so ... that আৰু such ... that ৰ পাৰ্থক্য মন কৰক: so বিশেষণ বা ক্ৰিয়া-বিশেষণৰ লগত, such বিশেষ্যৰ লগত: He was so tired that he slept, It was such a tiring day that he slept। such a ৰ ক্ৰম such + a + বিশেষণ + বিশেষ্য, আৰু so ৰ ক্ৰম so + বিশেষণ + a + বিশেষ্য: so tiring a day। এই ক্ৰমবোৰ ভুল কৰাটো সাধাৰণ, আৰু প্ৰতিটোৱেই এটা নম্বৰ।

    মনত ৰখাৰ নিয়ম

    • সমপদীয় সংযোগে সমান জোড়ে: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so।
    • অধীনতাবাচক সংযোগে আশ্ৰিত উপবাক্যক মূল উপবাক্যৰ লগত জোড়ে আৰু সময়, কাৰণ, চৰ্ত, ছাৰ, উদ্দেশ্য বা ফল বহন কৰে।
    • যুগল যোৰ ভাৰসাম্যত থাকিব: either ... or, neither ... nor, both ... and, not only ... but also, no sooner ... than।
    • No sooner ৰ লগত than; hardly আৰু scarcely ৰ লগত when।
    • though ৰ লগত but, বা because ৰ লগত so নিদিব।
    • Lest ৰ লগত should, not নহয়।
    • Conjunctive adverb ৰ আগত semicolon বা পূৰ্ণবিৰাম লাগে।
    • উপবাক্যৰ সম্পৰ্ক অনুসৰি linker বাছক।

    সমাধান কৰা উদাহৰণ

    He was ill, ___ he attended the class। সম্পৰ্ক বিৰোধ, গতিকে linker but বা yet। Because এ কাৰণ দিব, so এ ফল দিব, and এ যোগ কৰিব, কিন্তু অসুস্থ হৈও শ্ৰেণীত উপস্থিত থকাৰ আচৰিত ভাৱত এটাও খাপ নাখায়।

    ___ he had finished his work, he went home। সম্পৰ্ক সময়, আৰু প্ৰথম উপবাক্যৰ কাম দ্বিতীয়ৰ আগতে সম্পূৰ্ণ, গতিকে after শুদ্ধ। Before এ ক্ৰম ওলোটা কৰিব, while এ দুয়ো কাম একেলগে দেখুৱাব, আৰু unless এ প্ৰথম উপবাক্যক চৰ্ত কৰিব।

    No sooner ___ the bell rang than the students left। শুদ্ধ ৰূপ had the bell rung, কাৰণ no sooner ৰ লগত ওলোটা সহিত past perfect আহে। than ইতিমধ্যে বাক্যত আছে আৰু when ৰে সলাব নালাগে। সম্পূৰ্ণ বাক্য No sooner had the bell rung than the students left।

    She is not only a good singer ___ a good dancer। যোৰ not only ... but also, গতিকে ৰিক্ত স্থানত but also। কেৱল and এ যোৰ ভাঙিব, or এ অৰ্থ সলাব। দুয়ো গুণ সত্য, গতিকে যোগাত্মক যোৰ আৱশ্যক।

    য'ত নম্বৰ হেৰায়

    • though ৰ লগত but, বা because ৰ লগত so ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।

    • unless ৰ লগত আন ঋণাত্মক ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
    • দুটা স্বাধীন উপবাক্য কেৱল কমা আৰু however ৰে জোড়া।
    • ভুল অৰ্থ-গোটৰ linker বাছনি কৰা।
    পৰীক্ষাৰ টিপ: বিকল্প চোৱাৰ আগতে দুয়ো উপবাক্যৰ সম্পৰ্ক এটা শব্দত কওক: যোগ, বিৰোধ, কাৰণ, ফল, চৰ্ত, ছাৰ, সময় বা উদ্দেশ্য। তাৰ পিছত সেই গোটৰ পৰা linker বাছক। সম্পৰ্ক বাক্যত থাকে; linker মাত্ৰ তাৰ লেবেল। এই এটা অভ্যাসে চাৰিটা বিকল্পৰ পৰা তিনিটা লগে লগে আঁতৰাই দিয়ে আৰু সময় বচায়।

    How to prepare Static GK

    Static general knowledge is a memory section: awards, books, days, sports, inventions, organisations, space and defence. Eight topics and nearly fourteen hundred questions, all of them short facts that reward a monthly revision sheet.

    This page holds 8 topics and 1,377 questions. The heaviest are Awards & Honours (181), Space & Defence (180), Books & Authors (174); start with those, because they also carry the most repeated question types. The shortest topics — Organizations & Institutions (168) and Miscellaneous Static GK (168) — finish quickly on revision days.

    Order that works

    • Keep one sheet per topic: awards, books, days, sports, inventions, space, defence.
    • Revise the sheets monthly; static facts fade faster than concepts.
    • Learn who gives each award and in which field, not only the latest winner.

    Where marks are lost

    • Learning the latest winners without the award's field or the body that gives it.
    • Skipping defence and space as specialised; both are one-line questions.

    স্থিৰ সাধাৰণ জ্ঞান স্মৃতিভিত্তিক অংশ: বঁটা, কিতাপ, দিৱস, ক্ৰীড়া, আৱিষ্কাৰ, সংস্থা, মহাকাশ আৰু প্ৰতিৰক্ষা। আঠটা বিষয় আৰু প্ৰায় চৈধ্যশ প্ৰশ্ন, আটাইবোৰ চুটি তথ্য — মাহেকীয়া পুনৰাবৃত্তিৰ পত্ৰই ফল দিয়ে।

    এই পৃষ্ঠাত 8 টা বিষয় আৰু 1,377 টা প্ৰশ্ন আছে। আটাইতকৈ ডাঙৰবোৰ — বঁটা আৰু সন্মান (181), মহাকাশ আৰু প্ৰতিৰক্ষা (180), কিতাপ আৰু লেখক (174) — ইয়াৰপৰা আৰম্ভ কৰা, কাৰণ ইয়াতেই সৰ্বাধিক পুনৰাবৃত্তি হোৱা প্ৰশ্ন আহে। আটাইতকৈ চুটি বিষয়বোৰ — সংগঠন আৰু প্ৰতিষ্ঠান (168) আৰু বিবিধ স্থিৰ সাধাৰণ জ্ঞান (168) — পুনৰাবৃত্তিৰ দিনত সোনকালে শেষ হয়।

    কামৰ ক্ৰম

    • প্ৰতিটো বিষয়ৰ এপাত ৰাখা: বঁটা, কিতাপ, দিৱস, ক্ৰীড়া, আৱিষ্কাৰ, মহাকাশ, প্ৰতিৰক্ষা।

    • কোনে কোন বঁটা কোন ক্ষেত্ৰত দিয়ে শিকা, কেৱল শেহতীয়া বিজয়ীজন নহয়।

    Agriculture, Crops & Tea Economy of Assam

    Assam is essentially an agricultural state. A very large part of the population depends directly on farming, and agriculture is the backbone of the state's economy. The fertile alluvial soil, the warm humid climate and the monsoon rain make the two valleys of Assam natural granaries.

    Rice is the principal food crop and the staple food of the people. Assam grows three crops of paddy in a year: 1. Ahu (autumn paddy): sown in February–March with the help of pre-monsoon showers and harvested in June–July. 2. Sali (winter paddy): the main crop of Assam. It is sown in June–July with the beginning of the monsoon and harvested in November–December. 3.

    Colonial Economy: Tea, Immigration & the Line System

    Under British rule Assam's economy was transformed completely. The most dramatic change came with tea. The British had long searched for a source of tea outside China. It was Robert Bruce who, in 1823, found wild tea plants growing in the Singpho country of Upper Assam. After experiments, the tea industry was launched commercially: the first tea company, the Assam Tea Company, was formed in 1839, the first consignment of Assam tea reached London in 1838, and soon tea gardens spread over the valley.

    The tea industry brought a new economy and a new society to Assam: - Huge areas of forest and wasteland were cleared for tea gardens. - A very large number of labourers were brought from outside Assam (from Chotanagpur, Bihar, Odisha and other areas) under contract to work in the gardens.

    ব্ৰিটিছ শাসনত অসমৰ অৰ্থনীতি সম্পূৰ্ণৰূপে সলনি হৈছিল। আটাইতকৈ নাটকীয় পৰিৱৰ্তন আহিছিল চাহৰ জৰিয়তে। চীনৰ বাহিৰত চাহৰ উৎস বিচাৰি ব্ৰিটিছে দীৰ্ঘদিন ধৰি চেষ্টা কৰিছিল। ১৮২৩ চনত ৰবাৰ্ট ব্ৰুচে ওপৰা অসমৰ সিংফৌ অঞ্চলত বনৰীয়া চাহ-গছ বিচাৰি পাইছিল। পৰীক্ষা-নিৰীক্ষাৰ পিছত বাণিজ্যিকভাৱে চাহ উদ্যোগ আৰম্ভ হৈছিল: ১৮৩৯ চনত প্ৰথম চাহ কোম্পানী অসম কোম্পানী গঠন হয়, ১৮৩৮ চনত অসমৰ চাহৰ প্ৰথম খিনি লণ্ডনলৈ গৈছিল আৰু সোনকালেই উপত্যকাত চাহ-বাগিছা বিস্তৃত হৈছিল।

    চাহ উদ্যোগে অসমলৈ নতুন অৰ্থনীতি আৰু নতুন সমাজ লৈ আনিছিল: - চাহ-বাগিছাৰ বাবে বিপুল বনাঞ্চল আৰু অনাবাদি মাটি পৰিষ্কাৰ কৰা হৈছিল। - বাগিছাত কামৰ বাবে অসমৰ বাহিৰৰ পৰা (ছোটনাগপুৰ, বিহাৰ, ওড়িশা আদি অঞ্চলৰ পৰা) চুক্তিৰে বৃহৎ সংখ্যক শ্ৰমিক অনা হৈছিল।

    Static GK: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. The Nobel Prizes were established by the will of ন'বেল বঁটা কাৰ ইচ্ছাপত্ৰৰ দ্বাৰা প্ৰতিষ্ঠিত হৈছিল
      (A) Albert Einstein আলবাৰ্ট আইনষ্টাইন   (B) Marie Curie মেৰী কুৰি   (C) Thomas Edison টমাছ এডিছন   (D) Alfred Nobel আলফ্ৰেড ন'বেল
      Answer: Alfred Nobel Explanation: The Swedish inventor left his fortune for the prizes, which is why he is named. The others were scientists.
    • Q2. Alfred Nobel is best known for inventing আলফ্ৰেড ন'বেল ইয়াৰ উদ্ভাৱনৰ বাবে বিখ্যাত
      (A) the telephone টেলিফোন   (B) dynamite ডাইনামাইট   (C) the telescope দূৰবীক্ষণ   (D) the steam engine ভাপ ইঞ্জিন
      Answer: dynamite Explanation: His explosive made him wealthy enough to fund the prizes, which is why dynamite is named. The others had other inventors.
    • Q3. The Nobel Prizes were first awarded in ন'বেল বঁটা প্ৰথমবাৰ প্ৰদান কৰা হৈছিল
      (A) 1896 ১৮৯৬   (B) 1901 ১৯০১   (C) 1913 ১৯১৩   (D) 1929 ১৯২৯
      Answer: 1901 Explanation: The first awards followed Nobel's death, which is why the year is named. 1896 is the year he died.
    • Q4. The original Nobel Prizes were awarded in how many fields মূল ন'বেল বঁটা কিমান ক্ষেত্ৰত প্ৰদান কৰা হৈছিল
      (A) Five পাঁচ   (B) Four চাৰি   (C) Six ছয়   (D) Seven সাত
      Answer: Five Explanation: Physics, chemistry, medicine, literature and peace made five, which is why that count is named. Economics came later.

    How to prepare Static GK

    • Attempt each topic fully before checking the answers.
    • Note the rule behind every question you get wrong, not just the answer.
    • Return to the same topic after a few days to confirm retention.
    • Take the matching DHS & DME Technical Grade III mock test once the group is finished.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many questions are under Static GK?

    Static GK holds 1,382 bilingual questions across 8 topics, part of the General Knowledge & Current Affairs section of the DHS & DME Technical Grade III book.

    Which topics are inside Static GK?

    The topics are Awards & Honours, Books & Authors, Important Days & Dates, Sports & Games, Inventions & Discoveries, Organizations & Institutions, Space & Defence, Miscellaneous Static GK.

    Are these questions bilingual?

    Yes. Every question, option and explanation is given in both Assamese and English, so the same idea is reinforced in either language.

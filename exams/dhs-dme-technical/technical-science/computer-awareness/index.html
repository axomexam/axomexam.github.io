    How to prepare Computer Awareness

    Computer awareness is six topics of fixed material: fundamentals, hardware and software, MS Office, networking and the internet, cyber security, and DBMS with number systems. Three hundred questions, and almost all of them are learnable in a week.

    This page holds 6 topics and 300 questions. The heaviest are Computer Fundamentals (50), Hardware & Software (50), MS Office Suite (50); start with those, because they also carry the most repeated question types. The shortest topics — Cyber Security (50) and DBMS & Number System (50) — finish quickly on revision days.

    Order that works

    • Learn shortcuts, file extensions and storage units; they are fixed, easy marks.
    • For networking, learn the difference between LAN, WAN and the common protocols.
    • For cyber security, learn the common attacks and one defence for each.

    Where marks are lost

    • Confusing RAM with ROM, or a bit with a byte.
    • Leaving cyber security out because it looks like news rather than syllabus.

    Assam is essentially an agricultural state. A very large part of the population depends directly on farming, and agriculture is the backbone of the state's economy. The fertile alluvial soil, the warm humid climate and the monsoon rain make the two valleys of Assam natural granaries.

    Rice is the principal food crop and the staple food of the people. Assam grows three crops of paddy in a year: 1. Ahu (autumn paddy): sown in February–March with the help of pre-monsoon showers and harvested in June–July. 2. Sali (winter paddy): the main crop of Assam.     Colonial Economy: Tea, Immigration & the Line System

    Under British rule Assam's economy was transformed completely. The most dramatic change came with tea. The British had long searched for a source of tea outside China. It was Robert Bruce who, in 1823, found wild tea plants growing in the Singpho country of Upper Assam. After experiments, the tea industry was launched commercially: the first tea company, the Assam Tea Company, was formed in 1839, the first consignment of Assam tea reached London in 1838, and soon tea gardens spread over the valley.

    The tea industry brought a new economy and a new society to Assam: - Huge areas of forest and wasteland were cleared for tea gardens. - A very large number of labourers were brought from outside Assam (from Chotanagpur, Bihar, Odisha and other areas) under contract to work in the gardens.

    ব্ৰিটিছ শাসনত অসমৰ অৰ্থনীতি সম্পূৰ্ণৰূপে সলনি হৈছিল। আটাইতকৈ নাটকীয় পৰিৱৰ্তন আহিছিল চাহৰ জৰিয়তে। চীনৰ বাহিৰত চাহৰ উৎস বিচাৰি ব্ৰিটিছে দীৰ্ঘদিন ধৰি চেষ্টা কৰিছিল। ১৮২৩ চনত ৰবাৰ্ট ব্ৰুচে ওপৰা অসমৰ সিংফৌ অঞ্চলত বনৰীয়া চাহ-গছ বিচাৰি পাইছিল। পৰীক্ষা-নিৰীক্ষাৰ পিছত বাণিজ্যিকভাৱে চাহ উদ্যোগ আৰম্ভ হৈছিল: ১৮৩৯ চনত প্ৰথম চাহ কোম্পানী অসম কোম্পানী গঠন হয়, ১৮৩৮ চনত অসমৰ চাহৰ প্ৰথম খিনি লণ্ডনলৈ গৈছিল আৰু সোনকালেই উপত্যকাত চাহ-বাগিছা বিস্তৃত হৈছিল।

    চাহ উদ্যোগে অসমলৈ নতুন অৰ্থনীতি আৰু নতুন সমাজ লৈ আনিছিল: - চাহ-বাগিছাৰ বাবে বিপুল বনাঞ্চল আৰু অনাবাদি মাটি পৰিষ্কাৰ কৰা হৈছিল। - বাগিছাত কামৰ বাবে অসমৰ বাহিৰৰ পৰা (ছোটনাগপুৰ, বিহাৰ, ওড়িশা আদি অঞ্চলৰ পৰা) চুক্তিৰে বৃহৎ সংখ্যক শ্ৰমিক অনা হৈছিল।

    Computer Awareness: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. The part of a computer that performs arithmetic and logical operations is the কম্পিউটাৰৰ যি অংশই গাণিতিক আৰু যুক্তিমূলক কাম কৰে তাক কি বোলা হয়
      (A) control unit নিয়ন্ত্ৰণ ইউনিট   (B) memory unit স্মৃতি ইউনিট   (C) input unit ইনপুট ইউনিট   (D) ALU
      Answer: ALU Explanation: The arithmetic logic unit carries out addition, subtraction and comparisons. The control unit only directs traffic between the parts.
    • Q2. Which generation of computers used integrated circuits? কম্পিউটাৰৰ কোন প্ৰজন্মই সমন্বিত বৰ্তনী ব্যৱহাৰ কৰিছিল
      (A) second generation দ্বিতীয় প্ৰজন্ম   (B) fifth generation পঞ্চম প্ৰজন্ম   (C) third generation তৃতীয় প্ৰজন্ম   (D) first generation প্ৰথম প্ৰজন্ম
      Answer: third generation Explanation: Third generation machines replaced transistors with integrated circuits. The first used valves and the second used discrete transistors.
    • Q3. One kilobyte is equal to এক কিল'বাইট কিৰ সমান
      (A) 512 bytes ৫১২ বাইট   (B) 1024 bytes ১০২৪ বাইট   (C) 1000 bytes ১০০০ বাইট   (D) 1024 bits ১০২৪ বিট
      Answer: 1024 bytes Explanation: Memory is counted in powers of two, so a kilobyte is two to the tenth, that is 1024 bytes. Decimal 1000 is used only by disk makers.
    • Q4. The brain of a computer is the কম্পিউটাৰৰ মগজু হ'ল
      (A) CPU   (B) monitor মনিটৰ   (C) keyboard কীবৰ্ড   (D) hard disk হাৰ্ড ডিস্ক
      Answer: CPU Explanation: The central processing unit fetches, decodes and executes every instruction, so it is called the brain. The others are input or storage devices.

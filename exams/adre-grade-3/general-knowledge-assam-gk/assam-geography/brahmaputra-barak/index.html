    Brahmaputra & Barak Rivers

    The Brahmaputra and the Barak are the two great river systems of Assam. The Brahmaputra rises in Tibet as the Tsangpo, enters Assam in the north-east and flows through the whole length of the valley before entering Bangladesh.

    The Brahmaputra is called the Sorrow of Assam because of the floods it causes every year, and its major north-bank tributaries include the Subansiri and the Manas, while the Dhansiri and the Kopili flow from the south bank. The Barak rises in the hills of Manipur and flows through the Barak valley.

    • The Brahmaputra is known as the Tsangpo in Tibet.
    • The Subansiri and the Manas are north-bank tributaries.
    • The Barak flows through the Barak valley of Assam.
    • The Brahmaputra is called the Sorrow of Assam because of floods.

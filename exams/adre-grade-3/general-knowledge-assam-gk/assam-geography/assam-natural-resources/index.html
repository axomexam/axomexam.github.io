    Natural Resources

    Assam is rich in natural resources. It is the largest tea producing state of India, and the tea gardens of the Brahmaputra and Barak valleys are the backbone of its rural economy.

    Assam also produces petroleum and natural gas, and Digboi in upper Assam is one of the oldest oil fields in the world. Coal, limestone and plywood are other important resources of the state.

    • Assam is the largest tea producing state in India.
    • Digboi is one of the oldest oil fields in the world.
    • Coal and limestone are important minerals of Assam.
    • Tea gardens are the backbone of the rural economy.

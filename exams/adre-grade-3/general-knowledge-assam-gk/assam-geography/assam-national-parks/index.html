    National Parks & Sanctuaries

    Assam has several national parks and wildlife sanctuaries that protect some of the richest biodiversity in India. Kaziranga National Park is famous for the one-horned rhinoceros and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

    Manas National Park is known for the golden langur and the pygmy hog and is both a tiger reserve and a biosphere reserve. Nameri, Orang, Dibru-Saikhowa and Raimona are the other national parks of the state.

    • Kaziranga is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

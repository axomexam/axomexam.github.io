    Q1. Bohag Bihu is celebrated over how many days traditionally?
    ব'হাগ বিহু পৰম্পৰাগতভাৱে কিমান দিন ধৰি পালন কৰা হয়?
    (A) Nine days ন দিন
    (B) Seven days সাত দিন
    (C) Five days পাঁচ দিন
    (D) Three days তিনি দিন
    Answer: B) Seven days
    B) সাত দিন
    Explanation: Bohag Bihu is a seven-day festival beginning with Goru Bihu and ending with Sera Bihu.
    ব'হাগ বিহু সাত দিনীয়া উৎসৱ, গৰু বিহুৰ পৰা আৰম্ভ হৈ ছেৰা বিহুৰে শেষ হয়।

    Folk Culture, Bihu & Satriya

    Bihu is the chief festival of Assam and it is celebrated three times a year: Bohag Bihu in mid-April marks the Assamese new year, Kati Bihu in October is a quiet festival of the lamp, and Magh Bihu in January is the harvest festival of feasting.

    The Satriya culture of Assam grew out of the Vaishnava movement of Srimanta Sankardeva and Madhavdeva. Sattras are monastic institutions, and the Sattriya dance that developed there is recognised as one of the classical dances of India.

    • Bohag Bihu is celebrated in mid-April.
    • Magh Bihu is the harvest festival.
    • Srimanta Sankardeva founded the Vaishnava movement in Assam.
    • Sattriya is a classical dance form of India.

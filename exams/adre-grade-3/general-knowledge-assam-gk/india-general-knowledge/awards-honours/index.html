    Awards & Honours

    The highest civilian award of India is the Bharat Ratna, followed by the Padma Vibhushan, the Padma Bhushan and the Padma Shri. The Param Vir Chakra is the highest military decoration of the country.

    Among the literary and cultural awards, the Jnanpith Award is given for outstanding contribution to Indian literature and the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award for performing arts. Assam's Bhupen Hazarika received the Bharat Ratna and the Dadasaheb Phalke Award.

    • Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award of India.
    • Param Vir Chakra is the highest military decoration.
    • Jnanpith Award is given for literature.
    • Bhupen Hazarika received the Bharat Ratna.

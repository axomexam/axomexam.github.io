    Rivers, Mountains & States

    India is a land of great rivers and mountain ranges. The Ganga is the longest river of the country and it rises from the Gangotri glacier, while the Brahmaputra and the Indus are the other two great Himalayan rivers.

    The Himalayas in the north are the highest mountain range in the world, and the Western Ghats run along the western coast. India has twenty-eight states and eight union territories, and Assam is a state in the north-east.

    • The Ganga is the longest river of India.

    Assam & India Current Affairs

    Current affairs questions in the ADRE Grade III paper are drawn from the events of roughly the last six to twelve months. State events such as new schemes, appointments and infrastructure projects in Assam are asked alongside national developments.

    The reliable method is to keep a monthly diary of important events and revise it before the examination. Do not try to memorise every news item; note only what is repeated in the standard current affairs material.

    • Keep a monthly diary of important events.
    • Focus on the last six to twelve months.
    • Assam state events are asked along with national events.
    • Revise the diary once a week.

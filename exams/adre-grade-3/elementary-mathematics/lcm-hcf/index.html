    Q1. The HCF of 36 and 48 is —
    36 আৰু 48 ৰ গ.সা.উ. —
    (A) 12 ১২
    (B) 18 ১৮
    (C) 24 ২৪
    (D) 6 ৬
    Answer: A) 12
    A) ১২
    Explanation: 36 = 2^2 x 3^2 and 48 = 2^4 x 3, so HCF = 2^2 x 3 = 12.
    36 = 2^2 x 3^2 আৰু 48 = 2^4 x 3, গতিকে গ.সা.উ. = 2^2 x 3 = 12।
    Q2. The LCM of 15, 20 and 30 is —
    15, 20 আৰু 30 ৰ ল.সা.গু. —
    (A) 120 ১২০
    (B) 90 ৯০
    (C) 60 ৬০
    (D) 30 ৩০
    Answer: C) 60
    C) ৬০
    Explanation: 15 = 3x5, 20 = 2^2x5, 30 = 2x3x5, so LCM = 2^2 x 3 x 5 = 60.
    15 = 3x5, 20 = 2^2x5, 30 = 2x3x5, গতিকে ল.সা.গু. = 2^2 x 3 x 5 = 60।

    (A) the product of the numbers সংখ্যা দুটাৰ গুণফল

    (C) the sum of the numbers সংখ্যা দুটাৰ যোগফল
    (D) the difference of the numbers সংখ্যা দুটাৰ পাৰ্থক্য
    Answer: A) the product of the numbers
    A) সংখ্যা দুটাৰ গুণফল
    Explanation: For any two numbers, HCF x LCM = product of the numbers.

    Q12. The smallest square number that is divisible by 4, 6 and 10 is —
    4, 6 আৰু 10 ৰে বিভাজ্য আটাইতকৈ সৰু বৰ্গ সংখ্যা —
    (A) 120 ১২০

    Answer: B) 900

    4, 6, 10 ৰ ল.সা.গু. = 60 = 2^2 x 3 x 5; বৰ্গ কৰিবলৈ 3 x 5 = 15 ৰে পূৰণ কৰিলে 900।

    LCM & HCF

    The highest common factor and the lowest common multiple are asked in almost every Assam recruitment paper. The HCF is the largest number that divides each of the given numbers exactly, and the LCM is the smallest number that each of them divides exactly.

    For two numbers the product of the HCF and the LCM equals the product of the numbers, which is a useful check. Prime factorisation or the division method gives both values quickly once the multiplication tables are known.

    • The HCF divides the given numbers; the LCM is divisible by them.
    • The HCF of two co-prime numbers is one.
    • The LCM of two co-prime numbers is their product.
    • HCF multiplied by LCM equals the product of the two numbers.

    When two numbers have no common factor other than one, their LCM is simply the product of the two numbers.

    Q1. Statements: All roses are flowers. All flowers are plants. Conclusions: I. All roses are plants. II. All plants are roses. Which conclusion follows?
    বিবৃতি: All roses are flowers. All flowers are plants. সিদ্ধান্ত: I. All roses are plants. II. All plants are roses. কোনটো সিদ্ধান্ত ওলায়?
    (A) Only I follows কেৱল I ওলায়
    (B) Neither I nor II follows I বা II এটাও নোলায়
    (C) Both I and II follow I আৰু II দুয়ো ওলায়
    (D) Only II follows কেৱল II ওলায়
    Answer: A) Only I follows
    A) কেৱল I ওলায়
    Explanation: Roses are inside flowers and flowers inside plants, so all roses are plants; II does not follow.

    Syllogism / Statements & Conclusions

    In syllogism two or three statements are given and a conclusion must be tested against them. A conclusion is valid only when it follows from the statements in every possible case.

    The safest method is to draw a Venn diagram for the statements and then check whether the conclusion is true in every diagram that fits them. A conclusion that is true in one arrangement but not in another does not follow.

    • A conclusion must follow in every possible case.
    • Draw a Venn diagram for the statements.
    • If a conclusion fails in one diagram, it does not follow.
    • Words like all, some and no change the diagram completely.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    When does a conclusion follow from the statements?

    A conclusion follows only when it is true in every possible arrangement of the statements, not merely in one arrangement.

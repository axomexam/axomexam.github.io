    Pattern Completion

    Pattern completion gives a large figure with a part missing and asks which of the options fits the empty space. The correct option must match the lines, the curves and the shading of the surrounding figure.

    The method is to extend the lines that meet the missing part and see where they would go. A quick check of the corners of the empty space eliminates most wrong options.

    • Extend the lines that touch the missing part.
    • Match the curves and the shading of the surroundings.
    • Check the corners of the empty space.
    • Eliminate options that break the pattern.

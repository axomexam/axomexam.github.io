    Q1. Two positions of the same dice are shown. Which number is opposite to the face showing 1?
    একে লুডুৰ দুটা অৱস্থান দেখুওৱা হৈছে। ১ দেখুওৱা পিঠিখনৰ বিপৰীত পিঠিত কোনটো সংখ্যা থাকিব?
    (A) 6 ৬
    (B) 4 ৪
    (C) 2 ২
    (D) 5 ৫
    Answer: A) 6
    A) ৬
    Explanation: In the two views the face 1 is adjacent to 4, 5, 2, 3, so its opposite is the only remaining face, 6.
    দুটা দৃশ্যত ১ নম্বৰ পিঠিখন ৪, ৫, ২, ৩ ৰ কাষত আছে, গতিকে ইয়াৰ বিপৰীত পিঠি বাকী থকা ৬।

    Cubes & Dice

    A cube has six faces, twelve edges and eight corners, and a standard die carries the numbers one to six with the opposite faces adding up to seven. This last rule answers most dice questions.

    When a cube is painted and then cut into smaller cubes, the corner cubes have three painted faces, the edge cubes have two, the face cubes have one and the inner cubes have none.

    • A cube has six faces, twelve edges and eight corners.
    • Opposite faces of a standard die add up to seven.
    • Corner cubes of a painted cube have three painted faces.
    • Inner cubes of a painted cube have no painted face.

    Narration

    Narration — Direct আৰু Indirect Speech। • General English • ADRE 3.0 Grade III

    4 Questions with Answers

    Q1. Change to indirect: He said, 'I am tired.'
    পৰোক্ষলৈ সলাওক: He said, 'I am tired.'
    (A) He said that he is tired. He said that he is tired.
    (B) He said that he was tired. He said that he was tired.
    (C) He said that I was tired. He said that I was tired.
    (D) He says that he was tired. He says that he was tired.
    Answer: B) He said that he was tired.
    B) He said that he was tired.
    Explanation: The reporting verb is in the past, so the tense shifts back and I becomes he.
    ৰিপৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়া অতীতত, গতিকে কাল পিছলৈ যায় আৰু I এ he হয়।
    Q2. Change to indirect: She asked, 'Where do you live?'
    পৰোক্ষলৈ সলাওক: She asked, 'Where do you live?'
    (A) She asked where I live. She asked where I live.
    (B) She asked where I lived. She asked where I lived.
    (C) She asked that where I lived. She asked that where I lived.
    (D) She asked if I lived. She asked if I lived.
    Answer: B) She asked where I lived.
    B) She asked where I lived.
    Explanation: A wh-question keeps its question word and the tense shifts back.
    Wh-প্ৰশ্নই নিজৰ প্ৰশ্নবোধক শব্দ ৰাখে আৰু কাল পিছলৈ যায়।
    Q3. Change to indirect: He said, 'Please help me.'
    পৰোক্ষলৈ সলাওক: He said, 'Please help me.'
    (A) He requested to help him. He requested to help him.
    (B) He requested me to help him. He requested me to help him.
    (C) He said to help him. He said to help him.
    (D) He told help me. He told help me.
    Answer: B) He requested me to help him.
    B) He requested me to help him.
    Explanation: A request in indirect speech uses requested with the object and to.
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত অনুৰোধৰ বাবে requested, কৰ্ম আৰু to ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q4. Which linking word joins a reported statement?
    পৰোক্ষ বিবৃতি সংযোগ কৰা শব্দ কোনটো?
    (A) if if
    (B) that that
    (C) whether whether
    (D) to to
    Answer: B) that
    B) that
    Explanation: A reported statement is joined by that.
    পৰোক্ষ বিবৃতি that ৰে যোগ কৰা হয়।

    Narration

    Narration is the change of a sentence from direct speech to indirect speech or the reverse. In direct speech the exact words of the speaker are placed inside inverted commas.

    When a sentence is changed to indirect speech, the comma and the inverted commas are removed, a suitable linking word is added, and the tense and the pronouns are changed to suit the reporting verb.

    • Remove the inverted commas in indirect speech.
    • Change the tense one step back when the reporting verb is in the past.
    • Change the pronouns to match the speaker and the listener.
    • Statements take that, questions take if or whether, and commands take to.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which word links a reported question?

    A question in indirect speech is joined by if or whether, or by the question word itself such as what or where.

    উক্তি পৰিৱৰ্তন

    উক্তি পৰিৱৰ্তন হৈছে এটা বাক্যক প্ৰত্যক্ষ উক্তিৰ পৰা পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ বা ইয়াৰ বিপৰীতলৈ সলোৱা। প্ৰত্যক্ষ উক্তিত বক্তাৰ সঠিক শব্দবোৰ উদ্ধৃতি চিহ্নৰ ভিতৰত ৰখা হয়।

    এটা বাক্যক পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ সলাওতে কমা আৰু উদ্ধৃতি চিহ্ন আঁতৰোৱা হয়, উপযুক্ত সংযোগ শব্দ যোগ কৰা হয়, আৰু ক্ৰিয়া আৰু সৰ্বনামবোৰ ৰিপৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়াৰ লগত খাপ খুওৱা হয়।

    • পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত উদ্ধৃতি চিহ্ন আঁতৰাওক।
    • ৰিপৰ্টিং ক্ৰিয়া অতীতত থাকিলে কাল এখোজ পিছলৈ সলাওক।
    • বক্তা আৰু শ্ৰোতাৰ লগত মিলাই সৰ্বনাম সলাওক।
    • বিবৃতিয়ে that, প্ৰশ্নই if বা whether, আৰু আদেশই to লয়।

    সততে সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    পৰোক্ষ প্ৰশ্নত কোনটো শব্দে সংযোগ কৰে?

    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত প্ৰশ্নক if বা whether, বা প্ৰশ্নবোধক শব্দ যেনে what বা where ৰে যোগ কৰা হয়।

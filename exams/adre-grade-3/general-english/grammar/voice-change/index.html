    Q1. Change to passive: He writes a letter.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলনি কৰা: He writes a letter.
    (A) A letter is written by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হয়।
    (B) A letter was written by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হৈছিল।
    (C) A letter is wrote by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হয় (wrote)।
    (D) A letter written by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা লিখা এখন চিঠি।

    A) তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হয়।
    Explanation: Present simple passive: is/am/are + past participle + by.
    বৰ্তমান সাধাৰণ কৰ্মবাচ্য: is/am/are + অতীত কৃদন্ত + by।

    (A) The headmaster gave him a prize. প্ৰধান শিক্ষকে তেওঁক এটা পুৰস্কাৰ দিছিল।
    (B) The headmaster gives him a prize. প্ৰধান শিক্ষকে তেওঁক এটা পুৰস্কাৰ দিয়ে।
    (C) The headmaster is giving him a prize. প্ৰধান শিক্ষকে তেওঁক এটা পুৰস্কাৰ দি আছে।

    A) প্ৰধান শিক্ষকে তেওঁক এটা পুৰস্কাৰ দিছিল।

    (A) Assamese was spoken in Assam. অসমত অসমীয়া কোৱা হৈছিল।
    (B) Assamese is speaking in Assam. অসমত অসমীয়া কৈ আছে।

    (C) Did the letter write by her? তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা চিঠিখন লিখিছিল নেকি?
    (D) Has the letter written by her? তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা চিঠিখন লিখা হৈছে নেকি?

    B) তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা চিঠিখন লিখা হৈছিল নেকি?

    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলনি কৰা: Somebody has taken my umbrella.
    (A) My umbrella was taken. মোৰ ছাতাটো লৈ যোৱা হৈছিল।
    (B) My umbrella has been taken. মোৰ ছাতাটো লৈ যোৱা হৈছে।
    (C) My umbrella is taken. মোৰ ছাতাটো লৈ যোৱা হয়।
    (D) My umbrella had been taken. মোৰ ছাতাটো লৈ যোৱা হৈছিল (had)।
    Answer: B) My umbrella has been taken.
    B) মোৰ ছাতাটো লৈ যোৱা হৈছে।
    Explanation: When the doer is vague, by is dropped: has been taken.
    কৰ্তা অস্পষ্ট হ'লে by বাদ পৰে: has been taken।

    Voice Change

    Voice shows whether the subject acts or is acted upon. In the active voice the subject does the action, and in the passive voice the subject receives it.

    To change a sentence into the passive voice, the object becomes the subject, the verb becomes a form of be with the past participle, and the doer is introduced by the word by or left out.

    • Active: the subject performs the action.
    • Passive: the object becomes the subject.
    • The passive uses a form of be with the past participle.
    • Only transitive verbs can be changed into the passive voice.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can every sentence be changed into the passive voice?

    No. Only sentences with a transitive verb, that is a verb that has an object, can be changed into the passive voice.

    Q1. Change to passive: He writes a letter.
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলনি কৰা: He writes a letter.
    (A) A letter is written by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হয়।
    (B) A letter was written by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হৈছিল।
    (C) A letter is wrote by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হয় (wrote)।
    (D) A letter written by him. তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা লিখা এখন চিঠি।

    A) তেওঁৰ দ্বাৰা এখন চিঠি লিখা হয়।
    Explanation: Present simple passive: is/am/are + past participle + by.
    বৰ্তমান সাধাৰণ কৰ্মবাচ্য: is/am/are + অতীত কৃদন্ত + by।

    Voice Change

    Voice is the form of the verb that shows whether the subject of the sentence acts or is acted upon. When the subject does the action the sentence is in the active voice, and when the subject receives the action the sentence is in the passive voice. In the ADRE Grade III paper voice change is asked as a direct transformation, and the passive form also appears in error spotting and in sentence correction. The rules are few and regular, and once they are learnt the chapter becomes a reliable source of marks.

    In the active voice the doer comes first and the object comes last, as in Ram wrote the letter. In the passive voice the object becomes the subject and the doer, if it is mentioned at all, comes after the preposition by, as in the letter was written by Ram. Three changes take place together: the object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence, the verb is changed into a form of be with the past participle, and the subject of the active sentence becomes the object of by.

    The tense of the verb is kept in the passive voice, but the form changes. The simple present changes to am, is or are with the past participle, so that he writes a letter becomes a letter is written by him. The present continuous changes to am, is or are being with the past participle, so that he is writing a letter becomes a letter is being written by him. The present perfect changes to has or have been with the past participle, so that he has written a letter becomes a letter has been written by him.

    The past tenses follow the same pattern. The simple past changes to was or were with the past participle, so that he wrote a letter becomes a letter was written by him. The past continuous changes to was or were being with the past participle, so that he was writing a letter becomes a letter was being written by him. The past perfect changes to had been with the past participle, so that he had written a letter becomes a letter had been written by him.

    The future tenses are formed with will or shall. The simple future changes to will be with the past participle, so that he will write a letter becomes a letter will be written by him. The future perfect changes to will have been with the past participle, so that he will have written a letter becomes a letter will have been written by him. The future continuous has no natural passive form and is avoided in good writing.

    Modal verbs keep the modal and add be or have been with the past participle. He can write a letter becomes a letter can be written by him. He must write a letter becomes a letter must be written by him. He should have written a letter becomes a letter should have been written by him. The same pattern holds for may, might, could, would and ought to. This group is tested very often because it looks complicated but follows one simple shape.

    The pronoun changes from the subject form to the object form when it moves from the doer to the object of by, and from the object form to the subject form when it moves from the object to the subject. I becomes me, we becomes us, he becomes him, she becomes her, they becomes them, and you and it stay the same. So the sentence I wrote a letter becomes a letter was written by me, and the sentence he helped us becomes we were helped by him.

    An imperative sentence has a special passive form. The command close the door becomes let the door be closed, and the negative command do not close the door becomes let not the door be closed or the door should not be closed. A request such as please help me becomes you are requested to help me. An imperative that carries advice may also be turned with should, as in the sentence you should obey your parents for obey your parents.

    A sentence that has two objects offers a choice. The sentence he gave me a book may become I was given a book by him, where the indirect object becomes the subject, or a book was given to me by him, where the direct object becomes the subject. Both forms are correct, but the first is more natural in modern English. When a verb such as give, send, offer, teach or tell has two objects, the personal object is usually preferred as the subject of the passive sentence.

    Some sentences cannot be turned into the passive at all. An intransitive verb has no object, so the sentence he slept has no passive form. A sentence whose verb is a linking verb such as be, become or seem has no passive form. And the passive voice of a verb of perception such as hear, see or feel is used only in a limited way. In these cases the correct answer in an examination is the sentence itself, or a statement that the sentence has no passive form.

    The passive voice is not merely a grammatical exercise; it is used when the doer is unknown, unimportant or obvious. We say the thief was arrested because we do not know who arrested him, and we say the road is being repaired because the doer does not matter. When the doer is a vague person such as people, they or someone, the by phrase is dropped in the passive, so that people say becomes it is said. This is why so many passive sentences have no by phrase at all.

    Several errors are common in this chapter. Candidates forget to change the verb into a form of be with the past participle and instead leave the active verb, as in the letter was write. They change the tense by mistake, turning a simple past into a present perfect. They keep the subject pronoun in the subject form after by, writing by he instead of by him. They forget the word being in the continuous passive, writing the letter is written by him when the sentence means the letter is being written by him.

    To revise, draw a table with the active form on the left and the passive form on the right for each tense, and read it aloud until the pairs come without effort. Then take ten active sentences a day and turn them into the passive, and change back again, checking each time that the tense and the pronoun are right. Keep a note of the sentences that have no passive form. With this practice the voice change questions become mechanical and quick, and the marks follow.

    A passive sentence sometimes has no by phrase at all, because the doer is unknown or unimportant. In such a sentence the candidate must not invent a doer but only write the correct passive form. When the doer in the active sentence is a vague word such as people, they or someone, the by phrase is dropped in the passive, so that people say becomes it is said. Watching for this small point is what saves a mark when the question is set carefully.

    It is worth remembering that the tense of the verb is never changed in the passive voice; only its form is changed. The simple past stays the simple past and the simple present stays the simple present. Candidates often go wrong by changing the tense, turning he wrote a letter into a letter is written by him. To avoid this, fix the tense of the original sentence first and only then choose the passive form.

    A passive question sometimes gives a sentence with two objects, and then two passive forms are possible. From he gave me a book we may get I was given a book by him, or a book was given to me by him. Both are correct, but the first is more natural in modern English. When such a question comes, the form that sounds natural is usually the better choice.

    • Active: the doer is the subject. Passive: the receiver is the subject.
    • The passive is formed with a form of be and the past participle.
    • The tense is kept; only the form of the verb changes.
    • Continuous passive needs being: the letter is being written.
    • Modals keep the modal: a letter can be written by him.
    • Pronouns change case: by him, not by he; by me, not by I.
    • Imperative: close the door becomes let the door be closed.
    • Intransitive and linking verbs have no passive form.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is the letter was write wrong?

    The passive is formed with a form of be and the past participle, so the correct sentence is the letter was written.

    Why do we say by him and not by he?

    By is a preposition and a preposition takes the objective case, so the pronoun after by is him and not he.

    How is an imperative sentence changed to the passive?

    The command close the door becomes let the door be closed, and a request with please becomes you are requested to.

    Can every sentence be turned into the passive?

    No. An intransitive verb has no object and a linking verb has no passive form, so sentences such as he slept cannot be changed.

    দুটা object থকা বাক্যই এটা বাচনি দিয়ে। he gave me a book বাক্যৰ পৰা I was given a book by him হ'ব পাৰে, য'ত পৰোক্ষ object কৰ্তা হয়, বা a book was given to me by him হ'ব পাৰে, য'ত প্ৰত্যক্ষ object কৰ্তা হয়। দুয়ো ৰূপ শুদ্ধ, কিন্তু আধুনিক ইংৰাজীত প্ৰথমটো বেছি স্বাভাৱিক। give, send, offer, teach বা tell এনে দুটা object থকা ক্ৰিয়াৰ ক্ষেত্ৰত কৰ্মবাচ্যৰ কৰ্তা হিচাপে ব্যক্তিবাচক object সাধাৰণতে বাঞ্ছনীয়।

    কিছুমান বাক্য কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰিবই নোৱাৰি। অকৰ্মক ক্ৰিয়াৰ object নাথাকে, গতিকে he slept বাক্যৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ নাই। be, become বা seem এনে সংযোগী ক্ৰিয়া থকা বাক্যৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ নাই। আৰু hear, see বা feel এনে অনুভৱ-ক্ৰিয়াৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য সীমিতভাৱে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়। এই ক্ষেত্ৰত পৰীক্ষাত শুদ্ধ উত্তৰ বাক্যটো নিজেই, বা বাক্যটোৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ নাই বুলি এটা বক্তব্য।

    কৰ্মবাচ্য কেৱল ব্যাকৰণৰ অভ্যাস নহয়; ই ব্যৱহাৰ হয় যেতিয়া কৰ্তা অজ্ঞাত, নগণ্য বা স্পষ্ট। আমি the thief was arrested লিখোঁ কাৰণ কেৱে ধৰিছিল জানো নাই, আৰু the road is being repaired লিখোঁ কাৰণ কৰ্তাই গুৰুত্ব নাপায়। কৰ্তা people, they বা someone এনে অস্পষ্ট ব্যক্তি হ'লে কৰ্মবাচ্যত by বাক্যাংশ বাদ পৰে, গতিকে people say ৰ পৰা it is said হয়। এই কাৰণেই ইমান কৰ্মবাচ্য বাক্যত by বাক্যাংশ নাথাকে।

    এই অধ্যায়ত কেইটামান ভুল সাধাৰণ। প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে ক্ৰিয়া be ৰ ৰূপ আৰু past participle লৈ সলনি কৰিবলৈ পাহৰি কৰ্তৃবাচ্য ক্ৰিয়াই ৰাখে, যেনে the letter was write। তেওঁলোকে ভুলতে কাল সলনি কৰে, সৰল অতীতক বৰ্তমান পূৰ্ণ কৰে। কৰ্তা সৰ্বনাম by ৰ পিছত কৰ্তা ৰূপতে ৰাখে, by him ৰ সলনি by he লিখে। চলিত কৰ্মবাচ্যত being শব্দ পাহৰে, যেতিয়া বাক্যই the letter is being written by him বুজায় তেতিয়া the letter is written by him লিখে।

    পুনৰালোচনাৰ বাবে প্ৰতিটো কালৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য ৰূপ বাওঁফালে আৰু কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ সোঁফালে থকা এখন তালিকা আঁকি, যোৰবোৰ সহজে নহালৈকে জোৰেৰে পঢ়ক। তাৰপিছত প্ৰতিদিনে দহটা কৰ্তৃবাচ্য বাক্য লৈ কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক, আৰু পুনৰ ওলোটা কৰক, প্ৰতিবাৰে কাল আৰু সৰ্বনাম শুদ্ধ হৈছে নে নাই পৰীক্ষা কৰি। কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ নথকা বাক্যবোৰৰ টোকা ৰাখক। এই অভ্যাসেৰে বাচ্য পৰিৱৰ্তনৰ প্ৰশ্নবোৰ যান্ত্ৰিক আৰু দ্ৰুত হৈ পৰে, আৰু নম্বৰ আহি পায়।

    কৰ্মবাচ্যৰ প্ৰশ্নত কেতিয়াবা by বাক্যাংশ নাথাকেও, কাৰণ কৰ্তাজন অজ্ঞাত বা নগণ্য। এনে বাক্যত উত্তৰ দিওঁতে ভুৱা কৰ্তা যোগ নকৰিব, কেৱল শুদ্ধ কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপটো লিখিব। যদি দিয়া বাক্যৰ কৰ্তা people, they বা someone এনে অস্পষ্ট শব্দ হয়, তেন্তে কৰ্মবাচ্যত সেয়া বাদ পৰে, যেনে people say ৰ পৰা it is said হয়। এই সূক্ষ্মতাটো প্ৰশ্নত থাকিলে সাৱধানে চোৱাটোৱেই নম্বৰ ৰাখে।

    আন এটা কথা মনত ৰখা উচিত যে কৰ্মবাচ্যত ক্ৰিয়াৰ কাল কেতিয়াও সলনি কৰিব নালাগে; কেৱল ৰূপ সলনি হয়। সৰল অতীত সৰল অতীতেই থাকে, আৰু সৰল বৰ্তমান সৰল বৰ্তমানেই থাকে। প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে প্ৰায়ে কাল সলনি কৰি ভুল কৰে, যেনে he wrote a letter ক কৰা হয় a letter is written by him। এনে ভুল এৰাবলৈ প্ৰথমে মূল বাক্যৰ কাল নিৰ্ণয় কৰি তাৰপিছত কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ বাছনি কৰাটোৱেই নিৰাপদ পথ।

    কৰ্মবাচ্যৰ প্ৰশ্নত কেতিয়াবা এটা বাক্যত দুটা object থাকে, আৰু তেতিয়া দুটা কৰ্মবাচ্য ৰূপ সম্ভৱ হয়। উদাহৰণস্বৰূপে he gave me a book ৰ পৰা I was given a book by him বা a book was given to me by him — দুয়োটাই শুদ্ধ, কিন্তু প্ৰথমটো আধুনিক ইংৰাজীত বেছি স্বাভাৱিক। পৰীক্ষাত এনে প্ৰশ্ন আহিলে যি ৰূপটো স্বাভাৱিক শুনায় সেয়া বাছনি কৰাটোৱেই ভাল।

    • কৰ্তৃবাচ্য: কৰ্তাই কৰ্তা। কৰ্মবাচ্য: গ্ৰহণকাৰীয়েই কৰ্তা।
    • কৰ্মবাচ্য be ৰ এটা ৰূপ আৰু past participle ৰে গঠিত হয়।
    • কাল একেই থাকে; কেৱল ক্ৰিয়াৰ ৰূপ সলনি হয়।
    • চলিত কৰ্মবাচ্যত being লাগে: the letter is being written।
    • Modal য়ে modal ৰাখে: a letter can be written by him।

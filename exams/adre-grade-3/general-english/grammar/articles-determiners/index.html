    Q1. She waited for — hour.
    তাই — ঘণ্টা অপেক্ষা কৰিলে।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article কোনো আৰ্টিকল নাই
    Answer: B) an
    B) an
    Explanation: Hour begins with a vowel sound, so an is used.
    Hour স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, গতিকে an ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. He is — honest man.
    তেওঁ — সৎ মানুহ।
    (A) a a
    (B) an an
    (C) the the
    (D) no article কোনো আৰ্টিকল নাই
    Answer: B) an
    B) an
    Explanation: The h in honest is silent, so the word begins with a vowel sound.
    Honest ৰ h নীৰৱ, গতিকে শব্দটো স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়।
    Q3. — Ganga is a holy river.
    — গংগা এটা পবিত্ৰ নদী।
    (A) A A
    (B) An An
    (C) The The
    (D) No article কোনো আৰ্টিকল নাই
    Answer: C) The
    C) The
    Explanation: The definite article the is used before the names of rivers.
    নদীৰ নামৰ আগত নিৰ্দিষ্ট আৰ্টিকল the ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q4. Choose the correct sentence.
    শুদ্ধ বাক্যটো বাছনি কৰক।
    (A) He is a university student. He is a university student.
    (B) He is an university student. He is an university student.
    (C) He is the university student. He is the university student.
    (D) He is university student. He is university student.
    Answer: A) He is a university student.
    A) He is a university student.
    Explanation: University begins with the consonant sound y, so a is correct.
    University ব্যঞ্জনধ্বনি y ৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, গতিকে a শুদ্ধ।

    Articles

    The words a, an and the are called articles. A and an are indefinite articles and are used with a singular countable noun that is mentioned for the first time, while the is the definite article and points to a particular noun.

    The choice between a and an depends on the sound, not the letter. An is used before a vowel sound, so we write an hour and an honest man, but a university and a one-rupee coin.

    • Use an before a vowel sound, not before a vowel letter.
    • Use the when the noun is already known to the reader.
    • Do not use an article before a proper noun like Assam.
    • The is used before superlatives, for example the best.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do we say an hour but a university?

    Because the choice depends on the sound. Hour begins with a vowel sound, so it takes an, while university begins with the consonant sound y, so it takes a.

    কিয় an hour কিন্তু a university বোলা হয়?

    কাৰণ বাছনিটো ধ্বনিৰ ওপৰত নিৰ্ভৰ কৰে। Hour স্বৰধ্বনিৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, গতিকে an লয়, আনহাতে university ব্যঞ্জনধ্বনি y ৰে আৰম্ভ হয়, গতিকে a লয়।

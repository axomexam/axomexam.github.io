    Subject-Verb Agreement

    A verb must agree with its subject in number and person. A singular subject takes a singular verb and a plural subject takes a plural verb, and this rule decides many grammar questions.

    Care is needed with phrases placed between the subject and the verb. In the sentence the box of apples is heavy, the true subject is box, so the verb is singular.

    • Ignore words placed between the subject and the verb.
    • Two subjects joined by and take a plural verb.
    • Two subjects joined by or or nor take the verb of the nearer one.
    • Words like each, every and either take a singular verb.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the subject in the sentence the box of apples is heavy?

    The subject is box, not apples, so the singular verb is is correct. The phrase of apples only describes the box.

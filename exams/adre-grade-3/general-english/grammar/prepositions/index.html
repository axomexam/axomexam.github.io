    Prepositions

    A preposition is a word that shows the relation between a noun or a pronoun and another word in the sentence, and it almost always has an object. In the sentence the book is on the table, the word on shows the relation between the book and the table, and table is the object of the preposition. The ADRE Grade III paper asks prepositions more often than almost any other single grammar point, both in fill in the blanks and in error spotting, and the reason is simple: the same verb or adjective may take different prepositions in different senses, and there is no rule that predicts the choice with certainty. The only dependable method is to learn each preposition with the word it follows.

    Prepositions of place answer the question where. In is used for a large area or an enclosed space, so that we live in Assam and the pen is in the box. At is used for a point or a small place, so that we wait at the gate and the train stops at the station. On is used for a surface, so that the cup is on the table and the picture hangs on the wall. Over means above and across, as in the plane flew over the hill, and under means below, as in the cat is under the chair. Between is used for two things and among for more than two, so that we divide the money between the two brothers and among the four children.

    Prepositions of time follow their own set of rules. At is used for a point of time, as in at five o clock and at noon. On is used for a day or a date, as in on Monday and on the fifth of May. In is used for a month, a season, a year or a long period, as in in June, in winter and in 1990. Since is used with a point of time from which something began, as in since morning and since 2015, while for is used with a length of time, as in for two hours and for ten years. During means through the whole of a period, as in during the war, and by means not later than, as in finish it by Monday.

    Prepositions of movement and direction include to, into, onto, towards, through, across, along and past. To shows the goal of a movement, as in he went to Delhi. Into shows movement from outside to inside, as in she walked into the room. Onto shows movement to a surface. Through means from one side to the other inside something, as in the train went through the tunnel, while across means from one side to the other on a surface, as in he swam across the river. Along means following the length of something, as in we walked along the road.

    Certain verbs are followed by fixed prepositions and must be learnt as single units. We listen to music, wait for a bus, look at a picture, look for a lost key, look after a child and look into a matter. We consist of parts, deal in goods, deal with a person, depend on someone, insist on a thing, and comply with a rule. We agree with a person and agree to a proposal, we apologise for a mistake and apologise to a person, and we congratulate a person on a success. These verb-plus-preposition combinations are the single richest source of fill in the blank questions.

    Adjectives, too, take fixed prepositions. We are afraid of dogs, good at mathematics, bad at singing, angry with a person and angry at a thing, angry about an event, proud of a child, fond of sweets, jealous of a rival, sorry for a mistake, sorry about an event, confident of success, capable of doing work, famous for a quality, known for honesty, married to a person, engaged to a person, and addicted to a habit. We are also afraid of a thing, aware of a danger, ashamed of a fault, accused of a crime, deprived of a right, and tired of a routine. Each of these pairs is worth writing on a card.

    Nouns, as well, may be followed by a fixed preposition. We have a need for something, a demand for a thing, a reason for an act, a cause of a problem, a solution to a difficulty, a key to a lock, a reply to a letter, an answer to a question, an affection for a person, and a taste for music. A rise in price, a fall in temperature, an increase in population and a decrease in rainfall are all common in examination sentences. Learning a noun together with its preposition prevents the error that comes from translating directly from Assamese.

    The preposition is also used with certain set phrases that must be learnt whole. We say in time meaning before the deadline and on time meaning at the fixed time. We say at present, in future, in the end meaning finally, and at the end meaning at the last point. We say on foot, by bus, by train, by air, in a car and on a bicycle. We say at home, at work, in bed, on the way, in the morning, on Sunday morning, and at night. These phrases are fixed, and changing one word makes the phrase wrong.

    The most frequent error is the use of a wrong preposition with a verb or an adjective, and it comes from two sources. The first is the influence of the mother tongue, which leads a candidate to write married with instead of married to, or discuss about instead of discuss. The second is the confusion between two prepositions that look alike, such as since and for, between and among, on and in, and by and with. When a sentence is read with the suspect preposition removed and the correct question of place, time or manner is asked, the right preposition usually becomes clear.

    Some verbs take no preposition at all, and adding one is a common error. We discuss a matter, not discuss about a matter; we enter a room, not enter into a room; we reach a place, not reach at a place; we order a meal, not order for a meal; and we return home, not return to home. Conversely, some verbs need a preposition that candidates often drop, as in listen to music and reply to a letter. A short list of these two groups is worth memorising, because each one turns up regularly in the error-spotting section.

    A preposition is normally followed by a noun, a pronoun in the objective case, or a gerund. We say he is good at painting and not he is good at paint, and we say she is afraid of falling and not she is afraid of fall. A pronoun after a preposition takes the objective form, so that we write between you and me and not between you and I. These two rules explain a large share of the questions on the chapter, and they are easy to apply once they are known.

    Some prepositions are used to show relations other than place and time. Of shows possession or connection, as in the roof of the house. By is used for the doer in the passive voice, as in the letter was written by him. With is used for an instrument, as in he cut the rope with a knife. For shows purpose or benefit, as in this gift is for you. Without means in the absence of, as in he came without a coat. About and on are both used for a subject, though on suggests a more formal treatment.

    A practical method for the examination is to read the whole sentence before filling the blank, because the preposition is decided by the word it follows rather than by the blank alone. If the blank follows an adjective, recall the adjective-preposition pair; if it follows a verb, recall the verb-preposition pair; if it follows a noun, recall the noun-preposition pair. In error spotting, check every preposition in the sentence against the word before it. With a card of the common pairs revised for a week, this chapter becomes one of the easiest places to gain marks.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is married with wrong?

    The adjective married takes the preposition to, so the correct phrase is married to a person, never married with.

    What is the difference between since and for?

    Since is used with a point of time, as in since 2015, while for is used with a length of time, as in for five years.

    Why is between you and I wrong?

    A preposition takes the objective case, so the correct form is between you and me.

    Which words take no preposition after them?

    Discuss, enter, reach, order and return home take no preposition, so we write discuss the matter and not discuss about the matter.

    স্থানৰ পূৰ্বসৰ্গই ক'ত — এই প্ৰশ্নৰ উত্তৰ দিয়ে। In ডাঙৰ অঞ্চল বা আবদ্ধ ঠাইৰ বাবে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, গতিকে we live in Assam আৰু the pen is in the box। At এটা বিন্দু বা সৰু ঠাইৰ বাবে, গতিকে we wait at the gate আৰু the train stops at the station। On পৃষ্ঠৰ বাবে, গতিকে the cup is on the table আৰু the picture hangs on the wall। Over মানে ওপৰত আৰু সিটো কাষলৈ, যেনে the plane flew over the hill, আৰু under মানে তলত, যেনে the cat is under the chair। Between দুইৰ বাবে আৰু among দুইতকৈ বেছিৰ বাবে, গতিকে divide the money between the two brothers আৰু among the four children।

    সময়ৰ পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ নিজা নিয়ম। At এটা সময়ৰ বিন্দুৰ বাবে, যেনে at five o clock আৰু at noon। On এটা দিন বা তাৰিখৰ বাবে, যেনে on Monday আৰু on the fifth of May। In এটা মাহ, ঋতু, বছৰ বা দীঘল কালৰ বাবে, যেনে in June, in winter আৰু in 1990। Since এটা সময়ৰ বিন্দুৰ সৈতে য'ৰপৰা কিবা আৰম্ভ হৈছিল, যেনে since morning আৰু since 2015, আনহাতে for এটা সময়ৰ দৈৰ্ঘ্যৰ সৈতে, যেনে for two hours আৰু for ten years। During মানে গোটেই সময়জুৰি, যেনে during the war, আৰু by মানে তাতকৈ পিছত নহয়, যেনে finish it by Monday।

    গতি আৰু দিশৰ পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ ভিতৰত to, into, onto, towards, through, across, along আৰু past পৰে। To এটা গতিৰ লক্ষ্য দেখুৱায়, যেনে he went to Delhi। Into বাহিৰৰ পৰা ভিতৰলৈ গতি দেখুৱায়, যেনে she walked into the room। Onto পৃষ্ঠলৈ গতি দেখুৱায়। Through মানে কিবাৰ ভিতৰেদি এটা কাষৰ পৰা আন কাষলৈ, যেনে the train went through the tunnel, আনহাতে across মানে পৃষ্ঠত এটা কাষৰ পৰা আন কাষলৈ, যেনে he swam across the river। Along মানে কিবাৰ দীঘে দীঘে, যেনে we walked along the road।

    কিছুমান ক্ৰিয়াৰ পিছত নিৰ্দিষ্ট পূৰ্বসৰ্গ আহে আৰু ইহঁতক এটা একক হিচাপে শিকিব লাগে। আমি listen to music, wait for a bus, look at a picture, look for a lost key, look after a child আৰু look into a matter লিখোঁ। আমি consist of parts, deal in goods, deal with a person, depend on someone, insist on a thing, আৰু comply with a rule লিখোঁ। আমি agree with a person আৰু agree to a proposal, apologise for a mistake আৰু apologise to a person, আৰু congratulate a person on a success লিখোঁ। ক্ৰিয়া-পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ এই যোৰবোৰ ৰিক্ত স্থান পূৰণৰ প্ৰশ্নৰ সৰ্বাধিক উৎস।

    বিশেষণেও নিৰ্দিষ্ট পূৰ্বসৰ্গ লয়। আমি afraid of dogs, good at mathematics, bad at singing, angry with a person আৰু angry at a thing, angry about an event, proud of a child, fond of sweets, jealous of a rival, sorry for a mistake, sorry about an event, confident of success, capable of doing work, famous for a quality, known for honesty, married to a person, engaged to a person, আৰু addicted to a habit লিখোঁ। আমি afraid of a thing, aware of a danger, ashamed of a fault, accused of a crime, deprived of a right, আৰু tired of a routineও লিখোঁ। এই যোৰবোৰৰ প্ৰতিটো এখন কাৰ্ডত লিখাৰ যোগ্য।

    বিশেষ্যৰ পিছতো নিৰ্দিষ্ট পূৰ্বসৰ্গ আহিব পাৰে। আমি a need for something, a demand for a thing, a reason for an act, a cause of a problem, a solution to a difficulty, a key to a lock, a reply to a letter, an answer to a question, an affection for a person, আৰু a taste for music লিখোঁ। a rise in price, a fall in temperature, an increase in population আৰু a decrease in rainfall — এইবোৰ পৰীক্ষাৰ বাক্যত সাধাৰণ। বিশেষ্যক তাৰ পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ সৈতে একেলগে শিকিলে অসমীয়াৰ পৰা পোনপটীয়াকৈ অনুবাদ কৰি কৰা ভুল ৰোধ হয়।

    কিছুমান নিৰ্দিষ্ট বাক্যাংশতো পূৰ্বসৰ্গ ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যিবোৰ গোটেইটো শিকিব লাগে। আমি in time মানে সময়সীমাৰ আগতে আৰু on time মানে নিৰ্ধাৰিত সময়ত লিখোঁ। আমি at present, in future, in the end মানে শেষত, আৰু at the end মানে শেষ বিন্দুত লিখোঁ। আমি on foot, by bus, by train, by air, in a car আৰু on a bicycle লিখোঁ। আমি at home, at work, in bed, on the way, in the morning, on Sunday morning, আৰু at night লিখোঁ। এই বাক্যাংশবোৰ স্থিৰ, এটা শব্দ সলালে বাক্যাংশটো ভুল হয়।

    সৰ্বাধিক বাৰে হোৱা ভুল হৈছে ক্ৰিয়া বা বিশেষণৰ সৈতে ভুল পূৰ্বসৰ্গ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা, আৰু ইয়াৰ দুটা উৎস। প্ৰথমটো হ'ল মাতৃভাষাৰ প্ৰভাৱ, যাৰ ফলত প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে married to ৰ সলনি married with, বা discuss ৰ সলনি discuss about লিখে। দ্বিতীয়টো হ'ল একে দেখুৱা দুটা পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ মাজত বিভ্ৰম, যেনে since আৰু for, between আৰু among, on আৰু in, আৰু by আৰু with। সন্দেহজনক পূৰ্বসৰ্গ বাদ দি বাক্যটো পঢ়ি স্থান, সময় বা ধৰণৰ শুদ্ধ প্ৰশ্ন কৰিলে শুদ্ধ পূৰ্বসৰ্গ সাধাৰণতে স্পষ্ট হৈ পৰে।

    কিছুমান ক্ৰিয়াই কোনো পূৰ্বসৰ্গ নলয়, আৰু এটা যোগ কৰাটো সাধাৰণ ভুল। আমি discuss a matter লিখোঁ, discuss about a matter নহয়; enter a room লিখোঁ, enter into a room নহয়; reach a place লিখোঁ, reach at a place নহয়; order a meal লিখোঁ, order for a meal নহয়; আৰু return home লিখোঁ, return to home নহয়। ওলোটাকৈ, কিছুমান ক্ৰিয়াৰ প্ৰয়োজনীয় পূৰ্বসৰ্গ প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে প্ৰায়ে বাদ দিয়ে, যেনে listen to music আৰু reply to a letter। এই দুই গোটৰ এটা চুটি তালিকা মনত ৰখাৰ যোগ্য, কাৰণ প্ৰতিটো নিয়মিতভাৱে ভুল নিৰ্ণয় অংশত ওলায়।

    পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত সাধাৰণতে এটা বিশেষ্য, কৰ্ম কাৰকৰ সৰ্বনাম, বা এটা gerund আহে। আমি he is good at painting লিখোঁ, he is good at paint নহয়; আৰু she is afraid of falling লিখোঁ, she is afraid of fall নহয়। পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছৰ সৰ্বনামে কৰ্ম ৰূপ লয়, গতিকে between you and me লিখোঁ, between you and I নহয়। এই দুটা নিয়মেই অধ্যায়ৰ প্ৰশ্নৰ এটা ডাঙৰ অংশ ব্যাখ্যা কৰে, আৰু জনা হ'লে প্ৰয়োগ কৰা সহজ।

    কিছুমান পূৰ্বসৰ্গ স্থান আৰু সময়ৰ বাহিৰে আন সম্পৰ্কও দেখুৱায়। Of মালিকী বা সম্পৰ্ক দেখুৱায়, যেনে the roof of the house। By passive voice ত কৰ্তাৰ বাবে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে the letter was written by him। With যন্ত্ৰৰ বাবে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে he cut the rope with a knife। For উদ্দেশ্য বা লাভৰ বাবে, যেনে this gift is for you। Without মানে অবিহনে, যেনে he came without a coat। About আৰু on দুয়োটা বিষয়ৰ বাবে, যদিও on এ বেছি আনুষ্ঠানিক প্ৰকাশ বুজায়।

    পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে এটা ব্যৱহৃত পদ্ধতি হ'ল ৰিক্ত স্থান পূৰণ কৰাৰ আগতে গোটেই বাক্যটো পঢ়া, কাৰণ পূৰ্বসৰ্গ কেৱল ৰিক্ত স্থানৰ দ্বাৰা নহয়, তাৰ আগৰ শব্দৰ দ্বাৰাহে নিৰ্ণয় হয়। ৰিক্ত স্থান যদি বিশেষণৰ পিছত, তেন্তে বিশেষণ-পূৰ্বসৰ্গ যোৰা মনত পেলাওক; যদি ক্ৰিয়াৰ পিছত, তেন্তে ক্ৰিয়া-পূৰ্বসৰ্গ যোৰা; যদি বিশেষ্যৰ পিছত, তেন্তে বিশেষ্য-পূৰ্বসৰ্গ যোৰা। ভুল নিৰ্ণয়ত বাক্যৰ প্ৰতিটো পূৰ্বসৰ্গ তাৰ আগৰ শব্দৰ সৈতে মিলাই চাওক। সাধাৰণ যোৰবোৰৰ এটা কাৰ্ড এসপ্তাহ পুনৰালোচনা কৰিলে এই অধ্যায় নম্বৰ পোৱাৰ আটাইতকৈ সহজ ঠাই হৈ পৰে।

    • ডাঙৰ অঞ্চলত in, বিন্দুত at, পৃষ্ঠত on, দুইত between, দুইতকৈ বেছিত among।
    • সময়ৰ বিন্দুত at, দিনত on, মাহ বা বছৰত in, সময়ৰ বিন্দুৰপৰা since, সময়ৰ দৈৰ্ঘ্যত for।
    • Listen to, wait for, look after, depend on, insist on, comply with।
    • Afraid of, good at, angry with a person, angry at a thing, married to, addicted to।
    • A reason for, a cause of, a solution to, a key to, a reply to।
    • Discuss a matter, enter a room, reach a place আৰু order a meal য়ে কোনো পূৰ্বসৰ্গ নলয়।
    • পূৰ্বসৰ্গৰ পিছত বিশেষ্য, কৰ্ম সৰ্বনাম বা gerund আহে।
    • In time মানে সময়সীমাৰ আগতে আৰু on time মানে নিৰ্ধাৰিত সময়ত।

    সততে সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    married with কিয় ভুল?

    বিশেষণ married য়ে to পূৰ্বসৰ্গ লয়, গতিকে শুদ্ধ বাক্যাংশ married to a person, married with কেতিয়াও নহয়।

    since আৰু for ৰ মাজত পাৰ্থক্য কি?

    Since এটা সময়ৰ বিন্দুৰ সৈতে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে since 2015; আনহাতে for এটা সময়ৰ দৈৰ্ঘ্যৰ সৈতে, যেনে for five years।

    between you and I কিয় ভুল?

    পূৰ্বসৰ্গই কৰ্ম কাৰক লয়, গতিকে শুদ্ধ ৰূপ between you and me।

    কোনবোৰ শব্দৰ পিছত পূৰ্বসৰ্গ নাহে?

    Discuss, enter, reach, order আৰু return home য়ে পূৰ্বসৰ্গ নলয়, গতিকে discuss the matter লিখোঁ, discuss about the matter নহয়।

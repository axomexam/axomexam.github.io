    The Constitution of India came into force on 26 January 1950 and it is the longest written constitution of any country. The Preamble declares India a sovereign, socialist, secular and democratic republic.

    The Fundamental Rights are given in Part III of the Constitution and the Fundamental Duties in Part IV-A. The right to equality, the right to freedom, the right against exploitation, the right to freedom of religion and the right to constitutional remedies are the main fundamental rights.

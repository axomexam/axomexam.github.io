    President, PM, Governor & CM

    The President is the head of the Indian state and the Supreme Commander of the armed forces, and is elected indirectly by an electoral college. The Prime Minister is the head of the government and is appointed by the President.

    At the state level the Governor is the constitutional head appointed by the President, and the Chief Minister is the head of the state government. The Governor appoints the Chief Minister, who is usually the leader of the majority party in the Legislative Assembly.

    • The President is the head of the Indian state.
    • The Prime Minister is the head of the government.
    • The Governor is appointed by the President.
    • The Chief Minister heads the state government.

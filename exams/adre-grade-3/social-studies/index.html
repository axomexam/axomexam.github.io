    Social Studies for ADRE 3.0 Grade III — what this section covers

    Social Studies (সামাজিক বিজ্ঞান (Social Studies)) is one of the subjects in the ADRE 3.0 Grade III syllabus on axomexam. It currently holds 1,316 bilingual (Assamese & English) practice questions across 2 sub-categories, each question carrying four options, the correct answer and a worked explanation.

    Sub-categories in Social Studies

    • Political Science & Constitution ৰাজনীতি বিজ্ঞান আৰু সংবিধান — 605 questions.
    • Economy & Environment অৰ্থনীতি আৰু পৰিৱেশ — 711 questions.

    How to prepare Social Studies

    • Read the sub-category list above and decide the order you want to attempt them in.
    • Finish one sub-category at a time and read the explanation for every wrong answer.
    • Re-attempt the same sub-category after a few days to confirm the facts have stuck.

    Social Studies for the ADRE 3.0 Grade III examination. The questions and explanations follow the patterns used in recent Assam recruitment papers, and each topic is written so that the method can be reproduced in the examination hall.

    Economy & Environment: notes

    Poverty, Unemployment & Social Sector

    Poverty means a situation in which a person is unable to get the minimum necessities of life, such as food, clothing, shelter and education. In India, poverty is measured with the help of a poverty line, which is fixed in terms of money income or consumption expenditure. The poverty line is revised from time to time. The Tendulkar Committee (2009) defined the poverty line and estimated that about 21.9 percent of India's population was below the poverty line in 2011–12. The Rangarajan Committee (2014) gave a new method and a higher poverty line. The Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI), which was prepared with the help of NITI Aayog and the United Nations, is also used to measure poverty in India on the basis of health, education and standard of living.

    Agriculture, Crops & Tea Economy of Assam

    Assam is essentially an agricultural state. A very large part of the population depends directly on farming, and agriculture is the backbone of the state's economy. The fertile alluvial soil, the warm humid climate and the monsoon rain make the two valleys of Assam natural granaries.

    Rice is the principal food crop and the staple food of the people. Assam grows three crops of paddy in a year: 1. Ahu (autumn paddy): sown in February–March with the help of pre-monsoon showers and harvested in June–July. 2. Sali (winter paddy): the main crop of Assam. It is sown in June–July with the beginning of the monsoon and harvested in November–December. 3.

    প্ৰায় প্ৰতিখন জিলাতে ধান খেতি হয়, কিন্তু আটাইতকৈ গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ ধান খেতিৰ অঞ্চল উত্তৰপাৰৰ লখিমপুৰ আৰু ধেমাজি জিলা আৰু নগাঁও, শিৱসাগৰ আৰু ডিব্ৰুগড়ত।

    Economy & Environment: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. The environment includes — পৰিৱেশত কি অন্তৰ্ভুক্ত?
      (A) only living things কেৱল জীৱ   (B) all living and non-living things around us আমাৰ চাৰিওফালৰ সকলো জীৱ আৰু নিৰ্জীৱ বস্তু   (C) only non-living things কেৱল নিৰ্জীৱ বস্তু   (D) only human beings কেৱল মানুহ
      Answer: all living and non-living things around us Explanation: The environment includes all living and non-living things.
    • Q2. The biosphere is the zone of the earth where — জীৱমণ্ডল হৈছে পৃথিৱীৰ সেই অঞ্চল য'ত —
      (A) there is no life জীৱন নাই   (B) life exists জীৱন আছে   (C) only rocks exist কেৱল শিল আছে   (D) only water exists কেৱল পানী আছে
      Answer: life exists Explanation: The biosphere is where life exists.
    • Q3. The abiotic component of the environment includes — পৰিৱেশৰ অজৈৱিক উপাদানত কি অন্তৰ্ভুক্ত?
      (A) plants উদ্ভিদ   (B) animals প্ৰাণী   (C) microbes অণুজীৱ   (D) air, water and soil বায়ু, পানী আৰু মাটি
      Answer: air, water and soil Explanation: The abiotic component includes air, water and soil.
    • Q4. The biotic component of the environment includes — পৰিৱেশৰ জৈৱিক উপাদানত কি অন্তৰ্ভুক্ত?
      (A) air বায়ু   (B) plants, animals and microbes উদ্ভিদ, প্ৰাণী আৰু অণুজীৱ   (C) water পানী   (D) soil মাটি
      Answer: plants, animals and microbes Explanation: The biotic component includes living organisms.

    Decimals & Fractions for the RRB NTPC CBT

    Decimals and fractions are two ways of writing the same idea, and in the RRB NTPC Mathematics paper they are treated as one chapter. A fraction shows a part of a whole as one number over another, while a decimal writes that same part using place value and a point. Because the two forms are interchangeable, almost every question in this chapter can be attacked from either side, and the candidate who is comfortable moving between them saves a great deal of time.

    A fraction has a numerator on top and a denominator below. In a proper fraction the numerator is smaller than the denominator, so the value is less than one, as in 3/5. In an improper fraction the numerator is larger, so the value is more than one, as in 11/8. An improper fraction can be rewritten as a mixed number, which is a whole number joined to a proper fraction, so 11/8 becomes 1 3/8. To change a mixed number back, multiply the whole number by the denominator and add the numerator, keeping the same denominator.

    Equivalent fractions are fractions that look different but hold the same value. Multiplying or dividing the numerator and the denominator by the same non-zero number gives an equivalent fraction, so 2/4 and 3/6 both equal 1/2. This is the idea behind reducing a fraction to its lowest terms, where the numerator and denominator share no common factor other than one. Reducing a fraction makes it easier to compare and to handle in later steps, and examiners often present the answer in its lowest terms only.

    To turn a fraction into a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. The division either ends after a few places, giving a terminating decimal such as 3/4 equal to 0.75, or it goes on forever in a repeating block, giving a recurring decimal such as 1/3 equal to 0.333 and so on. A fraction in lowest terms gives a terminating decimal only when its denominator, after removing the factors 2 and 5, becomes 1. If any other prime remains in the denominator, the decimal will repeat, and this single rule decides the whole question type.

    Going the other way, a decimal is written as a fraction by using its place value. The decimal 0.375 means 375 thousandths, so it is 375/1000, which reduces to 3/8. A recurring decimal needs a short trick. For a single repeating digit, as in 0.444, the fraction is 4/9. For two repeating digits, as in 0.272727, the fraction is 27/99, which reduces to 3/11. For a decimal where a digit repeats after a non-repeating part, as in 0.1666, the value is 1/6. These three patterns cover nearly every recurring-decimal question that appears.

    Comparing fractions is a common question and there is a fast method. The surest way is to convert each fraction to a decimal and compare the values, since 5/8 equal to 0.625 is plainly greater than 3/5 equal to 0.6. A second method cross-multiplies the fractions, and a third gives all of them a common denominator. When several fractions must be arranged in order, converting to decimals is usually the quickest and the least error-prone, especially when the denominators are unrelated.

    Adding and subtracting fractions requires a common denominator. The smallest such denominator is the LCM of the given denominators, so to add 3/4 and 1/6 the LCM is 12 and the sum becomes 9/12 plus 2/12, which is 11/12. Multiplying fractions is simpler, because the numerators are multiplied together and the denominators together, so 2/3 times 3/4 equals 6/12, which is 1/2. Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal, so 3/7 divided by 9/14 becomes 3/7 times 14/9, which is 2/3.

    The LCM and the HCF extend naturally to fractions and are asked directly. The LCM of two fractions is the LCM of their numerators divided by the HCF of their denominators, so the LCM of 2/3 and 3/4 is 6 divided by 1, which is 6. The HCF of two fractions is the HCF of their numerators divided by the LCM of their denominators, so the HCF of 6/15 and 4/25 is 2 divided by 75, which is 2/75. These two formulas are worth memorising because they appear as direct one-mark questions.

    A decimal number carries its value in the position of each digit. The first place after the point is tenths, the second is hundredths, the third is thousandths, and so on. The number 0.0045 therefore has four digits after the point, and the digit 4 stands in the thousandths place. Reading the places correctly is what makes both conversion and rounding reliable, and it removes the guesswork from questions that ask for the number of decimal places.

    Multiplying decimals follows a simple counting rule. Multiply the two numbers as if they were whole numbers, then count the total number of decimal places in the two original numbers and put that many places in the answer. So 0.4 times 0.5 is found as 4 times 5 equal to 20, with two decimal places, giving 0.20, which is 0.2. Dividing by a decimal begins by shifting the point in both numbers to make the divisor a whole number, after which ordinary division gives the answer, so 0.36 divided by 0.6 becomes 36 divided by 60, which is 0.6.

    Rounding is a small skill that decides the answer to a surprising number of questions. To round to a given number of decimal places, look at the next digit; if it is 5 or more, raise the last kept digit by one, and if it is less than 5, leave it unchanged. So 0.375 to two places is 0.38, and 12.3456 to three places is 12.346. Rounding to the nearest whole number treats a fractional part of 0.5 or more as rounding up, so 3.5 becomes 4. Reading the question carefully tells you how many places are wanted.

    Fractions, decimals and percentages are three faces of one value, and the link is worth keeping ready. To turn a fraction into a percentage, multiply by 100, so 3/5 becomes 60 per cent and 5/8 becomes 62.5 per cent. To turn a percentage into a fraction, divide by 100 and reduce, so 25 per cent is 1/4 and 12.5 per cent is 1/8. To turn a decimal into a percentage, multiply by 100, so 0.2 becomes 20 per cent. These conversions turn up in profit, interest and data questions as well, so they pay off far beyond this chapter.

    Word problems in this chapter usually hide a division. If a rod 5/6 of a metre long is cut into pieces each 1/12 of a metre long, the number of pieces is found by dividing 5/6 by 1/12, which gives 10. If three-fourths of a number is 60, the number is 60 times 4 divided by 3, which is 80. If a man spends one-fourth and then one-third of 120 rupees, the money left is found by subtracting 30 and 40 from 120, which is 50. Recognising that the words describe a multiplication or a division is the whole skill.

    The chapter also carries a few direct facts. The value of 1 divided by 0 is undefined, because no number multiplied by 0 gives 1. A proper fraction is always less than one, and an improper fraction is always one or more. The reciprocal of a fraction flips it, so the reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2. Between any two numbers there are infinitely many fractions, because the average of any two fractions lies between them and the process can be repeated without end.

    The best way to prepare this chapter is to build speed on the small conversions and the four operations, and to keep the terminating-versus-recurring rule and the LCM and HCF of fractions at hand. Learn the common decimal values of the standard fractions, such as 1/8 equal to 0.125 and 5/8 equal to 0.625, so that comparison needs no calculation. With these tools the decimals and fractions questions in the paper become quick, safe marks.

    Key facts to remember

    • A proper fraction is less than 1; an improper fraction is 1 or more and can be written as a mixed number.
    • A fraction gives a terminating decimal only when, after removing the factors 2 and 5 from the denominator, only 1 remains.
    • Recurring decimal to fraction: one repeating digit gives d/9; two repeating digits give dd/99.
    • LCM of fractions = LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators.
    • HCF of fractions = HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators.
    • Add or subtract fractions after giving them the LCM of the denominators as a common denominator.
    • Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.
    • Multiplying decimals: multiply as whole numbers, then put the total number of decimal places in the answer.
    • Dividing by a decimal: shift the point in both numbers until the divisor is a whole number.
    • Rounding: look at the next digit; 5 or more rounds up, less than 5 stays.
    • Fraction to percentage: multiply by 100. Percentage to fraction: divide by 100 and reduce.
    • Learn standard values: 1/8 = 0.125, 3/8 = 0.375, 5/8 = 0.625, 1/16 = 0.0625.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I know whether a fraction gives a terminating or a recurring decimal?

    Reduce the fraction to its lowest terms and look at the denominator. If the denominator has only 2 and 5 as prime factors, the decimal terminates, as with 3/8 equal to 0.375. If any other prime remains, the decimal repeats, as with 1/3 equal to 0.333.

    What is the quickest way to compare two fractions?

    Convert each fraction to a decimal and compare the values, or cross-multiply. For 3/5 and 5/8, the decimals 0.6 and 0.625 show at once that 5/8 is greater, which is faster and safer than finding a common denominator.

    How do I divide by a decimal such as 0.04?

    Shift the decimal point in both numbers to the right until the divisor becomes a whole number, then divide. To find 1.2 divided by 0.04, shift two places so it becomes 120 divided by 4, which is 30.

    What is the LCM of two fractions?

    The LCM of two fractions is the LCM of their numerators divided by the HCF of their denominators. For 2/3 and 3/4 it is the LCM of 2 and 3, which is 6, divided by the HCF of 3 and 4, which is 1, giving 6.

    How do I change a recurring decimal such as 0.272727 into a fraction?

    Count the repeating digits. For two repeating digits write them over 99, so 0.272727 becomes 27/99, which reduces to 3/11. For one repeating digit write it over 9, so 0.444 becomes 4/9.

    ঘূৰণ এটা সৰু দক্ষতা যি আচৰিতভাৱে বহু প্ৰশ্নৰ উত্তৰ নিৰ্ধাৰণ কৰে। দিয়া দশমিক স্থানলৈ ঘূৰাবলৈ পিছৰ অংকটো চাওক; ই ৫ বা তাতকৈ বেছি হলে শেষত ৰখা অংকটো একেৰে বঢ়াওক, আৰু ৫ তকৈ কম হলে অলৰ ৰাখক। গতিকে ০.৩৭৫ দুটা স্থানলৈ ০.৩৮, আৰু ১২.৩৪৫৬ তিনিটা স্থানলৈ ১২.৩৪৬। আটাইতকৈ ওচৰৰ পূৰ্ণসংখ্যালৈ ঘূৰোৱাত ০.৫ বা তাতকৈ বেছি ভগ্নাংশ অংশ ওপৰলৈ ঘূৰে, গতিকে ৩.৫ = ৪। কিমান স্থান লাগে সেয়া প্ৰশ্নটো মন দি পঢ়িলেই জনা যায়।

    ভগ্নাংশ, দশমিক আৰু শতাংশ এটা মানৰ তিনিটা ৰূপ, আৰু এই সম্পৰ্কটো সাজু ৰখা উচিত। এটা ভগ্নাংশক শতাংশলৈ কৰিবলৈ ১০০ ৰে পূৰণ কৰক, গতিকে ৩/৫ = ৬০ শতাংশ আৰু ৫/৮ = ৬২.৫ শতাংশ। এটা শতাংশক ভগ্নাংশলৈ কৰিবলৈ ১০০ ৰে হৰণ কৰি সৰল কৰক, গতিকে ২৫ শতাংশ = ১/৪ আৰু ১২.৫ শতাংশ = ১/৮। এটা দশমিকক শতাংশলৈ কৰিবলৈ ১০০ ৰে পূৰণ কৰক, গতিকে ০.২ = ২০ শতাংশ। এই ৰূপান্তৰবোৰ লাভ, সুত আৰু তথ্য প্ৰশ্নতো আহে, গতিকে এইবোৰৰ লাভ এই অধ্যায়ৰ বাহিৰতো বহুত।

    এই অধ্যায়ৰ শব্দ-সমস্যাবোৰে সাধাৰণতে এটা হৰণ লুকুৱাই ৰাখে। ৫/৬ মিটাৰ দীঘল এডাল ১/১২ মিটাৰ দীঘল টুকুৰাত কাটিলে টুকুৰাৰ সংখ্যা ৫/৬ ক ১/১২ ৰে হৰণ কৰি পোৱা যায়, যি ১০। এটা সংখ্যাৰ তিনিভাগৰ চাৰি যদি ৬০ হয়, সংখ্যাটো ৬০ গুণ ৪ ÷ ৩, যি ৮০। এজন লোকে ১২০ টকাৰ প্ৰথমে এক-চতুৰ্থাংশ আৰু তাৰ পিছত এক-তৃতীয়াংশ খৰচ কৰিলে বাকী ধন ১২০ ৰ পৰা ৩০ আৰু ৪০ বিয়োগ কৰি পোৱা যায়, যি ৫০। শব্দবোৰে এটা পূৰণ বা এটা হৰণ বুজাইছে বুলি চিনি পোৱাটোৱেই গোটেই দক্ষতা।

    অধ্যায়টোত কেইটামান পোনপটীয়া তথ্যও আছে। ১ ক ০ ৰে হৰণ কৰাৰ মান অসংজ্ঞায়িত, কাৰণ ০ ৰে পূৰণ কৰি ১ নহয় এনে কোনো সংখ্যা নাই। এটা শুদ্ধ ভগ্নাংশ সদায় একতকৈ কম, আৰু এটা অশুদ্ধ ভগ্নাংশ সদায় এক বা তাতকৈ বেছি। এটা ভগ্নাংশৰ প্ৰতিক্ৰমে ইয়াক ওলোটা কৰে, গতিকে ২/৩ ৰ প্ৰতিক্ৰম ৩/২। যিকোনো দুটা সংখ্যাৰ মাজত অসীম ভগ্নাংশ থাকে, কাৰণ যিকোনো দুটা ভগ্নাংশৰ মাজৰ মান সিহঁতৰ মাজত থাকে আৰু প্ৰক্ৰিয়াটো অন্ত নোহোৱাকৈ পুনৰাবৃত্তি কৰিব পাৰি।

    এই অধ্যায়ৰ প্ৰস্তুতিৰ আটাইতকৈ ভাল উপায় হৈছে সৰু ৰূপান্তৰ আৰু চাৰিটা ক্ৰিয়াত গতি বঢ়োৱা, আৰু অন্ত-বনাম-আৱৰ্ত নিয়ম আৰু ভগ্নাংশৰ ল.সা.গু. আৰু গ.সা.গু. সাজু ৰখা। সাধাৰণ ভগ্নাংশৰ চিনাকি দশমিক মানবোৰ শিকি লওক, যেনে ১/৮ = ০.১২৫ আৰু ৫/৮ = ০.৬২৫, যাতে তুলনাত গণনাৰ প্ৰয়োজন নহয়। এই সঁজুলিবোৰৰ সৈতে প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ দশমিক আৰু ভগ্নাংশৰ প্ৰশ্নবোৰ দ্ৰুত আৰু নিৰাপদ নম্বৰ হৈ পৰে।

    মনত ৰখাৰ যোগ্য মূল তথ্য

    • এটা শুদ্ধ ভগ্নাংশ ১ তকৈ কম; এটা অশুদ্ধ ভগ্নাংশ ১ বা তাতকৈ বেছি আৰু মিশ্ৰ সংখ্যা হিচাপে লিখিব পাৰি।
    • এটা ভগ্নাংশে অন্ত দশমিক দিয়ে কেৱল যেতিয়া হৰৰ পৰা ২ আৰু ৫ উৎপাদক আঁতৰোৱাৰ পিছত কেৱল ১ থাকে।
    • আৱৰ্ত দশমিকৰ পৰা ভগ্নাংশ: এটা পুনৰাবৃত্তি অংকে d/৯ দিয়ে; দুটা পুনৰাবৃত্তি অংকে dd/৯৯ দিয়ে।
    • ভগ্নাংশৰ ল.সা.গু. = লৱৰ ল.সা.গু. / হৰৰ গ.সা.গু.।
    • ভগ্নাংশৰ গ.সা.গু. = লৱৰ গ.সা.গু. / হৰৰ ল.সা.গু.।
    • ভগ্নাংশ যোগ বা বিয়োগ কৰক হৰবোৰৰ ল.সা.গু. সাধাৰণ হৰ হিচাপে দিয়াৰ পিছত।
    • এটা ভগ্নাংশৰে হৰণ কৰা মানে ইয়াৰ প্ৰতিক্ৰমৰে পূৰণ কৰা।
    • দশমিক পূৰণ: পূৰ্ণসংখ্যা যেনেদৰে পূৰণ কৰক, তাৰ পিছত উত্তৰত মুঠ দশমিক স্থানৰ সংখ্যা ৰাখক।
    • দশমিকৰে হৰণ: হৰণক পূৰ্ণসংখ্যা নোহোৱালৈকে দুয়োটা সংখ্যাত বিন্দু লুটিয়াওক।
    • ঘূৰণ: পিছৰ অংকটো চাওক; ৫ বা তাতকৈ বেছি ওপৰলৈ ঘূৰে, ৫ তকৈ কম অলৰ থাকে।
    • ভগ্নাংশৰ পৰা শতাংশ: ১০০ ৰে পূৰণ কৰক। শতাংশৰ পৰা ভগ্নাংশ: ১০০ ৰে হৰণ কৰি সৰল কৰক।
    • চিনাকি মান শিকক: ১/৮ = ০.১২৫, ৩/৮ = ০.৩৭৫, ৫/৮ = ০.৬২৫, ১/১৬ = ০.০৬২৫।

    সততে সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    এটা ভগ্নাংশে অন্ত নাইবা আৱৰ্ত দশমিক দিয়ে কেনেকৈ জানিম?

    ভগ্নাংশটো আটাইতকৈ সৰল ৰূপলৈ কমাই হৰটো চাওক। হৰত কেৱল ২ আৰু ৫ মৌলিক উৎপাদক থাকিলে দশমিক অন্ত হয়, যেনে ৩/৮ = ০.৩৭৫। আন কোনো মৌলিক থাকিলে দশমিক পুনৰাবৃত্তি হয়, যেনে ১/৩ = ০.৩৩৩।

    দুটা ভগ্নাংশ তুলনা কৰাৰ আটাইতকৈ দ্ৰুত উপায় কি?

    প্ৰতিটো ভগ্নাংশক দশমিকলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰি মানবোৰ তুলনা কৰক, নাইবা ক্ৰছ-গুণ কৰক। ৩/৫ আৰু ৫/৮ ৰ বাবে দশমিক ০.৬ আৰু ০.৬২৫ এ তৎক্ষণাত দেখুৱায় ৫/৮ ডাঙৰ, যি সাধাৰণ হৰ বিচৰাতকৈ দ্ৰুত আৰু নিৰাপদ।

    ০.০৪ ৰ দৰে দশমিকৰে কেনেকৈ হৰণ কৰিম?

    হৰণক এটা পূৰ্ণসংখ্যা নোহোৱালৈকে দুয়োটা সংখ্যাত দশমিক বিন্দু সোঁফালে লুটিয়াওক, তাৰ পিছত হৰণ কৰক। ১.২ ÷ ০.০৪ বিচাৰিবলৈ দুটা স্থান লুটিয়াই ১২০ ÷ ৪ কৰক, যি ৩০।

    দুটা ভগ্নাংশৰ ল.সা.গু. কি?

    দুটা ভগ্নাংশৰ ল.সা.গু. হৈছে সিহঁতৰ লৱৰ ল.সা.গু. হৰৰ গ.সা.গু.ৰে হৰণ। ২/৩ আৰু ৩/৪ ৰ বাবে ই ২ আৰু ৩ ৰ ল.সা.গু. ৬, ৩ আৰু ৪ ৰ গ.সা.গু. ১ ৰে হৰণ, যি ৬।

    ০.২৭২৭২৭ ৰ দৰে আৱৰ্ত দশমিকক ভগ্নাংশলৈ কেনেকৈ সলনিম?

    পুনৰাবৃত্তি অংকবোৰ গণনা কৰক। দুটা পুনৰাবৃত্তি অংকৰ বাবে সিহঁতক ৯৯ ৰ ওপৰত লিখক, গতিকে ০.২৭২৭২৭ = ২৭/৯৯, যি ৩/১১ লৈ সৰল হয়। এটা পুনৰাবৃত্তি অংকৰ বাবে ইয়াক ৯ ৰ ওপৰত লিখক, গতিকে ০.৪৪৪ = ৪/৯।

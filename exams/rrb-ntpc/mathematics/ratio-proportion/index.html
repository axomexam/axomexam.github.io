    Ratio & Proportion for the RRB NTPC CBT

    Ratio and proportion form the language in which a large part of the RRB NTPC Mathematics paper is written. A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind by division, so a ratio of 3 to 4 means that the first quantity is three quarters of the second, or that the two are in the proportion 3 is to 4. Proportion is only the statement that two ratios are equal. Once this vocabulary is clear, the questions turn into simple arithmetic, and the same ideas come back in partnership, mixtures, ages and speed problems.

    A ratio must always be written in its simplest form, with whole numbers and no common factor. The ratio 0.6 to 0.8 is the same as 6 to 8, which reduces to 3 to 4. Ratios of fractions are cleared by multiplying both terms by the least common multiple of the denominators, so the ratio 3/4 to 5/6 becomes 9 to 10. A ratio has no units, because the quantities compared are of the same kind; the units cancel in the division. Keeping the ratio in lowest terms is not only tidy, it usually makes the later steps much easier.

    Dividing a quantity in a given ratio is the most common direct question. If a sum is to be divided in the ratio a to b, the total is first split into a plus b equal parts, and each share is the total divided by that number of parts and then multiplied by its own term. For a sum of 735 divided in the ratio 2 to 3 to 4, the parts are nine, so each part is 81, and the three shares are 162, 243 and 324. The same method extends to any number of terms, and a question that gives one share and asks for the total simply divides that share by its term to find one part.

    Finding a missing term is the heart of proportion. In the proportion a is to b as c is to d, the product of the means equals the product of the extremes, so a times d equals b times c. This cross-multiplication solves every question that gives three terms and asks for the fourth, which is called the fourth proportional. If the two middle terms are equal, as in a is to b as b is to c, then b is the mean proportional between a and c, and its value is the square root of the product of a and c. The third proportional to a and b is the term c such that a is to b as b is to c.

    Chained ratios are a favourite of the examiners. When a to b and b to c are given separately, the two must be joined by making the value of the common term b the same in both. If a to b is 2 to 3 and b to c is 4 to 5, multiply the first ratio by 4 and the second by 3 to get a to b as 8 to 12 and b to c as 12 to 15, so a to b to c is 8 to 12 to 15. Chaining three or more ratios works the same way, and the value of a to d is simply the product of the corresponding terms, which is often the fastest route to the answer.

    Several derived ratios have names and appear as direct questions. The duplicate ratio of a to b is the ratio of their squares, the sub-duplicate ratio is the ratio of their square roots, the triplicate ratio is the ratio of their cubes, and the compound ratio of two ratios is the ratio of the products of their corresponding terms. The ratio of the areas of two similar figures is the duplicate of the ratio of their sides, and the ratio of the volumes is the triplicate, so a question that gives the ratio of two squares or circles is answered by taking the square root to get the ratio of the sides or radii.

    Many questions give a ratio together with a condition that is added, subtracted or changed, and ask for the numbers. The method is to write the numbers as the ratio terms multiplied by a single unknown, then translate the condition into an equation in that unknown and solve it. If two numbers are in the ratio 5 to 3 and the ratio becomes 7 to 5 after 6 is added to each, write the numbers as 5x and 3x, set 5x plus 6 to 3x plus 6 equal to 7 to 5, cross-multiply and solve for x. The same template answers every age problem in which the ratio changes after a fixed number of years.

    The comparison of two persons through a ratio has its own small rule. If two quantities are in a given ratio and one of them is also related to a third quantity, the two relations must be joined through the common quantity. A frequent shape gives the ratio of two numbers and their sum or their difference. If the ratio is 5 to 7 and the sum is 144, the parts are twelve and each part is 12, so the numbers are 60 and 84. If the difference is given instead, the difference corresponds to the difference of the ratio terms, so a difference of 125 with the ratio 3 to 8 means five parts, each of 25, and the numbers 75 and 200.

    Word problems in partnership, mixtures and speeds all reduce to the same machinery. In a partnership the profit is shared in the ratio of the capital when the periods are equal, and in the ratio of capital multiplied by time when they are not. In a mixture the quantities of the components are in a given ratio, so the amount of any component is its term over the sum of the terms, multiplied by the total quantity. In speed problems the distances covered in the same time are in the ratio of the speeds, and the speeds themselves are in the ratio of the distances covered in the same time. Recognising which two quantities are being compared is the whole skill.

    Some questions are disguised proportions rather than ratios of parts. A statement that a certain number of workers can do a job in a certain number of days lets the number of workers and the number of days be compared through the total work, which stays fixed. Similarly, a statement about the cost of a certain quantity of an item fixes the rate, so any other quantity can be priced at once. These are proportion questions in words, and writing the two quantities as a ratio and equating it to the known ratio is the safest method, because it removes any doubt about whether the quantities are directly or inversely related.

    Finally, ratio and proportion reward neat setting out more than any other chapter. Always reduce a ratio to its lowest terms, always clear the fractions before comparing, and always write the unknown as a single symbol multiplying the ratio terms. The cross-multiplication rule, the mean and third proportional, the joining of chained ratios and the template for a changing ratio cover almost every question in the paper. With these ready, the ratio questions become quick and accurate, and the same tools carry straight over into partnership, mixtures, ages and speed, making this chapter one of the most useful in the whole syllabus.

    Key facts to remember

    • A ratio compares two like quantities by division and must be reduced to its lowest terms with whole numbers.
    • To divide a sum in the ratio a : b, split the total into a + b parts and give each share its own term.
    • In a proportion a : b = c : d, the product of the means equals the product of the extremes, so ad = bc.
    • The fourth proportional to a, b, c is bc ÷ a; the mean proportional between a and c is √(ac); the third proportional to a and b is b² ÷ a.
    • To join a : b and b : c, make the common term b equal in both, then read off a : b : c.
    • The duplicate ratio is the ratio of the squares, the sub-duplicate of the square roots, and the triplicate of the cubes.
    • When a ratio changes after adding or subtracting the same amount, write the numbers as the ratio terms times a single unknown and solve.
    • With a ratio and the sum, each part is the sum divided by the number of parts; with the difference, the difference corresponds to the difference of the ratio terms.
    • Profit in a partnership is shared in the ratio of capital × time.
    • Distances covered in the same time are in the ratio of the speeds.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I divide a sum in a given ratio?

    Add the terms of the ratio to get the number of equal parts, divide the total by that number to find one part, then multiply one part by each term. For 735 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4, one part is 735 ÷ 9 = 81, so the shares are 162, 243 and 324.

    What is the difference between the mean, third and fourth proportional?

    The fourth proportional to a, b, c is bc ÷ a. The mean proportional between a and c is √(ac), used when a : b = b : c. The third proportional to a and b is b² ÷ a, the c such that a : b = b : c. Each is a one-line formula once the shape is recognised.

    How do I join two ratios that share a common term?

    Make the value of the common term the same in both ratios by multiplying each ratio by a suitable number, then write the three terms in order. For a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, multiply to get a : b = 8 : 12 and b : c = 12 : 15, so a : b : c = 8 : 12 : 15.

    How do I solve a question where the ratio changes after adding a number?

    Write the numbers as the ratio terms multiplied by a single unknown, translate the condition into an equation, and solve. If the ratio 5 : 3 becomes 7 : 5 after adding 6, the numbers are 5x and 3x, and (5x + 6) : (3x + 6) = 7 : 5 gives x = 3.

    How are ratios used in partnership and speed problems?

    In a partnership the profit is shared in the ratio of capital × time, so equal periods mean the profit follows the capital. In speed problems the distances covered in the same time are in the ratio of the speeds, which turns many word problems into a simple ratio of two numbers.

    অংশীদাৰিত্ব, মিশ্ৰণ আৰু গতিৰ শব্দ-সমস্যা গোটেইখিনি একেই যন্ত্ৰলৈ হ্ৰাস হয়। অংশীদাৰিত্বত সময় সমান হ'লে লাভ মূলধনৰ অনুপাতত ভগাই লোৱা হয়, আৰু সময় অসমান হ'লে মূলধন গুণ সময়ৰ অনুপাতত। মিশ্ৰণত উপাদানবোৰৰ পৰিমাণ এটা দিয়া অনুপাতত, গতিকে যিকোনো উপাদানৰ পৰিমাণ তাৰ পদ ভাগ যোগফল পদ, গোটেইটো মুঠ পৰিমাণৰে পূৰণ। গতিৰ সমস্যাত একে সময়ত অতিক্ৰম কৰা দূৰত্ব গতিৰ অনুপাতত, আৰু গতি নিজেই একে সময়ত অতিক্ৰম কৰা দূৰত্বৰ অনুপাতত। কোন দুটা ৰাশি তুলনা কৰা হৈছে তাক চিনি পোৱাটোৱেই গোটেই দক্ষতা।

    কিছুমান প্ৰশ্ন অংশৰ অনুপাত নহয়, ছদ্মবেশী সমানুপাত। এটা নিৰ্দিষ্ট সংখ্যক শ্ৰমিকে এটা নিৰ্দিষ্ট সংখ্যক দিনত এটা কাম কৰিব পাৰে বুলি কোৱা বাক্যই শ্ৰমিকৰ সংখ্যা আৰু দিনৰ সংখ্যাক মুঠ কামৰ জৰিয়তে তুলনা কৰিব দিয়ে, যি স্থিৰ থাকে। একেইদৰে, এটা বস্তুৰ এটা নিৰ্দিষ্ট পৰিমাণৰ দামৰ বাক্যই হাৰ স্থিৰ কৰে, গতিকে আন যিকোনো পৰিমাণৰ দাম লগে লগে পোৱা যায়। এইবোৰ শব্দত সমানুপাতৰ প্ৰশ্ন, আৰু দুটা ৰাশিক এটা অনুপাত হিচাপে লিখি জনা অনুপাতৰ সৈতে সমান কৰাটো আটাইতকৈ নিৰাপদ পদ্ধতি, কাৰণ ই ৰাশিবোৰ প্ৰত্যক্ষ বা ব্যস্তভাৱে জড়িত নেকি সেই সন্দেহ আঁতৰায়।

    শেষত, অনুপাত আৰু সমানুপাতে আন যিকোনো অধ্যায়তকৈ পৰিষ্কাৰ সজ্জাক পুৰস্কৃত কৰে। এটা অনুপাত সদায় আটাইতকৈ কম পদলৈ সৰল কৰক, তুলনা কৰাৰ আগতে ভগ্নাংশ আঁতৰাওক, আৰু অজ্ঞাতক অনুপাতৰ পদবোৰ পূৰণ কৰা এটা চিহ্ন হিচাপে লিখক। বজ্ৰ-গুণনৰ নিয়ম, মধ্যসমানুপাতী আৰু তৃতীয় সমানুপাতী, শৃংখলাবদ্ধ অনুপাত জোৰা লগোৱা, আৰু সলনি হোৱা অনুপাতৰ সাঁচে প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ প্ৰায় প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্ন সামৰি লয়। এইবোৰ সাজু থাকিলে অনুপাতৰ প্ৰশ্নবোৰ দ্ৰুত আৰু সঠিক হয়, আৰু একেই সঁজুলি অংশীদাৰিত্ব, মিশ্ৰণ, বয়স আৰু গতিলৈ পোনপটীয়াকৈ যায়, যাৰ ফলত এই অধ্যায়টো গোটেই পাঠ্যক্ৰমৰ আটাইতকৈ উপযোগী অধ্যায়বোৰৰ এটা হৈ পৰে।

    মনত ৰখাৰ যোগ্য মূল তথ্য

    • এটা অনুপাতে একে ধৰণৰ দুটা ৰাশিক হৰণৰ জৰিয়তে তুলনা কৰে আৰু পূৰ্ণসংখ্যাৰে আটাইতকৈ কম পদলৈ সৰল কৰিব লাগে।
    • এটা ধন a : b অনুপাতত ভগাবলৈ, মুঠক a + b ভাগত ভগাই প্ৰতিটো অংশক তাৰ নিজৰ পদ দিয়ক।
    • a : b = c : d সমানুপাতত, মধ্যপদবোৰৰ গুণফল চৰমপদবোৰৰ গুণফলৰ সমান, গতিকে ad = bc।
    • a, b, c ৰ চতুৰ্থ সমানুপাতী bc ÷ a; a আৰু c ৰ মধ্যসমানুপাতী √(ac); a আৰু b ৰ তৃতীয় সমানুপাতী b² ÷ a।
    • a : b আৰু b : c জোৰা লাগাবলৈ, সাধাৰণ পদ b দুয়োটা অনুপাতত একে কৰি a : b : c পঢ়ি লওক।
    • দ্বিগুণ অনুপাত বৰ্গৰ অনুপাত, উপ-দ্বিগুণ বৰ্গমূলৰ, আৰু ত্ৰিগুণ ঘনৰ।
    • একে পৰিমাণ যোগ বা বিয়োগ কৰাৰ পিছত অনুপাত সলনি হ'লে, সংখ্যাবোৰ অনুপাতৰ পদ গুণ এটা অজ্ঞাত হিচাপে লিখি সমাধান কৰক।
    • অনুপাত আৰু যোগফলৰ সৈতে, প্ৰতিটো ভাগ মুঠক ভাগৰ সংখ্যাৰে হৰণ; পাৰ্থক্যৰ সৈতে, পাৰ্থক্যটো অনুপাতৰ পদবোৰৰ পাৰ্থক্যৰ সমান।
    • অংশীদাৰিত্বত লাভ মূলধন × সময়ৰ অনুপাতত ভগাই লোৱা হয়।
    • একে সময়ত অতিক্ৰম কৰা দূৰত্ব গতিৰ অনুপাতত।

    সততে সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    দিয়া অনুপাতত এটা ধন কেনেকৈ ভগাম?

    অনুপাতৰ পদবোৰ যোগ কৰি সমান ভাগৰ সংখ্যা পোৱা যায়, মুঠক সেই সংখ্যাৰে হৰণ কৰি এটা ভাগ বিচাৰি পোৱা যায়, তাৰ পিছত এটা ভাগক প্ৰতিটো পদেৰে পূৰণ কৰক। ৭৩৫ ক ২ : ৩ : ৪ অনুপাতত হ'লে এটা ভাগ ৭৩৫ ÷ ৯ = ৮১, গতিকে অংশ ১৬২, ২৪৩ আৰু ৩২৪।

    মধ্যসমানুপাতী, তৃতীয় আৰু চতুৰ্থ সমানুপাতীৰ মাজৰ পাৰ্থক্য কি?

    a, b, c ৰ চতুৰ্থ সমানুপাতী bc ÷ a। a আৰু c ৰ মধ্যসমানুপাতী √(ac), যেতিয়া a : b = b : c তেতিয়া ব্যৱহাৰ হয়। a আৰু b ৰ তৃতীয় সমানুপাতী b² ÷ a, অৰ্থাৎ c যাতে a : b = b : c। ৰূপটো চিনি পালে প্ৰতিটোৱে এক-শাৰীৰ সূত্ৰ।

    এটা সাধাৰণ পদ ভাগ কৰা দুটা অনুপাত কেনেকৈ জোৰা লগাম?

    প্ৰতিটো অনুপাতক উপযুক্ত সংখ্যাৰে পূৰণ কৰি সাধাৰণ পদৰ মান দুয়োটা অনুপাতত একে কৰক, তাৰ পিছত তিনিটা পদ ক্ৰমত লিখক। a : b = ২ : ৩ আৰু b : c = ৪ : ৫ হ'লে, পূৰণ কৰি a : b = ৮ : ১২ আৰু b : c = ১২ : ১৫, গতিকে a : b : c = ৮ : ১২ : ১৫।

    এটা সংখ্যা যোগ কৰাৰ পিছত অনুপাত সলনি হোৱা প্ৰশ্ন কেনেকৈ সমাধান কৰিম?

    সংখ্যাবোৰ অনুপাতৰ পদবোৰ এটা অজ্ঞাত ৰাশিৰে পূৰণ কৰি লিখক, চৰ্তটোক এটা সমীকৰণলৈ অনুবাদ কৰক, আৰু সমাধান কৰক। ৬ যোগ কৰাৰ পিছত ৫ : ৩ অনুপাত ৭ : ৫ হ'লে, সংখ্যা ৫x আৰু ৩x, আৰু (৫x + ৬) : (৩x + ৬) = ৭ : ৫ ৰ পৰা x = ৩।

    অংশীদাৰিত্ব আৰু গতিৰ সমস্যাত অনুপাত কেনেকৈ ব্যৱহাৰ হয়?

    অংশীদাৰিত্বত লাভ মূলধন × সময়ৰ অনুপাতত ভগাই লোৱা হয়, গতিকে সমান সময় মানে লাভ মূলধন অনুসৰি যায়। গতিৰ সমস্যাত একে সময়ত অতিক্ৰম কৰা দূৰত্ব গতিৰ অনুপাতত, যিয়ে বহু শব্দ-সমস্যাক দুটা সংখ্যাৰ সৰল অনুপাতলৈ পৰিণত কৰে।

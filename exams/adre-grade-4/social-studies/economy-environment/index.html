      Sample questions with answers

      1. Which sector employs the largest share of India's workforce?
        Options: Industry / Agriculture / Services / Mining
        Answer: Agriculture
        Explanation: Agriculture is the largest employer in India, engaging nearly half of the workforce.
      2. Which sector contributes the largest share to India's GDP?
        Options: Agriculture / Industry / Mining / Services
        Answer: Services
        Explanation: The services sector contributes the largest share to India's GDP.
      3. The primary sector of the economy includes which activities?
        Options: Agriculture, mining and fishing / Manufacturing and construction / Banking and insurance / Software services
        Answer: Agriculture, mining and fishing
        Explanation: The primary sector involves direct use of natural resources like farming, mining and fishing.
      4. The secondary sector of the economy is mainly concerned with:
        Options: Agriculture / Banking / Tourism / Manufacturing and industry
        Answer: Manufacturing and industry
        Explanation: The secondary sector transforms raw materials into finished goods through manufacturing.
      5. The tertiary sector is also known as:
        Options: Agriculture sector / Manufacturing sector / Service sector / Mining sector
        Answer: Service sector
        Explanation: The tertiary sector is the service sector, including banking, transport and IT.

      Sample questions with answers

      1. When was the Indian Constitution adopted?
        Options: 26 November 1949 / 9 December 1946 / 26 January 1950 / 15 August 1947
        Answer: 26 November 1949
        Explanation: The Constitution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on 26 November 1949.
      2. When did the Indian Constitution come into force?
        Options: 2 October 1950 / 26 January 1950 / 26 November 1949 / 15 August 1947
        Answer: 26 January 1950
        Explanation: The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, celebrated as Republic Day.
      3. Who was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constitution?
        Options: Sardar Patel / Dr. Rajendra Prasad / Dr. B.R. Ambedkar / Jawaharlal Nehru
        Answer: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
        Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.
      4. Who was the President of the Constituent Assembly?
        Options: Dr. S. Radhakrishnan / Jawaharlal Nehru / Dr. B.R. Ambedkar / Dr. Rajendra Prasad
        Answer: Dr. Rajendra Prasad
        Explanation: Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the permanent President of the Constituent Assembly.
      5. Who moved the Objectives Resolution in the Constituent Assembly?
        Options: Jawaharlal Nehru / Dr. B.R. Ambedkar / Dr. Rajendra Prasad / Sardar Patel
        Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru
        Explanation: Jawaharlal Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution on 13 December 1946.

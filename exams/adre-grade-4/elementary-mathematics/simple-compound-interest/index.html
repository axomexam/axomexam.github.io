      Sample questions with answers

      1. Find the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.
        Options: ₹500 / ₹1,000 / ₹1,100 / ₹1,200
        Answer: ₹1,000
        Explanation: SI = (P × R × T) / 100 = (5000 × 10 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000.
      2. Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 5% per annum for 3 years.
        Options: ₹1,200 / ₹1,000 / ₹1,400 / ₹1,500
        Answer: ₹1,200
        Explanation: SI = (8000 × 5 × 3) / 100 = ₹1,200.
      3. Find the simple interest on ₹2,400 at 7% per annum for 4 years.
        Options: ₹640 / ₹700 / ₹672 / ₹680
        Answer: ₹672
        Explanation: SI = (2400 × 7 × 4) / 100 = ₹672.
      4. Find the amount when ₹4,000 is invested at 8% simple interest per annum for 3 years.
        Options: ₹4,800 / ₹4,880 / ₹4,900 / ₹4,960
        Answer: ₹4,960
        Explanation: SI = (4000 × 8 × 3) / 100 = ₹960; Amount = 4000 + 960 = ₹4,960.
      5. Find the principal if the simple interest for 2 years at 5% per annum is ₹300.
        Options: ₹2,500 / ₹3,000 / ₹3,500 / ₹2,800
        Answer: ₹3,000
        Explanation: P = (SI × 100) / (R × T) = (300 × 100) / (5 × 2) = ₹3,000.

      Sample questions with answers

      1. A car covers 180 km in 3 hours. What is its speed?
        Options: 50 km/h / 90 km/h / 60 km/h / 45 km/h
        Answer: 60 km/h
        Explanation: Speed = Distance / Time = 180 / 3 = 60 km/h.
      2. A train covers 240 km in 4 hours. Find its speed.
        Options: 60 km/h / 50 km/h / 48 km/h / 72 km/h
        Answer: 60 km/h
        Explanation: Speed = 240 / 4 = 60 km/h.
      3. Find the distance covered by a car moving at 45 km/h for 4 hours.
        Options: 160 km / 180 km / 200 km / 150 km
        Answer: 180 km
        Explanation: Distance = Speed × Time = 45 × 4 = 180 km.
      4. How long will a cyclist take to cover 90 km at 15 km/h?
        Options: 5 hours / 8 hours / 4 hours / 6 hours
        Answer: 6 hours
        Explanation: Time = Distance / Speed = 90 / 15 = 6 hours.
      5. Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
        Options: 18 m/s / 25 m/s / 20 m/s / 22 m/s
        Answer: 20 m/s
        Explanation: 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.

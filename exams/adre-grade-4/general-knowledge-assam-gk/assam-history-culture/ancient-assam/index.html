    Q1. Who was the founder of the Varman dynasty of ancient Kamarupa?

    (A) Salastambha শালস্তম্ভ

    Ancient Assam

    Ancient Assam is one of the important topics of the Assam History and Culture part of the ADRE 3.0 Grade IV General Knowledge and Assam GK paper. The topic covers the earliest period of Assam's history, from the legendary times described in the epics down to the end of the Pala dynasty of Kamarupa around the twelfth century. In this long span the region was known first as Pragjyotisha and later as Kamarupa, and it was ruled by a series of dynasties such as the Danavas, the Varmans, the Salastambhas and the Palas. The topic tests the candidate's knowledge of the ruling houses, their great kings, their capitals and the literary and inscriptional sources from which their history is known. Because the questions are usually direct and factual, a clear time-line in the mind is the surest way to score in this topic.

    Meaning and Time-line of Ancient Assam

    Ancient Assam means the period before the arrival of the Ahoms in the thirteenth century. In the earliest descriptions the region was called Pragjyotisha, the land of eastern light and astronomy, and it was believed to be powerful and prosperous. From about the fourth century the name Kamarupa came into general use, and it continued until the medieval period. The main dynasties of this age ruled one after another, and the period of each one is remembered through stone inscriptions, copper-plate grants, coins and the accounts of foreign travellers. A simple time-line running from the legendary kings to the Palas makes the whole topic easy to revise.

    Main Dynasties of Ancient Assam

    • The legendary Danava dynasty, remembered as the earliest rulers of Pragjyotisha.
    • Naraka, the legendary king associated with the founding of the Kamakhya tradition.
    • Bhagadatta, the king of Pragjyotisha who fought in the Mahabharata war.
    • The Varman dynasty, from about 350 to 650, founded by Pushyavarman.
    • The Salastambha dynasty, from about 650 to 900, founded by Salastambha.
    • The Pala dynasty of Kamarupa, from about 900 to 1100, founded by Brahma Pala.
    • The later Kamata kingdom, which grew out of the old Kamarupa region.

    Sources of Ancient Assam's History

    The history of ancient Assam is known from several kinds of sources. The Varman kings issued the Nidhanpur copper-plate grant and the Dubi and other inscriptions. The Kamakhya temple, the Umachal rock inscription and the Ambari and other sites give evidence of the period. The Allahabad pillar inscription of Samudragupta mentions Kamarupa as a frontier kingdom. The Chinese traveller Xuanzang, also known as Hiuen Tsang, visited Kamarupa in the seventh century and left a valuable account. The Puranas, the Mahabharata and the Ramayana also contain references to Pragjyotisha. Coins and archaeological finds complete the picture.

    Important Kings and their Contributions

    Pushyavarman was the founder of the Varman dynasty and is regarded as the first historical ruler of Kamarupa. His descendants extended the kingdom and took part in the politics of northern India. Bhaskarvarman, the greatest of the Varmans, ruled in the seventh century and was a friend and ally of Harshavardhana of Kanauj; he invited Xuanzang to Kamarupa and patronised learning. After the Varmans, Salastambha founded a new dynasty and shifted the capital to Haruppeswara, in the region of present Tezpur. The Pala dynasty of Kamarupa followed, with Brahma Pala as its founder and Jaya Pala as its last important king, and its capital lay at Kamarupanagara, near present Guwahati.

    How This Topic Appears in the Examination

    Ancient Assam is usually asked as a small group of objective questions carrying one mark each. The questions ask for the founder of a dynasty, the capital of a king, the author of an inscription, the name of a foreign traveller or the title of a great ruler. Because the facts are short, precise and limited in number, this topic gives good returns for the time spent. A candidate who remembers the order of the dynasties and the key kings of each is unlikely to lose marks here.

    Memory Tips

    Remember the dynasties in order as Varman, Salastambha and Pala, and remember one great king for each, namely Bhaskarvarman, Salastambha and Brahma Pala. Attach every fact to a place, such as Pragjyotishpura, Haruppeswara and Kamarupanagara, because place names fix the memory. Learn the foreign traveller Xuanzang along with Bhaskarvarman, since the two are often asked together. Keep the legendary and the historical periods separate so that the epic kings are not confused with the Varman kings. A short handwritten time-line is the best revision aid for this topic.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Confusing Pragjyotisha with Kamarupa and treating the two names as different places.
    • Mixing the founder of one dynasty with that of another.
    • Forgetting the capital of a dynasty.
    • Placing the legendary king Bhagadatta among the historical Varman kings.
    • Confusing the Kamarupa Palas with the Pala rulers of Bengal.

    How to Prepare This Topic

    1. Draw a time-line of the dynasties from the legendary period to the Palas.
    2. For each dynasty, note the founder, the greatest king and the capital.
    3. Learn the important inscriptions, grants and the traveller's account.
    4. Revise the topic along with the Ahom and Koch kingdoms that came later.
    5. Test yourself with objective questions once a week.

    Additional Points to Remember

    The name Pragjyotisha is older than the name Kamarupa, and the change of name reflects the growing importance of the region in the early centuries of the Christian era. The Varman kings claimed descent from Naraka and Bhagadatta to connect themselves with the ancient epic tradition. The Nidhanpur grant is an important record of land grants made by Bhaskarvarman. The Salastambha kings moved the centre of power to the north bank of the Brahmaputra near Tezpur, and the later Palas ruled from the region of present Guwahati. Together these shifts show how the political centre of Kamarupa moved over time.

    Advanced Tips

    When a question mentions a copper-plate grant, connect it with the dynasty that issued it, because grants were a way for kings to record their rule. When a foreign traveller is mentioned, remember the king he met and the century in which he travelled. Do not confuse the Kamarupa Palas with the Palas of Bengal, since the two are different dynasties that ruled in different regions, though they shared a name. If a question asks for the last king of a dynasty, remember Jaya Pala for the Kamarupa Palas. Revising the map of the old capitals, Pragjyotishpura, Haruppeswara and Kamarupanagara, along the Brahmaputra helps to fix the whole topic in memory.

    Related Topics

    Ancient Assam is closely related to the other topics of Assam's History and Culture. It leads naturally to the Ahom and Koch kingdoms, which followed the older Kamarupa rulers. It shares its sources with the study of Assamese literature, since the inscriptions and grants are among the earliest examples of the language. It also connects with Assam's folk culture, because legends such as those of Naraka and Bhagadatta survived in local tradition. Studying these topics together gives a continuous picture of Assamese history and culture.

    প্ৰাগজ্যোতিষ নামটো কামৰূপ নামতকৈ পুৰণি, আৰু নামৰ এই পৰিৱৰ্তনে খ্ৰীষ্টীয় যুগৰ আৰম্ভণিৰ শতিকাবোৰত অঞ্চলটোৰ বঢ়া গুৰুত্ব প্ৰতিফলিত কৰে। বৰ্মন ৰজাসকলে নিজকে প্ৰাচীন মহাকাব্যিক পৰম্পৰাৰ সৈতে জড়াবলৈ নৰক আৰু ভগদত্তৰ বংশধৰ বুলি দাবী কৰিছিল। নিধানপুৰ ফলি ভাস্কৰবৰ্মনে দিয়া ভূমি-দানৰ এটা গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ অভিলেখ। শালস্তম্ভ ৰজাসকলে শক্তিৰ কেন্দ্ৰ ব্ৰহ্মপুত্ৰৰ উত্তৰ পাৰে তেজপুৰৰ ওচৰলৈ স্থানান্তৰ কৰিছিল, আৰু পিছৰ পালসকলে বৰ্তমান গুৱাহাটীৰ অঞ্চলৰপৰা ৰাজত্ব কৰিছিল। এই স্থানান্তৰবোৰে একেলগে দেখুৱায় যে কামৰূপৰ ৰাজনৈতিক কেন্দ্ৰ সময়ৰ লগে লগে কেনেকৈ লৰচৰ হৈছিল।

    উন্নত টিপছ

    এটা প্ৰশ্নত তামৰ ফলিৰ কথা উল্লেখ থাকিলে, ইয়াক জাৰি কৰা বংশৰ সৈতে জড়াই লওক, কাৰণ ফলি আছিল ৰজাসকলে নিজৰ ৰাজত্ব লিপিবদ্ধ কৰাৰ এটা উপায়। এজন বিদেশী পৰিব্ৰাজকৰ কথা উল্লেখ থাকিলে, তেওঁ দেখা ৰজা আৰু তেওঁ ভ্ৰমণ কৰা শতিকাটো মনত ৰাখক। কামৰূপৰ পালসকলক বংগৰ পালৰ সৈতে খেলিমেলি নকৰিব, কাৰণ দুয়োটা বেলেগ অঞ্চলত ৰাজত্ব কৰা বেলেগ বংশ, যদিও নাম একে। প্ৰশ্নই এটা বংশৰ শেষ ৰজা সোধালে, কামৰূপৰ পালসকলৰ বাবে জয়পাল মনত ৰাখক। পুৰণি ৰাজধানী প্ৰাগজ্যোতিষপুৰ, হৰুপ্পেশ্বৰ আৰু কামৰূপনগৰ ব্ৰহ্মপুত্ৰৰ কাষেৰে এখন মানচিত্ৰত পুনৰালোচনা কৰিলে গোটেই বিষয়টো স্মৃতিত স্থিৰ হয়।

    সম্পৰ্কিত বিষয়

    প্ৰাচীন অসম অসমৰ ইতিহাস আৰু সংস্কৃতিৰ আন বিষয়বোৰৰ সৈতে ঘনিষ্ঠভাৱে জড়িত। ই সহজেই আহোম আৰু কোচ ৰাজ্যলৈ লৈ যায়, যি পুৰণি কামৰূপ শাসকসকলৰ পিছত আহিছিল। ইয়াৰ উৎসবোৰ অসমীয়া সাহিত্যৰ অধ্যয়নৰ সৈতে ভাগ কৰে, কাৰণ শিলালিপি আৰু ফলিবোৰ ভাষাৰ আটাইতকৈ প্ৰাচীন নিদৰ্শনৰ ভিতৰত। ই অসমৰ লোকসংস্কৃতিৰ সৈতেও জড়িত, কাৰণ নৰক আৰু ভগদত্তৰ দৰে কিংবদন্তি স্থানীয় পৰম্পৰাত জীয়াই আছিল। এই বিষয়বোৰ একেলগে অধ্যয়ন কৰিলে অসমীয়া ইতিহাস আৰু সংস্কৃতিৰ এটা ধাৰাবাহিক ছবি পোৱা যায়।

    দ্ৰুত পুনৰালোচনা তালিকা

    • প্ৰাগজ্যোতিষ আছিল পুৰণি নাম, কামৰূপ আছিল পিছৰ নাম।
    • বৰ্মন বংশৰ প্ৰতিষ্ঠাপক পুষ্যভৰ্মন; মহান ৰজা ভাস্কৰবৰ্মন।
    • ভাস্কৰবৰ্মন হৰ্ষবৰ্ধনৰ সমসাময়িক আৰু মিত্ৰ আছিল।
    • চুয়ান জাঙে সপ্তম শতিকাত কামৰূপ ভ্ৰমণ কৰিছিল।
    • শালস্তম্ভ বংশ শালস্তম্ভই প্ৰতিষ্ঠা কৰিছিল; ৰাজধানী হৰুপ্পেশ্বৰ।
    • কামৰূপৰ পাল বংশ ব্ৰহ্মপালে প্ৰতিষ্ঠা কৰিছিল; শেষ ৰজা জয়পাল।

    সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    প্ৰাচীন অসমক কি বুলি কোৱা হৈছিল?

    প্ৰাচীন অসম প্ৰথমে প্ৰাগজ্যোতিষ আৰু পিছত কামৰূপ নামেৰে জনাজাত আছিল, আৰু দুয়োটা নাম বেলেগ সময়ত একেই অঞ্চলৰ বাবে ব্যৱহাৰ হৈছিল।

    কামৰূপৰ বৰ্মন বংশ কোনে প্ৰতিষ্ঠা কৰিছিল?

    পুষ্যভৰ্মনে চতুৰ্থ শতিকাৰ মাজভাগত বৰ্মন বংশ প্ৰতিষ্ঠা কৰিছিল, আৰু তেওঁৰ উত্তৰপুৰুষ ভাস্কৰবৰ্মন এই বংশৰ আটাইতকৈ বিখ্যাত ৰজা আছিল।

    কোন চীনা পৰিব্ৰাজকে কামৰূপ ভ্ৰমণ কৰিছিল?

    চীনা পৰিব্ৰাজক চুয়ান জাং, যাক হিউয়েন চাঙ বুলিও কোৱা হয়, ৰজা ভাস্কৰবৰ্মনৰ আমন্ত্ৰণত সপ্তম শতিকাত কামৰূপ ভ্ৰমণ কৰিছিল।

    প্ৰাচীন অসমৰ ৰাজধানীবোৰ কি আছিল?

    বৰ্মনসকলে প্ৰাগজ্যোতিষপুৰৰপৰা, শালস্তম্ভসকলে তেজপুৰৰ ওচৰৰ হৰুপ্পেশ্বৰৰপৰা, আৰু কামৰূপৰ পালসকলে বৰ্তমান গুৱাহাটীৰ ওচৰৰ কামৰূপনগৰৰপৰা ৰাজত্ব কৰিছিল।

    কামৰূপ পাল বংশৰ শেষ গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ ৰজা কোন আছিল?

    জয়পাল কামৰূপ পাল বংশৰ শেষ গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ ৰজা আছিল, যি প্ৰায় দ্বাদশ শতিকালৈকে ৰাজত্ব কৰিছিল।

      Sample questions with answers

      1. Who was the founder of the Varman dynasty of ancient Kamarupa?
        Options: Salastambha / Naranarayan / Pushyavarman / Bhaskarvarman
        Answer: Pushyavarman
        Explanation: Pushyavarman founded the Varman dynasty of Kamarupa in the 4th century CE.
      2. In which century did Pushyavarman establish the Varman dynasty?
        Options: 2nd century CE / 6th century CE / 4th century CE / 3rd century CE
        Answer: 4th century CE
        Explanation: Pushyavarman established the Varman dynasty in the 4th century CE, around 350 CE.
      3. What was the capital of the Varman dynasty?
        Options: Pragjyotishpura / Haruppeswara / Durjaya / Sonitpur
        Answer: Pragjyotishpura
        Explanation: The capital of the Varman dynasty was Pragjyotishpura, the present-day Guwahati.
      4. Bhaskarvarman was a ruler of which dynasty?
        Options: Varman dynasty / Chutia dynasty / Mlechchha dynasty / Pala dynasty
        Answer: Varman dynasty
        Explanation: Bhaskarvarman was the most illustrious ruler of the Varman dynasty of Kamarupa.
      5. The Chinese traveller Hiuen Tsang visited Kamarupa during the reign of which king?
        Options: Brahmapala / Tyagasimha / Bhaskarvarman / Pushyavarman
        Answer: Bhaskarvarman
        Explanation: Hiuen Tsang visited Kamarupa in 643 CE during the reign of Bhaskarvarman.

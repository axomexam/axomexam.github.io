      Sample questions with answers

      1. Who became the Prime Minister of India for a third consecutive term in 2024?
        Options: Narendra Modi / Rahul Gandhi / Amit Shah / Arvind Kejriwal
        Answer: Narendra Modi
        Explanation: Narendra Modi took oath as Prime Minister for a third term on 9 June 2024.
      2. In the 2024 Lok Sabha elections, which party won the highest number of seats?
        Options: Indian National Congress / Samajwadi Party / Aam Aadmi Party / Bharatiya Janata Party
        Answer: Bharatiya Janata Party
        Explanation: The BJP won the highest number of seats (240) in the 2024 Lok Sabha elections.
      3. The 2024 Lok Sabha election was which general election of India?
        Options: 18th / 17th / 16th / 19th
        Answer: 18th
        Explanation: The 2024 Lok Sabha election was the 18th general election of India.
      4. Who was elected Speaker of the 18th Lok Sabha?
        Options: Sumitra Mahajan / Meira Kumar / Om Birla / Sonia Gandhi
        Answer: Om Birla
        Explanation: Om Birla was elected Speaker of the 18th Lok Sabha.
      5. Who is the President of India?
        Options: Ram Nath Kovind / Pranab Mukherjee / Droupadi Murmu / Venkaiah Naidu
        Answer: Droupadi Murmu
        Explanation: Droupadi Murmu is the President of India.

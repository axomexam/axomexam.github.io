    Assam & India Current Affairs

    Assam and India Current Affairs is one of the two topics of the Current Affairs section of the ADRE 3.0 Grade IV General Knowledge and Assam GK paper. The topic covers the important events of the last six to eight months from the state of Assam, from the rest of India and from the world, with special attention to those events that have a bearing on Assam. Because the paper is meant for a state recruitment examination, the examiner usually prefers events that touch on Assam's administration, culture, economy, sports and people, along with the national events that every well-informed candidate is expected to know. The topic rewards regular reading rather than last-minute cramming, and the candidate who follows a small number of reliable sources throughout the year has a clear advantage.

    Meaning and Scope of the Topic

    Current affairs is the study of recent happenings that are important enough to be remembered. In an examination like ADRE it does not mean every news item of the day, but a selected set of events such as appointments, awards, schemes, summits, sports results, scientific developments and major state and national decisions. The scope is usually limited to the period mentioned in the notification, most often the eight months before the examination. The candidate must know both the fact and, in a few cases, the person or the place connected with the fact, because the question may be asked from either end.

    Main Areas Covered

    • Appointments to high offices in Assam and in India, such as governors, chief ministers, judges and heads of commissions.
    • Awards and honours, including the Padma awards, the Bharat Ratna and state awards.
    • Government schemes launched or expanded by the Centre and by the Assam government.
    • International events such as summits, agreements and visits of leaders.
    • Sports events, tournaments and the achievements of Indian and Assamese sportspersons.
    • Science and technology, including space missions, satellites and important discoveries.
    • Assam-specific events such as festivals, cultural programmes, new districts, bridges and development projects.
    • Economic news such as budgets, railway and highway projects and important industries.
    • Environment and wildlife, especially from the national parks and sanctuaries of Assam.
    • Books and authors, important days and the themes of those days.

    Types of Questions Asked

    The questions are almost always objective, with four options and one correct answer. Some ask for the name of a person, some for a place, some for a date or a year, and some for the theme of an event. A few questions are matching type, where a list of events is to be matched with a list of persons or places. Because the answer is a single fact, the candidate must have read the news carefully, and guessing is risky unless two or three options can be eliminated with confidence.

    Reliable Sources to Follow

    The best sources are a standard national newspaper, a good Assamese daily, the official websites of the Government of Assam and the Government of India, and a monthly current affairs magazine. A weekly revision of the notes made from these sources is more useful than reading many books at the end. For Assam, the state government's press releases and the local editions of the national papers carry the events that the examination is most likely to ask.

    How to Prepare This Topic

    1. Fix a small set of sources and read them every day.
    2. Keep a separate notebook divided month by month.
    3. Write only the fact, the person and the place in short points.
    4. Revise the previous month's notes at the start of every new month.
    5. Attempt a current affairs quiz every week to test your memory.

    How the Topic Appears in the Examination

    Current affairs normally appears as a small group of objective questions carrying one mark each. The questions are direct and can be answered in a few seconds by a candidate who remembers the fact, so the real work is done before the examination. Because the topic is so quick to answer, it is a good source of marks, and a candidate who ignores it loses easy marks that could have been secured with only a little regular reading.

    Memory Tips

    Group the news under headings such as appointments, awards, schemes, sports and Assam, so that the memory has a place to put each fact. Attach every fact to a person or a place, because a fact with a face is easier to remember. Use short forms for long names while making notes. Revise from the notebook rather than from the newspaper, since revision is what fixes a fact in memory. Discussing the week's important news with friends also helps recall.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Reading every detail and remembering none.
    • Starting preparation only a few days before the examination.
    • Ignoring Assamese state news while reading only national news.
    • Confusing the names of similar schemes, awards or offices.
    • Not revising the notes and hoping for the best.

    Additional Points to Remember

    For appointments, remember the office and the person together with the date if possible. For awards, remember the field in which the award was given. For schemes, remember who launched it and for whom it is meant. For sports, remember the event and the winner. For Assam, remember the district, the river or the institution connected with the event. These simple links turn separate facts into a connected picture, which is exactly what a good memory needs.

    Advanced Tips

    Make a one-page summary at the end of every month and revise these pages once a week in the final months. When two events seem similar, note the single point of difference that separates them, because the examiner often sets a question on exactly that difference. For the state portion, give extra attention to events that were reported in both the English and the Assamese press, since these are the events the paper is most likely to use. Finally, keep the last week's news fresh in mind, because recent events are often asked.

    Related Topics

    Current affairs is closely linked with the other parts of the General Knowledge and Assam GK paper. Government schemes, Assam history and culture, Assam geography and India general knowledge all overlap with current affairs, because a recent event often refers to a historical place, a geographical feature or a government scheme. Studying them together gives a fuller picture and reduces the total effort needed for the paper.

    Quick Revision Checklist

    • Read a fixed set of sources every day.
    • Keep month-wise notes of facts, names and places.
    • Give equal weight to Assam and to national news.
    • Revise last month's notes at the start of every month.
    • Attempt a weekly quiz to check your recall.
    • Keep the most recent week's news fresh before the examination.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What period does the current affairs section cover?

    It usually covers the six to eight months before the examination, though the notification mentions the exact period, so always read it carefully.

    Are Assam events more important than national events?

    Both are asked, but because this is a state examination, events connected with Assam are often given more weight, so do not ignore either.

    Which sources are best for current affairs?

    A standard national newspaper, a good Assamese daily, the official government websites and a monthly current affairs magazine are the most useful sources.

    How can I remember so many facts?

    Group facts under headings, attach each to a person or place, and revise your own month-wise notes regularly instead of rereading the newspaper.

    Is it enough to prepare current affairs at the end?

    সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনা সাধাৰণ জ্ঞান আৰু অসম GK প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ আন অংশবোৰৰ সৈতে ঘনিষ্ঠভাৱে জড়িত। চৰকাৰী আঁচনি, অসমৰ ইতিহাস-সংস্কৃতি, অসমৰ ভূগোল আৰু ভাৰতৰ সাধাৰণ জ্ঞান আটাইবোৰ সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনাৰ সৈতে ওপৰা-উপৰি হৈ যায়, কাৰণ এটা শেহতীয়া ঘটনাই প্ৰায়ে এটা ঐতিহাসিক ঠাই, ভৌগোলিক বৈশিষ্ট্য বা চৰকাৰী আঁচনিৰ কথা কয়। এইবোৰ একেলগে অধ্যয়ন কৰিলে পূৰ্ণ ছবি পোৱা যায় আৰু প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ বাবে প্ৰয়োজনীয় মুঠ পৰিশ্ৰম কমি যায়।

    • প্ৰতিদিনে এটা নিৰ্ধাৰিত উৎসগোট পঢ়ক।
    • সত্য, নাম আৰু ঠাইৰ মাহে মাহে টোকা ৰাখক।
    • অসম আৰু ৰাষ্ট্ৰীয় বাতৰিক সমান গুৰুত্ব দিয়ক।
    • প্ৰতি মাহৰ আৰম্ভণিতে আগৰ মাহৰ টোকা পুনৰালোচনা কৰক।
    • স্মৃতি পৰীক্ষা কৰিবলৈ সপ্তাহে এবাৰ কুইজ দিয়ক।
    • পৰীক্ষাৰ আগতে শেহতীয়া সপ্তাহৰ বাতৰি সতেজকৈ ৰাখক।

    সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনা অংশই কোন সময়ছোৱা সামৰি লয়?

    ই সাধাৰণতে পৰীক্ষাৰ আগৰ ছৰপৰা আঠ মাহ সামৰি লয়, যদিও বিজ্ঞাপনে সঠিক সময়ছোৱা উল্লেখ কৰে, সেয়ে সদায় সাৱধানে পঢ়ক।

    ৰাষ্ট্ৰীয় ঘটনাতকৈ অসমৰ ঘটনা বেছি গুৰুত্বপূৰ্ণ নেকি?

    দুয়োটা সোধা হয়, কিন্তু যিহেতু এইটো ৰাজ্যিক পৰীক্ষা, অসমৰ সৈতে জড়িত ঘটনাবোৰক প্ৰায়ে বেছি গুৰুত্ব দিয়া হয়, সেয়ে কোনো এটা উপেক্ষা নকৰিব।

    সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনাৰ বাবে কোনবোৰ উৎস শ্ৰেষ্ঠ?

    এখন মানক ৰাষ্ট্ৰীয় বাতৰি কাকত, এখন ভাল অসমীয়া দৈনিক, চৰকাৰী ৱেবছাইট আৰু এখন মাহেকীয়া সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনা আলোচনী আটাইতকৈ উপযোগী।

    ইমান সত্য কেনেকৈ মনত ৰাখিম?

    সত্যসমূহক শিতানত ভাগ কৰক, প্ৰতিটো এজন ব্যক্তি বা ঠাইৰ সৈতে জড়াওক, আৰু কাকত পুনৰপঢ়াৰ সলনি নিজৰ মাহে মাহে টোকা নিয়মীয়াকৈ পুনৰালোচনা কৰক।

    শেষত সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনা পঢ়িলেই হয়নে?

    নহয়। সাম্প্ৰতিক ঘটনাৰ বাবে বছৰজুৰি অলপ নিয়মীয়া পঢ়াৰ প্ৰয়োজন, কাৰণ অনেক সত্য শেষৰ অলপ দিনত মুখস্থ কৰিব নোৱাৰি।

      Sample questions with answers

      1. Which is the longest river of India?
        Options: Godavari / Brahmaputra / Ganga / Yamuna
        Answer: Ganga
        Explanation: The Ganga is the longest river in India.
      2. Which is the longest river that flows entirely within India?
        Options: Ganga / Krishna / Narmada / Godavari
        Answer: Godavari
        Explanation: The Godavari is the longest river flowing entirely within India.
      3. Which is the second longest river of India?
        Options: Krishna / Mahanadi / Yamuna / Godavari
        Answer: Godavari
        Explanation: The Godavari is the second longest river of India.
      4. Which river is known as 'Dakshin Ganga'?
        Options: Kaveri / Krishna / Tapti / Godavari
        Answer: Godavari
        Explanation: The Godavari is known as Dakshin Ganga.
      5. Which is the largest peninsular river of India?
        Options: Godavari / Narmada / Mahanadi / Kaveri
        Answer: Godavari
        Explanation: The Godavari is the largest peninsular river.

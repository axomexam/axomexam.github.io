      Voice Change

      Voice shows whether the subject of a sentence acts or is acted upon, and changing a sentence from one voice to the other is a fixed exercise in the ADRE Grade IV General English paper. There are two voices. In the active voice the subject performs the action, as in Ram writes a letter. In the passive voice the subject receives the action, as in a letter is written by Ram. The whole exercise follows a short set of steps, and a candidate who learns those steps can convert any sentence correctly. The method followed in the Master Book approach is to identify the object of the active sentence, make it the subject of the passive sentence, add the correct form of the verb be with the past participle of the main verb, and place the old subject after the word by. Every voice question is a careful application of these four steps.

      Basic Terms

      • Voice: the form of the verb that shows whether the subject acts or is acted upon.
      • Active voice: the subject performs the action.
      • Passive voice: the subject receives the action.
      • Agent: the doer of the action, usually placed after by in the passive.
      • Object: the receiver of the action in the active sentence.
      • Past participle: the third form of the verb, as in written, taken and made.

      The active has an object and the passive has an agent. The active sentence's object becomes the passive sentence's subject, and the active sentence's subject becomes the agent after by.

      The Four Steps of Conversion

      First, take the object of the active sentence and make it the subject. Second, choose the correct form of the helping verb be, matching the tense of the active sentence. Third, use the past participle of the main verb. Fourth, put the original subject after by, changing a pronoun to its objective form, as in he to him. Following these four steps in order gives the passive sentence without confusion.

      The Helping Verb by Tense

      Each tense requires its own form of be in the passive. The simple present uses is, am or are with the past participle. The simple past uses was or were. The present continuous uses is being or are being. The past continuous uses was being or were being. The present perfect uses has been or have been. The past perfect uses had been. The future uses will be. The perfect forms of the continuous and the future perfect continuous have no passive form in ordinary use, and a question will not ask for them.

      Changing Pronouns

      When the old subject is placed after by, a pronoun changes from the subjective to the objective case. I becomes me, we becomes us, he becomes him, she becomes her, they becomes them and who becomes whom. This change is a common point of testing, and forgetting it spoils an otherwise correct answer.

      Sentences with Two Objects

      Some verbs, such as give, offer and teach, take two objects. In such sentences the passive can be made in two ways, because either object can become the subject: he gave me a book, a book was given to me by him, or I was given a book by him. Both forms are correct, and the question usually accepts the more natural one.

      Commands and Negative Sentences

      In the passive, an imperative such as open the door becomes let the door be opened. A negative sentence keeps its negative word, so do not close the door becomes let not the door be closed. Requests beginning with please are turned into you are requested to. These special forms must be learned because the usual steps do not apply directly.

      Intransitive Verbs and Voice

      Only a transitive verb has an object and can be changed into the passive. An intransitive verb such as sleep, come or go has no object and therefore no passive form. A question may ask which sentence cannot be changed into the passive, and the answer is the one with an intransitive verb.

      How This Topic Appears in the Examination

      In ADRE Grade IV, APSC and Assam Police English papers this topic appears when a sentence must be changed from active to passive or from passive to active. The tested points are the correct helping verb for the tense, the past participle of the main verb, the change of pronoun, and the special forms for commands and negative sentences.

      Shortcut Methods for Quick Answers

      Do the four steps in order and do not skip any. Match the helping verb to the tense of the original sentence before anything else. Use the third form of the verb and not the second. Change the pronoun as soon as it goes after by. For commands, begin with let and keep the object before be.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Using the second form of the verb instead of the past participle.
      • Choosing the wrong form of be for the tense.
      • Forgetting to change the pronoun from the subjective to the objective case.
      • Trying to make a passive from an intransitive verb.
      • Leaving the agent after by in a sentence where by is not used, as in familiar expressions.

      How to Prepare This Topic

      1. Learn the four steps of conversion in order.
      2. Memorise the form of be used in each tense of the passive.
      3. Revise the third form of common irregular verbs.
      4. Practise pronoun changes and sentences with two objects.
      5. Finish with commands and negative sentences, and revise the steps weekly.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is the difference between active and passive voice?

      In the active voice the subject performs the action, as in the boy kicked the ball. In the passive voice the subject receives the action, as in the ball was kicked by the boy.

      How is the passive voice formed?

      The object of the active sentence becomes the subject, a form of the verb be is used according to the tense, the main verb takes its past participle, and the old subject follows after by.

      Which verbs cannot be changed into the passive?

      Intransitive verbs such as sleep, come and go cannot be changed, because they have no object. Only a transitive verb, which has an object, can form a passive.

      How is a command changed into the passive?

      An imperative such as open the door becomes let the door be opened. A negative command keeps its negative word, so do not open the door becomes let not the door be opened.

      What happens to the pronoun in the passive?

      A pronoun that moves after by changes to its objective form. He becomes him, she becomes her, they become them and I becomes me.

      Voice Change

      Voice য়ে দেখুৱায় যে বাক্যৰ subject এ কাৰ্য কৰে নে কাৰ্য ইয়াৰ ওপৰত হয়, আৰু এটা বাক্য এটা voice ৰ পৰা আনটোলৈ সলোৱাটো ADRE গ্ৰেড IV ৰ General English প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ এটা নিৰ্দিষ্ট অভ্যাস। দুটা voice আছে। Active voice ত subject এ কাৰ্য কৰে, যেনে Ram writes a letter। Passive voice ত subject এ কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণ কৰে, যেনে a letter is written by Ram। গোটেই অভ্যাসটো এটা চুটি ধাপৰ সংহতি অনুসৰণ কৰে, আৰু সেই ধাপবোৰ শিকা প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে যিকোনো বাক্য শুদ্ধভাৱে সলাব পাৰে। পদ্ধতিটো হৈছে active বাক্যৰ object চিনাক্ত কৰা, তাক passive বাক্যৰ subject কৰা, main verb ৰ past participle ৰ সৈতে be verb ৰ শুদ্ধ ৰূপ যোগ কৰা, আৰু পুৰণি subject ক by ৰ পিছত ৰখা। প্ৰতিটো voice প্ৰশ্ন এই চাৰিটা ধাপৰ সাৱধানে প্ৰয়োগ।

      মূল শব্দাৱলী

      • Voice: subject এ কাৰ্য কৰে নে কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণ কৰে সেয়া দেখুওৱা verb ৰ ৰূপ।
      • Active voice: subject এ কাৰ্য কৰে।
      • Passive voice: subject এ কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণ কৰে।
      • Agent: কাৰ্য কৰোঁতা, passive ত সাধাৰণতে by ৰ পিছত ৰখা হয়।
      • Object: active বাক্যত কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণকাৰী।
      • Past participle: verb ৰ তৃতীয় ৰূপ, যেনে written, taken আৰু made।

      Active ৰ এটা object থাকে আৰু passive ৰ এটা agent থাকে। Active বাক্যৰ object passive বাক্যৰ subject হয়, আৰু active বাক্যৰ subject by ৰ পিছত agent হয়।

      ৰূপান্তৰৰ চাৰিটা ধাপ

      প্ৰথমে active বাক্যৰ object লৈ তাক subject কৰক। দ্বিতীয়তে active বাক্যৰ tense ৰ সৈতে মিলাই be helping verb ৰ শুদ্ধ ৰূপ বাছনি কৰক। তৃতীয়তে main verb ৰ past participle ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক। চতুৰ্থতে পুৰণি subject ক by ৰ পিছত ৰাখি pronoun ক objective ৰূপলৈ সলাওক, যেনে he ৰ পৰা him। এই চাৰিটা ধাপ ক্ৰমে অনুসৰণ কৰিলে কোনো বিভ্ৰান্তি নোহোৱাকৈ passive বাক্য পোৱা যায়।

      Tense অনুসৰি Helping Verb

      প্ৰতিটো tense ৰ বাবে passive ত be ৰ নিজা ৰূপ লাগে। Simple present এ past participle ৰ সৈতে is, am বা are লয়। Simple past এ was বা were লয়। Present continuous এ is being বা are being লয়। Past continuous এ was being বা were being লয়। Present perfect এ has been বা have been লয়। Past perfect এ had been লয়। Future এ will be লয়। Continuous ৰ perfect ৰূপ আৰু future perfect continuous ৰ সাধাৰণ ব্যৱহাৰত passive নাথাকে, আৰু প্ৰশ্নই সেয়া নোসোধে।

      Pronoun সলোৱা

      পুৰণি subject ক by ৰ পিছত ৰাখিলে pronoun subjective ৰ পৰা objective case লৈ সলনি হয়। I ৰ পৰা me, we ৰ পৰা us, he ৰ পৰা him, she ৰ পৰা her, they ৰ পৰা them আৰু who ৰ পৰা whom। এই সলনিটোৱেই সঘন পৰীক্ষিত বিন্দু, আৰু ইয়াক পাহৰিলে আনে শুদ্ধ উত্তৰটো নষ্ট হয়।

      দুটা Object থকা বাক্য

      give, offer আৰু teach ৰ দৰে কিছুমান verb এ দুটা object লয়। এনে বাক্যত passive দুভাৱে কৰিব পাৰি, কাৰণ যিকোনো object subject হ'ব পাৰে: he gave me a book, a book was given to me by him, বা I was given a book by him। দুয়োটা ৰূপ শুদ্ধ, আৰু প্ৰশ্নই সাধাৰণতে বেছি স্বাভাৱিকটো গ্ৰহণ কৰে।

      আদেশ আৰু ঋণাত্মক বাক্য

      Passive ত open the door ৰ দৰে imperative এ let the door be opened হৈ পৰে। ঋণাত্মক বাক্যই তাৰ ঋণাত্মক শব্দ ৰাখে, সেয়ে do not close the door এ let not the door be closed হৈ পৰে। please ৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা অনুৰোধ you are requested to লৈ সলনি হয়। সাধাৰণ ধাপবোৰ পোনপটীয়াকৈ প্ৰযোজ্য নহয় বাবে এই বিশেষ ৰূপবোৰ শিকিব লাগে।

      Intransitive Verb আৰু Voice

      কেৱল transitive verb ৰ object থাকে আৰু তাক passive লৈ সলাব পাৰি। sleep, come বা go ৰ দৰে intransitive verb ৰ object নাথাকে, সেয়ে passive ৰূপো নাথাকে। কোনো এটা বাক্য passive লৈ সলাব নোৱাৰি বুলি প্ৰশ্নই সুধিব পাৰে, আৰু উত্তৰটো হ'ল intransitive verb থকা বাক্যটো।

      পৰীক্ষাত এই বিষয় কেনেকৈ আহে

      ADRE গ্ৰেড IV, APSC আৰু অসম আৰক্ষীৰ ইংৰাজী প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰত এই বিষয়টো এটা বাক্য active ৰ পৰা passive লৈ, বা passive ৰ পৰা active লৈ সলাবলৈ কোৱা হয়। পৰীক্ষিত বিন্দুবোৰ হৈছে tense ৰ বাবে শুদ্ধ helping verb, main verb ৰ past participle, pronoun ৰ সলনি, আৰু আদেশ তথা ঋণাত্মক বাক্যৰ বিশেষ ৰূপ।

      দ্ৰুত উত্তৰৰ চুটি পদ্ধতি

      চাৰিটা ধাপ ক্ৰমে কৰক আৰু এটাও এৰি নিদিব। আন যিকোনো কামৰ আগতে মূল বাক্যৰ tense ৰ সৈতে helping verb মিলাওক। verb ৰ দ্বিতীয় ৰূপ নহয়, তৃতীয় ৰূপ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক। by ৰ পিছলৈ যোৱাৰ লগে লগে pronoun সলাওক। আদেশৰ বাবে let ৰে আৰম্ভ কৰি object ক be ৰ আগত ৰাখক।

      এৰাই চলিবলগীয়া সাধাৰণ ভুল

      • Past participle ৰ সলনি verb ৰ দ্বিতীয় ৰূপ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
      • Tense ৰ বাবে be ৰ ভুল ৰূপ বাছনি কৰা।
      • Pronoun ক subjective ৰ পৰা objective case লৈ সলাবলৈ পাহৰি যোৱা।
      • Intransitive verb ৰ পৰা passive বনাবলৈ চেষ্টা কৰা।
      • by প্ৰয়োগ নোহোৱা চিনাকি প্ৰকাশত by ৰ পিছত agent ৰখা।

      এই বিষয়ৰ প্ৰস্তুতি কেনেকৈ কৰিব

      1. ৰূপান্তৰৰ চাৰিটা ধাপ ক্ৰমে শিকক।
      2. Passive ৰ প্ৰতিটো tense ত ব্যৱহৃত be ৰ ৰূপ মনত ৰাখক।
      3. সাধাৰণ অনিয়মিত verb ৰ তৃতীয় ৰূপ পুনৰালোচনা কৰক।
      4. Pronoun ৰ সলনি আৰু দুটা object থকা বাক্যৰ অভ্যাস কৰক।
      5. শেষত আদেশ আৰু ঋণাত্মক বাক্যৰে সামৰণি মাৰি ধাপবোৰ সাপ্তাহিকভাৱে পুনৰালোচনা কৰক।

      Voice Change ৰ মূল ধাৰণা আৰু প্ৰকাৰ

      Active voice ত subject এ নিজেই কাৰ্য কৰে, আৰু passive voice ত কাৰ্যৰ বলিত পৰে। Active: The boy broke the glass। Passive: The glass was broken by the boy। Passive voice গঢ়িবলৈ object ক subject কৰি, tense অনুযায়ী be verb যোগ কৰি, মূল verb ৰ past participle বহুৱাই, আৰু পুৰণি subject ক by ৰ লগত ৰখা হয়। এই চাৰিটা ধাপ মনত ৰখিলে যিকোনো বাক্য ৰূপান্তৰ কৰিব পাৰি।

      Perfect Continuous আৰু Modal ৰ Passive

      Present Perfect Continuous আৰু Past Perfect Continuous ৰ সাধাৰণতে passive voice নহয়। কিন্তু Perfect tense ৰ passive হয়: He has finished the work → The work has been finished by him। Modal verb থকা বাক্যৰ passive ত modal ৰ পিছত be বহে—You must do it → It must be done by you। Imperative বাক্যৰ passive ত Let + object + be + past participle ব্যৱহাৰ হয়—Open the door → Let the door be opened। এই ব্যতিক্ৰমবোৰ পৰীক্ষাত ঘনাই আহে।

      WH-Question আৰু Negative বাক্যৰ Passive

      WH-question ৰ passive গঢ়োতে প্ৰশ্ন-শব্দটো আগতে ৰাখি তাৰ পিছত helping verb আৰু subject বহুৱা হয়—Who wrote this book? → By whom was this book written? Negative বাক্যত not ক helping verb ৰ ঠিক পিছত ৰাখিব লাগে। Interrogative বাক্যত প্ৰশ্নৰ গঠন ধৰি ৰাখিব লাগে। এই ধৰণৰ ৰূপান্তৰত সহায়ক verb ৰ স্থান আৰু ৰূপত সৰু ভুল হ'লেও গোটেই বাক্য ভুল হৈ পৰে, সেয়ে সাৱধান হোৱা উচিত।

      ৰূপান্তৰ কৰাৰ পিছত পৰীক্ষা

      কোনো বাক্য passive কৰাৰ পিছত ইয়াৰ tense আগৰ দৰে একেই আছেনে, subject-verb agreement শুদ্ধনে, আৰু meaning একেই ৰাখিছেনে—এই তিনিটা কথাই যাচিব লাগে। Intransitive verb (যেনে sleep, go, come) ৰ passive নহয়, কাৰণ সিহঁতৰ object নাথাকে। যদি এনে verb ৰ passive লিখিব লাগে, তেন্তে সাধাৰণতে ইয়াৰ পৰিৱৰ্তে উপযুক্ত transitive verb বাছনি কৰিব লাগে। এই পৰীক্ষা কৰাৰ অভ্যাসেই নিৰ্ভুল উত্তৰ দিয়াৰ মূল।

      সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

      Active আৰু passive voice ৰ পাৰ্থক্য কি?

      Active voice ত subject এ কাৰ্য কৰে, যেনে the boy kicked the ball। Passive voice ত subject এ কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণ কৰে, যেনে the ball was kicked by the boy।

      Passive voice কেনেকৈ গঢ়া হয়?

      Active বাক্যৰ object subject হয়, tense অনুসৰি be verb ৰ এটা ৰূপ ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, main verb এ তাৰ past participle লয়, আৰু পুৰণি subject by ৰ পিছত আহে।

      কোনবোৰ verb passive লৈ সলাব নোৱাৰি?

      sleep, come আৰু go ৰ দৰে intransitive verb সলাব নোৱাৰি, কাৰণ ইহঁতৰ object নাথাকে। object থকা transitive verb এহে passive গঢ়িব পাৰে।

      এটা আদেশ passive লৈ কেনেকৈ সলনি হয়?

      open the door ৰ দৰে imperative এ let the door be opened হয়। ঋণাত্মক আদেশই তাৰ ঋণাত্মক শব্দ ৰাখে, সেয়ে do not open the door এ let not the door be opened হয়।

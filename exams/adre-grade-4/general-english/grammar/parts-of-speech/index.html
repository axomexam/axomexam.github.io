      Sample questions with answers

      1. Identify the part of speech of the word 'beautifully' in the sentence: She sings beautifully.
        Options: Adjective / Adverb / Noun / Verb
        Answer: Adverb
        Explanation: 'Beautifully' modifies the verb 'sings', so it is an adverb.
      2. Identify the part of speech of the word 'play' in the sentence: The children play in the park.
        Options: Adjective / Adverb / Verb / Noun
        Answer: Verb
        Explanation: 'Play' shows the action of the children, so it is a verb.
      3. Identify the part of speech of the word 'talented' in the sentence: Rina is a talented singer.
        Options: Adjective / Adverb / Noun / Verb
        Answer: Adjective
        Explanation: 'Talented' describes the noun 'singer', so it is an adjective.
      4. Identify the part of speech of the word 'on' in the sentence: The cat sat on the mat.
        Options: Conjunction / Preposition / Adverb / Adjective
        Answer: Preposition
        Explanation: 'On' shows the relation between 'sat' and 'mat', so it is a preposition.
      5. Identify the part of speech of the word 'and' in the sentence: He and his brother went out.
        Options: Preposition / Adverb / Conjunction / Interjection
        Answer: Conjunction
        Explanation: 'And' joins two words, so it is a conjunction.

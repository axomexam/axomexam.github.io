      Subject-Verb Agreement

      Subject-verb agreement is the rule that the verb of a sentence must match its subject in number and person, and it is one of the most regularly tested points of English grammar in the ADRE Grade IV, APSC and Assam Police papers. The principle itself is simple: a singular subject takes a singular verb, and a plural subject takes a plural verb. The difficulty lies not in the rule but in finding the real subject of the sentence, because words placed between the subject and the verb often hide it. The method followed in the Master Book approach is to locate the true subject first, set aside every phrase that merely describes it, and only then choose the verb. A candidate who learns to do this in one steady step solves almost every agreement question.

      Basic Terms

      • Subject: the person or thing that performs the action or is described.
      • Verb: the word that shows the action or state.
      • Singular and plural: one versus more than one.
      • Person: first, second or third.
      • Concord: another name for agreement between subject and verb.
      • Intervening phrase: words that come between the subject and the verb.

      The whole topic rests on one idea: find the subject, ignore the words in between, and make the verb agree with the subject and not with the nearest noun.

      The Basic Rule

      A singular subject takes a singular verb, and a plural subject takes a plural verb. In the present tense the third person singular verb usually ends in s, as in he plays and the boy plays, while the plural takes the plain form, as in they play and the boys play. The verb to be is irregular and takes is with singular subjects and are with plural subjects.

      Words Between Subject and Verb

      A phrase placed between the subject and the verb does not change the number of the subject. In the sentence the quality of the mangoes is good, the subject is quality and not mangoes, so the verb is singular. The same care is needed with phrases beginning with with, along with, together with, as well as and in addition to, which do not affect the number of the subject.

      Subjects Joined by and

      Two subjects joined by and normally take a plural verb, as in Ram and Shyam are friends. But when the two nouns refer to the same person or thing, or form a single idea, the verb is singular, as in bread and butter is my breakfast. When the subjects are regarded as one combined unit, the singular is also used.

      Either, Neither, Each and Every

      Either, neither, each, every, everyone, somebody and nobody are singular and take a singular verb. The phrase each of and one of is followed by a plural noun but still takes a singular verb, as in one of the boys is absent. These words are tested very often because the plural noun that follows them tempts the candidate to use a plural verb.

      Collective Nouns

      A collective noun such as team, crowd or committee takes a singular verb when the group acts as one unit, and a plural verb when the members act separately. In the sentence the committee has taken a decision the verb is singular, while the committee are divided in their opinion uses a plural verb because the members differ.

      Special Cases

      A few subjects deserve special care. Distance, time, money and weight used as a single quantity take a singular verb, as in ten kilometres is a long distance. Nouns that are plural in form but singular in meaning, such as news and mathematics, take a singular verb. A phrase beginning with a number and a plural noun, as in a number of students, takes a plural verb, while the number of students takes a singular verb.

      How This Topic Appears in the Examination

      In ADRE Grade IV, APSC and Assam Police English papers this topic appears as a blank in which the correct form of the verb must be chosen, or as an error detection sentence in which the verb does not agree with its subject. The sentences are designed so that a phrase sits between the subject and the verb, or so that a singular word such as each, every or one of introduces a plural noun.

      Shortcut Methods for Quick Answers

      Cross out every phrase between the subject and the verb and read the sentence with only the subject and the verb. Decide the number of the subject before looking at the options. Treat each, every, either, neither and one of as singular signals. When two subjects are joined by and, use a plural verb unless the two form one idea.

      Common Mistakes to Avoid

      • Making the verb agree with the noun nearest to it instead of with the subject.
      • Being misled by a phrase beginning with with or as well as.
      • Using a plural verb after each, every, either or neither.
      • Forgetting that news and mathematics are singular.
      • Confusing a number of with the number of.

      How to Prepare This Topic

      1. Learn the basic singular and plural forms of the common verbs.
      2. Practise picking out the real subject from a long sentence.
      3. Revise the singular words each, every, either, neither and one of.
      4. Work on collective nouns and on subjects joined by and.
      5. Finish with a mixed set of error detection sentences and read them carefully.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is the main rule of subject-verb agreement?

      A singular subject takes a singular verb and a plural subject takes a plural verb. The verb must agree with the subject and not with any noun that happens to stand near it.

      Do phrases like with and as well as change the verb?

      No. These phrases do not change the number of the subject. The subject remains the same, so the verb agrees with the subject and not with the noun after the phrase.

      Why does one of the boys take a singular verb?

      Because the real subject is one and not boys. The phrase of the boys only describes the one, so the verb is singular, as in one of the boys is absent.

      When does a collective noun take a plural verb?

      When the members of the group act as separate individuals rather than as one unit, as in the committee are divided in their opinion. When the group acts together, the verb is singular.

      Is news singular or plural?

      News is plural in form but singular in meaning, so it takes a singular verb, as in the news is good. The same holds for subjects such as mathematics and physics.

      একবচন subject এ একবচন verb আৰু বহুবচন subject এ বহুবচন verb লয়। বৰ্তমান কালত তৃতীয় পুৰুষ একবচনৰ verb সাধাৰণতে s ৰে শেষ হয়, যেনে he plays আৰু the boy plays, আনহাতে বহুবচনে সাধাৰণ ৰূপ লয়, যেনে they play আৰু the boys play। to be verb টো অনিয়মিত আৰু একবচন subject ৰ সৈতে is, বহুবচনৰ সৈতে are লয়।

      Subject আৰু Verb ৰ মাজৰ শব্দ

      Subject আৰু verb ৰ মাজত বহা phrase এ subject ৰ সংখ্যা নসলায়। the quality of the mangoes is good বাক্যটোত subject হৈছে quality, mangoes নহয়, সেয়ে verb একবচন। with, along with, together with, as well as আৰু in addition to ৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা phrase ৰ ক্ষেত্ৰতো এই সাৱধানতা লাগে, কাৰণ এইবোৰে subject ৰ সংখ্যা সলনি নকৰে।

      বিশেষ ক্ষেত্ৰ

      কিছুমান subject ৰ বাবে বিশেষ সাৱধানতা লাগে। এটা একক পৰিমাণ হিচাপে ব্যৱহৃত দূৰত্ব, সময়, ধন আৰু ওজন এ একবচন verb লয়, যেনে ten kilometres is a long distance। ৰূপত বহুবচন কিন্তু অৰ্থত একবচন থকা noun, যেনে news আৰু mathematics, একবচন verb লয়। এটা সংখ্যা আৰু বহুবচন noun ৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা phrase, যেনে a number of students, এ বহুবচন verb লয়, আনহাতে the number of students এ একবচন verb লয়।

      পৰীক্ষাত এই বিষয় কেনেকৈ আহে

      ADRE গ্ৰেড IV, APSC আৰু অসম আৰক্ষীৰ ইংৰাজী প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰত এই বিষয়টো verb ৰ শুদ্ধ ৰূপ বাছি ল'বলগীয়া খালী ঠাই, বা verb ই তাৰ subject ৰ সৈতে নিমিলা ভুল ধৰাৰ বাক্য হিচাপে আহে। বাক্যবোৰ এনেকৈ সজা হয় যাতে subject আৰু verb ৰ মাজত এটা phrase বহে, বা each, every বা one of ৰ দৰে এটা একবচন শব্দই বহুবচন noun আগত আনে।

      দ্ৰুত উত্তৰৰ চুটি পদ্ধতি

      Subject আৰু verb ৰ মাজৰ প্ৰতিটো phrase কাটি দি কেৱল subject আৰু verb এৰে বাক্যটো পঢ়ক। বিকল্প চোৱাৰ আগতে subject ৰ সংখ্যা ঠিক কৰক। each, every, either, neither আৰু one of ক একবচনৰ সংকেত হিচাপে ধৰক। and ৰে দুটা subject জোৰা লাগিলে দুয়োটা এটা ধাৰণা নহ'লে বহুবচন verb ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক।

      এৰাই চলিবলগীয়া সাধাৰণ ভুল

      • Subject ৰ সলনি verb ৰ ওচৰৰ noun ৰ সৈতে verb মিলোৱা।
      • with বা as well as ৰে আৰম্ভ হোৱা phrase ৰ দ্বাৰা ভুল পথেৰে যোৱা।
      • each, every, either বা neither ৰ পিছত বহুবচন verb ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
      • news আৰু mathematics একবচন বুলি পাহৰি যোৱা।
      • a number of আৰু the number of মিলাই পেলোৱা।

      এই বিষয়ৰ প্ৰস্তুতি কেনেকৈ কৰিব

      1. সাধাৰণ verb ৰ মূল একবচন আৰু বহুবচন ৰূপ শিকক।
      2. দীঘল বাক্যৰ পৰা প্ৰকৃত subject উলিওৱাৰ অভ্যাস কৰক।
      3. each, every, either, neither আৰু one of ৰ একবচন শব্দবোৰ পুনৰালোচনা কৰক।
      4. collective noun আৰু and ৰে জোৰা লগা subject ৰ ওপৰত কাম কৰক।
      5. শেষত মিশ্ৰিত ভুল-ধৰা বাক্যৰ সংহতিএৰে সামৰণি মাৰি সাৱধানে পঢ়ক।

      সংখ্যা আৰু Verb ৰ মূল নিয়ম পুনৰালোচনা

      Subject-verb agreement ৰ মূল কথা এটাই: একবচন subject ল'লে একবচন verb, আৰু বহুবচন subject ল'লে বহুবচন verb। বৰ্তমান কালত তৃতীয় পুৰুষ একবচনৰ লগত verb ৰ লগত s যোগ হয়—He plays, She writes। কিন্তু I আৰু You ৰ লগত মাত্ৰ play আৰু write বহে। অতীত কালত বেছিভাগ verb ৰ ৰূপ একেই থাকে। এই মূল ধাৰণাটো স্পষ্ট হ'লে extended প্ৰশ্নবোৰত ভুল কম হয়।

      বাক্যাংশ আৰু Clause ৰ প্ৰভাৱ

      Subject আৰু Verb ৰ মাজত দীঘল বাক্যাংশ বা clause থাকিলে যিকোনো প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে বিভ্ৰান্ত হয়। যেনে 'The bag with all the books is heavy' — ইয়াত প্ৰকৃত subject হৈছে The bag, সেয়ে is বহে। 'The books with the bag are heavy' — ইয়াত books বহুবচন, সেয়ে are। along with, as well as, together with, including ৰ দৰে শব্দই subject ক বহুবচন নকৰে। মাজৰ বাক্যাংশ আওকাণ কৰি প্ৰকৃত subject বিচাৰি উলিওৱাটোৱেই এই প্ৰশ্নৰ কৌশল।

      Collective Noun আৰু বিশেষ Subject

      Collective noun ৰ বাবে গোটটো এটা বুলি ভাবিলে একবচন verb বহে—The team is playing well। কিন্তু সদস্যসকলে ভিন্নভিন্নভাৱে কাম কৰিলে বহুবচন verb ব্যৱহাৰ কৰিব পাৰি—The team are arguing among themselves। Each, every, either, neither ৰ লগত সদায় একবচন verb বহে। Mathematics, Physics, News ৰ দৰে শব্দ বাহিৰত বহুবচন যেন লাগিলেও একবচন verb লয়। এই ব্যতিক্ৰমবোৰ ADRE ৰ প্ৰশ্নত বাৰে বাৰে দেখা যায়।

      সংখ্যা আৰু Construction ৰ বিশেষ নিয়ম

      'One of the boys' ৰ পিছত plural noun কিন্তু একবচন verb আহে—One of the boys is present। 'A number of' ৰ লগত plural verb, কিন্তু 'The number of' ৰ লগত singular verb। None আৰু Some একবচন বা বহুবচন—অৰ্থৰ ওপৰত নিৰ্ভৰ কৰি। দূৰত্ব, মূল্য বা পৰিমাণক এটা একক বুলি ধৰিলে একবচন verb লয়—Ten kilometres is a long walk। এই সূক্ষ্ম নিয়মবোৰ মনত ৰাখিলে উচ্চ নম্বৰ পোৱাটো সহজ।

      সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

      Subject-verb agreement ৰ মূল নিয়ম কি?

      একবচন subject এ একবচন verb আৰু বহুবচন subject এ বহুবচন verb লয়। Verb ই subject ৰ সৈতে মিলিব লাগে, ওচৰত থকা যিকোনো noun ৰ সৈতে নহয়।

      with আৰু as well as ৰ দৰে phrase এ verb সলায়নে?

      নসলায়। এই phrase বোৰে subject ৰ সংখ্যা নসলায়। Subject একেই থাকে, সেয়ে verb ই phrase ৰ পিছৰ noun ৰ সৈতে নহয়, subject ৰ সৈতে মিলে।

      one of the boys এ একবচন verb কিয় লয়?

      কাৰণ প্ৰকৃত subject হৈছে one আৰু boys নহয়। of the boys phrase টোৱে কেৱল one ক বৰ্ণনা কৰে, সেয়ে verb একবচন, যেনে one of the boys is absent।

      Collective noun এ কেতিয়া বহুবচন verb লয়?

      গোটৰ সদস্যবোৰে এটা একক হিচাপে নহয়, পৃথক ব্যক্তি হিচাপে কাম কৰিলে, যেনে the committee are divided in their opinion। গোটটোৱে একেলগে কাম কৰিলে verb একবচন।

      News একবচন নে বহুবচন?

      News ৰূপত বহুবচন কিন্তু অৰ্থত একবচন, সেয়ে ই একবচন verb লয়, যেনে the news is good। mathematics আৰু physics ৰ দৰে subject ৰ ক্ষেত্ৰতো একেই।

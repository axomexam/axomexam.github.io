      Sample questions with answers

      1. Choose the word that is most similar in meaning to 'Abundant'.
        Options: Plentiful / Scarce / Bridge / Basket
        Answer: Plentiful
        Explanation: 'Plentiful' is a synonym of 'Abundant'; both have a similar meaning.
      2. Choose the word that is most similar in meaning to 'Bold'.
        Options: Timid / Brave / Cloud / Pencil
        Answer: Brave
        Explanation: 'Brave' is a synonym of 'Bold'; both have a similar meaning.
      3. Choose the word that is most opposite in meaning to 'Abundant'.
        Options: Plentiful / Elephant / Scarce / Curtain
        Answer: Scarce
        Explanation: 'Scarce' is an antonym of 'Abundant'; it has the opposite meaning.
      4. Choose the word that is most opposite in meaning to 'Bold'.
        Options: Brave / Kitchen / Desert / Timid
        Answer: Timid
        Explanation: 'Timid' is an antonym of 'Bold'; it has the opposite meaning.
      5. Choose the word that is most similar in meaning to 'Calm'.
        Options: Peaceful / Agitated / Coin / Pencil
        Answer: Peaceful
        Explanation: 'Peaceful' is a synonym of 'Calm'; both have a similar meaning.

    Venn Diagrams

    Venn Diagrams are a visual reasoning topic of the ADRE Grade IV paper in which the relationship between two or more classes is shown with the help of circles. The candidate may be asked to choose the diagram that best represents a given group of items, or to say which items a shown diagram represents, or to count how many persons belong to a particular region. The topic is close to set theory and is best solved by understanding the four basic ways in which two circles can be placed.

    Meaning of a Venn Diagram

    A Venn diagram uses circles inside a rectangle to represent classes and their relationships. Each circle stands for one class, and the rectangle stands for the whole universe. The way the circles overlap tells whether the classes share members or not. The same idea is used in everyday questions such as "Which diagram best shows Doctors, Men and Fathers?" where the three classes partly overlap. Reading the picture correctly is the whole skill of this topic.

    Four Basic Relationships

    • Fully overlapping — one class is completely inside another, as in "All roses are flowers".
    • Partly overlapping — the classes share some members, as in "Some doctors are men".
    • Separate or disjoint — the classes share no member, as in "Cats and dogs".
    • Three circles — one may be inside another, or all three partly overlap, depending on the items.

    Common Examples

    Mothers, Women and Teachers are three classes in which all mothers are women, so the mother circle lies inside the women circle, while some women are teachers, so the teacher circle overlaps the women circle. In Birds, Parrots and Fish, all parrots are birds and no bird is a fish, so the parrot circle lies inside the bird circle and the fish circle is separate. In Flowers, Roses and Lilies, both roses and lilies are flowers, so two separate circles lie inside the flower circle.

    Counting Regions

    Some questions give the number of persons in each class and its overlaps and ask for the number in a particular region. The safest method is to start filling the innermost region where all the circles overlap, then move outward to the two-circle overlaps, and finally fill the single circles. Always subtract the already-filled numbers. If the total is given, the number outside the circles can also be found by subtracting the sum of all filled regions from the total.

    Worked Example

    Suppose among thirty students, fifteen play cricket, twelve play football and five play both. The number who play only cricket is 15 − 5 = 10 and only football is 12 − 5 = 7. The number who play at least one game is 10 + 5 + 7 = 22, so 30 − 22 = 8 students play neither game. In a relationship question, "Doctors, Men, Fathers" is best shown by three circles that partly overlap, because a doctor may be a man, a man may be a father, and a father may be a doctor, but none is fully inside another.

    How This Topic Appears in the Examination

    Venn diagram questions usually appear as two to four objective questions of one mark each. They may be relationship questions asking for the best diagram, or counting questions with given numbers. The relationship type is answered by matching the statement to one of the four basic patterns, and the counting type is answered by careful addition and subtraction. The questions are quick when the circles are drawn neatly.

    Memory Tips

    Decide first whether the classes are independent, partly related or fully related, because this fixes the pattern. For "All A are B" put A inside B; for "Some A are B" make the circles overlap; for "No A is B" keep them separate. In counting questions, always begin from the innermost overlap and work outwards. Draw the circles roughly but keep the overlapping portions large enough to write numbers inside.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Putting a class inside another when the statement says only "some".
    • Overlapping circles when the statement says "no".
    • Forgetting to subtract the overlap while filling the single circles.
    • Ignoring the part outside the circles in counting questions.
    • Matching the diagram by guess without checking every statement.

    How to Prepare This Topic

    1. Learn the four basic circle patterns by heart.
    2. Practise converting statements into diagrams.
    3. Solve counting questions starting from the innermost region.
    4. Check the answer by adding all regions back to the total.
    5. Practise both relationship and counting questions daily.

    More Solved Examples

    In a survey of fifty people, thirty like tea, twenty-five like coffee and fifteen like both. The number who like only tea is 30 − 15 = 15, only coffee is 25 − 15 = 10, and at least one drink is 15 + 15 + 10 = 40, so ten people like neither. For a relationship question, "Teachers, Fathers, Men" is shown by three circles that partly overlap, because a teacher may be a father, a father is usually a man, and a teacher may be a man, but none of the classes lies fully inside another.

    Advanced Tips for the Exam

    For relationship questions, check the pattern that matches every part of the statement, not just the first word. For counting questions, always begin at the centre where all the circles overlap, and subtract as you move outward. If three classes are given, fill the triple overlap first, then the three double overlaps, and finally the single circles. Draw the circles large enough to hold the numbers inside them. Always verify by adding all the regions and comparing the sum with the given total.

    Quick Revision Checklist

    • Four basic patterns: inside, overlap, separate and triple.
    • "All A are B" means A lies inside B.
    • "Some A are B" means the circles overlap.
    • "No A is B" means the circles are separate.
    • Counting starts from the innermost region.
    • Total = sum of all regions plus the outside part.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a Venn diagram?

    A Venn diagram uses circles inside a rectangle to show the relationship between classes, such as inside, overlapping or separate.

    How do I show "All A are B" in a Venn diagram?

    Draw the circle of A completely inside the circle of B, because every member of A is also a member of B.

    How do I solve counting questions with Venn diagrams?

    Start with the innermost overlap where all circles meet, then fill the two-circle overlaps by subtracting, then the single circles, and finally the part outside the circles.

    How can I check my answer in a counting question?

    Add up all the regions you have filled and see whether the total matches the number given in the question. The difference, if any, is the part outside the circles.

    Are Venn diagrams related to syllogism?

    Yes. Both use circles to show relationships between classes, and the same diagram can often be used to test conclusions in syllogism questions.

    অধিক সমাধান কৰা উদাহৰণ

    পঞ্চাশজনৰ এটা জৰীপত ত্ৰিশজনে চাহ ভাল পায়, পঁচিশজনে কফি ভাল পায় আৰু পোন্ধৰজনে দুয়োটা ভাল পায়। কেৱল চাহ ভাল পোৱা সংখ্যা ৩০ − ১৫ = ১৫, কেৱল কফি ২৫ − ১৫ = ১০, আৰু অন্ততঃ এটা ভাল পোৱা সংখ্যা ১৫ + ১৫ + ১০ = ৪০, সেয়ে দহজনে এটাও ভাল নাপায়। সম্পৰ্কৰ প্ৰশ্নত "শিক্ষক, পিতৃ, পুৰুষ" তিনিটা কিছু অংশ ওপৰা-উপৰিকৈ পৰা বৃত্তৰ জৰিয়তে দেখুওৱা হয়, কাৰণ এজন শিক্ষক পিতৃ হ'ব পাৰে, এজন পিতৃ সাধাৰণতে পুৰুষ, আৰু এজন শিক্ষক পুৰুষ হ'ব পাৰে, কিন্তু এটাও শ্ৰেণী আনটোৰ সম্পূৰ্ণ ভিতৰত নাই।

    উন্নত টিপছ

    সম্পৰ্কৰ প্ৰশ্নত কেৱল প্ৰথম শব্দটো নহয়, বিবৃতিৰ প্ৰতিটো অংশৰ সৈতে খাপ খোৱা আৰ্হিটো পৰীক্ষা কৰক। গণনাৰ প্ৰশ্নত সদায় আটাইকেইটা বৃত্ত ওপৰা-উপৰিকৈ পৰা কেন্দ্ৰৰপৰা আৰম্ভ কৰি বাহিৰলৈ যাওঁতে বিয়োগ কৰি যাওক। তিনিটা শ্ৰেণী দিয়া থাকিলে প্ৰথমে তিনিওটা বৃত্তৰ ওপৰা-উপৰি, তাৰ পিছত তিনিটা দুটা বৃত্তৰ ওপৰা-উপৰি, আৰু শেষত একক বৃত্ত পূৰণ কৰক। সংখ্যা ধৰিব পৰাকৈ বৃত্তবোৰ ডাঙৰকৈ আঁকক। সদায় আটাইবোৰ অঞ্চল যোগ কৰি দিয়া মুঠৰ সৈতে মিলাই পৰীক্ষা কৰক।

    দ্ৰুত পুনৰালোচনা তালিকা

    • চাৰিটা মৌলিক আৰ্হি: ভিতৰত, ওপৰা-উপৰি, পৃথক, ত্ৰি-বৃত্ত।
    • "সকলো A হৈছে B" মানে A, B ৰ ভিতৰত।
    • "কিছু A হৈছে B" মানে বৃত্ত দুটা ওপৰা-উপৰিকৈ পৰে।
    • "কোনো A B নহয়" মানে বৃত্ত দুটা পৃথক।
    • গণনা আটাইতকৈ ভিতৰৰ অঞ্চলৰপৰা আৰম্ভ হয়।
    • মুঠ = আটাইবোৰ অঞ্চলৰ যোগফল যোগ বাহিৰৰ অংশ।

    সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    ভেন চিত্ৰ কি?

    ভেন চিত্ৰত এটা আয়তৰ ভিতৰত বৃত্ত ব্যৱহাৰ কৰি শ্ৰেণীবোৰৰ সম্পৰ্ক দেখুওৱা হয়, যেনে ভিতৰত, ওপৰা-উপৰিকৈ বা পৃথক।

    ভেন চিত্ৰত "সকলো A হৈছে B" কেনেকৈ দেখুওৱাম?

    A ৰ বৃত্ত B ৰ বৃত্তৰ ভিতৰত সম্পূৰ্ণৰূপে আঁকক, কাৰণ A ৰ প্ৰতিটো সদস্য B ৰো সদস্য।

    ভেন চিত্ৰৰে গণনাৰ প্ৰশ্ন কেনেকৈ সমাধান কৰিম?

    আটাইকেইটা বৃত্ত মিলা আটাইতকৈ ভিতৰৰ ওপৰা-উপৰিৰপৰা আৰম্ভ কৰক, তাৰ পিছত বিয়োগ কৰি দুটা বৃত্তৰ ওপৰা-উপৰি, তাৰ পিছত একক বৃত্ত, আৰু শেষত বৃত্তৰ বাহিৰৰ অংশ পূৰণ কৰক।

    গণনাৰ প্ৰশ্নত উত্তৰ কেনেকৈ পৰীক্ষা কৰিব পাৰি?

    পূৰণ কৰা আটাইবোৰ অঞ্চল যোগ কৰি প্ৰশ্নত দিয়া মুঠৰ সৈতে মিলে নে নাই চাওক। পাৰ্থক্য, যদি থাকে, হৈছে বৃত্তৰ বাহিৰৰ অংশ।

    ভেন চিত্ৰ যুক্তিবিন্যাসৰ সৈতে সম্পৰ্কিতনে?

    হয়। দুয়োতে শ্ৰেণীবোৰৰ সম্পৰ্ক দেখুওৱাবলৈ বৃত্ত ব্যৱহাৰ কৰে, আৰু একেই চিত্ৰ প্ৰায়ে যুক্তিবিন্যাসৰ প্ৰশ্নত সিদ্ধান্ত পৰীক্ষা কৰিবলৈ ব্যৱহাৰ কৰিব পাৰি।

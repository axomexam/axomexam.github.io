      Sample questions with answers

      1. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 1 o'clock?
        Options: 30° / 60° / 45° / 90°
        Answer: 30°
        Explanation: Each hour is 360° ÷ 12 = 30°, so at 1 o'clock the angle is 1 × 30° = 30°.
      2. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 2 o'clock?
        Options: 120° / 30° / 90° / 60°
        Answer: 60°
        Explanation: Each hour is 360° ÷ 12 = 30°, so at 2 o'clock the angle is 2 × 30° = 60°.
      3. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3 o'clock?
        Options: 120° / 60° / 90° / 180°
        Answer: 90°
        Explanation: Each hour is 360° ÷ 12 = 30°, so at 3 o'clock the angle is 3 × 30° = 90°.
      4. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 4 o'clock?
        Options: 90° / 120° / 150° / 60°
        Answer: 120°
        Explanation: Each hour is 360° ÷ 12 = 30°, so at 4 o'clock the angle is 4 × 30° = 120°.
      5. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 5 o'clock?
        Options: 150° / 120° / 180° / 90°
        Answer: 150°
        Explanation: Each hour is 360° ÷ 12 = 30°, so at 5 o'clock the angle is 5 × 30° = 150°.

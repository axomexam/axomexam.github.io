    Pattern Completion

    Pattern Completion is a non-verbal reasoning topic of the ADRE 3.0 Grade IV paper in which a design, a picture or a geometric figure is shown with a part of it cut away or left blank, and the candidate must choose from the options the piece that will complete the pattern without disturbing its design. The topic tests the eye for continuity, symmetry and repetition, because the missing piece must carry forward the same lines, the same shading and the same spacing as the rest of the figure. It is a very visual question and needs no calculation, but it demands close attention to the corners and the edges where the missing piece joins the main figure. A candidate who checks the joints carefully will almost always find the correct option quickly.

    Meaning of a Pattern Completion Question

    In a pattern completion question a large figure is supplied with a small portion removed, and four or five pieces are offered as options. Exactly one of these pieces fits into the gap so that the design continues smoothly. The piece must match the figure in three ways: the lines must join without a break or a bend, the shading must continue in the same direction, and the spacing of the design must remain the same. The wrong options are made to look correct but they break the design at one or more places along the boundary of the missing part.

    What to Check in a Pattern Completion Question

    • The lines of the design must continue in the same direction across the joint.
    • The thickness of the lines must be the same as in the rest of the figure.
    • The shading must continue with the same tone and the same pattern.
    • The spacing between repeated elements must remain the same.
    • The corners of the piece must fit the corners of the gap exactly.
    • A curved line must join another curved line smoothly, without a kink.
    • Symmetrical designs need a piece that keeps the symmetry unbroken.
    • Repeated motifs must be completed with the same motif at the same angle.
    • The piece must not introduce a line or a shade that does not exist elsewhere.
    • The edges of the piece must meet the edges of the gap without overlap or gap.

    Types of Questions

    Some questions use a geometric design such as a grid, a chequered pattern or a set of triangles, and the missing part is a square or a rectangle. Some use a picture such as a flower, an animal or a flag, and the missing part contains a piece of the picture. Some use a repeating border or a wallpaper pattern in which one tile is missing. A few questions use a symmetrical figure with one half removed and ask for the missing half, which is a special case of pattern completion closely related to mirror images.

    How to Solve a Pattern Completion Question

    1. Study the lines and the shading that run into the gap.
    2. Note the direction and the spacing of the pattern at the edge of the gap.
    3. Check each option at the boundary, line by line.
    4. Reject options that break a line, a curve or a shade.
    5. Confirm the answer by imagining the piece placed in the gap.

    Worked Examples

    If a grid of squares has one square missing and the options are single squares, the correct square is the one whose lines and shading line up with the surrounding grid. If a chequered pattern has a dark square at the top-left of the gap and a light square at the top-right, the correct piece must have a dark square at its top-left and a light square at its top-right to keep the checkerboard unbroken. If a design has diagonal lines running from the top-left to the bottom-right, the missing piece must carry the same diagonal lines at the same angle. If a curved design is broken at the gap, the correct piece must continue the curve smoothly.

    How This Topic Appears in the Examination

    Pattern completion is usually asked as a small group of objective questions carrying one mark each. The figures are simple but the options are drawn very carefully to look alike, so the candidate must compare the edges rather than the whole piece. The question can be solved in a few seconds by an experienced candidate who checks the joints first, while a candidate who looks only at the overall appearance may be deceived. Regular practice builds the habit of examining the boundary of the gap, which is the surest way to the answer.

    Memory Tips

    Always begin with the boundary of the missing part, because the answer is decided there. Follow every line that touches the gap and see whether the option continues it without a break. Check the shading direction, since a piece with the correct lines but the wrong shading is a common trap. For symmetrical designs, use the other half of the figure as a mirror to predict the missing half. If two options look equally good, compare the corners, because the intended answer fits perfectly at every corner of the gap.

    Common Mistakes to Avoid

    • Choosing a piece by its overall look without checking the boundary.
    • Ignoring the direction of the shading.
    • Accepting a piece whose lines join with a slight bend or break.
    • Forgetting the spacing of repeated motifs.
    • Mistaking a mirror-image piece for the correct one in a symmetrical design.

    How to Prepare This Topic

    1. Practise completing grids, chequered patterns and borders on paper.
    2. Solve at least fifteen pattern completion questions every day.
    3. Redraw the figure and the chosen piece to check the joint.
    4. Study symmetrical designs and their mirror halves.
    5. Time yourself so that each question takes less than a minute.

    Additional Solved Examples

    If a wall pattern of bricks has a missing brick, the correct brick must have the same row offset as the bricks above and below, so that the joints stagger correctly. If a triangular design has a missing triangle and the surrounding triangles all point upwards, the missing triangle must also point upwards. If a dotted pattern has a missing portion and the dots are evenly spaced, the correct piece must carry dots at the same spacing and in the same alignment. If a figure is symmetrical about a vertical line and the left half is missing, the correct piece is the mirror image of the right half.

    Advanced Tips

    When the design is complex, first mark the points where lines meet the boundary of the gap, because the correct piece must have lines meeting the boundary at exactly those points. For a repeating design, count the number of repetitions across the whole figure and see how many the missing piece should contain. In shaded designs, note whether the tone changes gradually or in steps, and match the piece accordingly. If the figure is symmetrical, test the candidate piece by folding the figure mentally along the line of symmetry. Finally, do not be tempted by a piece that looks attractive but disturbs the rhythm of the design, because a pattern must repeat evenly throughout.

    Related Non-Verbal Topics

    Pattern Completion is closely related to Mirror Image and Water Image, Paper Folding and Cutting and Figure Series. Mirror images appear directly in symmetrical designs where one half completes the other. Paper folding and cutting uses the same idea of a missing part, because the unfolded holes are the missing pieces of the folded sheet. Figure series uses repetition and regular change, which is also the basis of a continuous pattern. Practising these topics together makes the eye sensitive to continuity, symmetry and repetition, which is exactly what pattern completion demands.

    Quick Revision Checklist

    • Start with the boundary of the gap.
    • Every line must continue without a break or a bend.
    • Shading must continue in the same tone and direction.
    • Spacing of repeated motifs must stay the same.
    • In symmetrical figures, use the other half as a mirror.
    • Compare all corners of the piece with the gap before answering.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is a pattern completion question?

    It shows a design with a part missing and asks you to choose the piece that completes the design without breaking its lines, shading or spacing.

    What should I check first in a pattern completion question?

    Check the boundary of the gap first, because the correct piece must join the surrounding lines, curves and shading exactly at that boundary.

    Why are the wrong options so similar?

    The wrong options usually have the right overall shape but break the design at one line, one corner or one shade, so the boundary must be checked carefully.

    How does symmetry help in these questions?

    In a symmetrical design the missing half is simply the mirror image of the visible half, so you can predict the correct piece from the other side of the figure.

    How much time should each question take?

    A pattern completion question should take only a few seconds to about a minute once you are used to checking the boundary of the gap.

    5. সময় মাপি চলক যাতে প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্ন এক মিনিটতকৈ কমত হয়।

    অধিক কৰা উদাহৰণ

    যদি ইটাৰ এটা বেৰ আৰ্হিত এটা ইটা অনুপস্থিত, শুদ্ধ ইটাটোৰ শাৰীৰ অফচেট ওপৰ-তলৰ ইটাৰ দৰেই থাকিব লাগে, যাতে জোৰবোৰ সঠিকভাৱে পৰস্পৰ হৈ যায়। যদি এটা ত্ৰিভুজ ডিজাইনত এটা ত্ৰিভুজ অনুপস্থিত আৰু চাৰিওফালৰ ত্ৰিভুজবোৰ ওপৰলৈ মূখ কৰা, অনুপস্থিত ত্ৰিভুজটোও ওপৰলৈ মূখ কৰিব লাগে। যদি এটা বিন্দুৰ আৰ্হিত এটা অংশ অনুপস্থিত আৰু বিন্দুবোৰ সমান ব্যৱধানত, শুদ্ধ টুকুৰাটোৱে একেই ব্যৱধানত আৰু একেই ৰেখাত বিন্দু বহন কৰিব লাগে। যদি এটা চিত্ৰ উলম্ব ৰেখাৰ সৈতে প্ৰতিসম আৰু বাওঁ অৰ্ধটো অনুপস্থিত, শুদ্ধ টুকুৰাটো সোঁ অৰ্ধৰ দাপোণ-প্ৰতিবিম্ব।

    উন্নত টিপছ

    ডিজাইনটো জটিল হ'লে, প্ৰথমে ৰেখাবোৰে খালী ঠাইৰ সীমাক যি বিন্দুত চুইছে সেই বিন্দুবোৰ চিহ্নিত কৰক, কাৰণ শুদ্ধ টুকুৰাৰ ৰেখাবোৰে ঠিক সেই বিন্দুবোৰতে সীমাক চুইব লাগে। পুনৰাবৃত্তি হোৱা ডিজাইনৰ বাবে, গোটেই চিত্ৰত পুনৰাবৃত্তিৰ সংখ্যা গণি অনুপস্থিত টুকুৰাত কিমান থাকিব লাগে চাওক। কটা-কটা ডিজাইনত, টোন ক্ৰমে ক্ৰমে সলনি হয় নে খোজত সলনি হয় মন কৰি সেই অনুসৰি টুকুৰা মিলাওক। যদি চিত্ৰটো প্ৰতিসম, প্ৰতিসমতাৰ ৰেখাৰে চিত্ৰটো মানসিকভাৱে ভাঁজ কৰি প্ৰাৰ্থী টুকুৰাটো পৰীক্ষা কৰক। শেষত, এটা টুকুৰা দেখাত সুন্দৰ যেন লাগিলেও ডিজাইনৰ ছন্দ বিঘ্নিত কৰে যদি ইয়াক প্ৰলোভিত নহ'ব, কাৰণ আৰ্হি গোটেইখনত সমানভাৱে পুনৰাবৃত্তি হ'ব লাগে।

    সম্পৰ্কিত অশব্দিক বিষয়

    আৰ্হি সম্পূৰ্ণতাৰ লগত দাপোণ-জল প্ৰতিবিম্ব, কাগজ ভাঁজ-কটা আৰু চিত্ৰ শৃংখলাৰ ঘনিষ্ঠ সম্পৰ্ক আছে। দাপোণ প্ৰতিবিম্ব প্ৰতিসম ডিজাইনত পোনপটীয়াকৈ আহে য'ত এটা অৰ্ধে আনটো সম্পূৰ্ণ কৰে। কাগজ ভাঁজ-কটাতো অনুপস্থিত অংশৰ একেই ধাৰণা ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, কাৰণ মুকলি কৰি পোৱা ফুটাবোৰ ভাঁজ কৰা কাগজৰ অনুপস্থিত টুকুৰা। চিত্ৰ শৃংখলাই পুনৰাবৃত্তি আৰু নিয়মীয়া পৰিৱৰ্তন ব্যৱহাৰ কৰে, যি এটা ধাৰাবাহিক আৰ্হিৰো ভিত্তি। এই বিষয়বোৰ একেলগে অনুশীলন কৰিলে ধাৰাবাহিকতা, প্ৰতিসমতা আৰু পুনৰাবৃত্তিৰ বাবে চকু সংবেদনশীল হয়, যি আৰ্হি সম্পূৰ্ণতাই ঠিক দাবী কৰে।

    দ্ৰুত পুনৰালোচনা তালিকা

    • খালী ঠাইৰ সীমাৰপৰা আৰম্ভ কৰক।
    • প্ৰতিটো ৰেখা ভাঙি বা বেঁকা নোহোৱাকৈ আগবাঢ়িব লাগে।
    • কটা-কটা একেই টোন আৰু দিশত আগবাঢ়িব লাগে।
    • পুনৰাবৃত্তি হোৱা নক্সাৰ ব্যৱধান একেই থাকিব লাগে।
    • প্ৰতিসম চিত্ৰত আন অৰ্ধটোক দাপোণ হিচাপে ব্যৱহাৰ কৰক।
    • উত্তৰ দিয়াৰ আগতে টুকুৰাৰ আটাইবোৰ কোণ খালী ঠাইৰ সৈতে মিলাওক।

    সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    আৰ্হি সম্পূৰ্ণতা প্ৰশ্ন কি?

    ই এটা অংশ অনুপস্থিত থকা ডিজাইন দেখুৱায় আৰু ইয়াৰ ৰেখা, কটা-কটা বা ব্যৱধান ভাঙি নোহোৱাকৈ ডিজাইন সম্পূৰ্ণ কৰা টুকুৰাটো বাছনি কৰিবলৈ কয়।

    আৰ্হি সম্পূৰ্ণতা প্ৰশ্নত আগে কি পৰীক্ষা কৰিব?

    আগে খালী ঠাইৰ সীমা পৰীক্ষা কৰক, কাৰণ শুদ্ধ টুকুৰাটোৱে ঠিক সেই সীমাতে চাৰিওফালৰ ৰেখা, বক্ৰ আৰু কটা-কটাৰ সৈতে মিলিব লাগে।

    ভুল বিকল্পবোৰ ইমান একে কিয়?

    ভুল বিকল্পবোৰৰ গোটেই ৰূপ প্ৰায়ে শুদ্ধ, কিন্তু সিহঁতে এটা ৰেখা, এটা কোণ বা এটা কটা-কটাত ডিজাইন ভাঙে, সেয়ে সীমা সাৱধানে পৰীক্ষা কৰিব লাগে।

    এই প্ৰশ্নত প্ৰতিসমতাই কেনেকৈ সহায় কৰে?

    এটা প্ৰতিসম ডিজাইনত অনুপস্থিত অৰ্ধটো দৃশ্যমান অৰ্ধৰ দাপোণ-প্ৰতিবিম্ব, সেয়ে চিত্ৰৰ আনফালৰপৰা শুদ্ধ টুকুৰাৰ আগানুমান কৰিব পাৰি।

    প্ৰতিটো প্ৰশ্নত কিমান সময় লাগিব?

    খালী ঠাইৰ সীমা পৰীক্ষা কৰাৰ অভ্যাস হ'লে আৰ্হি সম্পূৰ্ণতাৰ প্ৰশ্ন কেইছেকেণ্ডৰপৰা প্ৰায় এক মিনিটত লাগে।

    present perfect ৰ কৰ্মবাচ্য 'has been submitted' ৰ কৰ্তৃবাচ্য হৈছে 'has submitted'।
    Q45. Change into indirect speech: She said, 'Where are you going?'
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিলৈ ৰূপান্তৰ কৰক: She said, 'Where are you going?'
    (A) She asked where I was going.
    (B) She asked where was I going.
    (C) She said where I was going.
    (D) She asked where I am going.
    Answer: A) She asked where I was going.
    Explanation: A wh-question in indirect speech uses statement word order: 'where I was going'.
    পৰোক্ষ উক্তিত wh-question এ statement ৰ word order লয়: 'where I was going'।
    Q46. ___ you please help me?
    আপুনি মোক সহায় কৰিব ___?
    (A) Should
    (B) Must
    (C) May
    (D) Could
    Answer: D) Could
    Explanation: 'Could' is used to make a polite request.
    ভদ্ৰভাৱে অনুৰোধ কৰিবলৈ 'could' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q47. Everyone has arrived, ___?
    সকলোৱে আহি পাইছে, ___?
    (A) hasn't he
    (B) haven't they
    (C) hasn't they
    (D) have they
    Answer: B) haven't they
    Explanation: 'Everyone' is treated as plural in a question tag, so 'haven't they' is used.
    question tag ত 'everyone' ক plural হিচাপে ধৰা হয়; সেয়ে 'haven't they' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q48. If I were you, I ___ accept the offer.
    যদি মই তোমাৰ ঠাইত থাকিলোহেঁতেন, মই প্ৰস্তাৱটো ___ গ্ৰহণ কৰিলোহেঁতেন।
    (A) will
    (B) would
    (C) can
    (D) shall
    Answer: B) would
    Explanation: The second conditional uses 'would' plus the base verb in the main clause.
    second conditional ত main clause ত 'would' আৰু verb ৰ base form ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q49. Choose the correct negative sentence with 'doesn't':
    'doesn't' ৰ সৈতে শুদ্ধ ঋণাত্মক বাক্যটো বাছক:
    (A) She don't knows the answer.
    (B) She doesn't knows the answer.
    (C) She doesn't know the answer.
    (D) She not know the answer.
    Answer: C) She doesn't know the answer.
    Explanation: After 'doesn't', the base form 'know' is used, not 'knows'.
    'doesn't' ৰ পিছত 'knows' নহয়, verb ৰ base form 'know' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q50. She ___ to school every day.
    তাই প্ৰতিদিনে বিদ্যালয়লৈ যায়।
    (A) go
    (B) goes
    (C) going
    (D) gone
    Answer: B) goes
    Explanation: With a third-person singular subject in the simple present, 'goes' is used.
    simple present ত third-person singular subject ৰ সৈতে 'goes' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q51. They ___ playing football now.
    সিহঁতে এতিয়া ফুটবল খেলি আছে।
    (A) is
    (B) was
    (C) are
    (D) were
    Answer: C) are
    Explanation: 'They' takes the plural verb 'are' in the present continuous.
    present continuous ত 'they' ৰ সৈতে plural verb 'are' লগে।
    Q52. He ___ a letter yesterday.
    তেওঁ কালি এখন চিঠি লিখিছিল।
    (A) write
    (B) writes
    (C) written
    (D) wrote
    Answer: D) wrote
    Explanation: 'Yesterday' shows past time, so 'wrote' is used.
    'yesterday' এ অতীত কাল বুজায়, সেয়ে 'wrote' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q53. I ___ this book for two hours.
    মই এই কিতাপখন দুঘণ্টা ধৰি পঢ়ি আছোঁ।
    (A) read
    (B) have been reading
    (C) am read
    (D) was reading
    Answer: B) have been reading
    Explanation: 'Have been reading' shows an action continuing for two hours.
    'have been reading' য়ে দুই ঘণ্টাৰ পৰা চলি থকা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q54. The past participle of 'go' is:
    'go' ৰ past participle কি?
    (A) went
    (B) goed
    (C) gone
    (D) going
    Answer: C) gone

    Q55. By next year, she ___ her degree.
    অহা বছৰৰ ভিতৰত তাই ডিগ্ৰী সম্পূৰ্ণ কৰিব।
    (A) will complete
    (B) will have completed
    (C) completed
    (D) completes
    Answer: B) will have completed
    Explanation: 'Will have completed' is future perfect, used with 'by next year'.
    'by next year' ৰ সৈতে future perfect 'will have completed' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q56. If I ___ rich, I would help the poor.
    মই ধনী হ'লে দুখীয়াক সহায় কৰিলোঁহেঁতেন।
    (A) am
    (B) was
    (C) were
    (D) will be
    Answer: C) were
    Explanation: In a second conditional, 'were' is used with 'I'.
    দ্বিতীয় conditional ত 'I' ৰ সৈতে 'were' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q57. The sentence 'The letter was written by Ram' is in:
    'The letter was written by Ram' বাক্যটো কি voice ত আছে?
    (A) Active voice সক্ৰিয় বাচ্য
    (B) Passive voice কৰ্মবাচ্য
    (C) Direct speech প্ৰত্যক্ষ উক্তি
    (D) Interrogative প্ৰশ্নবোধক
    Answer: B) Passive voice
    B) কৰ্মবাচ্য
    Explanation: The subject receives the action, so the sentence is in the passive voice.
    কৰ্তাই কাৰ্য গ্ৰহণ কৰিছে, সেয়ে বাক্যটো passive voice ত আছে।
    Q58. Change into passive: 'She sings a song.'
    কৰ্মবাচ্যলৈ সলাওক: 'She sings a song.'
    (A) A song is sung by her.
    (B) A song was sung by her.
    (C) A song is sang by her.
    (D) A song has sung by her.
    Answer: A) A song is sung by her.
    Explanation: The passive of 'She sings a song' is 'A song is sung by her.'
    'She sings a song' ৰ passive হৈছে 'A song is sung by her.'
    Q59. He said, 'I am busy.' The indirect form is:
    তেওঁ ক'লে, 'I am busy.' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) He said that he is busy.
    (B) He said that he was busy.
    (C) He said that I was busy.
    (D) He said that he has been busy.
    Answer: B) He said that he was busy.
    Explanation: In indirect speech, 'am' changes to 'was' after a past reporting verb.
    indirect speech ত past reporting verb ৰ পিছত 'am' → 'was' হয়।
    Q60. She said to me, 'Are you coming?' The indirect form is:
    তাই মোক ক'লে, 'Are you coming?' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) She asked me if I was coming.
    (B) She told me that I was coming.
    (C) She asked me that I am coming.
    (D) She said me if I am coming.
    Answer: A) She asked me if I was coming.
    Explanation: A yes/no question becomes an 'if' clause in indirect speech.
    yes/no প্ৰশ্ন indirect speech ত 'if' clause লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q61. The teacher said, 'Do not make a noise.' The indirect form is:
    শিক্ষকে ক'লে, 'Do not make a noise.' পৰোক্ষ উক্তি কি?
    (A) The teacher said that do not make a noise.
    (B) The teacher told not make a noise.
    (C) The teacher forbade them to make a noise.
    (D) The teacher asked that they do not make a noise.
    Answer: C) The teacher forbade them to make a noise.
    Explanation: A negative command becomes 'forbade ... to' in indirect speech.
    নিষেধাত্মক আদেশ indirect speech ত 'forbade ... to' লৈ পৰিণত হয়।
    Q62. Neither he nor his friends ___ present.
    তেওঁও নাই, তেওঁৰ বন্ধুসকলেও উপস্থিত নাই।

    (B) was
    (C) are
    (D) has
    Answer: C) are
    Explanation: With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'friends'.
    'neither...nor' ৰ সৈতে verb ওচৰৰ subject 'friends' ৰ লগত মিলে।
    Q63. The quality of the apples ___ good.

    (D) is
    Answer: D) is
    Explanation: The subject is 'quality' (singular), so 'is' is used.
    subject হৈছে 'quality' (singular), সেয়ে 'is' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q64. Each of the boys ___ given a prize.
    প্ৰতিজন ল'ৰাক বঁটা দিয়া হৈছে।

    (B) are
    (C) was
    (D) have
    Answer: C) was
    Explanation: 'Each' is singular, so it takes the singular verb 'was'.
    'each' singular, সেয়ে ই singular verb 'was' লয়।
    Q65. I look forward to ___ you.
    মই আপোনাক লগ পোৱাৰ বাবে অপেক্ষা কৰি আছোঁ।

    Q66. He is used to ___ early.

    Q70. He ___ finished his work before I arrived.

    (A) has
    (B) had
    (C) have
    (D) was
    Answer: B) had
    Explanation: 'Had finished' is past perfect, showing an action completed earlier.
    'had finished' past perfect, যি আগতে সম্পন্ন হোৱা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়।
    Q71. It ___ raining since morning.

    (B) was
    (C) has been
    (D) have been
    Answer: C) has been

    Q72. The sentence 'May you live long!' is:
    'May you live long!' বাক্যটো কি প্ৰকাৰৰ?
    (A) Assertive নিৰ্দেশাত্মক
    (B) Interrogative প্ৰশ্নবোধক
    (C) Optative ইচ্ছাসূচক
    (D) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    Answer: C) Optative
    C) ইচ্ছাসূচক
    Explanation: 'May you live long!' expresses a wish, so it is optative.
    'May you live long!' এ ইচ্ছা প্ৰকাশ কৰে, সেয়ে ই optative।
    Q73. What a beautiful flower! This sentence is:
    What a beautiful flower! এই বাক্যটো কি?
    (A) Assertive নিৰ্দেশাত্মক
    (B) Imperative আদেশাত্মক
    (C) Interrogative প্ৰশ্নবোধক
    (D) Exclamatory বিস্ময়সূচক
    Answer: D) Exclamatory
    D) বিস্ময়সূচক
    Explanation: 'What a beautiful flower!' expresses strong feeling, so it is exclamatory.
    'What a beautiful flower!' য়ে তীব্ৰ অনুভূতি প্ৰকাশ কৰে, সেয়ে ই exclamatory।

    Q76. The book is ___ the table.

    Q77. He died ___ cholera.

    Q78. I prefer tea ___ coffee.

    Q79. He congratulated me ___ my success.

    Aptitude & Basic Mathematics: syllabus and what is asked

    This part carries 35 questions in two blocks. Reasoning and mental ability covers coding-decoding, number and alphabet series, blood relations, direction sense, syllogism and Venn diagrams, seating arrangement, statement and assumption or conclusion, analogy, odd one out and mathematical operations. Quantitative aptitude and basic mathematics covers number system, LCM and HCF, simplification, percentage, profit, loss and discount, ratio, proportion and partnership, simple and compound interest, time and work, pipes and cisterns, time, speed, distance, trains and boats, averages, mixtures and alligation. The standard is Class X (HSLC) and above.

    Aptitude & Basic Mathematics: worked questions

    A few worked questions from this page, with the marked answer and the explanation, so you can see the level and the format before you start practising.

    • Q1. Find the average of 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25. ৫, ১০, ১৫, ২০ আৰু ২৫ ৰ গৰ নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      (A) 12 ১২   (B) 13 ১৩   (C) 15 ১৫   (D) 17 ১৭
      Answer: 15 Explanation: Sum = 5 + 10 + 15 + 20 + 25 = 75. Average = Sum / Count = 75 / 5 = 15.
    • Q2. Find the average of the first 5 even natural numbers. প্ৰথম ৫ টা যুগ্ম স্বাভাৱিক সংখ্যাৰ গৰ নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      (A) 6 ৬   (B) 7 ৭   (C) 8 ৮   (D) 10 ১০
      Answer: 6 Explanation: First 5 even numbers are 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. Sum = 30. Average = 30 / 5 = 6.
    • Q3. The average of the first n natural numbers is 15. Find the value of n. প্ৰথম n টা স্বাভাৱিক সংখ্যাৰ গৰ ১৫। n ৰ মান নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      (A) 25 ২৫   (B) 27 ২৭   (C) 29 ২৯   (D) 31 ৩১
      Answer: 29 Explanation: Average of first n natural numbers = (n + 1) / 2 = 15, so n + 1 = 30 and n = 29.
    • Q4. Find the average of the squares of the first 10 natural numbers. প্ৰথম ১০ টা স্বাভাৱিক সংখ্যাৰ বৰ্গৰ গৰ নিৰ্ণয় কৰা।
      (A) 35 ৩৫   (B) 38.5 ৩৮.৫   (C) 40 ৪০   (D) 42 ৪২
      Answer: 38.5 Explanation: Sum of squares = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 = 10 × 11 × 21 / 6 = 385. Average = 385 / 10 = 38.5.

      Sample questions with answers

      1. A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above the cost price and allows a discount of 25%. Find his profit percent.
        Options: 10% / 15% / 5% / No profit no loss
        Answer: 5%
        Explanation: Let CP = 100, so MP = 140. SP = 140 × 75/100 = 105. Profit% = (105 - 100)/100 × 100 = 5%.
      2. Find the selling price of an article whose cost price is Rs 2000 and profit is 12.5%.
        Options: Rs 2200 / Rs 2300 / Rs 2500 / Rs 2250
        Answer: Rs 2250
        Explanation: SP = CP × (100 + Profit%)/100 = 2000 × 112.5/100 = Rs 2250.
      3. A man bought a horse for Rs 5000 and sold it at a loss of 8%. Find the selling price.
        Options: Rs 4600 / Rs 4500 / Rs 4700 / Rs 4200
        Answer: Rs 4600
        Explanation: SP = 5000 × (100 - 8)/100 = 5000 × 92/100 = Rs 4600.
      4. An article is sold for Rs 1120 at a profit of 12%. Find the cost price.
        Options: Rs 1050 / Rs 1000 / Rs 980 / Rs 1100
        Answer: Rs 1000
        Explanation: CP = SP × 100/(100 + Profit%) = 1120 × 100/112 = Rs 1000.
      5. A dishonest dealer sells his goods at a profit of 10% and also uses a weight of 900 g instead of 1 kg. Find his total profit percent.
        Options: 20% / 10% / 22.22% / 25%
        Answer: 22.22%
        Explanation: Taking 1000 g cost = Rs 100, cost of 900 g = Rs 90. He charges for 1000 g with 10% profit, so SP = Rs 110. Profit% = (110 - 90)/90 × 100 = 22.22%.

      Sample questions with answers

      1. Find the LCM of 8, 9, 12 and 15.
        Options: 180 / 240 / 360 / 720
        Answer: 360
        Explanation: 8 = 2³, 9 = 3², 12 = 2² × 3, 15 = 3 × 5. LCM = 2³ × 3² × 5 = 360.
      2. Find the HCF of 84 and 126.
        Options: 42 / 21 / 84 / 14
        Answer: 42
        Explanation: 84 = 2² × 3 × 7 and 126 = 2 × 3² × 7. HCF = 2 × 3 × 7 = 42.
      3. Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 4 and their HCF is 5. Find their LCM.
        Options: 30 / 60 / 45 / 75
        Answer: 60
        Explanation: Numbers are 3x5=15 and 4x5=20. LCM(15,20) = 60. Formula: LCM = 3 × 4 × 5 = 60.
      4. Find the least number which when divided by 5, 6, 7 and 8 leaves a remainder 3 in each case.
        Options: 840 / 841 / 843 / 846
        Answer: 843
        Explanation: LCM(5,6,7,8) = 840. Required number = 840 + 3 = 843.
      5. Find the largest 4-digit number exactly divisible by 12, 15 and 18.
        Options: 9000 / 990 / 9990 / 9900
        Answer: 9900
        Explanation: LCM(12,15,18) = 180. Largest 4-digit multiple = 55 × 180 = 9900.

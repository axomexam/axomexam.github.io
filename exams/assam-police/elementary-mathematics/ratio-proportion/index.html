      Sample questions with answers

      1. Find the mean proportional between 4 and 25.
        Options: 14.5 / 10 / 20 / 12
        Answer: 10
        Explanation: Mean proportional = square root of (4 × 25) = square root of 100 = 10.
      2. Find the third proportional to 9 and 12.
        Options: 14 / 15 / 18 / 16
        Answer: 16
        Explanation: Let the third proportional be x. Then 9 : 12 = 12 : x, so × = (12 × 12) / 9 = 16.
      3. Find the fourth proportional to 5, 7 and 15.
        Options: 20 / 21 / 24 / 18
        Answer: 21
        Explanation: Let the fourth proportional be x. Then 5 : 7 = 15 : x, so × = (7 × 15) / 5 = 21.
      4. If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, find A : C.
        Options: 8 : 15 / 2 : 5 / 3 : 5 / 8 : 12
        Answer: 8 : 15
        Explanation: Make B common: A : B = 2 : 3 = 8 : 12 and B : C = 4 : 5 = 12 : 15. So A : C = 8 : 15.
      5. What number must be added to each term of 3 : 5 so that the ratio becomes 5 : 7?
        Options: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4
        Answer: 2
        Explanation: Let × be added: (3 + x) / (5 + x) = 5 / 7, so 21 + 7x = 25 + 5x, 2x = 4, × = 2.

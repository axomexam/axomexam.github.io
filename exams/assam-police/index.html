      Sample questions with answers

      1. Choose the correct synonym of 'Yield'.
        Options: Resist / Defend / Withstand / Surrender
        Answer: Surrender
        Explanation: 'Yield' means to give way or surrender; 'Surrender' is its synonym.
      2. Choose the correct antonym of 'Zeal'.
        Options: Apathy / Enthusiasm / Fervor / Passion
        Answer: Apathy
        Explanation: 'Zeal' means great enthusiasm; 'Apathy' is its antonym.
      3. Choose the correct antonym of 'Ample'.
        Options: Plenty / Insufficient / Abundant / Generous
        Answer: Insufficient
        Explanation: 'Ample' means enough or more than enough; 'Insufficient' is its antonym.
      4. Choose the correct antonym of 'Bold'.
        Options: Brave / Courageous / Timid / Daring
        Answer: Timid
        Explanation: 'Bold' means showing courage; 'Timid' is its antonym.
      5. Choose the correct synonym of 'Coarse'.
        Options: Smooth / Fine / Soft / Rough
        Answer: Rough
        Explanation: 'Coarse' means rough in texture; 'Rough' is its synonym.

      How to prepare

      • Attempt the questions first and read the explanation only after answering.
      • Note the facts you get wrong and revise them the same day.
      • Repeat the same sub-topic after a few days to confirm your recall.
      • Practise in both Assamese and English so the wording never slows you down.

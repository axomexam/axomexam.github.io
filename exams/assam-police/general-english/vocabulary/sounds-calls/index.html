      Sounds and Calls of Animals and Birds for the Assam Police Constable (AB & UB) Exam

      Questions on the sounds and calls of animals and birds belong to the vocabulary part of the General English paper, and they are among the most scoring questions in the whole section. The examiner gives the name of an animal or a bird and asks for the word that names the sound it makes, or gives the sound and asks which creature makes it. There is no passage to read and no rule to apply; the answer depends entirely on a clear and correctly grouped list. The difficulty is that many creatures share the same verb, that a few sounds have two accepted names, and that a small number of words look alike but belong to different animals. This page arranges the sounds by group, marks the shared words, explains the common confusions, and lists the high-yield pairs that repeat in the paper. The material follows the standard grammar and vocabulary reference books used for Assam recruitment examinations and is reviewed before it is published.

      How This Portion Is Set

      The examiner usually gives one animal and four possible sounds, or four animals and asks which one makes a given sound. Sometimes the question is reversed and the sound is given while the animal has to be supplied. In every case the answer comes from memory, so a well-grouped list is the whole of the preparation. Because the same words repeat across several animals, a candidate who groups by sound rather than by animal answers faster and with fewer mistakes.

      Domestic Animals and Their Sounds

      The sounds of the animals we see every day are asked most often.

      • Dog barks; a puppy yelps; a dog also growls when it warns and whines when it is sad.
      • Cat mews or meows; a kitten mews softly and a contented cat purrs.
      • Cow moos or lows; a calf also moos; an ox bellows.
      • Sheep, goat and lamb bleat; a ewe bleats.
      • Horse neighs or whinnies; a foal also neighs.
      • Donkey brays; a mule also brays.
      • Pig grunts; a camel and a hippopotamus also grunt.

      Wild Animals and Their Sounds

      In the wild the same word often covers a whole family of animals.

      • Lion roars; a roar is the deep sound of a lion.
      • Tiger, bear, leopard, jaguar, panther and gorilla growl.
      • Wolf and coyote howl.
      • Fox yelps; a fox may also bark.
      • Elephant trumpets; a rhinoceros snorts.
      • Deer, buffalo, bison, moose and koala bellow; an alligator and a crocodile also bellow.
      • Monkey chatters; a squirrel, a raccoon and a magpie also chatter.
      • Snake hisses; a skunk, a vulture and a tortoise also hiss.

      Birds and Their Calls

      Bird calls carry some of the most pleasant and most frequently asked words.

      • Hen clucks; a chick cheeps; a rooster crows.
      • Duck quacks; a duckling also quacks.
      • Goose, gander, swan and flamingo honk; a gosling also honks.
      • Turkey gobbles.
      • Owl hoots; a chimpanzee also hoots.
      • Dove and pigeon coo.
      • Cuckoo cuckoos; a sparrow, a grasshopper, a cricket and a canary chirp.
      • Crow caws; a raven croaks; a peacock screams.
      • Parrot, parakeet, macaw and penguin squawk.
      • Hawk, eagle, falcon, bat and seagull screech.

      Insects, Reptiles and Water Creatures

      This group is small but it appears often enough to be learnt.

      • Bee, fly, mosquito and wasp buzz.
      • Frog and toad croak.
      • Cicada sings; a whale sings.
      • Dolphin clicks; a mouse, a rat and a rabbit squeak.
      • Cockroach hisses.
      • Hyena laughs; a llama hums.

      Words Shared by Several Animals

      A few sounds are so general that many creatures use them, and the paper likes to test exactly this overlap.

      • Bark: dog, seal, sea lion, baboon and fox.
      • Growl: tiger, bear, lion, leopard, jaguar, panther, gorilla and dog.
      • Bleat: sheep, goat, lamb, ewe, kid and panda.
      • Bellow: ox, deer, buffalo, bison, alligator, crocodile, moose and koala.
      • Grunt: pig, camel and hippopotamus.
      • Honk: goose, gander, swan, flamingo and gosling.
      • Buzz: bee, fly, mosquito and wasp.
      • Croak: frog, toad and raven.
      • Chatter: monkey, squirrel, raccoon, magpie and myna.
      • Screech: hawk, eagle, falcon, bat and seagull.
      • Squawk: parrot, parakeet, macaw and penguin.
      • Hiss: snake, skunk, vulture, cockroach and tortoise.
      • Squeak: mouse, rat and rabbit.
      • Chirp: sparrow, grasshopper, cricket, canary, cheetah and locust.
      • Howl: wolf and coyote.
      • Bray: donkey, mule and zebra.
      • Hoot: owl and chimpanzee.
      • Sing: whale and cicada.
      • Coo: dove and pigeon.

      More Than One Name for the Same Sound

      Some creatures have two accepted words, and the candidate should recognise both. A dog barks and also growls and whines; a horse neighs and also whinnies; a cat mews and also meows and purrs; a fox yelps and also barks; a coyote and a wolf howl, and both may at times be said to yelp. When the paper offers two words that both seem right, the more precise and more common word is the intended answer.

      Traps That Cost Marks

      • Writing that a lion growls, when the exact word for a lion is roar.
      • Forgetting that a raven croaks although a crow caws.
      • Mixing the bleat of a goat with the neigh of a horse.
      • Writing that a snake buzzes, when a snake hisses.
      • Confusing the trumpet of an elephant with the honk of a goose.
      • Using bark for a cat, when a cat mews.

      High-Yield Pairs to Memorise

      • Lion and roar; dog and bark; cat and mew.
      • Cow and moo; horse and neigh; donkey and bray.
      • Sheep and bleat; pig and grunt; elephant and trumpet.
      • Hen and cluck; duck and quack; goose and honk.
      • Owl and hoot; crow and caw; peacock and scream; cuckoo and cuckoo.
      • Bee and buzz; frog and croak; snake and hiss.

      A Revision Plan for This Block

      1. Make four columns: domestic, wild, birds, and insects and reptiles.
      2. Write every sound as a verb, as in "a dog barks", so the word is learnt in use.
      3. Mark the shared words with a star so that one word covers several animals.
      4. Revise one group a day and test yourself by covering the animal column.
      5. Revise the whole list twice a week and write one sentence for each sound.

      Sounds Used in Sentences

      The paper often tests a sound inside a sentence, and the candidate must supply the verb that fits the subject and the tense. A sentence may speak of a dog that barks at night, a cow that moos in the field, or an owl that hoots from a tree. Reading the sentence first, and asking which creature is named and what it does, answers the question more safely than recalling a list without looking at the context.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What sound does a lion make?

      A lion roars. The deep sound of a lion is called a roar, and it is the most commonly asked sound in this section.

      What is the sound made by a snake called?

      The sound made by a snake is called a hiss. A skunk, a vulture and a tortoise also hiss.

      Which animals buzz?

      A bee, a fly, a mosquito and a wasp buzz. The word buzz names the humming sound made by these insects.

      What is the sound of a donkey called?

      The sound of a donkey is called a bray. A mule and a zebra also bray.

      What is the sound made by an elephant called?

      The sound made by an elephant is called a trumpet. It is quite different from the honk of a goose or the bellow of a buffalo.

      অসম আৰক্ষী কনিষ্টবল (AB & UB) পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে জন্তু আৰু চৰাইৰ শব্দ আৰু ধ্বনি

      জন্তু আৰু চৰাইৰ শব্দ আৰু ধ্বনিৰ প্ৰশ্ন সাধাৰণ ইংৰাজী প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ শব্দভাণ্ডাৰ অংশৰ ভিতৰত পৰে, আৰু এইবোৰ গোটেই অংশৰ ভিতৰত আটাইতকৈ বেছি নম্বৰ দিয়া প্ৰশ্ন। পৰীক্ষকে জন্তু বা চৰাই এটাৰ নাম দি তাৰ শব্দৰ শব্দটো সুধে, বা শব্দটো দি কোনে কৰে সেয়া সুধে। ইয়াত পঢ়িবলৈ কোনো অনুচ্ছেদ নাই আৰু প্ৰয়োগ কৰিবলৈ কোনো নিয়ম নাই; উত্তৰ সম্পূৰ্ণৰূপে এটা স্পষ্ট আৰু সঠিকভাৱে গোট খোৱা তালিকাৰ ওপৰত নিৰ্ভৰ কৰে। অসুবিধা হ'ল যে বহু প্ৰাণীয়ে একেই ক্ৰিয়া ভাগ কৰে, কেইটামান শব্দৰ দুটা স্বীকৃত নাম থাকে, আৰু অলপ শব্দই একে যেন লাগে কিন্তু বেলেগ জন্তুৰ। এই পৃষ্ঠাই শব্দবোৰ গোট অনুসৰি সজায়, ভাগ কৰা শব্দবোৰ চিহ্নিত কৰে, সাধাৰণ বিভ্ৰান্তিবোৰ বুজায়, আৰু প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰত পুনৰাবৃত্তি হোৱা উচ্চ-লাভজনক যোৰবোৰ দিয়ে। সমল অসম নিযুক্তি পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত মানক ব্যাকৰণ আৰু শব্দভাণ্ডাৰৰ প্ৰসংগ পুথিৰ আধাৰত, আৰু প্ৰকাশৰ আগতে পৰ্যালোচনা কৰা হয়।

      এই অংশ কেনেকৈ সোধা হয়

      পৰীক্ষকে সাধাৰণতে এটা জন্তু আৰু চাৰিটা সম্ভাব্য শব্দ দিয়ে, বা চাৰিটা জন্তু দি এটা শব্দ কোনে কৰে সুধে। কেতিয়াবা প্ৰশ্ন ওলোটা হয় আৰু শব্দটো দি জন্তুটো সুধে। প্ৰতিটো ক্ষেত্ৰতে উত্তৰ স্মৃতিৰ পৰা আহে, সেয়ে ভালদৰে গোট খোৱা তালিকাখনেই গোটেই প্ৰস্তুতি। যিহেতু একেই শব্দ কেইবাটাও জন্তুৰ ক্ষেত্ৰত পুনৰাবৃত্তি হয়, সেয়ে জন্তুৰ সলনি শব্দ অনুসৰি গোট কৰা প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে বেছি সোনকালে আৰু কম ভুলেৰে উত্তৰ দিয়ে।

      এই খণ্ডৰ পুনৰাবৃত্তি পৰিকল্পনা

      1. চাৰিটা স্তম্ভ বনাওক: ঘৰচীয়া, বন্য, চৰাই, আৰু পোক-পৰুৱা তথা সৰীসৃপ।
      2. প্ৰতিটো শব্দ ক্ৰিয়া হিচাপে লিখক, যেনে "a dog barks", যাতে শব্দটো ব্যৱহাৰত শিকা যায়।
      3. ভাগ কৰা শব্দবোৰ তৰা চিহ্ন দি চিহ্নিত কৰক, যাতে এটা শব্দই কেইবাটাও জন্তু সামৰে।
      4. দিনে এটা গোট পুনৰাবৃত্তি কৰক আৰু জন্তুৰ স্তম্ভ ঢাকি নিজকে পৰীক্ষা কৰক।
      5. গোটেই তালিকা সপ্তাহত দুবাৰ পুনৰাবৃত্তি কৰক আৰু প্ৰতিটো শব্দৰ বাবে এটা বাক্য লিখক।

      বাক্যত ব্যৱহৃত শব্দ

      প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰই প্ৰায়ে এটা বাক্যৰ ভিতৰত শব্দ পৰীক্ষা কৰে, আৰু প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে কৰ্তা আৰু কালৰ লগত খাপ খোৱা ক্ৰিয়াটো দিব লাগে। এটা বাক্যই ৰাতি bark কৰা কুকুৰ, পথাৰত moo কৰা গৰু, বা গছৰ পৰা hoot কৰা ফেঁচাৰ কথা ক'ব পাৰে। প্ৰথমে বাক্যটো পঢ়ি, আৰু কোনটো প্ৰাণীৰ নাম লোৱা হৈছে আৰু ই কি কৰে সেয়া সুধি, প্ৰসংগ নোচোৱাকৈ তালিকা মনত পেলোৱাতকৈ বেছি নিৰাপদভাৱে উত্তৰ দিয়া যায়।

      সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

      সিংহই কি শব্দ কৰে?

      সিংহই roar কৰে। সিংহৰ গভীৰ শব্দক roar বোলা হয়, আৰু এই অংশত আটাইতকৈ বেছি সোধা শব্দ ইয়েই।

      সাপৰ শব্দক কি বোলা হয়?

      সাপৰ শব্দক hiss বোলা হয়। Skunk, vulture আৰু tortoiseও hiss কৰে।

      কোনবোৰ প্ৰাণীয়ে buzz কৰে?

      Bee, fly, mosquito আৰু wasp এ buzz কৰে। Buzz শব্দই এই পোকবোৰৰ গুণগুণনি ধ্বনিৰ নাম দিয়ে।

      গাধৰ শব্দক কি বোলা হয়?

      গাধৰ শব্দক bray বোলা হয়। Mule আৰু zebraও bray কৰে।

      হাতীৰ শব্দক কি বোলা হয়?

      হাতীৰ শব্দক trumpet বোলা হয়। ই goose ৰ honk বা buffalo ৰ bellow ৰ পৰা সম্পূৰ্ণ বেলেগ।

    Q1. Choose the correct option: She ___ to school every day.
    শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছনি কৰক: She ___ to school every day.
    (A) goes goes
    (B) go go
    (C) going going
    (D) gone gone
    Answer: A) goes
    A) goes
    Explanation: In the simple present tense a third-person singular subject (She) takes the verb form 'goes'.
    Simple present tense ত তৃতীয় পুৰুষ একবচন কৰ্তা (She) ৰ সৈতে 'goes' ৰূপ ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    Q2. Choose the correct option: He ___ his homework now.
    শুদ্ধ বিকল্পটো বাছনি কৰক: He ___ his homework now.
    (A) is doing is doing
    (B) does does
    (C) did did
    (D) has done has done
    Answer: A) is doing
    A) is doing
    Explanation: 'Now' shows an action in progress, so the present continuous 'is doing' is used.
    'Now' এ চলি থকা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়, গতিকে present continuous 'is doing' ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।

    Tenses Rules for the Assam Police Constable (AB & UB) Exam

    Tense is the form of the verb that tells us the time of an action and also whether the action is complete, continuing or habitual, and it is the single largest part of the General English paper. The examiner usually gives a sentence with a blank and four forms of a verb, and the candidate must choose the form that fits the time and the sense. There is no long passage to read; the answer depends on a few clear rules and on the time words placed in the sentence. The difficulty is that several forms look alike, that a few verbs are irregular, and that two tenses can sometimes both seem possible until the time marker settles the choice. This page arranges the twelve tenses by time, gives the time words that point to each, sets out the rules of sequence, and lists the high-yield patterns that repeat in the paper. The material follows the standard grammar reference books used for Assam recruitment examinations and is reviewed before it is published.

    How This Portion Is Set

    The examiner usually gives one sentence with a blank and four verb forms, and asks for the correct one. Sometimes the whole sentence is given and the error in the verb has to be found. In both cases the time words, such as now, yesterday, since, already and tomorrow, decide the tense before the verb form is even considered. A candidate who reads the sentence for its time signal first answers faster and with fewer mistakes.

    The Twelve Tenses at a Glance

    • Simple present: I write; simple past: I wrote; simple future: I shall write.
    • Present continuous: I am writing; past continuous: I was writing; future continuous: I shall be writing.
    • Present perfect: I have written; past perfect: I had written; future perfect: I shall have written.
    • Present perfect continuous: I have been writing; past perfect continuous: I had been writing; future perfect continuous: I shall have been writing.

    Present Tenses and Their Uses

    The simple present is used for habitual actions, universal truths and fixed timetables, as in she goes to school every day, water boils at 100 degrees and the train leaves at seven. The present continuous is used for an action happening now or around now, as in he is doing his homework now. The present perfect joins the past to the present, as in I have lost my keys, and the present perfect continuous shows an action that began in the past and is still going on, as in she has been living here for ten years.

    Past Tenses and Their Uses

    The simple past names a finished action at a known past time, as in I met him last Sunday. The past continuous shows an action that was in progress when another action happened, as in he was watching television when I called. The past perfect shows the earlier of two past actions, as in the train had left before we reached the station, and the past perfect continuous shows an action that had been going on for some time in the past, as in he had been walking for two hours when we met him.

    Future Tenses and Their Uses

    The simple future names an action yet to happen, as in we shall go to Delhi next week. The future continuous shows an action that will be in progress, as in I shall be waiting for you. The future perfect shows an action that will be complete before a point in the future, as in he will have finished his work by five tomorrow, and the future perfect continuous shows how long an action will have been going on.

    Time Markers That Point to the Tense

    Certain words almost always fix the tense, and they should be learnt as signals.

    • every day, usually, always and generally point to the simple present.
    • now, at present and look point to the present continuous.
    • yesterday, last week and ago point to the simple past.
    • while and when with a past action point to the past continuous.
    • already, just, never, ever, so far and yet point to the present perfect.
    • since and for with an action still continuing point to the present perfect continuous.
    • before, after and by the time with two past actions point to the past perfect.
    • tomorrow, next week and soon point to the future.

    Rules We Must Remember

    • With a universal truth or a habitual fact the simple present is used even if the main clause is in the past.
    • After since and for, when the action continues, the present perfect or the present perfect continuous is used.
    • In a sentence with two past actions, the earlier one takes the past perfect and the later one the simple past.
    • After if, when, until, as soon as and before, the present tense is used even when the sense is of the future.
    • The third person singular takes s or es in the simple present.
    • The perfect tenses always use the past participle, as in have seen and had done.

    Traps That Cost Marks

    • Using the simple past where the present perfect is needed, as in I saw him already instead of I have seen him already.
    • Forgetting the past perfect for the earlier of two past actions.
    • Writing has went instead of has gone, since the past participle is needed.
    • Using since with a period and for with a point of time, as in since two hours.
    • Using will with if or when, as in if it will rain, instead of if it rains.
    • Mixing the continuous form with a non-continuous verb such as know or believe.

    High-Yield Rules to Memorise

    • She goes to school every day; water boils at 100 degrees.
    • He is doing his homework now; look, the baby is sleeping.
    • I have seen this movie already; she has lived here since 2015.
    • The train had left before we reached the station.
    • He will have finished his work by five tomorrow.
    • We shall go to Delhi next week.

    A Revision Plan for This Block

    1. Learn the twelve tenses in a table, with one example sentence for each.
    2. Learn the time markers as a separate list, because they decide the tense.
    3. Write the form of each tense with the same verb, as in write, wrote, written.
    4. Learn the irregular past forms of the common verbs by heart.
    5. Practise ten fill-in-the-blank sentences a day and read the time marker before choosing.

    Tenses Used in Sentences

    The paper tests tense inside a sentence, and the candidate must supply the form that agrees with the subject and the time. A sentence may speak of a boy who goes to school every day, a girl who is cooking dinner when the guests arrive, or a team that has won the match. Reading the sentence first, and asking when the action happens, answers the question more safely than recalling a table of forms without looking at the context.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which tense is used with since and for?

    When the action continues up to the present, the present perfect or the present perfect continuous is used with since and for, as in she has lived here since 2015 and he has been working for two hours.

    When do we use the past perfect tense?

    The past perfect is used for the earlier of two past actions, as in the train had left before we reached the station.

    Which tense is used for universal truths?

    The simple present is used for universal truths and habitual facts, as in water boils at 100 degrees and the sun rises in the east.

    Can we use will after if or when?

    No. After if, when, until, as soon as and before, the present tense is used even when the sense is of the future, as in if it rains, we shall stay at home.

    What is the difference between the simple past and the present perfect?

    The simple past names a finished action at a known past time, as in I met him yesterday, while the present perfect links the past to the present, as in I have lost my keys and cannot find them.

    কাল (Tense) হৈছে ক্ৰিয়াৰ সেই ৰূপ যিয়ে কাৰ্যৰ সময় আৰু কাৰ্যটো সম্পূৰ্ণ, চলি থকা নে অভ্যাসগত সেয়া কয়, আৰু এইটোৱেই সাধাৰণ ইংৰাজী প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰৰ আটাইতকৈ ডাঙৰ অংশ। পৰীক্ষকে সাধাৰণতে এটা বাক্যত খালী ঠাই আৰু ক্ৰিয়াৰ চাৰিটা ৰূপ দি সময় আৰু অৰ্থৰ লগত খাপ খোৱা ৰূপটো বাছনি কৰিবলৈ কয়। ইয়াত পঢ়িবলৈ দীঘল অনুচ্ছেদ নাই; উত্তৰ কেইটামান স্পষ্ট নিয়ম আৰু বাক্যত থকা সময়-শব্দৰ ওপৰত নিৰ্ভৰ কৰে। অসুবিধা হ'ল যে কেইবাটাও ৰূপ একে যেন লাগে, কেইটামান ক্ৰিয়া অনিয়মিত, আৰু কেতিয়াবা সময়-চিহ্নই বাছনি স্থিৰ নকৰালৈকে দুটা কাল দুয়োটা শুদ্ধ যেন লাগে। এই পৃষ্ঠাই বাৰটা কাল সময় অনুসৰি সজায়, প্ৰতিটোলৈ সূচনা কৰা সময়-শব্দ দিয়ে, ক্ৰমৰ নিয়মবোৰ দিয়ে, আৰু প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰত পুনৰাবৃত্তি হোৱা উচ্চ-লাভজনক আৰ্হিবোৰৰ তালিকা দিয়ে। সমল অসম নিযুক্তি পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত মানক ব্যাকৰণ প্ৰসংগ পুথিৰ আধাৰত, আৰু প্ৰকাশৰ আগতে পৰ্যালোচনা কৰা হয়।

    এই অংশ কেনেকৈ সোধা হয়

    পৰীক্ষকে সাধাৰণতে এটা খালী ঠাই থকা বাক্য আৰু ক্ৰিয়াৰ চাৰিটা ৰূপ দি শুদ্ধটো সুধে। কেতিয়াবা গোটেই বাক্যটো দি ক্ৰিয়াৰ ভুল বিচাৰিবলৈ কয়। দুয়ো ক্ষেত্ৰতে now, yesterday, since, already আৰু tomorrow দৰে সময়-শব্দই ক্ৰিয়াৰ ৰূপ চোৱাৰ আগতেই কাল নিৰ্ধাৰণ কৰে। যি প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে প্ৰথমে সময়ৰ সংকেতৰ বাবে বাক্যটো পঢ়ে তেওঁ বেছি সোনকালে আৰু কম ভুলেৰে উত্তৰ দিয়ে।

    বাৰটা কাল এক নজৰত

    • Simple present: I write; simple past: I wrote; simple future: I shall write.
    • Present continuous: I am writing; past continuous: I was writing; future continuous: I shall be writing.
    • Present perfect: I have written; past perfect: I had written; future perfect: I shall have written.
    • Present perfect continuous: I have been writing; past perfect continuous: I had been writing; future perfect continuous: I shall have been writing.

    বৰ্তমান কাল আৰু ইয়াৰ প্ৰয়োগ

    Simple present অভ্যাসগত কাৰ্য, সাৰ্বজনীন সত্য আৰু নিৰ্দিষ্ট সময়সূচীৰ বাবে ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে she goes to school every day, water boils at 100 degrees আৰু the train leaves at seven। Present continuous এতিয়া বা এতিয়াৰ আশে-পাশে হৈ থকা কাৰ্যৰ বাবে, যেনে he is doing his homework now। Present perfect ত অতীতক বৰ্তমানৰ সৈতে জুৰে, যেনে I have lost my keys, আৰু present perfect continuous এ অতীতত আৰম্ভ হৈ এতিয়াও চলি থকা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়, যেনে she has been living here for ten years।

    অতীত কাল আৰু ইয়াৰ প্ৰয়োগ

    Simple past এ জনাজাত অতীত সময়ত শেষ হোৱা কাৰ্যৰ নাম দিয়ে, যেনে I met him last Sunday। Past continuous এ দেখুৱায় যে এটা কাৰ্য চলি থকাৰ সময়ত আন এটা ঘটিছিল, যেনে he was watching television when I called। Past perfect এ দুটা অতীত কাৰ্যৰ আগৰটো দেখুৱায়, যেনে the train had left before we reached the station, আৰু past perfect continuous এ অতীতত কিছু সময় ধৰি চলি থকা কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়, যেনে he had been walking for two hours when we met him।

    ভৱিষ্যৎ কাল আৰু ইয়াৰ প্ৰয়োগ

    Simple future এ এতিয়াও ঘটা নাইকিয়া কাৰ্যৰ নাম দিয়ে, যেনে we shall go to Delhi next week। Future continuous এ চলি থাকিবলগীয়া কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়, যেনে I shall be waiting for you। Future perfect এ ভৱিষ্যতৰ এটা বিন্দুৰ আগতে সম্পূৰ্ণ হ'বলগীয়া কাৰ্য দেখুৱায়, যেনে he will have finished his work by five tomorrow, আৰু future perfect continuous এ দেখুৱায় কাৰ্য কিমান সময় ধৰি চলি থাকিব।

    কাল নিৰ্দেশ কৰা সময়-চিহ্ন

    কিছুমান শব্দই প্ৰায় সদায় কাল স্থিৰ কৰে, আৰু এইবোৰ সংকেত হিচাপে শিকা উচিত।

    • every day, usually, always আৰু generally এ simple present লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • now, at present আৰু look এ present continuous লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • yesterday, last week আৰু ago এ simple past লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • অতীত কাৰ্যৰ সৈতে while আৰু when এ past continuous লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • already, just, never, ever, so far আৰু yet এ present perfect লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • এতিয়াও চলি থকা কাৰ্যৰ সৈতে since আৰু for এ present perfect continuous লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • দুটা অতীত কাৰ্যৰ সৈতে before, after আৰু by the time এ past perfect লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।
    • tomorrow, next week আৰু soon এ ভৱিষ্যৎকাল লৈ আঙুলিয়ায়।

    মনত ৰখা নিয়ম

    • সাৰ্বজনীন সত্য বা অভ্যাসগত তথ্যৰ সৈতে মূল বাক্য অতীতত থাকিলেও simple present ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    • Since আৰু for ৰ পিছত কাৰ্য চলি থাকিলে present perfect বা present perfect continuous ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    • দুটা অতীত কাৰ্য থকা বাক্যত আগৰটোৱে past perfect আৰু পিছৰটোৱে simple past লয়।
    • If, when, until, as soon as আৰু before ৰ পিছত অৰ্থ ভৱিষ্যতৰ হ'লেও present tense ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।
    • Simple present ত তৃতীয় পুৰুষ একবচনত s বা es যোগ হয়।
    • Perfect কালত সদায় past participle ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে have seen আৰু had done।

    নম্বৰ কাঢ়ি নিয়া ফান্দ

    • Present perfect লাগিবলগীয়া ঠাইত simple past ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা, যেনে I have seen him already ৰ সলনি I saw him already।
    • দুটা অতীত কাৰ্যৰ আগৰটোৰ বাবে past perfect পাহৰি যোৱা।
    • Has went ৰ সলনি has gone লিখা, কাৰণ past participle লাগে।
    • সময়সীমাৰ সৈতে since আৰু বিন্দুৰ সৈতে for ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা, যেনে since two hours।
    • If বা when ৰ সৈতে will ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা, যেনে if it will rain ৰ সলনি if it rains।
    • Know বা believe দৰে অ-চলনশীল ক্ৰিয়াৰ সৈতে continuous ৰূপ মিলাই পেলোৱা।

    মনত ৰখাৰ যোগ্য উচ্চ-লাভজনক নিয়ম

    • She goes to school every day; water boils at 100 degrees.
    • He is doing his homework now; look, the baby is sleeping.
    • I have seen this movie already; she has lived here since 2015.
    • The train had left before we reached the station.
    • He will have finished his work by five tomorrow.
    • We shall go to Delhi next week.

    এই খণ্ডৰ পুনৰাবৃত্তি পৰিকল্পনা

    1. বাৰটা কাল এটা তালিকাত, প্ৰতিটোৰ বাবে এটা উদাহৰণ বাক্যৰ সৈতে শিকক।
    2. সময়-চিহ্নবোৰ সুকীয়া তালিকা হিচাপে শিকক, কাৰণ এইবোৰেই কাল নিৰ্ধাৰণ কৰে।
    3. একে ক্ৰিয়াৰে প্ৰতিটো কালৰ ৰূপ লিখক, যেনে write, wrote, written।
    4. সাধাৰণ ক্ৰিয়াবোৰৰ অনিয়মিত অতীত ৰূপ মনত ৰাখক।
    5. দিনে দহটা খালী-ঠাইৰ বাক্য অনুশীলন কৰক আৰু ৰূপ বাছনিৰ আগতে সময়-চিহ্ন পঢ়ক।

    বাক্যত ব্যৱহৃত কাল

    প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰই এটা বাক্যৰ ভিতৰত কাল পৰীক্ষা কৰে, আৰু প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে কৰ্তা আৰু সময়ৰ লগত মিলা ৰূপটো দিব লাগে। এটা বাক্যই প্ৰতিদিনে স্কুললৈ যোৱা ল'ৰা, অতিথি অহাৰ সময়ত ৰান্ধি থকা ছোৱালী, বা খেল জিকি লোৱা দলৰ কথা ক'ব পাৰে। প্ৰথমে বাক্যটো পঢ়ি, আৰু কাৰ্যটো কেতিয়া ঘটে সেয়া সুধি, প্ৰসংগ নোচোৱাকৈ ৰূপৰ তালিকা মনত পেলোৱাতকৈ বেছি নিৰাপদভাৱে উত্তৰ দিয়া যায়।

    সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    Since আৰু for ৰ সৈতে কোনটো কাল ব্যৱহাৰ হয়?

    কাৰ্য বৰ্তমানলৈকে চলি থাকিলে since আৰু for ৰ সৈতে present perfect বা present perfect continuous ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে she has lived here since 2015 আৰু he has been working for two hours।

    Past perfect tense কেতিয়া ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা হয়?

    দুটা অতীত কাৰ্যৰ আগৰটোৰ বাবে past perfect ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে the train had left before we reached the station।

    সাৰ্বজনীন সত্যৰ বাবে কোনটো কাল ব্যৱহাৰ হয়?

    সাৰ্বজনীন সত্য আৰু অভ্যাসগত তথ্যৰ বাবে simple present ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে water boils at 100 degrees আৰু the sun rises in the east।

    If বা when ৰ পিছত will ব্যৱহাৰ কৰিব পাৰি নেকি?

    নোৱাৰি। If, when, until, as soon as আৰু before ৰ পিছত অৰ্থ ভৱিষ্যতৰ হ'লেও present tense ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে if it rains, we shall stay at home।

    Simple past আৰু present perfect ৰ মাজত কি পাৰ্থক্য?

    Simple past এ জনাজাত অতীত সময়ত শেষ হোৱা কাৰ্যৰ নাম দিয়ে, যেনে I met him yesterday, আনহাতে present perfect এ অতীতক বৰ্তমানৰ সৈতে জুৰে, যেনে I have lost my keys and cannot find them।

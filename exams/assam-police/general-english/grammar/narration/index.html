    Narration (Direct and Indirect Speech) for the Assam Police Constable (AB & UB) Exam

    Narration is the way we report the words of a speaker, either in the speaker's own words, called direct speech, or in our own words, called indirect speech. The General English paper tests narration by giving a sentence in direct speech and asking for its indirect form, or the reverse, and the question carries good marks because the change follows a fixed method. There is no passage to read; the answer depends on the kind of sentence, on the tense of the reporting verb, and on a short list of words that must be changed. The difficulty is that each kind of sentence, whether a statement, a question, a command or an exclamation, has its own reporting verb and its own pattern, and that pronouns and time words change by rule. This page explains the method, gives the rules for the change of tense, pronoun and word, handles each kind of sentence, and lists the high-yield patterns that repeat in the paper. The material follows the standard grammar reference books used for Assam recruitment examinations and is reviewed before it is published.

    How This Portion Is Set

    The examiner usually gives a sentence in direct speech and asks for its indirect form, or gives an indirect sentence and asks for the direct form. Sometimes four indirect forms are given and the correct one has to be chosen. In every case the candidate must first decide whether the sentence is a statement, a question, a command or an exclamation, then choose the reporting verb, and then apply the changes of tense, pronoun and word. A candidate who follows this fixed method answers faster and with fewer mistakes.

    Direct Speech and Indirect Speech

    • Direct speech repeats the exact words of the speaker and uses inverted commas, as in he said, "I am tired."
    • Indirect speech reports the words without inverted commas, as in he said that he was tired.
    • The clause outside the commas is called the reporting clause, and the words inside are called the reported speech.

    The Steps of the Change

    1. Remove the inverted commas and join the two parts with the right word.
    2. Change the tense of the reported speech if the reporting verb is in the past.
    3. Change the pronouns according to the person of the speaker and the listener.
    4. Change the words that show nearness in time and place to words that show distance.

    Change of Tense

    When the reporting verb is in the past, the tense of the reported speech moves one step back.

    • Simple present becomes simple past, as in am becomes was.
    • Present continuous becomes past continuous, as in is reading becomes was reading.
    • Present perfect becomes past perfect, as in have finished becomes had finished.
    • Simple past becomes past perfect, as in saw becomes had seen.
    • Will becomes would, shall becomes should, can becomes could, may becomes might and must becomes had to.

    Change of Pronouns

    The pronoun is changed according to the person of the speaker and the listener. First person pronouns change to the person of the speaker, second person pronouns change to the person of the listener, and third person pronouns do not change. In he said, "I am tired", I becomes he because the speaker is he.

    Change of Words Showing Time and Place

    • now becomes then; here becomes there.
    • this becomes that; these become those.
    • today becomes that day; yesterday becomes the previous day or the day before.
    • tomorrow becomes the next day or the following day.
    • last night becomes the previous night; next week becomes the following week.
    • ago becomes before; thus becomes so.

    Statements

    A statement is joined with that, and the reporting verb said is used, as in he said that he was tired. When the listener is named, said to becomes told, as in mother told me that she was busy.

    Questions

    A question is joined with if or whether when it has no question word, and with the question word itself when there is one. The reporting verb becomes asked, and the word order becomes that of a statement.

    • She said, "Do you like tea?" becomes she asked if I liked tea.
    • He said to me, "Where do you live?" becomes he asked me where I lived.
    • Who broke the glass becomes he asked who had broken the glass.

    Commands, Requests and Advice

    A command, a request or a piece of advice is joined with to, and the verb becomes an infinitive. The reporting verb is chosen to suit the sense, such as ordered, told, requested, advised or warned. A negative command uses not to.

    • The officer said, "Go away at once!" becomes the officer ordered us to go away at once.
    • He said to her, "Please help me." becomes he requested her to help him.
    • Mother said to me, "Don't touch the wire." becomes mother warned me not to touch the wire.

    Exclamations and Wishes

    An exclamation is joined with that, and the reporting verb becomes exclaimed with joy, sorrow or surprise, according to the sense. A wish or a prayer takes wished or prayed.

    • He said, "Hurrah! We have won the match." becomes he exclaimed with joy that they had won the match.
    • She said, "Alas! I failed the exam." becomes she exclaimed with sorrow that she had failed the exam.
    • He said, "May you live long!" becomes he prayed that I might live long.

    Cases Where the Tense Does Not Change

    • When the reporting verb is in the present tense, as in he says that he is tired.
    • When the reported speech is a universal truth, as in the teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun.
    • When the reported speech is a habitual fact.

    Traps That Cost Marks

    • Forgetting to change the tense when the reporting verb is in the past.
    • Changing the third person pronouns, which do not change at all.
    • Keeping now, today and tomorrow unchanged.
    • Using that after a question instead of if or whether.
    • Using to with a negative command instead of not to.
    • Changing the tense of a universal truth.

    High-Yield Sentences to Memorise

    • He said, "I am tired": he said that he was tired.
    • She said, "I will come tomorrow": she said that she would come the next day.
    • He said to me, "Where do you live?": he asked me where I lived.
    • He said to her, "Please help me.": he requested her to help him.
    • The officer said, "Go away!": the officer ordered us to go away.
    • He said, "Hurrah! We have won.": he exclaimed with joy that they had won.

    A Revision Plan for This Block

    1. Learn the four steps of the change and apply them in the same order every time.
    2. Learn the tense-change table by heart, with one example each.
    3. Learn the pronoun rule and the list of time and place words.
    4. Learn the pattern of each kind of sentence separately.
    5. Practise ten sentences a day, from direct to indirect and from indirect to direct.

    Narration Used in Sentences

    The paper tests narration inside a full sentence, and the candidate must change the whole structure and not merely one word. A sentence may report a boy who said that he was tired, a girl who asked where I lived, or an officer who ordered us to go away. Reading the sentence first, and deciding its kind, answers the question more safely than recalling a pattern without looking at the context.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the difference between direct and indirect speech?

    Direct speech repeats the exact words of the speaker and uses inverted commas, as in he said, "I am tired." Indirect speech reports the words in our own words, as in he said that he was tired.

    How do pronouns change in indirect speech?

    First person pronouns change to the person of the speaker, second person pronouns change to the person of the listener, and third person pronouns do not change, as in he said, "I am tired" becomes he said that he was tired.

    How is a question changed into indirect speech?

    A question is joined with if or whether when it has no question word and with the question word when there is one, the reporting verb becomes asked, and the word order becomes that of a statement, as in he asked me where I lived.

    Does the tense change in indirect speech for a universal truth?

    No. The tense does not change for a universal truth, as in the teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun.

    How is a command changed into indirect speech?

    A command is joined with to and the verb becomes an infinitive, with a reporting verb such as ordered, told or advised, as in the officer ordered us to go away. A negative command uses not to.

    অসম আৰক্ষী কনিষ্টবল (AB & UB) পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে উক্তি (Narration / Direct-Indirect Speech)

    Narration হৈছে বক্তাৰ কথাক প্ৰকাশ কৰাৰ ধৰণ, হয় বক্তাৰ নিজৰ শব্দত, যাক direct speech বোলা হয়, নহয় আমাৰ নিজৰ শব্দত, যাক indirect speech বোলা হয়। সাধাৰণ ইংৰাজী প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰই direct speech ত বাক্য দি তাৰ indirect ৰূপ সুধি, বা ওলোটা কৰি narration পৰীক্ষা কৰে, আৰু এই প্ৰশ্নই ভাল নম্বৰ কঢ়িয়ায় কাৰণ পৰিৱৰ্তন এটা নিৰ্দিষ্ট পদ্ধতি অনুসৰণ কৰে। ইয়াত পঢ়িবলৈ অনুচ্ছেদ নাই; উত্তৰ বাক্যৰ ধৰণ, reporting verb ৰ কাল, আৰু সলাবলগীয়া অলপ শব্দৰ ওপৰত নিৰ্ভৰ কৰে। অসুবিধা হ'ল যে প্ৰতিটো ধৰণৰ বাক্য — statement, question, command বা exclamation — ৰ নিজা reporting verb আৰু নিজা আৰ্হি থাকে, আৰু সৰ্বনাম তথা সময়-শব্দ নিয়ম অনুসৰি সলনি হয়। এই পৃষ্ঠাই পদ্ধতিটো বুজায়, কাল, সৰ্বনাম আৰু শব্দ পৰিৱৰ্তনৰ নিয়ম দিয়ে, প্ৰতিটো ধৰণৰ বাক্য সামৰে, আৰু প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰত পুনৰাবৃত্তি হোৱা উচ্চ-লাভজনক আৰ্হিবোৰৰ তালিকা দিয়ে। সমল অসম নিযুক্তি পৰীক্ষাৰ বাবে ব্যৱহৃত মানক ব্যাকৰণ প্ৰসংগ পুথিৰ আধাৰত, আৰু প্ৰকাশৰ আগতে পৰ্যালোচনা কৰা হয়।

    এই অংশ কেনেকৈ সোধা হয়

    পৰীক্ষকে সাধাৰণতে direct speech ত বাক্য দি তাৰ indirect ৰূপ সুধে, বা indirect বাক্য দি direct ৰূপ সুধে। কেতিয়াবা চাৰিটা indirect ৰূপ দি শুদ্ধটো বাছনি কৰিবলৈ কয়। প্ৰতিটো ক্ষেত্ৰতে প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে প্ৰথমে নিৰ্ণয় কৰিব লাগে যে বাক্যটো statement, question, command নে exclamation, তাৰপিছত reporting verb বাছনি কৰি কাল, সৰ্বনাম আৰু শব্দৰ পৰিৱৰ্তন প্ৰয়োগ কৰিব লাগে। যি প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে এই নিৰ্দিষ্ট পদ্ধতি অনুসৰণ কৰে তেওঁ বেছি সোনকালে আৰু কম ভুলেৰে উত্তৰ দিয়ে।

    Direct Speech আৰু Indirect Speech

    • Direct speech এ বক্তাৰ সঠিক শব্দ পুনৰাবৃত্তি কৰি inverted comma ব্যৱহাৰ কৰে, যেনে he said, "I am tired."
    • Indirect speech এ inverted comma অবিহনে কথাক প্ৰকাশ কৰে, যেনে he said that he was tired।
    • Comma ৰ বাহিৰৰ অংশক reporting clause আৰু ভিতৰৰ শব্দক reported speech বোলা হয়।

    পৰিৱৰ্তনৰ পদক্ষেপ

    1. Inverted comma আঁতৰাই শুদ্ধ শব্দৰে দুয়ো অংশ জুৰক।
    2. Reporting verb অতীতত থাকিলে reported speech ৰ কাল সলাওক।
    3. বক্তা আৰু শ্ৰোতাৰ পুৰুষ অনুসৰি সৰ্বনাম সলাওক।
    4. সময় আৰু স্থানৰ নৈকট্য দেখুওৱা শব্দক দূৰত্ব দেখুওৱা শব্দলৈ সলাওক।

    কালৰ পৰিৱৰ্তন

    Reporting verb অতীতত থাকিলে reported speech ৰ কাল এখোজ পিছুৱায়।

    • Simple present → simple past, যেনে am → was।
    • Present continuous → past continuous, যেনে is reading → was reading।
    • Present perfect → past perfect, যেনে have finished → had finished।
    • Simple past → past perfect, যেনে saw → had seen।
    • Will → would, shall → should, can → could, may → might আৰু must → had to।

    সৰ্বনামৰ পৰিৱৰ্তন

    বক্তা আৰু শ্ৰোতাৰ পুৰুষ অনুসৰি সৰ্বনাম সলনি হয়। প্ৰথম পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম বক্তাৰ পুৰুষলৈ, দ্বিতীয় পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম শ্ৰোতাৰ পুৰুষলৈ সলনি হয়, আৰু তৃতীয় পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম নসলনি। He said, "I am tired" ত I, he হৈ পৰে কাৰণ বক্তা he।

    সময় আৰু স্থান দেখুওৱা শব্দৰ পৰিৱৰ্তন

    • now → then; here → there।
    • this → that; these → those।
    • today → that day; yesterday → the previous day বা the day before।
    • tomorrow → the next day বা the following day।
    • last night → the previous night; next week → the following week।
    • ago → before; thus → so।

    Statement

    Statement ক that ৰে জুৰে আৰু reporting verb said ব্যৱহাৰ হয়, যেনে he said that he was tired। শ্ৰোতাৰ নাম লোৱা হ'লে said to, told হৈ পৰে, যেনে mother told me that she was busy।

    Question

    Question ত প্ৰশ্ন-শব্দ নাথাকিলে if বা whether ৰে জুৰে, আৰু প্ৰশ্ন-শব্দ থাকিলে সেই শব্দৰে জুৰে। Reporting verb asked হয়, আৰু শব্দ-ক্ৰম statement ৰ দৰে হয়।

    • She said, "Do you like tea?" → she asked if I liked tea।
    • He said to me, "Where do you live?" → he asked me where I lived।
    • Who broke the glass → he asked who had broken the glass।

    Command, Request আৰু Advice

    Command, request বা advice ক to ৰে জুৰে আৰু ক্ৰিয়া infinitive হয়। অৰ্থ অনুসৰি reporting verb বাছনি কৰা হয়, যেনে ordered, told, requested, advised বা warned। নঞৰ্থক command ত not to ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।

    • The officer said, "Go away at once!" → the officer ordered us to go away at once।
    • He said to her, "Please help me." → he requested her to help him।
    • Mother said to me, "Don't touch the wire." → mother warned me not to touch the wire।

    Exclamation আৰু Wish

    Exclamation ক that ৰে জুৰে আৰু অৰ্থ অনুসৰি reporting verb exclaimed with joy, sorrow বা surprise হয়। Wish বা prayer য়ে wished বা prayed লয়।

    • He said, "Hurrah! We have won the match." → he exclaimed with joy that they had won the match।
    • She said, "Alas! I failed the exam." → she exclaimed with sorrow that she had failed the exam।
    • He said, "May you live long!" → he prayed that I might live long।

    কাল নসলনি হোৱা ক্ষেত্ৰ

    • Reporting verb বৰ্তমান কালত থাকিলে, যেনে he says that he is tired।
    • Reported speech সাৰ্বজনীন সত্য হ'লে, যেনে the teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun।
    • Reported speech অভ্যাসগত তথ্য হ'লে।

    নম্বৰ কাঢ়ি নিয়া ফান্দ

    • Reporting verb অতীতত থাকিলে কাল সলাবলৈ পাহৰি যোৱা।
    • তৃতীয় পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম সলোৱা, যি কেতিয়াও নসলনি।
    • Now, today আৰু tomorrow অপৰিৱৰ্তিত এৰি যোৱা।
    • Question ৰ পিছত if বা whether ৰ সলনি that ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
    • নঞৰ্থক command ত not to ৰ সলনি to ব্যৱহাৰ কৰা।
    • সাৰ্বজনীন সত্যৰ কাল সলোৱা।

    মনত ৰখাৰ যোগ্য উচ্চ-লাভজনক বাক্য

    • He said, "I am tired": he said that he was tired.
    • She said, "I will come tomorrow": she said that she would come the next day.
    • He said to me, "Where do you live?": he asked me where I lived.
    • He said to her, "Please help me.": he requested her to help him.
    • The officer said, "Go away!": the officer ordered us to go away.
    • He said, "Hurrah! We have won.": he exclaimed with joy that they had won.

    এই খণ্ডৰ পুনৰাবৃত্তি পৰিকল্পনা

    1. পৰিৱৰ্তনৰ চাৰিটা পদক্ষেপ শিকি প্ৰতিবাৰ একেই ক্ৰমত প্ৰয়োগ কৰক।
    2. কাল-পৰিৱৰ্তনৰ তালিকা মনত ৰাখক, প্ৰতিটোৰ বাবে এটা উদাহৰণৰ সৈতে।
    3. সৰ্বনামৰ নিয়ম আৰু সময়-স্থানৰ শব্দৰ তালিকা শিকক।
    4. প্ৰতিটো ধৰণৰ বাক্যৰ আৰ্হি সুকীয়াকৈ শিকক।
    5. দিনে দহটা বাক্য direct ৰ পৰা indirect আৰু indirect ৰ পৰা direct ৰূপেৰে অনুশীলন কৰক।

    বাক্যত ব্যৱহৃত Narration

    প্ৰশ্নপত্ৰই এটা সম্পূৰ্ণ বাক্যৰ ভিতৰত narration পৰীক্ষা কৰে, আৰু প্ৰাৰ্থীয়ে কেৱল এটা শব্দ নহয়, গোটেই গঠন সলাব লাগে। এটা বাক্যই tired বুলি কোৱা ল'ৰা, মই ক'ত থাকোঁ সুধা ছোৱালী, বা যাবলৈ আদেশ দিয়া বিষয়াৰ কথা ক'ব পাৰে। প্ৰথমে বাক্যটো পঢ়ি, ইয়াৰ ধৰণ নিৰ্ণয় কৰি, প্ৰসংগ নোচোৱাকৈ আৰ্হি মনত পেলোৱাতকৈ বেছি নিৰাপদভাৱে উত্তৰ দিয়া যায়।

    সাধু সোধা প্ৰশ্ন

    Direct আৰু indirect speech ৰ মাজত কি পাৰ্থক্য?

    Direct speech এ বক্তাৰ সঠিক শব্দ inverted comma ৰ সৈতে পুনৰাবৃত্তি কৰে, যেনে he said, "I am tired." Indirect speech এ আমাৰ শব্দত প্ৰকাশ কৰে, যেনে he said that he was tired।

    Indirect speech ত সৰ্বনাম কেনেকৈ সলনি হয়?

    প্ৰথম পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম বক্তাৰ পুৰুষলৈ, দ্বিতীয় পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম শ্ৰোতাৰ পুৰুষলৈ সলনি হয়, আৰু তৃতীয় পুৰুষৰ সৰ্বনাম নসলনি, যেনে he said, "I am tired" → he said that he was tired।

    Question ক indirect speech লৈ কেনেকৈ সলনি কৰা হয়?

    প্ৰশ্ন-শব্দ নাথাকিলে if বা whether ৰে আৰু থাকিলে প্ৰশ্ন-শব্দৰে জুৰে, reporting verb asked হয়, আৰু শব্দ-ক্ৰম statement ৰ দৰে হয়, যেনে he asked me where I lived।

    সাৰ্বজনীন সত্যৰ বাবে indirect speech ত কাল সলনি হয় নেকি?

    নহয়। সাৰ্বজনীন সত্যৰ বাবে কাল সলনি নহয়, যেনে the teacher said that the earth revolves around the sun।

    Command ক indirect speech লৈ কেনেকৈ সলনি কৰা হয়?

    Command ক to ৰে জুৰে আৰু ক্ৰিয়া infinitive হয়, ordered, told বা advised দৰে reporting verb ৰ সৈতে, যেনে the officer ordered us to go away। নঞৰ্থক command ত not to ব্যৱহাৰ হয়।

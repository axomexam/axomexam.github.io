    Official Sources & References

    Official exam portals, notification checks and reference material used to prepare and verify study content on axomexam.in.

    Study material is only useful if it matches the official examination. This page lists the official portals we monitor and explains how a notification is verified before it affects the content on axomexam.in. We are an independent website and are not connected to any of these organisations.

    Official exam portals

    Exam body What to check there Official portal
    SLPRB Assam Assam Police constable and sub-inspector notifications, syllabus, admit cards and results slprbassam.in
    SLRC Assam ADRE Grade III and Grade IV notifications, exam pattern and results slrcassam.in
    APSC Combined Competitive Examination and other commission examinations apsc.nic.in
    Staff Selection Commission CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS and other central notifications and answer keys ssc.gov.in
    Indian Railways RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP and other railway recruitment notices indianrailways.gov.in
    Gauhati High Court Grade III and Grade IV recruitment notices and results ghconline.gov.in

    How a notification is verified

    1. We open the notice on the official portal of the recruiting body, not on a coaching or news website.
    2. We check the advertisement number, the post, the number of vacancies and the last date against the PDF published by the recruiting body.
    3. We check whether a corrigendum or extension notice has been issued after the first advertisement.
    4. We update the relevant page only after the details match the official document.
    5. Where the official information is unclear, the page says so instead of filling the gap with a guess.

    Reference material

    Besides official notifications, our explanations are checked against standard school-level textbooks, public previous year question papers and widely accepted subject references. Questions and explanations are written in our own words. We do not reproduce chapters from commercial books, and the mention of any book elsewhere on the site is not a claim that its text has been copied.

    Reporting a source problem

    If a link is broken, a notification has changed or a page cites a source incorrectly, write to axomexam@outlook.com with the page address. See the editorial policy for how corrections are handled.

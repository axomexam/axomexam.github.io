      Privacy Policy

      This Privacy Policy explains how axomexam.in ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you visit or use our website, study material, mock tests, e-books, downloadable PDF notes and our Android app. It also describes the choices you have and how you can control the information connected with your use of the platform.

      By accessing or using axomexam.in you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of it, please do not use the website.

      1. Information We Collect

      1.1 Information you give us

      You can read, practise and download almost all study material without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, address, date of birth or payment details to access content. Personal information is collected only when you choose to provide it:

      • Contact form and email: your name, email address, subject and the content of your message.
      • Submit Q&A / contribution form: any name or email you enter along with the questions or corrections you submit.
      • Copyright or takedown requests: the details you provide to support your request.

      1.2 Information collected automatically

      Like most websites, when you visit axomexam.in our servers and third-party service providers automatically record certain technical information, including IP address, browser type and version, device type and operating system, language, the pages you visit, the date and time of your visit, the time spent and the referring page or link. This information is used in aggregate for security, troubleshooting, performance and usage analysis and does not directly identify you.

      1.3 Preferences stored on your device

      We use your browser's local storage to remember choices such as your selected language (English or Assamese) and light or dark theme. These settings stay on your device and are not used to identify you.

      2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

      Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. We and our third-party partners use cookies, web beacons, pixels and similar technologies for the purposes described below.

      • Strictly necessary / preference cookies: to keep the site working and remember your theme and language choices.
      • Analytics cookies: to understand how visitors use the site so that we can improve content and speed.
      • Advertising cookies: set by Google and other advertising partners to display and measure advertisements, including personalised advertising.

      You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the website (for example theme and language memory) may not work as intended. See section 3 below for how to opt out of personalised advertising specifically.

      3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

      axomexam.in is supported by advertising so that the study material can remain free. We use Google AdSense and may also work with other third-party advertising networks and vendors.

      • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and/or other websites on the internet.
      • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
      • Depending on your region and consent choices, Google may serve either personalised or non-personalised ads. Non-personalised ads are based on the content of the page rather than your past behaviour.
      • Third-party ad networks may collect information directly through their own cookies, web beacons or similar technologies, and their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies, not ours.

      How to opt out of personalised advertising

      • Visit Google Ads Settings to opt out of personalised advertising by Google.
      • Visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising.
      • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, visit www.youronlinechoices.eu to manage your advertising preferences.
      • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

      Opting out of personalised advertising does not mean you will stop seeing ads; it means the ads you see may be less relevant to your interests.

      4. How We Use Information

      • To operate, maintain and improve the website, content and app.
      • To respond to your queries, corrections, contributions and takedown requests.
      • To display and measure advertising that keeps the platform free.
      • To monitor for security incidents, abuse, spam and technical errors.
      • To understand which subjects and features are most useful and to plan new content.
      • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

      5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

      We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited situations:

      • Service providers and advertising partners: such as Google AdSense, hosting, analytics and email providers, who process information on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies.
      • Legal requirements: where we are required to disclose information to comply with a law, regulation, legal process or enforceable government request.
      • Protection of rights: where disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, the safety of users or the public, or to investigate fraud or security issues.
      • Business transfer: if the website or its assets are transferred or merged, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

      6. Data Retention

      We keep contact and contribution messages only for as long as needed to respond to and resolve them, and then for a reasonable period for record-keeping. Automatically collected technical and analytics data is retained in aggregate form for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Advertising partners retain data according to their own retention policies.

      7. Data Security

      We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including serving the website over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

      8. Your Rights and Choices

      Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

      • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
      • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
      • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
      • Opt out of personalised advertising (see section 3).
      • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

      To exercise any of these rights, email us at axomexam@outlook.com. We will respond within a reasonable period and may need to verify your request. Users in India may also exercise rights available under applicable data protection law.

      9. Children's Privacy

      axomexam.in is intended for candidates preparing for competitive examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age applicable in your country). If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

      10. Third-Party Links

      Some pages link to external websites such as official recruitment portals, app stores or reference sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies before using them.

      11. International Data Transfers

      Our service providers, including Google, may process and store information in countries other than your own. Where required, such transfers are carried out using appropriate safeguards as described in the providers' own privacy policies.

      12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

      We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page, and the "Last updated" date below will be revised. Continued use of the website after changes means you accept the updated policy.

      13. Contact Us

      If you have any questions, concerns or requests about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us:

      • Email: axomexam@outlook.com
      • Contact page: axomexam.in/contact
